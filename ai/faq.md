# Lichters FAQ: real questions from teens

Six small daily ticks, done honestly for years, matter more than any perfect day. Short answers to the questions people ask most.

## What if I miss a day?

Nothing happens. The day stays empty and you tick the next one. Two in a row is where habits usually die, so the only rule is: never miss twice. Don't tick a day you didn't do just to keep it green. A red, honest grid is worth more than a green, fake one.

## I don't have a business. Can I still tick b?

Yes, if you spend the 5+ focused hours building one: looking for a problem people pay to solve, talking to possible customers, making a first small offer. Watching videos about "how to start a business" mostly doesn't count. Your first idea will probably fail. That's fine at 13. More: https://dlichters.com/ai/business.md

## What if I have no time for 5 hours on school days?

6 hours school, 9 sleep, 2 training and 1 for meals leave 6, enough for 5. Friends fit into breaks and lunch; free periods count. Many people also find hours in their feeds. On weekends and holidays, do more. Never cut sleep for it.

## Why these six habits?

The goal is the happiest whole life, not just the happiest teen years. So the list mixes things that pay off now (friends, sleep, training) with things that pay off over decades (business, eating). Each has a page with the research, for example https://dlichters.com/ai/friends.md and https://dlichters.com/ai/sleep.md

## How do I fit all six into a school day?

An example: up at 7:00. Lunch with friends, phones away (f). Fruit or vegetables at every meal, water to drink (e). 14:00 to 17:00 Business, phone in another room (b). 17:00 gym or sport (t). 19:30 to 21:30 Business again (b). Feeds only after the ticks, under 30 minutes (o). 21:30 phone out of the bedroom (s).

## Can I change or add habits?

No. If you can edit the list, you'll quietly remove the hard ones on a bad day. Six fixed habits mean you never have to decide what counts.

## Why no meditation?

You can meditate privately, it just gets no box. There are only six spots, and the evidence for teens is weaker. A large UK school study (the MYRIAD trial, about 8,400 students) found mindfulness lessons did no better than normal lessons.

## Why no "wake up at 5"?

Because it usually steals sleep. Teen body clocks shift later, so a teen in bed at 22:00 and up at 5:00 is short on sleep every day. I tried 5:00 at 13.

## Why is gratitude only optional?

Because a forced list turns fake fast. In 2023 I kept a daily gratitude list, and a lot of it was written only to keep my streak alive. Now it's an optional last step of Sleep: "3 good things you've never written before".

## Does lunch with friends count for f?

Yes, if your phone is away and you're actually talking. Family counts too.

## What counts as a feed for o?

Endless streams picked for you: TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Stories, X, Reddit. Messaging a real person doesn't count. Details: https://dlichters.com/ai/offline.md

## What does it cost?

Nothing. Lichters is completely free. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

## What happens to my data?

Your ticks are saved on your phone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud so they sync between your devices. Your ticks and your name are also stored encrypted in the app's public iCloud database, so friends you accept can see your grid.

## Do I have to be perfect?

No. A month with mostly green beats a year of planning the perfect routine. The rules underneath the habits: https://dlichters.com/ai/attitudes.md

## What is my Lichters ID?

Every Lichters user gets a number, starting with 0000 0001. It is linked to your iCloud account, so there is no sign-up and it stays the same on all your devices. Your ticks and your name are saved encrypted, in one record that also holds this number. The number itself is not encrypted. Friends only see your grid after you accept them. Support (me, David) can also see your grid, so I can help when something goes wrong. To add a friend, tap "Lichters" to open the Guide, then "Invite a friend to see your habits". You get a link to send on WhatsApp or Messages; whoever taps it sends you a friend request.
