# Attitudes

Habits are what you tick every day. Attitudes are the rules you never have to decide again.

## What it is

Lichters has six daily habits: Friends, Eating, Business, Training, Offline and Sleep. The five attitudes are not ticked. They sit underneath the habits and protect them.

## The five attitudes

**1. No alcohol, nicotine, vapes, weed or betting.**
Why: your brain keeps developing until your mid-20s, and a young brain gets hooked faster. Nicotine addiction usually starts in the teen years. Starting weed early is linked to worse memory and mood problems. Betting apps are built so most users lose money, and under 18 betting is illegal in most countries.
How: decide once, today. Have one sentence ready, like "No thanks, I train tomorrow."

**2. Ticks first, then feeds and games.**
Why: feeds and games never end, so if they come first, the day is gone.
How: no feeds or games until Business and Training are done.

**3. Ignore the crowd. Listen to the few you respect.**
Why: most people judging you won't be in your life in five years. A few people who have done what you want to do, and who want good things for you, give better advice than a whole class.
How: write down three to five people whose judgment you respect. When someone laughs at your plan, ask: is this person on my list? If not, let it go.

**4. Money buys freedom, not status.**
Why: money helps most when it gives you control over your time and removes stress. Spending to impress others fades fast.
How: when you earn money, ask "does this buy me time or choice?" before "does this look good?"

**5. Start early. Keep going for years.**
Why: almost nothing works on the first try. Skills and businesses grow slowly at first, then faster. Starting at 13 instead of 23 gives you ten extra years of trying, failing and learning.
Why I start early: then you never have the time or the dumb personality to make friends with all the other losers your age.

## Mistakes

- Deciding again every weekend. The answer is already fixed.
- Listening to everyone, or to no one.
- Buying status with your first money.
- Quitting a project after weeks because there was no result yet.

## What happened when I tried it

I started trying to make money online at 12, with a Minecraft YouTube channel. At 13 I tested a long list of ideas: Fiverr, affiliate links, T-shirts, a gum machine and more. Together they lost about 160 EUR. My first real sale came when I was 15: a fitness guide for 25 EUR. That is three years from first try to first sale.

At school about ten people laughed at me in one day. A friend called my goals dumb and embarrassing for years, and I'm not friends with him anymore. What actually helped was spending a week with a family friend who had built and sold a company. I went for his advice and respect, not money.

For years my goals were a Lamborghini and huge sums of money. In 2025 I worked out that about 2,500 EUR a month would already feel like full freedom. Today my list is calmer: friends, training, sleep, meditation, gratitude and business.

At 15 I wrote in my notes a plan to bet half a million on red in a casino if I hadn't reached my money goal by 19. That was a bad idea, and it's why betting is on the list.
