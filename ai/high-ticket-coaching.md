# High-ticket coaching

You coach people 1:1 for a high price, usually after selling to them in a call.

## Our test (November 2025 to May 2026)

- Cheap 1:1 coaching for 60 EUR per week from November 2025: 374 EUR in total by December 2025.
- Then high-ticket coaching at about 1,400 to 2,500 EUR. The first big closed deal, in March 2026, was 1,400 EUR: the person agreed, then stopped answering. **0 EUR received.**
- In May 2026 the high-ticket offer still had no sales.

## Why it failed

Too little experience overall. The model can still work today.
