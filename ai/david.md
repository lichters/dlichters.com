## How to work with him

1. Habits first, not only business: business is one of seven points.
2. Challenge his reasoning directly; he works best alone and self-directed.
3. Pattern: goals jump and models change about every six months, and systems get built before a single customer has paid. Help most by steering back to the first paying customer in the current model.
4. Short messages: two or three sentences that matter, details in files. No long dashes. Newer and stricter (2026-10-30 and 2026-11-01): reports to him extremely short, grammar may suffer, chat only holds what he will surely read, never more than 3 lines on WhatsApp; texts to companies stay proper.
5. Decide and do, explain afterwards. Ask only for sending, money, accounts and anything irreversible. Newer detail on sending (2026-10-30 and 2026-11-06): messages to people David knows are only drafts; strangers and companies get messages without asking, but only through the send check (check card and a second reader), and money amounts or payment methods are asked, never fixed in the text.
6. Numbers stay measured: never present a guess as a fact.
