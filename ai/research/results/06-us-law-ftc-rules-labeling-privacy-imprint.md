## US law: FTC rules, labeling, privacy, imprint

**US product safety and labeling for dog beds and toys from China** (2026-09-29)  
No single "CPSC approval". Pure pet toys have no mandatory CPSC toy standard; ASTM F963 and CPSIA apply to children's products, not to an item just because it is called a toy. Stuffed articles may need state law labels (e.g. Pennsylvania, Ohio, Massachusetts registrations for filling). Country-of-origin marking ("Made in China") required. Regular customs entry since the 800 USD de minimis ended.

**FTC Mail, Internet or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule (16 CFR 435)** (2026-09-29)  
Applies to prepaid goods incl. China direct shipping. Clear stated shipping time = that time; no stated time = 30 days. "Shipment" means handing to the carrier, not delivery. Any shipping promise needs a "reasonable basis". If you will miss it: notify before the deadline with the option to wait or cancel for a full refund (incl. shipping) within 7 business days (card: 1 billing cycle); no store credit instead. "Estimated" does not protect a forecast without basis. Order-by text needs time and time zone.

**Privacy policy for a US-only Shopify shop with Meta Pixel and CAPI** (2026-09-29)  
Visible English privacy policy adapted to Shopify and Meta; small size alone does not exempt from state laws. CalOPPA has no size threshold. CCPA: prior-year gross revenue over 26,625,000 USD (adjusted from 1 Jan 2025), or 100,000+ California consumers/households bought, sold or shared, or 50 %+ revenue from selling/sharing data. Do a nationwide sale/sharing opt-out honoring GPC, cookie banner, opt-out page. Shopify template is a base; add a Meta Pixel plus CAPI and GPC section. "Enhanced" data sharing is enough for CAPI. Follow-up 2026-09-30: "Maximum" data sharing is not proven to block CAPI after a US opt-out/GPC; LDU means transfer with restriction; Meta lists 14 or 11 LDU states, Virginia missing; so write "stop or limit" Meta transmissions, not "stop".

**Christmas order-by wording under the FTC Mail Order Rule** (2026-09-29)  
Core: a shipping forecast with a reasonable basis at the time of advertising, separation of shipping and delivery, timely delay notices with cancel/refund rights. "Estimated" instead of "guaranteed" is not enough. No universal Christmas cut-off follows. No wording can rule out non-delivery chargebacks. Without a written supplier lead time: no Christmas promise; 11 "Order by Dec" ad lines depended on that. Version A (no promise) and B (with date, ET UTC-5).

**Mandatory info for an EU-based shop selling to US consumers** (2026-09-30)  
Imprint under the home-country law, clear shipping/returns/refund/price info, privacy policy. FTC: no stated time means ship within 30 days (50 if merchant credit application); full refund incl. shipping within 7 business days when not shipped; returns voluntary but must be disclosed. CCPA threshold 100,000 consumers bought/sold/shared.

**German imprint for a US-only shop** (2026-09-30)  
Yes: a German-established operator needs an imprint with full business address under § 5 DDG (in force since 14 May 2024) even if it sells only to the US. Shopify Payments Germany: companies need a provable German business address and separately the home addresses of verified persons; sole traders must live in Germany. Meta advertiser verification wants the verified organization's street address, not a US address. A P.O. box or forwarding address does not work across imprint, Shopify Payments and Meta. Keep home, business and return addresses separate.

**FTC rule for delivery promises and order-by dates** (2026-09-30)  
Christmas shipping or delivery promises only with a factual basis at publication; no clear time means 30 days. If on-time shipping becomes impossible, offer a compliant delay option or cancel and refund; silence is only OK in limited first-delay cases. Overseas fulfillment does not move the responsibility to the supplier; required refunds may not be replaced by store credit.

**Selling dog toys and treats from China** (2026-09-30)  
Treats: before import clarify FDA facility status, ingredients, labeling, Prior Notice, standard or simplified FSVP, state rules; animal ingredients may trigger APHIS. Dog-only toys: no automatic children's toy limits or CPC, but general product safety, import rules and state rules apply. Start with simple tested toys without loose small parts; China treats only after documented regulatory release.

**Textile Fiber Act (TFPIA) for dog clothing** (2026-09-30)  
TFPIA does not apply to dog-only sweaters and bandanas: the FTC lists dog coats and other pet clothing as not covered, so fiber %, maker name and RN are not required by TFPIA. Imports from China still need "Made in China" (or equivalent English origin) on the item or, if allowed, the container.

**Selling after the Christmas cut-off (FTC and Meta)** (2026-10-01)  
Allowed if ad and shop do not misleadingly imply Christmas arrival and you have a reasonable basis for shipping claims. "Not in time for Christmas. Delivery after Christmas" is a clear lower-risk disclosure but does not replace the shipping deadline and the delay/cancel/refund process. No verified Meta rejection for that truthful disclosure.

**FTC delay notice mail (16 CFR 435.2)** (2026-10-01)  
Structure: affected order/item, justified new ship date or justified "cannot determine", choice between waiting and cancel with full prompt refund, and the correct consequence of no answer. Shopify Flow can trigger the check after our 2-business-day threshold, but needs a business-day calendar, reliable carrier hand-off data and a transactional mail action. The notice must go out within the promised period.

**Labeling and safety for dog beds from China** (2026-10-01)  
Physical English origin marking ("Made in China") required on the item. Law labels, registration and filling analysis must be checked separately: Pennsylvania has a wide scope; an Ohio pet exemption is not confirmed. Origin also voluntarily online; secure state and CPSC classification in writing with the supplier, plus tests and certificates.

**FTC endorsement rules for paid creator videos with a real dog** (2026-10-01)  
Paid endorsements must be clearly disclosed even without a visible creator handle; real experiences and substantiated product claims required. A real dog only proves the dog is real, not product efficacy. FTC Endorsement Guides (official edition 1 Jan 2026) and the Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule (in force 21 Oct 2024).

**Showing a similar third-party bed in video** (2026-10-03)  
FTC judges the net impression of picture, sound and text; different lettering or embroidery color is material; a footnote "may vary" does not cure it. Meta bans deceptive presentation (rejection up to account ban); plus refunds and chargebacks. Same rule for AI videos: only use if shape, "Merry Christmas" lettering, colors and fur match the supplier's real photos.

**Money-back guarantee and refund policy requirements** (2026-10-08)  
For a "money-back guarantee" 16 CFR 239.3 applies (not 239.4, which is lifetime guarantees): full purchase price on request, conspicuous disclosure of material conditions, actual prompt performance (CFR edition 1 Jan 2026). These rules create no general return window for non-defective online purchases. Shipping rule: substantiated express or implied ship promises, timely individual delay options, automatic cancellation with full timely refund where required; the Chinese supplier does not relieve the seller. Align terms, ads, shipping policy and refund policy so they never contradict.

**Contradiction between Terms and Refund policy** (2026-10-09)  
No study quantifies the conversion loss of a visible contradiction ("no cancellations" vs "free cancellation within 12 hours"). Baymard self-reported abandonment: 13 % unsatisfactory return policy, 19 % no trust with card data (list updated 22 Sep 2025); not additive. Fix the conflict immediately and honor the cancellation window.

**"30-day money-back guarantee" vs "satisfaction guarantee"** (2026-10-09)  
No direct conversion test. FTC guides allow both terms only with full purchase price refund on request and clearly disclosed material conditions; "satisfaction" is no safe substitute for "exchange/store credit only" (16 CFR 239.3). Contradictions between badge and policy create deception risk that fine print does not cure. Under Part 435 a blanket "no cancellations" clause cannot cut the statutory cancel right for unshipped orders; refund within 7 working days.

## Minors and payment providers (historical)

**Minors (16/17) and PayPal / Shopify Payments / Whop** (2026-10-03)  
PayPal Germany: under 18 cannot open any account, also not business; a family-court authorization under § 112 BGB extends legal capacity for the business but does not override PayPal's 18+ rule. Shopify Payments requires 18+ account holder. Whop: guardian route.

**Shopify Payments age 18 (historical)** (2026-10-04)  
A minor may work on the shop, but Shopify Payments may not be set up with his data; an adult (parent/guardian or real owner/representative) must set it up with own data as account representative, else closure.

## AI tools, Claude, Meta and Shopify connectors

**Which Perplexity model to use (test of 11 models, same question)** (2026-09-28)  
GPT-6 Sol deep research: most sources (86) and consistent math; Claude Opus 5.5 deep research deep but missed the current pet number; GPT-6 Sol (normal) and Grok 4.7 careful and right; "Best" (auto), Nemotron, GLM unsuited for numbers (old data, undated agency blogs); Gemini only Flash, too thin. Lesson: depth does not protect against the wrong number; always check the key figure at its source. Reference figure then: Triple Whale 18 Aug 2026 pets CPM 16.27 USD, CPA 38.51, CTR 2.37 %, ROAS 1.60 (data 1 Aug 2025-31 Jul 2026); the often-quoted 9.56 USD CPM is 2025 full-year all markets.

**AI automation for a two-person Shopify shop** (2026-09-29)  
Order: Shopify Inbox Agent (0 USD) for repeat chat questions, Shopify Flow (free) with Sidekick for risk/stock/fulfillment exceptions, native AI reports of Shopify/Google/Meta, one research assistant. Gorgias, Triple Whale, Motion only after a proven volume problem. Shopify Basic 29 USD/month yearly or 39 USD monthly.

**Autonomous AI agent setups in e-commerce** (2026-09-29)  
"Best" means highest proven result at controlled risk, not maximum freedom. Daily loop: measure, diagnose, change, test, roll out, roll back. Typical missing parts before full autonomy: reliable evaluation, rollback, permissions, human approval for risky actions.

**Tools that let Claude read and edit Meta ad accounts** (2026-09-30)  
Windsor.ai Forever Free: 0 USD/month for one Meta account, read plus create/edit campaigns, ad sets, ads, budgets via MCP. Official Meta Ads MCP server recommended (Meta enforces account action locks and budget limits server-side); Claude Free suffices for a custom connector. Own Marketing API route: a Meta developer app, a system user in the Business Portfolio with app and ad account assigned, a restrictive system-user token (ads_read for reporting, ads_management for changes). Internal use by app-role people: Standard Access suffices; business verification and App Review only for Advanced Access / broader use.

**Official Shopify connector (MCP) in Claude** (2026-09-30)  
Can write products, collections, inventory and new percentage discount codes; read orders, customers, analytics; edit unpublished themes. Blocked: live theme changes, theme publishing, refunds, other payment/order actions. Fulfillment writes, full theme file operations, policies, payouts not reliably available. No extra cost; works on Claude Free.

**Automating one Meta ad account with Claude** (2026-10-03)  
Official Meta Ads MCP server since 29 Apr 2026: Meta OAuth, supports Claude, read and write, no own developer app or App Review, 0 USD during open beta. Create new objects paused and have a human confirm all writes. Never touch or work around restricted accounts.

**Full Admin API access for our own AI agent on Shopify Basic** (2026-10-03)  
Build your own single-store app in the Shopify Dev Dashboard with the GraphQL Admin API and the client-credentials token that auto-rotates every 24 h. Give only needed scopes; plan restrictions still apply.

**Several Claude Max subscriptions and API prices** (2026-10-04)  
Each founder may use his own Max subscription for his own normal work. Stacking several Max subscriptions or accounts as a quota pool to run 20-30 parallel Claude Code agents is not clean: Max is an individual consumer product, limits apply to "ordinary, individual usage", accounts may not be shared, automated/non-human use outside explicitly allowed ways is prohibited (Consumer Terms 8 Oct 2025, Help Center 22/24 Sep 2026). Reports of 13 banned Max 20x accounts in July 2026 (cause unconfirmed). Proper route for many agents: API with an organization account, API keys, workspaces, spend/rate limits. Users must be 18+ (or local age of consent).

**Claude Code on the web with Max 20x** (2026-10-04)  
Parallel cloud sessions allowed, no published maximum; they share the same 5-hour window and weekly limit as the local CLI (no extra pool). No compute fee for the cloud machine; extra usage costs extra. A local CLI with its own API key bills via the API, not Max. Decision: cloud sessions give no extra capacity.

**Anthropic API prices (4 Oct 2026, unverified)** (2026-10-04)

## Cold email

**Cold email ramp from 40 to 70 per day (5-7 mailboxes, German SMB recipients)** (2026-09-30)  
Raise 10-20 % every 3-5 sending days; stop raising at 1 % hard bounces, pull back or pause at 2 %; do not wait for 0.3 % spam complaints. Instantly ramp: +2 emails per mailbox per day. Google documents daily spam rates but no numeric safe ramp. Complaint rates are not a "spam budget".

## Founders and billionaires: what the data says

**How self-made billionaires decided in their first years** (2026-11-06)  
The proven common pattern is less "think big" than: spot a concrete advantage, test it against real behavior, concentrate resources on the decisive bottleneck. "Started with little or no capital" does not fit everyone: Bezos had early family and venture money, Koum had significant savings, Colin Huang was a successful founder before Pinduoduo. Evidence grades: A contemporaneous primary (letters, filings), B retrospective primary (interviews), C institutional/indirect. Bezos (Amazon 1994-1999), 1997 shareholder letter: judge investments by long-term market position, kill failing programs, fund winners; metrics customer growth and repeat purchases; IPO prospectus 15 May 1997: books chosen for huge selection, limited shelf space offline, informational buying.

**How the richest e-commerce/consumer founders reached their first 100k-1M profit** (2026-11-06)  
Breakthroughs came from a better product, credible distribution access or a more reliable supply chain, not from more ad budget. For most, the first 100k-1M profit is not publicly documented; early revenue, funding and valuation must not stand in for profit (Warby Parker, Hims had high revenue long before net profit). Shein (Chris Xu): cross-border online selling, first wedding dresses then women's fashion, SEM background; no own big production at first. Temu: started Sept 2022 inside an existing group, not a zero start.

**Survivorship bias in billionaire advice** (2026-11-06)  
Biographies show what successful people had, not how often people with those traits succeed. Denrell (Organization Science, June 2003): undersampling failures makes risky concentrated strategies look good. Azoulay, Jones, Kim & Miranda (2018/2020, about 2.7 M US founders, 2007-2014): average founding age of the top 0.1 % fastest-growing firms 45; a 50-year-old was about 1.8x as likely as a 30-year-old to be a top-growth founder. Relevant experience, risk-limited entry and medium rather than maximum risk-taking do better than the all-or-nothing young founder story; correlations, not guarantees.

## Miscellaneous checked facts

**US holiday dates** (2026-10-03)  
Thanksgiving 2026: Thursday 26 November 2026 (Black Friday 27 Nov, Cyber Monday 30 Nov). Christmas 2026 is a Friday.

**Cancelling a German VPS contract (Contabo)** (2026-10-05)  
Normal cancellation ends the VPS only at contract term end, no refund. Separate: 14-day consumer withdrawal, and a contract a minor concluded without parental consent (void/pending under § 108 BGB, unwinding possible). Contabo German T&Cs dated December 2025.

**Minor's VPS contract (§ 108 BGB)** (2026-10-07)  
A parent as legal representative can refuse approval under § 108(1) BGB without the 14-day limit (unlike consumer withdrawal); then the contract is void and payments incl. setup fees are generally refundable, minus possible counterclaims.
