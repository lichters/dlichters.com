## 1. The north star

David's document "NORDSTERN" stands above every other document. Every project, plan and automated round exists only because of it. The stores, apps, idea machine and money are means; this is the purpose.

**The goal:** reach David's happiness list as close to 100% as possible, and then hold it for as many years in a row as possible. That is why longevity belongs in every plan: a life that fulfills the list and is short has missed the goal.

**The seven main points (= the seven daily habits), as of 2026-09-19:** 1 Friends, 2 Meditation, 3 Business, 4 Documentation, 5 Training, 6 Gratitude, 7 Sleep. Business is one of seven, not all seven.

At the top of his monthly note (September 2026) in capitals: "NEVER FORGET: YOUR DAILY LIFE FEELS TEN TIMES HAPPIER WHEN YOU HAVE ONE HUNDRED PERCENT ON YOUR HABITS." His own short form: green tracker = good life.

**The full happiness list ("Life Goal"), verbatim from his note of 2026-09-06:**
1. Notice everything below and be grateful for it
2. Be physically healthy (especially sleep)
3. Deep nature at speed, great terrain, perfect snow, huge space, no people, a few friends
4. Beauty (home, car, view, reflection, nature)
5. A wife who is my favorite person
6. Good sex life
7. Multiple kids and dogs
8. Family I stay close to
9. Multiple real friends who truly know me
10. Teach one person, for years
11. Make progress towards meaningful goals
12. Build something that helps people
13. Be respected by people whose judgement I respect
14. Control time, work and location
15. A home that feels like home
16. Good food
17. Good music
18. Comfort (bed, clothes, gear)
19. Dance
20. A car that I love
21. Vacation to keep learning new things (skydive)
22. Document it all

(The list moves from what you are, to who you're with, to what you do, to what you enjoy.)

**What he does now to get there asap:** sleep, train, make friends, make money, film everything, get the jaw surgery (Bimax) asap. **Goal milestone:** 5 million USD, a wife he loves, 5 great friends, a perfect hometown.

**How every plan is measured, before every bigger decision, in every chat and automated round:**
1. Which point of the list does it bring closer, and which does it cost?
2. If it costs sleep, training or friends for business, that price is said out loud, in hours, not paid silently.
3. Does it help hold the list longer (health, money as a cushion, calm), or only reach it briefly?

**Additions of 2026-09-20:**
- **A relationship is practice, not a milestone.** Points 5 and 6 were an end state with no path. The path is having relationships; one that ends is a repetition that makes the end state reachable, so an ended relationship counts as progress on point 5.
- **Saying the goals out loud is the friend filter.** Point 9 had no mechanism. Now: tell everyone the goals in full. Timo understood; most others laughed. Both answers are valuable because they sort in minutes what otherwise takes years.
- **The creator was there and is gone.** At 12 Minecraft, 13 fitness, 14 self-improvement, 15 TikTok tutorials, at 16 almost nothing: the only point where David went backwards. Habit "D" now means *publishing*, not just filming. It also carries points 12, 13 and 9. The objection "I need 5 really good friends and 1,000 EUR per video first" was struck: early creators had a camera and a gym; money and team came from the videos, not before them.
- **What the list does not protect yet:** health now, not in 40 years (over 1.85 m, under 67 kg; two years ago 71 kg at a smaller height); school and a possible exchange year (since 2026-09-23 the exchange year is an idea without planning, not a deadline); money buys points 3, 4, 14, 20, 21 but not 5, 9, 10, and business must not pay for those three.
- **100 loyal fans, then someday 100,000.** Goal (David, 2026-09-20): win back 100 loyal fans, and over many years 100,000 on YouTube, "not forced, only with things he wants to post". A loyal fan is someone who answers and gets an answer; the measure is the number of real conversations, not followers. 100,000 is a consequence (typically 3 to 6 years of steady posting) and is not measured or discussed this year. The real risk is not effort but topic: four channels in four years, each audience thrown away at the switch. The one decision that changes the outcome: choose a topic he still wants to post about at 20.

**Physical boundaries that every plan must respect:** sleep 8 to 10 hours, training, friends, meditation, and business at least 5 hours a day (never suggest less).
