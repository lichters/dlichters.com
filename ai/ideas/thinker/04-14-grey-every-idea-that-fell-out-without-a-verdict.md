## 15. Legal findings that cut across many ideas

(Our own research notes, not legal advice. For binding answers our lawyer list applies.)
- **Bundesanzeiger database right.** Its imprint (fetched 2026-09-25) claims database protection under §§ 87a ff. UrhG and forbids use outside legal provisions without the publisher's consent. § 87b (1) also covers repeated, systematic copying of insubstantial parts if it conflicts with normal exploitation; the publisher sells the raw data itself (info service about 500 EUR/year). Open: whether § 5 UrhG (official notices are not copyrighted) covers mandatory notices of private firms. A full feed is the core risk; per-customer few rows is lower but not zero risk. LR itself holds about 34,500 rows.
- **Register portal terms (handelsregister.de):** "systematic pulls to build, extend or update parallel full or partial registers are not permitted". Kills archives and monthly mass checks; single lookups by register number and buyers looking up themselves (§ 9 (1) HGB) are fine.
- **§ 34c (1) no. 1 GewO:** proving a purchase opportunity for real estate needs a broker license, so sell only to brokers as acquisition data, never to buyers.
- **§ 57a StBerG:** tax advisors may not advertise aimed at a single mandate, so they are not buyers of trigger lists.
- **§ 49b (3) BRAO:** lawyers may not pay for referrals. **RDG:** collection and individual legal checks are licensed legal services. **§§ 2, 5 StBerG:** no tax returns for others.
- **§ 34d GewO:** insurance tipster limits (unchecked). **Art. 14 GDPR:** names of sole traders and liquidators are personal data; deliver firm data only until solved. **UWG § 7:** cold calls and cold mail to companies need care; letters and visits are the safe channel.
- **Liquidation mechanics used everywhere:** § 65 (2) GmbHG creditor call, § 70 liquidator must turn assets into money, § 73 (1) one-year blocking period and (3) personal liability, § 74 (2) books stored 10 years with a custodian; § 394 FamFG deletion of asset-less firms with 3-month objection; § 152 UmwG sole traders can only spin off.

## 16. Honest assessment and outdated assumptions

- **It never produced a test result.** 22 test cards, 0 tests. The bottleneck was never ideas but David's time, an ad account, a counter and money; the cards each needed 20 to 60 minutes of David plus accounts.
- **It barely killed anything in real runs:** 20 of 23 checked ideas lived (2026-09-27: 11/12; 2026-10-04: 9/11). Stage 4 said "partly occupied" to 12 of 12 on 2026-09-27. Calibration shows it does kill clearly occupied and clearly forbidden cases, but the borderline (a cheaper partial solution occupies it) was never measured.
- **Diversity was poor:** before v7, 12 of 15 selected ideas were liquidation variants. Partly because agents saw the LR context. The register core dominated everything; nearly every survivor depends on the same Bundesanzeiger/register data and the same legal risks.
- **Rediscovery failed:** 0 of 65 blind ideas resembled LR. Ideas from only our own data land on outreach tooling.
- **Outdated in the prompts and cards (correct these when reusing):** "David, 16" is correct. "Who is the legal sender, minors cannot invoice" lines in the cards are obsolete. Stripe is set up. Business registration only above 20,000 EUR revenue. The generator prompt's goal "100,000 USD by 2027-09-01" is outdated: David's goal since 2026-11-06 is 500,000 USD profit by 2027-12-31 over many attempts; 2026-09-01 is no deadline. Business shape "800 EUR per unit, under 1 h human time" still stands from the hunt but was never validated by a sale.
- **Note on David's other rules:** research questions go to Perplexity, never to agents with web search; tests that message companies go through our send checks; never message people David knows.

## 17. How to restart it, or run it by hand in any chat

**Only on David's word.** David switched the Thinker off on 2026-10-14 ("wastes our usage") and its scheduled jobs were unloaded. Nothing below is a reason to restart it or to propose a restart; the 2026-10-07 subscription block is not why it is off. This section exists so it can be rebuilt if David asks.

By hand, in one chat, the whole method fits in these steps:
1. **Signals:** list 10 to 30 hard measured facts only we have (section 11 is the current stock). For each: number, source, why it is not public, who might pay.
2. **Generate blind:** for each of the six directions in section 3 separately (ideally separate chats that do not see each other), 5 ideas, each with a named buyer group and a price with source; incomplete ideas go to a grey list with what is missing. Give the dead list (section 13) and grey list (section 14) so nothing is repeated.
3. **Sort:** max 16, max 3 per core, weights: private signal, path to 10,000 EUR, low human time, testable for 50 EUR in 7 days.
4. **Who sells it:** ask Perplexity the stage-4 question from section 4. Kill only on "same result, same buyer, free or cheaper, and he uses it", with a quote. Free raw data is not occupied; a pricier competitor is price proof.
5. **Two pessimists:** money angle and law angle; kill only on a law with paragraph, an unobtainable license, or blocked data. Everything else becomes a test question.
6. **Repair before burying:** refute with a source, or the smallest change (other country, buyer, form, price, slice of the source). Recheck once.
7. **Test card:** behavior, threshold fixed before, max 50 EUR and 7 days, honest, what David must do, expected value. Put 20 % of killed ideas into tests as controls.
8. **Log** every verdict in a ledger and every test with threshold before result. A passed control means the judges were wrong: make it a "must live" calibration case.
9. **Calibrate any judge change** against the cases in section 6: Liquidationsradar (UK and DE versions), Stotles and Zapier must live; lawyer referral fees, unregistered collection and VAT returns must die; Attorney Share and Expert Witness Profiler cases must be occupied. Wrongly killed may never rise.

Automated restart needs: a Claude account that allows `claude -p` (subscription access was disabled from 2026-10-07), a market-search route for stage 4, generators and the repairer (Perplexity), agents run outside the home folder so they do not inherit our context, per-stage tool restriction, a content-based health check (not exit codes), and a check for limit messages before trusting any run.
