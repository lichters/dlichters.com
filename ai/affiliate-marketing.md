# Affiliate marketing: earning a commission on other people's products

You link products and earn a commission when someone buys through your link.

## Our test (2023)

Affiliate links in YouTube Shorts and TikTok videos. Views were possible (an earlier gaming channel had one video with 100,000 views), but there was no audience for the products being linked.

**Result: 0 euros.** Not a single commission.

## Why it failed

Too little experience overall, and no audience for the linked products. The model can still work today; one failed test does not mean it can't.
