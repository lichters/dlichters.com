# Data products: selling lists made from public data

You turn public data into a list or alert that someone pays for every month.

## Our test (August 2026)

The idea: read official notices in Germany about companies going bankrupt or closing, and tell nearby trade businesses, so they could buy equipment cheaply or win the customers. First goal: 13 customers at 79 euros a month. An AI-built system checked over 9,000 small trade businesses for a working email address and sent emails automatically every 40 minutes.

**Result: 888 emails delivered, 10 clicks, 0 paying customers.**

## Why it failed

Weeks went into the machine and almost no time into two questions: does someone already sell this, and will one real person pay? Too little experience overall. The model can still work today.
