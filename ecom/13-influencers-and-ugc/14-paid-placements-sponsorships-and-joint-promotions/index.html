Paid placements, sponsorships and joint promotions

Section 13.14 · 14 of 21 in this chapter · 7 min read

In short

  • Offer advertisers a free seven-day test before they sign a contract.
  • Measure each test with a tagged link and a free analytics tool, and check sales after one week.
  • Compare the ad's cost with the sales it brings, then decide.
  • A podcast episode or video can keep bringing visitors for three to five years.

Why Creators Sell Ad Space

1 min
  • Content creators (people who make videos, posts or podcasts) and large-scale platforms earn revenue by placing advertisements across their websites, YouTube channels, Instagram feeds and audio podcasts.
  • Offer advertisers a free seven-day evaluation period, which is a trial to judge the ad.
  • The trial shows whether the placement delivers measurable results (results you can count, such as sales) before they sign a binding agreement (a contract they must keep).
  • This appeals to most companies actively searching for additional advertising opportunities.

Testing a Placement Before You Commit

1 min

Procedure for each prospect (a possible partner) that runs paid ads:

  1. Complete your Dream 100 Worksheet, which is your sheet for the Dream 100 list (a list of the 100 best partner targets). Identify people and companies in your niche (your narrow topic area) across all influencer categories (types of people with an audience who promote products).
  2. Work through the list and sort out only those that run paid ads.
  3. Locate an "advertise" or "contact us" button on their site, or contact them directly via their preferred social channel.
  4. Send your outreach message (your first contact message) proposing a free one-week trial.
  5. During the trial week, track traffic (visitors to your site) using UTM parameters (short tags added to a link that show where each visitor came from) in Google Analytics (a free tool that counts visits and sales) to measure sales generated.
  6. After one week, review your Google Analytics data for that traffic source to see sales and revenue.
  7. Compare the cost of the ad to the sales generated and decide whether to sign a contract.

Advertising Early, Before Relationship Building

1 min
  • Take advertising opportunities with Dream 100 prospects early and directly, without waiting to finish building the relationship first, as long as the cost fits your budget. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • Paid advertising can be offered before you reach the 10-engagement threshold (the point of 10 engagements, meaning 10 interactions such as likes, comments or shares). Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • If a site shows an advertise-with-us option (a link for buying ads), you can buy ad space right away. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • The purchase makes the owner aware of your business. Older advice, 2021-2022.

Booking Advertising on a Partner Site

1 min

Procedure:

  1. Find the advertise-with-us link (a link for buying ads), which is often in a footer (the bottom strip of a page) or on a contact page of a forum, blog or website.
  2. Submit the contact form with your details.
  3. Wait for the rate card (the price list), which lists prices per placement (per ad spot), such as a banner ad (a wide picture ad) or a sidebar ad (an ad in the side column).
  4. Compare the prices with your budget.
  5. If you can afford it, start the booking conversation and buy the placement. If you cannot, keep engaging in other ways and return to it once it fits your budget.

Older advice, 2021-2022.

Banner Ads on Forums and Blogs

1 min
  • Forums (online discussion boards) and blogs (websites with regular articles) often sell banner ad space. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • You pay the owner to display your banner, which places your brand in front of the same audience every day. Older advice, 2021-2022.

Podcast and YouTube Sponsorships

1 min
  • You pay for a sponsored episode (an episode paid for by a sponsor). Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • Or you pay for a sponsor mention (a short ad read out by the host), which is often read at the start of the episode. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • Podcasters and YouTubers in your niche should be added to the Dream 100 list as well. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • A sponsored YouTube video or podcast episode can stay online and rank (appear high in search results) for three to five years. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • Long after it is published, it keeps delivering traffic (visitors) and brand awareness (how many people know your name). Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • Sponsorships like this do not necessarily have to be expensive. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • Example: a seller of wine coolers can add wine podcasts to the Dream 100 list and offer to sponsor an episode. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • Thousands of wine enthusiasts may hear that episode over the years, so the sponsorship reaches a relevant audience. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • Pitch (offer) two placements to the same partner in one outreach, such as a podcast guest slot and a guest blog post.
  • This works because one contact can open two traffic sources.
  • Influencer traffic works better through advertising on niche authority blogs or websites (trusted sites about a narrow topic) than through paying random individuals in the niche.
  • Authority sites bring more relevant readers.

Paid and Free Product Promotion

1 min
  • Paid product promotion: some prospects openly publish rates for mentioning your product in a video or for sending an email blast (one email sent to their whole list). Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • You pay the rate for the placement you choose, so the cost depends on what they offer. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • Free product promotion: the prospect promotes your products to their audience or email list at no cost, usually because they like them. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • In return you promote their channel, business or content to your audience, for example with a recommendation or a blog post about them. Older advice, 2021-2022.

Flat Fee Versus Commission

1 min
  • A flat-fee ad placement (one fixed price) can be cheaper than a commission deal (you pay a share of each sale). Older advice, 2020.
  • Example: a banner that costs a couple hundred USD per month but brings over 10,000 USD in monthly sales. Older advice, 2020.
  • Pay for placement first when you can afford it. Older advice, 2020.

Product-for-Post and Celebrity Video Deals

1 min
  • Product-for-post deals (you send a product, and they make a post) can pay a creator in product, for example a board valued at up to $800, sent to a smaller creator with high views and engagement.
  • In return the creator makes a video, posts it to the channel and gives a shout-out (a public mention) naming the brand.
  • A celebrity can be paid to record a video that is sent to you rather than posted on their channel.
  • You then post it on your own social media and website, so the celebrity's name and face lend credibility (trust) to your store.

Endorsements and Specialist Reviews

1 min
  • Endorsements (public approval) from recognized authorities or celebrities in your market, such as PGA Tour golfers (top professional golfers) endorsing golf products, are a strong sign of trust. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • These endorsements provide credible third-party approval (praise from someone outside your company). Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • A specialist such as a chiropractor or doctor can review the product on camera.
  • A 30 to 40 second review is enough.
  • Pay the specialist and use the review on the site, landing pages (single pages built to sell one offer) and social media.
  • Only ask a specialist for a review if the product quality is good, since their name will be attached to the product.
  • A simple product with a clear problem is a good fit for this approach.

Meme Pages and Repeating Promotions

1 min
  • Paid placements on TikTok meme pages (popular joke-picture accounts) can be used as a repeating promotion (the same paid promotion, run again) for a product.
  • Run it about every one to two months.
  • Launch a new batch (a new round of posts) when the audience gained from the previous batch has been used up and the follower base starts to recycle (the same people keep seeing it).
  • Meme pages can post paid content for brands without issue.
  • Influencers may refuse if their followers expect them to appear in the video.
  • Use meme pages to repurpose UGC (user-generated content, meaning content made by customers or fans, reused in a new form).

Joint Promotions and Collaborations

1 min
  • Joint promotions (promotions you run together with another creator) let you get your message in front of another creator's audience while they reach yours. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • Most markets are large enough that this mutual exchange works without taking customers from each other. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • Joint promotion formats include content collaborations, giveaways (free prize draws) and competitions. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • A podcast can be sponsored for one episode, which places your link and product in front of its listeners. Older advice, 2021-2022.
  • Collaborate on social media with suppliers, blogs, forums and Facebook groups in your niche by tagging them (naming them in a post) or working together on posts. Older advice, 2021.
  • This brings the right reach and followers early. Older advice, 2021.

Free Resources for Influencers

1 min
  • Offer influencers a free training course, guide or educational resource to promote to their audience.
  • This works as an alternative to affiliate commissions (a share of each sale paid to the partner).
  • Influencers build credibility by offering exclusive free value (something only their followers get).
  • Their followers gain access to the resource, and you gain traffic and backlinks (links from other sites to yours).

Lead Magnet Placements

1 min
  • A lead magnet (a free offer that collects email addresses) can bring traffic from blog banner ads, sponsorships of Facebook groups and forums, Facebook ads aimed at people interested in the niche or its events, and influencer posts.
  • Test these placements with a free offer instead of only using direct discount offers.
  • The lead magnet must match the site, community or influencer where it appears.
  • An offer that fits the audience gets opt-ins (people who sign up), while an off-topic offer gets clicks that never turn into sales.
