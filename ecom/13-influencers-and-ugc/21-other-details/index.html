In short

  • Buy thumbnails and channel art cheaply from freelance designers on Fiverr, but know this advice is from 2021 to 2022.
  • For a TikTok test, follow the 15 steps in order, starting with a campaign named after the product plus "testing one."
  • Use Pinterest group boards only after your profile looks real. Then send visitors to your store and show them ads again.
  • Put a real customer quote in every collection letter.
  • Book two coaching calls each month before the month ends, because unused calls do not carry over.

Creative assets

  • A thumbnail is the small preview picture for a YouTube video. A freelance designer is a person who works for pay per job. Fiverr is a website where you hire them.
  • The course recommends buying custom thumbnails and channel cover art from freelance designers on Fiverr, instead of making them yourself. The channel cover art is the banner at the top of your YouTube channel page.
  • This is older advice (2021 to 2022).

TikTok ad testing

Procedure for launching a test campaign. A test campaign is a short trial of ads to see what works.

  1. Create a new campaign named after the product plus "testing one," and choose ABO (ad group budget optimization: you set the budget on each ad group). An ad group is a set of ads that share the same audience and settings.
  2. Create the first ad group named "Open 1," with the external website option (sends people to your store), and select your pixel (a tracking code that records what visitors do on your store).
  3. Set the conversion event (the action you count as a success) to complete payments, and set the placement (where the ad shows) to TikTok only.
  4. Turn off comments and video download.
  5. Select the countries (United States and Canada for the standard setup) and the language English.
  6. Choose the gender: all, or female for beauty products.
  7. Choose the age group: 18+ for open targeting (a wide audience), or 18 to 24 for teen-oriented products.
  8. Check the audience size (how many people the ad can reach). For open targeting in the US and Canada, it is often above 100 million.
  9. Set the start to the next day at midnight. Set the schedule to all day, or from 7 a.m. to midnight.
  10. Set optimization (what TikTok aims for) to lowest cost (TikTok spends to get the most results for the least money).
  11. Select the identity (the name and picture people see on the ad): brand name and logo, or your own account if it has engaging videos.
  12. Upload the creative (the ad picture or video), and add the ad text.
  13. Set the call-to-action button (the button people press, such as Shop Now) to Shop Now, and paste the UTM URL (a store link with tags that show which ad sent the visitor).
  14. Submit and publish the campaign.
  15. Duplicate (copy) the ad group to create the other ad groups, changing only the audience.

Pinterest group boards

Procedure for using group boards as a traffic source. A group board is a shared Pinterest board that many people can post to. Traffic means visitors to your store.

  1. Build a strong Pinterest profile with relevant boards, so it looks credible (trustworthy) when you contact group board owners.
  2. Find group boards in your niche (your product area), for example through the BoardDeckHQ.com directory (a list of group boards), and get access to pin on them.
  3. Post your product pins and content pins to the group boards.
  4. Drive traffic from the group board pins back to your store.
  5. Retarget the visitors who arrive from the group boards. Retargeting means showing ads again to people who already visited your store.

Customer collection letters

  • Put a real customer testimonial (a written praise or review from a real buyer) in every letter, including collection letters (letters asking for an unpaid bill).
  • An unpaid bill often reflects doubt about value, not refusal to pay.
  • Use the quote to restate value for the customer, meaning show again what the product is worth to them.

Coaching calls

  • Use two one-on-one coaching calls (private sessions with a coach) each month.
  • Unused calls do not roll over (carry over to the next month).
  • Book them before the month ends. They reset on the first of the next month.
