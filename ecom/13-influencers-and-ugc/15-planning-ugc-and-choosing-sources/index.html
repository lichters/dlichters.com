Playbook › 13. Influencers and UGC

Planning UGC and choosing sources

Section 13.15 · 15 of 21 in this chapter · 10 min read

In short

  • Do not pay for professional video creators until cheap tests show that people buy.
  • Start with your own phone videos, supplier photos, or cheap freelancers on Fiverr.
  • Pay for UGC (videos filmed by real people, not studios) only after the product sells well.
  • Prices mix pounds and dollars, so compare only one currency and check live prices before you order.
This section has 13 parts. Tap one to open it.

What UGC Is and Why It Matters

1 min
  • UGC means user-generated content. It is a video filmed by a real person or creator on a phone, not in a studio.
  • Viewers often trust these videos more than polished brand videos.
  • So UGC is a common format for high-performing video ads. High-performing means getting strong results.
  • Polished ad-style videos made for TikTok ads got almost no views in the referenced case.
  • Reading the feedback and the zero-view results showed the format was wrong.
  • Switching fully to a UGC style fixed the problem.

When to Invest in UGC

2 min
  • Do not spend £120 or more on UGC creators until cheaper ad tests have shown sales. An ad test is a small, cheap ad run to see if people buy.
  • Use image ads or basic videos first.
  • Pay for expensive UGC, such as Billow-style professional creators, only after product-audience fit is proven. Product-audience fit means the right people want the product.
  • Outsource full scripted creative production only for products already proven as winners. Outsource means paying others to do the work. Scripted means made from a written script.
  • This is also right for brands with inventory and data. Inventory means stock you hold.
  • Do not invest in this production for pure dropshipping tests. Dropshipping means selling products that a supplier ships to the customer, so you never hold stock.
  • The reason: clips and footage are easy to source for those tests. Sourcing means finding them.
  • Buy UGC from marketplaces such as below.app, or hire local creators, only once the product has sales and the store is scaling. A marketplace is a website where many sellers offer services. Scaling means growing sales.
  • In the early phase, use supplier photos instead. A supplier is the company that provides the product.
  • Hire UGC creators or an agency once the product shows sales traction and you cannot make your own videos. Sales traction means steady sales. An agency is a company that manages work for you.
  • Outsourcing video creation makes sense once money is available but time is scarce.
  • An external creator team trained to produce converting hooks and calls to action usually beats cheap freelance marketplace creatives.
  • A hook is the first seconds of an ad that stop people from scrolling away.
  • A call to action tells viewers what to do next, such as buying.
  • Creatives means the ad videos and images. Converting means leading to sales. Freelance means self-employed people hired per job.
  • Validate a product with proof of concept before spending heavily. Proof of concept means a small test that shows the idea can work.

Scale-up trigger: owned creatives through UGC

Owned creatives means ad videos you own and can reuse. A scale-up trigger is the signal that it is time to grow.

  1. Reach the product's first sales of around $1,000 to $2,000.
  2. Send the product to content creators or freelancers.
  3. Ask them to market the product using different marketing angles than the competitors. An angle is the reason you give a customer to buy. Competitors are other sellers of similar products.
  4. Use the resulting videos as your own creatives.

Test Cheaply First

1 min
  • For a fast test, find the product on Amazon with fast delivery (one to two days).
  • Order it, and film UGC as quickly as possible.
  • Fulfilling from Amazon speeds up UGC testing. Fulfilling means shipping the order to the customer.
  • Test an unproven product with cheaper options before paying for established creators. Unproven means it has no sales history yet.
  • Look for cheaper UGC creators on Fiverr, or film your own videos first. Fiverr is a website where freelancers offer services.
  • Freelance video creation on Fiverr can deliver two to three videos for about 30 to 50 in one example.
  • Use this option to produce original creatives for testing.
  • UGC-style TikTok ad videos from Fiverr sellers who research TikTok, YouTube, AliExpress and Alibaba can cost about 20 to 30 USD or less per video. AliExpress and Alibaba are online shops for suppliers.
  • Ask the seller about their research and content before placing an order.
  • When outsourcing ads or graphics to freelancers, keep control of the ad copy. Ad copy is the written words in an ad. Graphics are design images.
  • Give the designer the core benefits, the offer, and the direction. The offer is the deal you give customers.
  • Reason: graphic designers are not e-commerce experts. E-commerce means online selling.

Bootstrap Your Own Content

1 min

Bootstrap means starting with what you have and little money.

  • Film your own videos first.
  • A product can be shown by you, a friend, or in a product order from a store.
  • This gives free creatives while you test.
  • A long-form UGC video of about six minutes, where a presenter explains how the product works, is mostly seen on affiliate-marketing products. Long-form means a long video. A presenter is the person on camera. Affiliate marketing means earning a commission on sales made through your links.
  • Film a version yourself with friends, or use a UGC marketplace such as Billo or Fiverr.
  • Creators can film their own videos with a small set of products from bulk orders, instead of you paying for production. Bulk orders are large orders bought at once.
  • If a supplier offers free products in exchange for content, accept.
  • You get the product and can use the content for product pages and social media. Product pages are the pages on your store that sell an item.
  • Post it on Facebook, Instagram and YouTube with the product name.
  • Seasonal products often struggle to get on-season footage once the season has ended. Footage means raw video material. Seasonal means tied to one time of year.
  • Collect footage in advance.

Source Creators at Low Cost

1 min

Procedure: find low-cost creators

  1. Find creators in your niche on Instagram with roughly 3,000 to 50,000 followers. A niche is a small topic market. Followers are people who follow an account. Many of them will film a single video without a large fee.
  2. Use a creator-discovery tool such as Modash to find creators with a high engagement rate. A tool is software. Engagement rate means how often people like, comment or share a post.
  3. Message promising creators directly on Instagram.
  4. Order content through a UGC marketplace that lets you chat with creators, and ask for raw clips rather than a finished video. Raw clips are unedited footage.
  5. Use your own footage, a friend, or a product order from a store to produce free creative while you test.

Procedure: find TikTok creators

  1. Search the niche keyword on TikTok, for example fishing. A keyword is the word you type into search.
  2. Open the accounts that appear and check their views.
  3. If the largest accounts are too expensive, scroll down until views drop off, and hire smaller creators with the same niche.
  4. Small creators are usually cheaper.

Compare Sources

1 min

Freelance Marketplaces: Fiverr and Upwork

1 min
  • Fiverr is a freelancer marketplace where UGC creators can be found by searching UGC.
  • Buyers can review past work.
  • Prices are described as fair.
  • Fiverr and Upwork can be used to hire video creators.
  • Fiverr and Upwork can be used to hire video editors and freelance UGC creators when footage and story already exist. Editors cut raw footage into finished videos.
  • Upwork offers a cheaper skilled-labour alternative.
  • Fiverr and training a TikTok creator were suggested as sources of UGC talent. Talent means people who can do the work.
  • Freelance marketplace creatives often lack hook and call-to-action skills.

Platform Marketplaces: Billow, Billo and below.app

1 min
  • Billow (also spelled Billo or Bilo) is a complete UGC platform.
  • You define a video order, pay, and then choose from creators who apply.
  • It is used for ads and is said to offer the most options of the platforms listed here.
  • Billo UGC is an alternative for long-form videos, along with Fiverr.
  • Bilo was once a recommended UGC source, but its quality was described as dropping after it grew.
  • A UGC marketplace that lets you chat with creators is useful for requesting raw clips.
  • below.app is a UGC marketplace, to be used only once the product has sales.

TikTok Creator Marketplace and TikTok Creative Center

1 min
  • The TikTok Creator Marketplace (creatormarketplace.tiktok.com) lets brands run campaigns that creators apply to. A brand is your company name. A campaign is one set of ads or videos with a goal.
  • It is used to get UGC made by creators who respond to a brand brief. A brief is the instruction sheet you give creators.
  • The TikTok Creative Marketplace requires a TikTok business account.
  • Top-performing ads in the TikTok Creative Center often use UGC-style videos made by creators or agencies. The Creative Center is TikTok's site for browsing ads that perform well.
  • Many promote dropshipping products from AliExpress, so they are useful leads for sourcing. A lead is a possible product or contact to check.

X/Twitter, LinkedIn and Facebook Groups

1 min

X (called Twitter before) is a social media site. LinkedIn is a work-focused social site. Facebook Groups are online communities.

Procedure: find UGC creators on Twitter (variant A)

  1. Search for ugc or ugc creator.
  2. Sort results by Top.
  3. Use the advanced search to narrow results. Advanced search means search with filters.
  4. Contact the creators who post UGC services directly.

Procedure: hire a UGC creator on Twitter (variant B)

  1. Open the Twitter search bar and type a keyword such as UGC.
  2. Switch to the Latest tab to see recent posts.
  3. Open promising profiles and check their work, such as an Etsy shop (an online store), YouTube channel or TikTok account.
  4. Review their past UGC videos for editing quality. Editing quality means how well the video is cut and polished.
  5. Message them to offer a paid collaboration.

Procedure: find UGC creators on Twitter (X) (variant C)

  1. Search for UGC content creator.
  2. Open profiles of people who offer content creation services.
  3. Check their posts and their Media tab to see past work. The Media tab shows photos and videos.
  4. If the work fits, send them a direct message to agree on terms. Terms means price and conditions.

Other channels are X hashtags such as #ugc and #ugccreator. A hashtag is a word with # used to group posts.

Other channels are LinkedIn job posts.

Other channels are Facebook groups for local actors.

Agencies and Full Production

1 min
  • Companies found by searching for UGC content creator on Google are a UGC option.
  • Shop around and compare them.
  • Outsourced video production for Facebook ads can work in this order:
    1. Hand the Facebook and Google ad management to an agency.
    2. The agency sends scripts to content creators. A script is the written words and actions for a video.
    3. The creators shoot the videos based on those scripts.
    4. An agency editor cuts the footage into finished ads.
  • Hire an agency to produce UGC once the product shows sales traction and you cannot make your own videos.

Local Creators, Micro-influencers and Models

1 min

Procedure: local platform production

  1. Send the product to a local platform that creates custom videos from user-generated content.
  2. Hire several models to show unboxing, use and comfort. Models are people who appear on camera. Unboxing means opening the package on camera.
  3. Find an influencer from the target audience to show how to use the product. An influencer is a person with an online following. The target audience is the people you want to sell to.
  4. Use the resulting videos as social proof for paid ads. Social proof means signs that others trust the product, such as positive videos or reviews.

Procedure: micro-influencer footage

  1. Identify micro-influencers who create content you would like to use. Micro-influencers are creators with a small following.
  2. Reach out to them with an offer.
  3. Send merchandise in return for video footage and b-roll that you can use on your own social accounts. Merchandise means your products. B-roll means extra supporting clips.
  4. Confirm you have secured the footage rights before relying on the partnership for content. Footage rights means permission to use the video.

Micro-influencers can be given merchandise in exchange for video footage and b-roll for the brand's own accounts.
