Playbook › 13. Influencers and UGC

Relationship building before the pitch

Section 13.7 · 7 of 21 in this chapter · 7 min read

In short

  • Before you message a target person, get about 10 interactions with their posts, like comments or replies. Use less if they post rarely.
  • Only interact where you can really help. Skip people you cannot help.
  • Thank creators every time, and give praise with no request attached.
  • Results come slowly. Keep doing this every day, and do not expect quick wins.
This section has 8 parts. Tap one to open it.

Dream 100 Engagement Before Outreach

1 min

Dream 100 means your list of the 100 people you most want to reach. Outreach means contacting them directly.

  • Rule of 10: Before you contact a Dream 100 prospect (a person on your list) directly, aim for at least 10 engagements with their content. An engagement is any interaction, such as a comment, a reply or another action. Treat this as a guideline and use judgment rather than a hard rule. If the prospect posts less often, reduce the number in proportion. **
  • Lower number for periodic publishers: The source gives two reduction rules:
    • Use the proportional reduction for any prospect who posts less often.
    • For prospects who publish only now and then, about 5 engagements before reaching out is enough. **
  • Back catalogue counts: A back catalogue means a person's older posts. Engagements on older posts count toward the number. Going through their older posts helps you reach 10 faster. **
  • The number is a default: If a prospect replies to your emails and asks about your business, you can contact them directly much earlier. **
  • Engage only where you add value: Engage with a Dream 100 prospect only when their audience is one you can truly help. If you cannot add real value for that audience, skip the prospect. Do not comment just to be seen. **
  • Relationships create opportunities: In high-ticket dropshipping (you sell expensive products, and a supplier ships them to the customer, so you hold no stock), relationships with people in your market open many chances. Those chances stay hidden if your business stays isolated. Starting conversations makes you known to them. Every business in the space should do it. **

Engagement Routine

1 min
  1. Interact with each prospect's content in their communities and forums.
  2. Provide helpful responses to community members.
  3. Continue until you reach at least 10 meaningful interactions.
  4. Then proceed to direct outreach.

**

A simple daily Dream 100 routine, in order: **

  1. Open YouTube and check whether the creators you follow or subscribe to have posted new videos you can watch.
  2. Check your inbox for newsletters (regular emails from lists you joined). Reply to any that are relevant.
  3. Visit the Facebook groups or forums (online discussion boards) you belong to. Review the day's new posts.
  4. Where you have relevant knowledge of the market, products or space, write a short helpful paragraph on a few posts. You do not need to answer every post.
  • Low-effort option: Leave brief, sincere comments on a creator's new video. For example, praise a specific point or ask a follow-up question. These take little focused thinking and still make you visible to people in your market. **
  • Patience: Dream 100 activity pays off over the long term, not as a quick win. Do not rush it or expect results within days. The only requirement is to keep doing it regularly. **

Public Appreciation and Shout-Outs

1 min
  • Praise without strings: Public appreciation means praising someone in public. Examples are writing about a creator's work, linking to it, or building on it and sharing the result. This builds relationships without needing a reply. Expecting a response creates pressure, so give praise with no strings attached.
  • Shout-outs grow the brand's account: An influencer shout-out is a post where an influencer (a person with a large audience) mentions your brand. On the creator's account, shout-outs can grow your brand's own Instagram account. As that account grows, creators may contact the brand directly. This is an inbound source of partnerships, meaning they come to you.
  • Name influencers in your content: Mention relevant influencers by name in your content. People like being talked about and praised. A featured influencer is much more likely to share your content than to answer a cold request (a first contact with no prior relationship).

Procedure for featured influencers:

  1. Build a Dream 100 list of the most relevant influencers in your niche (your narrow market).
  2. When a post mentions one of them, send them a direct message.
  3. Praise their account. Tell them they were featured in your content. Include the link and ask for a share. Do not send the same generic message to all influencers.

Template for the message to a featured influencer:

Hi [Influencer name], I really love your account and your content about [their topic]. We featured you in our new guide [guide title] on our website, and I wanted to make sure you saw it. Here is the link: [URL]. If you think it's useful for your audience, I would really appreciate a share. Thanks, [Your name]

Thanks and Gratitude

1 min
  • Thank every creator: Thank influencers every time, whether you pay them, send free product or work on a gifted basis (free product in exchange for content, with no fee). A long-term relationship makes creators more willing to help again, even for free.
  • Gratitude pays off later: Expressing gratitude and aiming for a long-term relationship pays off later. A creator who likes working with you may promote your product again, even for free.

Contributor thank-you email, sent on the day the article goes live:

Subject: Your article is live

Hi [Contributor name],

Thank you again for contributing to our article [Article title]. It is now live here: [URL]

If it fits your channels, I would really appreciate it if you could share it with your audience, for example on your blog, your Facebook page or group, or your website. Nothing mandatory, just a casual request.

Thanks again,
[Your name]

Mentions and Roundups

1 min
  • Best of the interwebs procedure: A roundup is a post that collects the best content from several sources. Best of the interwebs is a roundup of the best links from around the web. **
    1. Publish the collection and name each featured creator or site.
    2. After publishing, contact each featured owner or creator by email, using the outreach template.
    3. Tell them they were featured. Say they are welcome to share the piece with their audience, email list or website.
    4. Expect that not all will share. Some will. Use the contact to build relationships in your market.
  • Roundups use others' attention: Best of the interwebs and expert roundups (posts that collect quotes or tips from several experts) build relationships in the market. They use other people's attention. This helps before you have earned attention yourself. **
  • Attention matrix before roundups: Before you create roundup content, prepare a list of the experts in your market. This list is called an attention matrix. It tells you whom to contact for quotes. Use this list as the source of contributors for expert roundup posts. (Older step, 2021-22.)
  • Tagging brands in trend videos: Tagging means naming a brand's account in your post. You can tag a brand in a trend-based, meme-style video (a short, joke-style video that follows a popular trend). This can earn a reply from the brand and free publicity if the video goes viral (spreads very widely). Tag only when the content is strong. Brands mostly respond to big views.

Community and Seeding Through Relationships

1 min
  • Community creates unpaid content: Build a sense of community and real care for creators, so they feel close to the brand. Creators with a close connection often post unpaid content for the brand. This adds value beyond the paid deliverables (the work you pay for).
  • Open, ongoing relationships: The goal of outreach is a friendly, ongoing relationship. In it, creators feel comfortable sharing their own content ideas and bouncing ideas off the brand (talking ideas through with you). This openness produces the best and most worthwhile content.
  • Earn product through relationships: Celebrity wear was earned through relationships, not paid for. Attend artists' events, such as concerts. Where possible, buy an extra ticket for backstage access (entry to the area behind the stage). Meet their team and explain your vision: who you are and what makes your designs different.

Trust and Transparency Before the Pitch

1 min
  • Show numbers first: Build trust before the pitch (your sales request) by showing numbers. Without your own track record (proof of past results), you can use case studies (detailed examples) from other industries. They show the approach works. But they must not be presented as your own results.
  • No cold requests: Influencers rarely accept a deal based on a cold request. First build the relationship with a call. Bring a prepared promotion script (what to say) and a brief (a short plan of what you need). Influencers do not know how to promote, use scarcity (limited availability) or trigger emotion.
  • Full transparency from the start: Be fully transparent about what the collaboration looks like, from the beginning. This builds a solid foundation. Stay flexible in how you work together, especially with larger influencers. Frame the deal as mutually beneficial (good for both sides).

The Partnership Call

1 min
  • Format: The call can be a phone call, a Zoom call or a Skype call (video call apps). The platform matters less than agreeing on a time and having the conversation. **
  • Purpose: The first partnership call is a getting-to-know-you conversation, not a sales call. Outcomes are hard to predict, so no fixed script is needed. **
  • Core question: Ask how you can work together to benefit both businesses. You share an audience. Your product is relevant to their customers. Their content is relevant to yours. **
  • Preparation: **
    1. Review the prospect's content and learn what they do.
    2. Check whether they promote other products, run affiliate deals (they earn a commission for each sale they bring in), or sell their own products.
    3. Use that knowledge to suggest suitable partnership options on the call.
  • Call after following for a while: Calling after you have followed a prospect's content for some time makes the conversation easier. You already understand their business and topics. A cold call (a call with no prior contact) with little research is much harder. **
