Abandoned cart and checkout recovery

Section 16.16 · 16 of 24 in this chapter · 6 min read

In short

  • An abandoned cart is a cart a shopper filled but left without buying. Find these in Shopify under Orders.
  • Follow up fast, ideally within minutes.
  • Early on, contact each person by hand. Later, set up automatic emails.
  • Show ads again to people who left, using Facebook and Google.
  • Expect 1 in 10 calls to be answered. That is normal.

Finding Abandoned Carts in Shopify

  • Abandoned carts appear in the Shopify admin (the back office where you run your store) under the Orders dropdown menu.
  • Abandoned checkouts (orders started but not paid) are listed under Orders > Abandoned Checkouts.
  • The list shows the checkout number, order date, customer, email status and whether the cart was recovered (won back as a sale).

Nightly Check Routine

Check abandoned carts every night in the Shopify admin.

  1. Open Orders in the admin panel.
  2. Click Abandoned carts.
  3. Note who abandoned which products.
  4. Follow up with each one, or have someone do this for you.

Speed and Channel Priority

  • Following up on abandoned carts is one of the first automations (tasks a tool runs by itself) worth building for a dropshipping store (a store that ships products straight from suppliers).
  • Send the follow-up as fast as possible after a shopper leaves, ideally within minutes, not hours.
  • Abandoned-cart recovery works best through direct calls and text messages as soon as possible. (2023)
  • Email runs automatically in the background.
  • A Shopify and Slack link (Slack is a team chat app) can send an alert with the customer's name and cart items, so you can reach out quickly.

Email Follow-Up

  • At the very least, send an email to every abandoned cart.
  • Early on, calling them directly is a way to recover more sales.
  • Once the store is established, run abandoned cart follow-up as an automated email flow (a series of emails a tool sends on its own). For example, build one in Klaviyo (an email marketing tool).
  • The Klaviyo app can run the abandoned cart reminder flow.

Manual Abandoned Checkout Emails (before automating)

  1. In Shopify, click Orders.
  2. In the dropdown, click Abandoned checkouts.
  3. Open the records that include customer details, and email each customer.
  4. Start with manual emails before automating this step.

A template is a ready-made text you copy and fill in. Replace the bracketed parts before sending the manual email:

Subject: Quick question about your order

Hi [customer first name], this is [your name], the owner of [store name].com. I noticed you had [items in cart] in your cart but did not finish your order. I wanted to check whether anything stopped you from ordering from our store. If you have any questions or comments that would help us understand why you did not complete the order, please reply. I am happy to help and to do what we can to earn your business.

[Your name]
[Store name]

Phone and SMS (text message) Follow-Up

Call Recovery Procedure

  1. Keep automated follow-up emails as the first step.
  2. Set a delay timer (a timer that waits before acting) of a few minutes after a cart is started and not completed.
  3. Send an alert with the customer's name, products and phone number. Use Slack, or Zapier (a tool that links apps so they pass information to each other) as an alternative.
  4. Call as soon as possible, and ask whether there are questions or problems with the order.

Call Script

Hi [customer first name], this is [your name] from [store name]. I noticed you started an order for [product name] but did not finish checking out. Is there anything I can help with, such as a question about the product, the shipping time or the payment options?

Personal Outreach

  • Contact people who left items in their cart and ask what happened. A direct conversation can turn them into buyers.
  • Personal outreach can recover sales that automated messages miss.

Visitors Without Contact Details

  • Visitors who start checkout but leave before entering any contact details cannot be followed up personally.
  • Reach them through remarketing ads only (ads shown again to people who visited your store).

Messenger and Ad Funnel (Day by Day)

A funnel is the set of steps a shopper goes through, one after another.

Tool Stack

  • The automated abandoned-cart funnel uses five tools: Facebook, Shopify, an email marketing tool, Minichat (a bot that sends messages in Facebook Messenger), and Zapier to connect Minichat with Shopify.
  • Minichat and Zapier both have free plans. Their paid plans were listed at under 20 USD per month.

Messenger and Social Retargeting

  • Keep Facebook and Instagram retargeting set up properly. Influencer visitors who did not buy can be retargeted to convert (become buyers) later.
  • Add Messenger abandoned cart messages to capture visitors who add items but leave.

Funnel Steps

Key words for the table:

  • A sequence is a series of messages sent one after another.
  • A collection ad shows several products in one ad.
  • Scarcity means making the offer look limited in time or stock.
  • Social proof means showing that other people bought and liked the product.
Day after abandoned cart Shopper status Action Channels
0 to 2 Has not bought Reminder that the cart is still waiting. Assume the shopper was distracted, busy or comparing prices. Do not offer a discount yet. Messenger sequence and Facebook ads
3 to 4 Still has not bought Offer the easiest incentive: often free shipping, or 10% off if free shipping is not the lowest-cost option. Include the discount code and a link back to the store. Messenger, collection ad and email
5 to 7 Still has not bought Send the best offer and add scarcity. Use social proof on days 5 and 6. On day 7, make the best discount expire within 12 hours. Video ads and emails

Sources: Day 0 to 2; Day 3 to 4; Day 5 to 7

  • Reach the shopper through several channels, because many buyers need multiple touchpoints (contacts) before they purchase. They may not check a single channel.
  • Each message should show that the store wants the sale, since competition is high.

Offers

  • Abandoned cart reminders work better when they include a valuable offer. The offer gives the shopper a reason to return and finish checkout.

Cart Audiences and Exclusions

  • An audience is a group of people the ad tool keeps, based on what they did on your store.
  • An exclusion audience is a group you leave out of an ad.

Fallback Without Abandoned Cart Data

A fallback is a backup plan.

  • When you have no abandoned cart data, use Facebook retargeting ads to target people who viewed your products or added items to their cart.
  • Combine Facebook retargeting with Google remarketing using the same audience to maximize visibility.

Audience Setup Procedure

  1. Create a custom audience (a group built from your own store visitors) of people who visited your cart page.
  2. Create an exclusion audience of people who reached the thank-you, confirmation or checkout-complete page.
  3. Use a generic retargeting image, such as your store logo or a range of your products. It does not have to be the exact product left in the cart.
  4. Show a message such as "Did life get in the way? Come back and finish your order."
  5. Optionally, after a while, show a 5 or 10 percent discount code to people who have still not bought. Never show it to buyers.

Trigger Setting

  • For the abandoned-cart campaign, use the Add to Cart event (a signal that fires when someone adds an item to their cart) as the trigger (the signal that starts an action) when building Facebook audiences.
  • In the collection ad audience settings, select Add to Cart from the viewed product, add to cart and purchase options.

Three-Audience Sequence

Set up these three audiences once, so the shopper moves through the funnel automatically.

Audience Added to cart Excludes Funnel step
1 0 to 2 days ago, not purchased nothing Reminder
2 3 to 4 days ago, not purchased last 2 days Easy offer
3 5 to 7 days ago, not purchased previous 4 days Best offer and deadline
  • The wording for audience 2 is unclear, but this table matches the funnel timing above.

Expected Results

  • Retargeting abandoned cart visitors on Facebook can recapture up to 25 percent of lost sales. This works on top of the automated abandoned-cart email sequence.

Testing and Benchmarks

  • The commonly cited average abandoned cart rate (the share of carts left without a buy) is 67%, based on a 2015 study.
  • An estimate puts the current figure higher, in the 70s, but gives no source for it.
  • Test automated follow-up for abandoned checkouts.
  • A low answer rate (about 1 in 10 calls answered) is normal.
  • Phone follow-up by hand often does better than automation alone.
