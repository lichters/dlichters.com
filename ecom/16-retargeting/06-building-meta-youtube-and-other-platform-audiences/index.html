Playbook › 16. Retargeting

Building Meta, YouTube and other platform audiences

Section 16.6 · 6 of 24 in this chapter · 5 min read

In short

  • Show ads again to people who already know you. Start with the people closest to buying.
  • A new store with little traffic should use the longest window, 180 days.
  • Cold video ads fill the warm pool by themselves once viewers watch half the video.
  • For each new post, retarget your groups, build lookalike groups from them, and repeat.

Audience Tiers by Engagement Depth

Source C sorts retargeting audiences into two levels: low-level and high-level engagement.

  • Retargeting means showing ads again to people who already had contact with you.
  • Engagement means any contact, such as watching, clicking, liking or buying.
  • Low-level audiences are easy to capture on fresh pixels and ad accounts. A pixel is a small code on your website that records visits. A fresh pixel or ad account has no past data.
  • High-level audiences need Shopify or Klaviyo data and more historic data. Shopify is a store platform. Klaviyo is an email tool. Historic data means records from the past.

Meta Website Visitor Audiences

  • Meta website-visitor audiences can be built for 7, 30 or 180 days. Meta is the company behind Facebook and Instagram. A website-visitor audience is a saved group of people who visited your site.
  • A new store with little traffic should use the largest window, 180 days. Little traffic means few visitors.
  • Facebook website-visitor custom audiences should be built for 7, 30, 60 and 180 days. A custom audience is a saved group of people that ads can target.
  • Each window can carry different ads. The longer the window, the less warm the audience gets, meaning less recent interest.
  • The sources differ on window sets. lists 7, 30 and 180 days. adds 60 days. Use the 7/30/180 set for a new store with little traffic. Use the 7/30/60/180 set when each window needs its own ads.
  • Source C lists "All website visitors, 30 days" as a fresh-pixel audience. Source A recommends 180 days for a new store with little traffic. The sources do not state a traffic threshold between the two.
  • A newly created Meta custom audience can take up to 3 days to finish matching profiles. Matching means Meta finds the people in the group.
  • Ads can run during this time. The audience grows as it fills.

Engagement Audiences on Meta, YouTube and Facebook Pages

  • Many ad platforms let you target people who interacted with a website, YouTube channel or Facebook page. An ad platform is a company where you run ads, such as Meta or Google. **
  • Knowing where your buyers already spend time gives you a shortcut to placing ads and offers in front of them. **
  • Subscribers of a niche YouTube channel or a Facebook page are a pre-qualified audience. Pre-qualified means already likely to be interested. A niche is a narrow topic. **
  • Because they chose to follow a niche source, they are more likely to engage with your offer. **
  • Facebook can also build segmented audiences. Segmented means groups split by behavior. **
  • These come from user behavior on your site and on the Facebook network, in addition to Google segmentation. **
  • People who engage with a boosted post can be saved as remarketing audiences. A boosted post is a post you paid to show to more people. Engagement means likes, comments or shares.
  • Use these audiences later for remarketing campaigns, and to show future page posts to some of them.
  • A brand that had been posting content built a Facebook engagement audience of about 280,000 people and an Instagram custom audience of about 140,000 people. They engaged in the past 365 days.
  • Pools of this size can be shown new content at very low cost per reach. Reach means the number of people who see your ad. Pool means a group of people.
  • Check their size regularly as the audience grows.

Cold Video Ads and the BOF Warm Pool

  • Cold video ads are ads shown to people who do not know you yet. Cold means no contact yet.
  • BOF means bottom of funnel: people close to buying. A warm pool is a group of people who already showed interest.
  • Cold video ads automatically feed the BOF warm pool. No manual setup is required.
  • Viewers who complete 50% or more of a cold video are added to a custom audience for BOF retargeting.
  • Source C lists "Video views 50% to 95%" as a retargeting audience.
  • Source D uses 50% or more as the entry point to the warm pool. The sources do not explain the 95% upper bound.

Procedure:

  1. Deploy (run) cold video ads to broad audiences. Some viewers buy immediately, but most do not.
  2. Automatically, viewers who complete 50% or more of the video are added to a custom warm audience pool.
  3. BOF ads immediately retarget this pool with all five concept angles at lower CPMs. CPM means the cost to show your ad 1,000 times. A concept angle is one idea or hook for an ad.
  4. As cold video spending continues, the warm pool grows daily. BOF ROAS increases automatically without BOF campaign changes. ROAS means return on ad spend: sales earned per dollar spent on ads.

Audience Growth Cycle: Content, Engagement and Lookalikes

  • Retarget the seed audience and the engagement audiences with new content each time a post is published. A seed audience is the starting group the platform learns from.
  • Then build lookalike audiences from those groups and repeat the cycle. A lookalike audience is a group of new people who act like your group.

Procedure:

  1. Publish content that is funny or educational, and promote it to the seed audience.
  2. Build custom audiences from people who liked, commented on, or shared the content.
  3. Create lookalike audiences from the seed and engagement audiences.
  4. Show new content to the seed, engagement, and lookalike audiences.
  5. Repeat until the target market knows the brand.

Lookalike and Similar Audiences from Retargeting Pools

  • Any retargeting audience can be turned into a similar audience in Google. In Facebook, the same idea is called a lookalike audience. **
  • The platform then finds new users who resemble that group in behavior and demographics. Demographics means facts such as age and location. **
  • This gives more targeted cold traffic than broad prospecting. Prospecting means showing ads to new people. Cold traffic means people who do not know you yet. **
  • Buyers are the strongest seed for lookalike audiences.
  • Source C marks them as "great for LLAs" and needs Shopify or Klaviyo data to build them. LLAs means lookalike audiences.

Google Remarketing for Cart and Product-Page Visitors

  • Set up Google remarketing for cart and product-page visitors. Cart visitors are people who added items to their cart. Product-page visitors are people who looked at one product.
  • This lets you reach the same audience you are targeting on Facebook.
