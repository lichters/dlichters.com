Playbook › 15. Email and SMS

Platform choice, account setup and sender identity

Section 15.2 · 2 of 17 in this chapter · 16 min read

In short

  • Pick Klaviyo or Drip. Both connect to Shopify, and the customer sees the same email either way.
  • Send from a sales address on your own domain, never a personal inbox.
  • Before any flow email goes live, set Smart sending to off and UTM tracking to on.
  • Use a single opt-in so fewer people drop out of your list.
  • An email still in Draft is never sent.

Platform Choice, Account Setup and Sender Identity

1 min

Choosing an Email Platform

3 min
  • The customer sees only the email itself, either plain text (simple text) or HTML (designed layout). Every email provider delivers it the same way. The software choice does not change what the customer receives or how they respond.
  • No import tool moves flows (automatic email sequences), lists and automations from one platform to another. Switching requires manual setup. Factor this rebuild time into any decision to change.
  • Drip and Klaviyo both connect to Shopify (the online store software) and sync customer data (keep it matched) for automated email. Choose either one; the choice comes down to preference.
  • Drip is an e-commerce autoresponder (a tool that sends automatic emails based on what a customer does). It sends automated sequences, such as abandoned cart emails (emails sent to people who left items in their basket without buying), and syncs customer and order data from Shopify. It is one of two recommended options.
  • Klaviyo is an autoresponder that also covers SMS (text messages) and forms. It connects to Shopify in the same way as Drip and is a valid alternative.
  • Klaviyo is the most widely used email marketing app for Shopify and is recommended for its strong built-in automations (ready-made automatic emails).
  • Klaviyo is the tool used to build the automated flows. An active Klaviyo account is the only requirement to start.
  • Klaviyo is installed on Shopify to send automated emails for bounced visits (people who leave a page without acting) and abandoned carts. Set it up before the store takes traffic (visitors).
  • Send promotions and abandoned cart flows straight from Klaviyo. Add contacts to Shopify only when they buy, become abandoned carts, or sign up through a Shopify-native opt-in form (a sign-up box built into Shopify).
  • Clavio is an email and SMS marketing app used for sign-up forms and pop-ups (small boxes that appear on the page), automated flows (welcome series, abandoned carts, thank-you emails), and email campaigns (one-off mass sends) to the collected list.
  • The Clavio setup follows the Klaviyo-type setup, so the same steps apply to both.
  • Shopify Email is recommended over Klaviyo mainly because it is much more cost-effective (more value for the money).
  • A store already using Klaviyo with a healthy return on ad spend (ROAS: the revenue earned for each dollar spent on ads) has no reason to switch immediately. A store that wants to save money should switch.
  • A vendor claims that, at the time of publication, Shopify Email covered about 99% of what Klaviyo can do. Verify this against current features before relying on it.
  • Shopify Email can replace older email platforms without paid add-ons (extra paid tools). It includes AI-assisted features for writing campaigns and images.
  • Ontraport is a marketing automation platform (software for automatic marketing tasks). It is expensive but offers many controls and triggers (events that start an email) for high volumes, similar to Infusionsoft (another automation tool).
  • Mailchimp is a cheaper option that works well for a standard Shopify autoresponder. Clavio can produce designed email templates.
  • For a pure affiliate site (a site that earns commission by selling other companies' products), use GetResponse as the autoresponder. Its free first month lets one affiliate sale pay for the service, and it covers the features needed for affiliate marketing without extra cost.
  • Older advice (2021-22) recommended Drip for all e-commerce emails, including post-purchase (emails sent after a purchase), browse abandonment (emails to people who looked at products but did not add them to a cart) and flows, especially with Shopify integration. Klaviyo remained a decent alternative. Switching was only advisable for new setups, since recreating existing flows is a lot of work. **
  • Older advice (2020) described Klaviyo as an email marketing app used for email marketing and autoresponders for the stores. **
  • Older advice (2021) stated that the newest version of the email setup uses Klaviyo for email capture (collecting addresses) and does not require Privy (a pop-up app). Keeping Privy is fine if it is already installed. **

Formula for Shopify Email cost above the free allowance:

Cost per month = ((emails sent in the month − 10,000) ÷ 1,000) × $1
Below 10,000 emails per month: $0
Example: 15,000 emails → (15,000 − 10,000) ÷ 1,000 × $1 = $5

Shopify Messaging: Installation and Branding

1 min
  • Shopify Messaging is Shopify's free app for sending marketing emails and SMS messages from the store. It is used for email campaigns and automations that are set up in later steps.

Procedure: Install Shopify Messaging

  1. Open the Shopify admin (the store's control panel) and click Apps.
  2. Search for "Shopify Messaging" in the Shopify App Store (Shopify's app shop) and pick the best match at the top.
  3. Click Install.
  4. Accept the access permissions Shopify asks for and click Install again.
  5. Once installed, open the app's settings and complete the configuration.

Procedure: Configure Template Branding in Shopify Messaging settings (Template Branding sets how every email looks)

  1. Open Settings > Template Branding and click Customize.
  2. Upload the store logo.
  3. Choose the font. Sans Serif is the example choice, but any font used on the store can be selected.
  4. Set text and background colors. The example uses black and white.
  5. Set the button fill to the store's brand color.
  6. Leave the address and store name fields as they are.
  7. Click Save.

Checklist after installing Shopify Messaging

  1. Install the app from the Shopify App Store.
  2. Set Template Branding (logo, font, colors, button fill brand color) and save.
  3. Check the sender email is sales@yourdomain.com (the address emails come from) and enter the same address under Notification Sections (the place where Shopify sets the sender for each type of notice).
  4. Keep Double opt-in inactive. (Double opt-in means the customer must click a confirmation email before they join.)
  5. Keep Optimize Open Tracking active. (It counts when people open your emails.)
  6. Leave UTM defaults unchanged. (UTM are tags added to links so you can see where a sale came from.)

Klaviyo: Account and Signup Form Setup

2 min

Procedure: Install and create the account

  1. Search for Klaviyo in the Shopify App Store, click Add app, then Install app.
  2. Enter your sender name (the name customers see) and sender email address (the address emails come from) and click Continue.
  3. Select your business goal, here to earn more revenue.
  4. Select email only as the channel (the way you reach people).
  5. Open the dashboard (the main control screen) and start with a signup form.

Procedure: Install and connect

  1. Install Klaviyo from the Shopify App Store. It is registered with the same email address used for the Shopify account.
  2. Connect Klaviyo to the Shopify store.
  3. Turn on website tracking (a small piece of code on the site that records visits).
  4. Import existing contacts, starting with email contacts and newsletter subscribers. This step can be skipped if none exist.
  5. Optionally build the brand library by uploading logo and brand colors, which Klaviyo then applies to all email templates.
  6. Optionally invite team members.

Procedure: Initial setup including the signup form

  1. Enter sender name and sender email (for example, a name from the brand and a contact@ address on the store domain, the store's web address).
  2. Select business goal (earn more revenue) and channel (email only).
  3. Create a signup form via Signup forms in the left menu.
  4. Choose a pop-up design, name it and add it to a subscriber list (newsletter).
  5. Replace the percentage offer with a fixed dollar offer (for example, save $5 with the first order).
  6. Replace the picture with a product or gift image. Free images are available, for example from unsplash.com (a free photo site).
  7. On the success step (the screen shown after sign-up), communicate the discount code created in Shopify.
  8. Publish the form.

Procedure: Clavio setup

  1. Go to Apps > All recommended apps, open the Shopify App Store, search Clavio, and click Install.
  2. Create the Clavio account and enter a sender name (for example, a support representative's name) and a sender email address on the store's domain (for example, a contact address).
  3. Choose email only.
  4. Set up core flows: welcome series, abandoned checkout, and order confirmation series. Then use campaigns for one-off sends.

Integration and interface checks

  • In Klaviyo's integrations area (the page that links other apps), check that the store integration is listed as connected. Turning on the site option adds the JavaScript (code that makes sign-up boxes work) that the sign-up box needs to the storefront (the public shop pages). **
  • When Klaviyo updates its interface (its screen layout), check the current account layout before following these steps. The interface may differ for new accounts even if existing accounts remain unchanged. **
  • Check that promotional (sales-focused) and evergreen (always running) sequences are live in the account before relying on them. **

Drip: Setup Procedure

1 min

Procedure: Drip Email Marketing setup **

  1. In the Shopify App Store, search for drip and click Drip Email Marketing (Klaviyo can be searched the same way).
  2. Click Install app. You are taken to the Drip sign-up page.
  3. Create a Drip account with first name, last name, email, password and a goal, complete the human check (a test to prove you are not a robot) and click Start free trial.
  4. Return to Shopify and approve the subscription. The trial starts at $0.00 USD, and then click Start free trial.
  5. Check the sender business address that loads from your Shopify account. Edit it in the box if needed, then click Continue.
  6. Wait for the account to sync (match its data) in the background. Confirm that Drip shows the store as connected and that its tracking snippet (a small piece of code that records visits) is installed on the site.
  7. Leave the detailed email setup (sequences and flows) for later, when it becomes relevant.

Sender Name

1 min
  • Use a person's name as the sender name, such as a founder, yourself, a customer service rep, or a fictional persona (an invented character), combined with the brand. For example, "John from ABC Lawnmowers" works better than the brand name alone.
  • In a welcome or flow email, the sender name works better as a named person from the store plus the store name, such as a customer representative, than as a bare brand name. A personal sender name makes the message feel like a reply from a human.
  • Use a named customer representative as the sender name, combined with the store name, and send from the store's own email address. This makes automated emails feel personal and recognisable.
  • The Klaviyo sender name and sender email are requested at account setup and appear on every email. Use a real person's first name as the sender name, so the email reads as a personal message rather than a company broadcast.
  • Set the Klaviyo sender name to a person's first name plus the brand, for example "First name from Brand", with a sender email address on the store's own domain. This gives customers a personal sender they recognise that matches the store.

Business Domain Email

1 min
  • Use a business email address on your own domain (name@yourdomain) for suppliers and customers, not a free address such as Gmail, Hotmail or Yahoo. Free addresses make a store look like a small hobby operation, which lowers trust. **
  • Keep business email separate from your personal inbox and accounts. Mixing the two makes supplier and customer correspondence (messages) hard to find and manage. **
  • Create one core business address first, such as support@, sales@, hello@ or info@, and use it on the website and for customer contact. Add addresses for specific purposes later. **
  • Do not put a personal or free email address on the website or in supplier outreach. Customers and suppliers expect a domain-based address. **
  • The business address is the one customers use to contact you at launch and the one shown on the website. Set it up before the site goes live. **
  • The personal email address entered during Google Workspace sign-up (Google's business email and office tool suite) receives the welcome message and serves as the account recovery address (the backup way back into the account). Use an address you can reliably access. **
  • Google may ask for a phone-number check on a newly created account as a security measure. Creating many accounts in a short time can make this check appear more often. **
  • The verification screen during Gmail activation says the page can be closed and reopened later. Closing the window is not a failed setup. Check the admin console (the control panel) to confirm the activation status. **
  • Pricing differs by source:
    • A Google Workspace search-result snippet (a short preview shown in search results) showed plans from $8.40 per user per month when it was captured. **
    • The standard cost quoted was about $12 per month. **
    • Check the live pricing page for currency and region before budgeting, because prices change.

Physical Address and Footer

1 min
  • Klaviyo asks for a physical postal address to comply with anti-spam rules (rules against unwanted email). Use a registered business address, such as a low-cost London office space or a virtual office (a business address with mail service, no desk), rather than a personal home address. Update the company registration address and the website when the new address is in place.
  • The footer (the bottom block of an email) in the example email template library carries the sender's business identity: an ABN (Australian Business Number, a tax ID), a street address and an unsubscribe link (a button to leave the list). Outside Australia, use the equivalent company registration number and the business's postal address in the same footer block, so every email includes them automatically.
  • Include the business address in the footer of business emails, such as marketing emails.
  • In Klaviyo emails, remove the organization name or full address from the email footer if you do not want it shown. The two sources differ on whether the address is optional: the rule above applies to business emails in general, and this option applies to stores that choose not to display it.

Opt-In Settings

1 min
  • Choose unconfirmed opt-in when the autoresponder allows it. Unconfirmed opt-in adds people at once, because subscribers are added immediately with no extra step. Confirmed opt-in sends a confirmation email that many subscribers never see or click, which loses a share of your list.
  • Use a single opt-in with a welcome discount instead of double opt-in, because fewer than 5 percent of subscribers confirm a double opt-in email.
  • Shopify Email: Settings > double opt-in. A double opt-in sends a confirmation email after the customer subscribes and asks them to confirm. Leave the option at its default, "not required", because it adds friction (extra steps that make people quit) to sign-ups.
  • Shopify Messaging: set Double opt-in to inactive.
  • Configuration for the Messaging app: disable double opt-in, enable optimize open tracking, and leave UTM settings at default values.

Tracking, UTM and Smart Sending

2 min

Settings summary. Smart sending is a setting that can delay or skip an email so one person is not over-emailed. UTM tracking adds tags to links so you can see which email brought in a sale.

  • In every Clavio email, set Smart sending to off and UTM tracking to on before you switch the email from Draft (not sent yet) to Live (sends). The setting window opens when you click the email in the flow.
  • Before a Clavio email goes live, set Smart sending to off and UTM tracking to on in the email settings panel. Then set the email from Draft to Live.
  • For every flow email, make sure Smart sending is off and UTM tracking is on before setting the email to Live. UTM tracking lets you measure the revenue from each email efficiently, which Smart sending would otherwise interfere with.
  • In Klaviyo, set Smart sending to off and UTM tracking to on in each flow email.
  • In Klaviyo emails, Smart sending should be off and UTM tracking on. The flow then sends each message on schedule and the traffic can be tracked.
  • The UTM parameters in Shopify Messaging are appended (added) to links in emails. This lets sales from emails be tracked in Google Analytics (Google's traffic tool) and in the Messaging app. Keep them at their defaults.
  • Do not modify the UTM campaign structure or advanced tracking settings in Shopify Messaging.
  • Keep the default option to track all email opens in Shopify Email. This gives the most accurate open-rate reporting for emails sent through Shopify Email.
  • Optimize Open Tracking in Shopify Messaging should be switched on, so email opens are tracked.

Going Live and Final Checks

1 min
  • An email that stays in Draft is never sent. Each flow email must be switched from Draft to Live after it is edited.
  • Branding and settings changes are not applied unless they are saved. Confirm that all Shopify Email settings are saved before moving on. Otherwise, new automations and campaigns may not carry the intended configuration.
  • Before relying on the Shopify Messaging setup, run the checklist in the Shopify Messaging section above.

Migration Between Platforms

1 min
  • When moving from Klaviyo to Shopify Email, check your Shopify customer list first. Klaviyo sign-ups usually come through Shopify opt-ins, so many contacts may already be in Shopify and may not need an export.
  • Contacts are added to Shopify only when they buy, become abandoned carts, or sign up through a Shopify-native opt-in form, so a Klaviyo-to-Shopify move should be checked against those routes.
  • Switching platforms requires manual rebuilding of flows and automations. Factor this rebuild time into the decision.
  • Older advice (2021-22) said switching is only advisable for new setups, since recreating existing flows is a lot of work. **
  • A store that wants to save money should switch to Shopify Email. A store already using Klaviyo with a healthy return on ad spend has no reason to switch immediately.
