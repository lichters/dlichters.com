In short

  • Use filter rules in Gmail to move junk email out of your inbox automatically.
  • Share files as view-only links, and let each person save their own copy.
  • Your email list and follower lists are valuable. Build them and keep them engaged.
  • Follow-up emails must give help and ask for one action. Put the link in the P.S. line. Do not copy old sales tricks.

Inbox and team tools

  • In Gmail, create filter rules (automatic sorting rules) that send unwanted email straight into a folder. Spam then does not clutter your inbox every time you log in.
  • To share a Google Docs or Google Sheets file (online document or spreadsheet) for a team to copy, set the share link to view-only. Anyone with the link can read it.
    • The recipient then opens File > Make a copy to save their own editable version in their Google Drive (online file storage).

Building and owning audiences

  • The email list and the lists of followers and engagers (people who like, comment or reply) are the assets used to attract partners and sales opportunities.
    • A brand with an engaged list can do business with related companies easily.
  • Pinterest with a linked store and product pins (posts that link to a product) costs nothing to set up. It is not a major source of sales compared with Google and Microsoft.
  • Instagram Broadcast Channels (one-way announcement channels) suit brands with an already engaged audience. Check the minimum follower count before investing time, because one may be required to create a channel. Also check whether the format fits your page.
  • Broadcast Channels work like a VIP club. Followers feel they get behind-the-scenes access.
    • The brand can post one-way, meaning only the brand sends messages. It can also run quick polls and ask for future video ideas.
    • Followers can answer, but they cannot freely send messages to the whole group.
  • Broadcast messages are a one-way stream, so encourage emoji reactions (small icon replies) rather than expecting full replies. Reactions are a low-effort way for followers to feel connected to the brand.
  • Livecom's channel page with the stored RCPs (the saved contact records the source names) is segmented (split into groups) inside Klaviyo (an email marketing tool). So you can promote the streams to those segments.
    • Videos embedded in the bubbles can also be reused in Klaviyo email campaigns.
  • Messenger (Facebook's chat app) broadcasts were presented as having higher open and click rates than email, because most people already have Messenger on their phone.
    • The source figures were shown only as a screenshot, so the exact percentages are not stated in the source.

Affiliate follow-up emails

  • Affiliate follow-up emails (emails that promote a link paying you a commission when someone buys) serve two purposes. They build trust through helpful content, screenshots and case studies. They also ask the reader to take a specific action.
    • Emails that only give content and never ask for anything usually produce no sales.
  • Use this follow-up template (a ready-made message with blanks to fill in):
Subject: Quick check-in

Hi {first_name},

How is your progress going? I wanted to see if you have started making money online yet. If you have not, here is the resource I recommend to start: {affiliate_link}

It helped me, and I can personally vouch for it. Let me know if you have any questions.

P.S. If you have not started yet, {affiliate_link} is the best place to begin.
  • Place the affiliate link at the end of follow-up emails, usually in a P.S. line (a postscript, an extra line after the signature). Link to the specific sales page.
    • Owning a subscriber's email gives you long-term contact until they unsubscribe (leave the list).
    • The content must stay valuable enough that they keep opening.

Collecting extra shipping charges after the order

Older advice (2020).

  • To collect expedited (faster) or upgraded shipping charges after the order, create a draft order (an order not yet paid) in Shopify (online store software) or send a PayPal invoice (a payment request). Then send it to the customer for the additional payment.

Subscriber opt-out and re-subscription

not marked as older by the 2019-2022 rule.

  • Opting out of a single email from a membership or course list can stop all further emails from that list.
    • To receive updates again, give written permission to the support team so they can re-add the address.

Product title tags

Title tags are the page titles that show in search results and browser tabs.

  • Add the words "buy" and "online" to product page title tags, as long as the store already has some traffic momentum (steady visitors). Expect roughly 10-30% more traffic and a larger sales lift.
    • The typical template is "Buy [Product Name] Online", followed by the brand name if it fits.
    • Some CMSs (content management systems, the software that runs the website) cannot do that order. Use "[Product Name] Online" instead.
  • Add buy/online to product title tags in one of three ways:
    1. Edit each product manually if you have few products (about 10 products in 15 minutes).
    2. On larger CMS stores, use an SEO plugin (an add-on that helps search ranking) with a title and meta description template for product pages. A meta description is the short summary shown under the title in search results.
    3. For thousands of products, export to CSV (a spreadsheet saved as plain text with commas between values). Use formulas in Excel to build the new titles in spare columns, then import the file back.

Sales claims (not practice rules)

Older material (2021-22). This is sales copy from a course's own sales offer, not a practice rule. Do not copy its claims or its urgency tactics (pressure to buy fast) into your own store.

  • A Dropship Breakthrough offer (selling products you do not stock, shipped by the supplier) was presented with a 42% discount from a $4,997 annual price to $2,987 annual.
    • It also had a monthly option of $9.97 for three months, and a 72-hour deadline.
    • The stated 42% does not match the prices: $2,987 is about 40% below $4,997.
