Email, SMS and customer lists

Section 5.12 · 12 of 22 in this chapter · 19 min read

In short

  • Use Klaviyo as your main email tool. Most advice in this text uses it.
  • Install your email tools before visitors arrive, so automatic emails run from day one.
  • Check the current pricing page for Klaviyo's free limit. The limit changes.
  • Shopify Email is free up to 10,000 emails a month, but it is less deep than Klaviyo.
  • Keep your current tool unless you have a clear reason to switch, because moving automatic emails is slow and costly.
More key points
  • Clean your email list about every six months. Remove addresses that are fake, bounced, or spam.
  • Warm up your sending address for a couple of months before you rely on it, with a warm-up tool.
  • Check where your emails land (inbox or spam) with a second tool. Your email platform's numbers are not always fully accurate.
  • Use a separate tool for each job.
  • Automate only after the manual process works. Manual means done by hand.

Choosing the Email and SMS Platform

5 min
  • Klaviyo (email software that sends and tracks emails) is the platform most advice in this chapter uses. It connects deeply to Shopify (the online store software). It handles automation (emails that send by themselves when something happens), segmentation (sorting contacts into groups), SMS (text messages to phones), and templates (ready-made email designs). It reports the revenue that email brings in.
  • Install Klaviyo before the store gets traffic (visitors), so automatic emails are running from the start.
  • Klaviyo covers all email: campaigns (one-time emails sent to a list), abandoned cart emails (sent to visitors who added items but did not buy), and messages to vendors.
  • Klaviyo sends abandoned cart emails and promotions directly, without first pushing every contact into Shopify.
  • Klaviyo is the platform for lead flows (automatic email series for new sign-ups; a lead is a possible customer). Its dashboard (the summary screen) shows the revenue those emails create, so you can check whether a lead flow works.
  • Klaviyo is used for email and SMS, including sign-up forms, flows (automatic email series), campaigns, and segments.
  • Klaviyo stores the email addresses captured by exit-intent overlays (pop-ups that appear when a visitor is about to leave), and shows how many visitors signed up.
  • Klaviyo is a strong choice for stores of this size. It is used instead of a heavy CRM (customer relationship management software, a tool for tracking customers).
  • Klaviyo is the preferred email platform for Shopify because of its deep connection to it. Omnisend was not checked, because Klaviyo met every need.
  • Klaviyo is the recommended email tool. It supports exit-intent overlays and embeddable opt-in forms (sign-up forms you place on a page) for sign-up pages.
  • Klaviyo is recommended for abandoned cart flows and other automated messages.

Shopify Email, Shopify Messaging, and Omnisend

  • Shopify Email is a free, built-in email tool. It includes 10,000 emails per month free, then charges fractions of a penny per email. It lacks Klaviyo's template library (collection of ready-made designs), but it connects natively (built in, no link needed) and needs no extra app.
  • Shopify Email is included with a Shopify account. It sends up to 10,000 emails a month at no extra cost, and is recommended over Klaviyo for cost reasons. Klaviyo is more advanced but becomes expensive as the list grows (2024 advice).
  • Shopify Email covers most of what Klaviyo does for new stores, and the first 10,000 emails every month are free. It is not necessarily better than Klaviyo, and switching is not required (2023 advice).
  • Shopify Email is free for up to 10,000 emails per month and can replace Klaviyo for most stores. Klaviyo remains a strong option, but Shopify Email now covers most of its functions at no cost (2023 advice).
  • Shopify Email is a free email marketing app built by Shopify. It sends automated follow-up emails and promotional campaigns to the store's list. It does not handle day-to-day one-to-one emails, which go through your regular mailbox.
  • Shopify Email includes Abandoned Checkout and Discount Reminder automation templates, and a built-in AI tool (artificial intelligence, software that writes or improves text) that improves and customizes template messages.
  • Shopify Messaging is a free app from Shopify for email and SMS, used mainly for marketing and follow-up messages.
  • Shopify Messaging is the main app for abandoned checkout automation, opened through the Shopify Admin (the store's control panel). Shopify Flow (a visual editor for building workflows, step by step, with boxes and arrows) lets you change and customize the workflow.
  • The Shopify Messaging dashboard shows emails sent, open rate (share of people who open an email), conversion rate (share of people who buy after an email), and revenue. A date range selector lets you choose the reporting period.
  • Omnisend Email & SMS is an email and SMS platform. It is added at a later stage, alongside tools such as Klaviyo, not at the foundation stage.

When to Keep or Switch Email Tools

Advice disagrees on whether Shopify Email should replace Klaviyo. Use the rule that fits your stage.

  • New store, no existing list: Shopify Email can be tested from the first day as a comparison. It can replace Klaviyo for most stores at no cost (2023).
  • Automation depth (how advanced the automatic emails can be): At the time of this advice, Klaviyo still offered deeper automation than Shopify's built-in tool. Switch to Shopify Email only if it matches Klaviyo's automation depth.
  • Large or growing list: Shopify Email is recommended for cost. Klaviyo is more advanced but becomes expensive as the list grows (2024).
  • Existing Klaviyo user: There is no reason to switch to another email tool. Switching large email flows is costly and the results are uncertain, so keep Klaviyo.
  • Any existing working tool: If an existing email or opt-in tool already works and you are comfortable with it, there is no reason to switch. Migration (moving your data to another tool) usually creates a lot of unnecessary work. Switching makes sense for a new business, or when you are looking for something new.
  • Other alternatives: Drip is a comparable alternative to Klaviyo. It has a different interface for building flows (automatic email series) and moving contacts between lists.
  • Deciding factor: Email automation platforms such as Klaviyo and Drip do similar things, with similar depth of Shopify connection. The main deciding factor is usability, especially how flows are built and how contacts move between lists. A faster tool lets you produce more automations.
  • Lock-in (being stuck with a tool): Only the email automation platform is costly to change later. Flows and lists cannot be imported into another platform, so they must be rebuilt by hand.
  • Klaviyo at the time of this advice: Klaviyo was still the platform used for welcome, upsell (offering a bigger or extra product), and other flows, with triggers (events that start a flow) set per product or event.
  • Do not pay twice: Do not pay for both Klaviyo and Privy. Switch your opt-in forms to Klaviyo instead.

Installing the Tools

2 min

Klaviyo for Shopify

  1. In Shopify, open Apps, then Apps and sales channels, then open the Shopify App Store.
  2. Search for Klaviyo, click Install, then click Install apps.
  3. Register with the same email address used for the Shopify account. The Klaviyo dashboard opens.
  4. Connect Klaviyo to Shopify, if it is not connected yet.
  5. Turn on website tracking and enable it for the store.
  6. Under import, upload contacts (email contacts recommended) and newsletter subscribers, or skip this step if none exist yet.
  7. Open the brand library and add the logo and brand colors. Klaviyo applies them to all email templates.
  8. Invite team members, or skip this step.

Shopify Email

  1. Log in to the Shopify admin panel of the store.
  2. Click Apps in the left navigation.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the installed apps list and click All recommended apps.
  4. Under Made by Shopify, find Shopify email.
  5. If it is not listed, click Find more apps in the Shopify app store, search for "email", and select Shopify email.
  6. Click Install, then click Install app to grant the requested permissions.
  7. Confirm that "Email" now appears under Apps.
  • The Shopify Email app dashboard has Templates and Settings sections. Automations and campaigns are also created from this app, after its basic configuration is complete.
  • Open Apps in the Shopify admin and click the Email app. Click Settings to reach the sender email, double opt-in (the sign-up needs a second confirmation click), open tracking (counting who opens emails), and template branding options. Template branding is at the bottom of the page.
  • The first email may ask the recipient to display images from the sender. After clicking this, images show in that email, and future emails display images by default.
  • Besides a browser color picker, look up the brand's six-digit hex color code (a color code made of six letters and numbers) in the store's own Shopify account settings. Paste that hex value into the Template Branding color field, such as the button fill, so emails match the site.

Other Store Apps Installed Alongside Email

  • Klaviyo and SMSBump are both installed. Klaviyo handles email automation, and SMSBump handles SMS.
  • An assistant installed and set up Klaviyo and SMS Bump for a client's store.
  • Clavio and SMS Bump are recommended for installation. Tell the team which apps to install and how to set them up.

Abandoned Checkout and Cart Flows

1 min
  • Email and SMS apps are needed to win back visitors who added items to the cart or started checkout without buying. This improves customer retention (keeping customers coming back).
  • Clavio's flows matter most when running paid or TikTok traffic (visitors from paid ads or TikTok). They send reminder emails every few hours to visitors who added to cart but did not buy.
  • Clavio can handle email remarketing (emailing past visitors again) and SMS for abandoned carts once the store has traffic. One tip: use a second phone number for SMS sign-ups, with the Text Free app, to keep personal numbers private.
  • Klaviyo is installed to send automated emails, including for bounced visits and abandoned carts.
  • Klaviyo builds the abandoned checkout flow, the email templates, and the A/B tests (comparing two versions to see which works better). Use Preview & test to send test emails, and Manage template to edit the shared layout. Save before going live.
  • ChatGPT (an AI writing tool) drafts the abandoned cart copy from a brand primer (a short guide to your brand's voice). Keep the working prompts in one document, and update them when a version performs better.

Checking Abandoned Cart Flows on Single-Page Checkout (2023 advice)

Single-page checkout means the whole checkout is on one page.

  1. Opt in with a test account on the checkout form.
  2. Place a test order.
  3. Confirm that the abandoned cart emails and Zapier triggers both arrive. Zapier is a tool that links apps together automatically.

Flow Settings Before Going Live

  • The Klaviyo flow Settings panel lists Smart Sending (limits how often one person gets emails), UTM Tracking (tags links so you can see where visits came from), and Additional Filters (rules that choose who gets the flow) as separate toggles (on/off switches). Check each one before the flow goes live.
  • Additional Filters was switched off in the example flow. Add filters only when a specific audience rule is needed.

Welcome, Lead, and Post-Purchase Flows

1 min
  • Klaviyo flows are automations that fire after a customer action, such as an order or a delivery.
  • Klaviyo flows are used for many automatic messages, not only review requests. Common examples are birthday emails and shipping confirmations, all triggered by a customer action.
  • Set the flow trigger in Klaviyo to "When someone subscribes to" the sign-up pop-up's list. In the example, the pop-up was an OptiMonk offer of 10% and 15%, so the flow starts at sign-up.
  • Klaviyo can automate quote follow-up emails with flows and filters for separate brands. Building these requires a lot of setup for each brand's templates and images. Automate follow-ups only after the manual template system works, so the templates are already tested.
  • Feedback Genius is an autoresponder (a tool that sends emails automatically) that connects to your Amazon account. It sends email sequences 5, 6, and 10+ days after purchase. It enables upselling, sending e-book links, and requesting reviews without manual emails. At about $19 per month, it becomes essential once you reach 30-50 sales per day, because manual follow-up becomes impractical.

Sign-Up Forms, Pop-Ups, and Lead Capture

4 min

Choosing a Pop-Up Tool

  • Klaviyo is used for sign-up forms, including the exit-intent pop-up that collects phone numbers.
  • Klaviyo now includes its own sign-up forms and exit-intent pop-ups, so one tool can handle email capture and the flows. This replaces older standalone pop-up apps such as Privy.
  • Klaviyo's built-in sign-up form builder creates the welcome pop-up.
  • Klaviyo's embed code (a snippet of code that places the form on your site) connects forms to a Shopify page.
  • Klaviyo handles exit-intent overlays in this setup, replacing Privy, which was dropped because it was no longer needed. Exit-intent overlays can be hidden on specific pages where discounts are restricted.
  • Klaviyo now covers both email marketing and coupon or pop-up offers for newer stores. Privy is no longer needed alongside Klaviyo, and an existing Privy paid plan can be dropped once Klaviyo is in use.
  • Klaviyo now handles the email functions previously done by Privy, while After Ship remains part of the setup.
  • Privy is a Shopify app used to capture emails from site visitors with pop-ups and offers. The brand reported an email engagement rate of about 20% (share of emails that get opened or clicked) from this approach.
  • Optimonk is a pop-up builder for multi-step pop-ups with email and SMS sign-ups. It is chosen over Klaviyo pop-ups for stronger visual design and faster load speed.
  • OptinMonster builds content opt-ins (forms that collect emails inside content): sidebar forms (a box beside the page), inline forms (a box inside the page, such as inside a blog post or collection page), and exit-intent pop-ups. The same tool can place a related download in the middle of an article.
  • Email opt-in apps such as OptinMonster and email campaign tools such as Drip can be turned on before a store drives paid traffic. This captures visitors who are not ready to buy.

Building an Optimonk Campaign

Procedure:

  1. Ask the client to install Optimonk from the Shopify app store on their store.
  2. Have the client send you an Optimonk team invite, so you do not need Shopify admin access.
  3. Open app.optimonk.com with the invite, and confirm you can see the client's account.
  4. Create a new campaign from scratch, select the relevant domain, and click Next to open the blank canvas.
  • The paintbrush tool copies the style of one element, such as a button, onto another. Use it to make a new button match an existing one, then change the label or color only where needed.
  • Known issue: Optimonk's hover effect (the change that happens when the cursor moves over a button) on some buttons does not stick after the cursor moves away. After styling, move the cursor in and out of every button on desktop and mobile to confirm the hover state behaves as intended.

Connecting Optimonk to Klaviyo

Note: this guide spells the tool "Optimunk" in the connection steps. Other parts of this text name it Optimonk.

An API key is a long code that lets two apps talk to each other safely. A private key is a second code kept secret.

  1. In Klaviyo, click Settings in the left menu and open the API section.
  2. Copy the public API key and enter it in Optimunk under Integrations > Add new integration > Klaviyo.
  3. Back in Klaviyo, click Create Private API Key.
  4. Name the key Optimunk and set Select Access Level to Full Access. The options are Custom Key, Read-Only Key, and Full Access. Full Access gives access to every API scope (every permitted action).
  5. Click Create, and copy the private key into Optimunk.
  6. Click Setup integration in Optimunk, and continue.
  7. In Optimunk, open the Klaviyo integration, click Select a list, and choose the list you just created in Klaviyo. Then continue to field mapping (matching the form's fields to the fields Klaviyo stores).

Facebook Lead Ads

Lead ads are Facebook ads with a form that collects contact details inside Facebook.

Setup in Facebook Creator Studio:

  • Navigate to Creator Studio → Tools → Lead Ads Forms. Create a new form, upload a brand image, add a headline, and set fields (Name and Email at minimum). Add a privacy statement, then integrate with Klaviyo via Integrations → Facebook integration.

Connecting the lead form to Klaviyo:

  1. Go to the Klaviyo dashboard.
  2. Click Brand (bottom left), then Integrations.
  3. Add the Facebook integration, and log in.
  4. Attach the lead form created in Facebook Creator Studio.
  5. Anyone who submits the form automatically enters a custom Klaviyo list.
  6. Create lead generation campaigns targeting that list.

Other Capture Tools

  • Shop Message is a Shopify app for Messenger marketing (Facebook's chat app). After installation, it adds an automatic opt-in to the product detail page and sends abandoned cart messages to the customer's Messenger inbox.
  • A notify-me-when-back-in-stock app at about $5 per month captures email sign-ups from visitors who cannot buy yet. This works as proof of demand for pre-orders (taking orders before stock arrives) or stock-outs (items sold out).

Quizzes and Contests

1 min
  • Typeform is used to build quizzes on the website, and the quiz results trigger automatic follow-up emails.
  • Typeform, Quizzer, and WriteMessage are tools for running online quizzes and surveys that collect email addresses. Their prices differ, so cost is the deciding factor when the results are otherwise comparable. Choose based on the price fit for your budget.
  • Quiz tools such as Typeform, WriteMessage, and Quizzer capture emails before showing results. The quiz is embedded on a dedicated page of the Shopify store.
  • For quiz-based newsletter sign-ups, search the Shopify App Store for a freemium app (a free version with paid upgrades) or an inexpensive quiz app. Typeform is expensive and is not a Shopify app.
  • A contest tool grows the list. It is one of the separate tools used for each email marketing job.

SMS Marketing

1 min
  • SMSBump is a dedicated SMS marketing app that includes GDPR compliance (following the EU privacy law) and quiet-hour sending rules (times when texts are not sent).
  • SMSBump is an SMS tool that also includes abandoned cart recovery. It can be used as an alternative to Klaviyo for SMS-based cart recovery.
  • Klaviyo is used for email automation, and SMSBump is used for SMS marketing. Both are needed to set up automated flows for abandoned carts, abandoned checkouts, and welcome messages.
  • Recart is an SMS tool that also lets you create email-style templates for SMS. SMSBump is a similar alternative. Klaviyo is used for email and pop-up rules.
  • Klaviyo is an email and SMS platform, and SMS Bump is installed alongside it.

Messaging Channels and Personal Outreach

1 min
  • Minichat is a Facebook Messenger bot (an automatic chat program) that sequences messages the way an email campaign does. Once set up, it sends automatically. Each message in a sequence is scheduled, so it does not need manual sending.
  • Bonjoro can send personal thank-you messages to survey respondents. Create a separate list in Bonjoro for survey responses, distinct from the new-customer welcome thank-you flow. Send one pre-recorded message per response.

Building Emails

1 min

Klaviyo Email Builder

  • The Content panel offers blocks (pieces you drag into the email) such as Text, Button, Horizontal rule, Product, Layout, Columns, Image, Header bar, Social links, Table, Section, and Spacer. The Product block shows the abandoned items.
  • Set the Section Styles fields in Klaviyo: Background, Content color, Section color, Border, Padding, Columns, and Stack on mobile. Check each section on both Desktop and Mobile previews.
  • Product block placeholders (blank spots that fill in product details automatically) appear in the builder. Liquid is the code language these tags use. Check the exact code in your account. These show each abandoned item with its quantity and line price:
Title: {{ item.product.title }}; Quantity: {{ item.quantity }}; Total: {% currency_format item.line_price|floatformat:2 %}
  • Klaviyo is used to send the promotional emails for each holiday.
  • Klaviyo is the email platform used for lead flows and campaigns. Use a saved template for each new blog post, with the featured image, title, short description, and a button to the blog. Send a manual broadcast each time a new blog post goes live.

Design Assets and AI Drafting

  • Canva (an online design tool) can build image elements for emails, such as a collage of customer testimonial screenshots. Place the finished image in an image block inside the email.
  • Claude and Claude Design (AI tools) can generate a new email module (a reusable email section) for a use case the existing library does not cover. Upload the new module to the library for reuse.

Campaigns, Segments, and Audiences

1 min
  • Klaviyo is an email tool.
  • Klaviyo can export lists of opted-in non-buyers for Facebook custom audiences. Opted-in means people said yes to your emails. Non-buyers means people who have not bought yet.
  • A custom audience is a group of people you upload to Facebook, so your ads reach them.
  • Klaviyo can segment by product purchase. A segment is a group of contacts picked by a rule, such as people who bought a certain product.
  • Klaviyo stores the email list. It shows how much revenue the list generates. Revenue means money earned.

List Hygiene and Deliverability

1 min

List hygiene means keeping your email list clean. Deliverability means how often emails reach the inbox instead of spam.

Cleaning the List

1 min
  • Upload your email list as a CSV. A CSV is a plain spreadsheet file.
  • Upload it to a verification website. This is a site that checks whether addresses are real.
  • It shows counts of verified, spam trap, complainer, bot, and undeliverable addresses.
    • Spam trap: a fake address used to catch senders with bad lists.
    • Complainer: a person who marks your email as spam.
    • Bot: an automatic program, not a real person.
    • Undeliverable: an email that cannot be delivered.
  • Remove the bad ones.
  • Do this about every six months (2024 source).
  • Third-party validators are outside tools. They check for invalid (wrong) and unreachable (cannot be reached) addresses.
  • Validators are used during list cleaning. Import means loading a list into a tool.
  • The cleaned list is what gets imported into the new provider. A provider is the email service company you use.

Warming Up the Domain

1 min

A domain is the web address you send email from. Warming up means slowly building trust for that address, so email providers accept your mail.

  • Warmup Inbox (warmupinbox.com) is a domain warm-up tool.
  • It connects to a sending inbox, which is the mailbox you send from.
  • It sends emails automatically. It simulates (fakes) opens, replies, and clicks. It moves emails out of spam.
  • Run it continuously for a couple of months, alongside the provider's own warm-up.
  • Warm-up tools can show inbox versus spam placement for each mailbox provider. A mailbox provider is the company behind an inbox, for example Gmail.
  • This gives you a second opinion on deliverability.
  • This matters because the email platform's own metrics (numbers that measure results) are not always fully accurate.

Platform Architecture

1 min

Platform architecture means how your tools are set up to work together.

  • Each email marketing job is best handled by a separate tool.
  • An email automation platform sends and segments emails. Automation means the tool sends emails by itself, based on rules.
  • An opt-in form tool collects addresses through pop-ups and inline forms. A pop-up is a box that appears on a page. An inline form is a form built into the page text.
  • A contest tool grows the list.
  • A quiz or survey tool collects addresses through questions.
  • Quiz and survey tools differ in price.
  • When results are otherwise comparable, cost decides.
  • Automate only after the manual process works. Manual means done by hand.
  • Build flows once templates are tested. A flow is a series of automatic emails. A template is a ready-made email design.

More details

  • Klaviyo is a widely used email and SMS marketing app for Shopify with strong prebuilt automations. It handles sign-up forms, flows, segments and campaigns from one place.
