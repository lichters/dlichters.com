Affiliates, outreach and promotion channels

Section 5.21 · 21 of 22 in this chapter · 16 min read

In short

  • Refersion runs an affiliate program, where partners earn money for sales they send you.
  • Refersion costs money: it needs a paid monthly subscription.
  • Wait on affiliates: the 2023 advice says not to start an affiliate program in a new store's early phase.
  • Use Dashboard > Manage > Creatives to upload banners for your partners.
  • Check every partner contact yourself: AI-made lists often have broken links and wrong emails.

Column letters (A, B, C ...) refer to the research worksheet from section 2.3.

This section has 12 parts. Tap one to open it.

Refersion: what it does

2 min
  • Refersion is affiliate management software for e-commerce stores. Affiliate software runs a program where partners (affiliates) earn money for sales they send you.
  • You set Refersion up on your site.
  • Place an affiliate signup link in your website footer (the bottom strip of every page).
  • Upload banner ad creatives (ready-made ad images) for affiliates to download.
  • Refersion tracks commissions (payments per sale) and sales.
  • Refersion is recommended for managing affiliate programs.
  • It organizes banner creatives.
  • It tracks affiliate performance.
  • It handles payments.
  • It gives partners a login portal (a private sign-in page) where they get promotional assets (ready-made marketing material) and create tracking links (special links that record who sent a visitor).
  • Affiliate software such as Refersion generates unique affiliate links (special links that record which partner sent a visitor).
  • It tracks cookies (small files saved in a visitor's browser to remember the partner).
  • It shows dashboards (control panels with charts) of affiliate sales, click-through rates (the share of people who click a link after seeing it), and pending payouts (money owed but not yet paid).
  • It integrates with Shopify (an online store platform).
  • Refersion is a Shopify app (an add-on that plugs into Shopify) for tracking affiliate programs, managing partnerships, commissions, and approvals.
  • Refersion is a Shopify affiliate and referral app (a referral means a customer recommends a friend). It is used to pay partners or customers commissions for sales they generate.
  • Refersion is an affiliate tool that pays creators (people who make content) or partners for sales they generate.
  • It suits affiliate-style programs in which payment depends on results.
  • Refersion is an affiliate program platform used to manage affiliate partners and track their sales.
  • Refersion is a simple tool for running a store affiliate program.
  • Refersion is a Shopify app used to create an affiliate program.
  • Niche blogs (small blogs about one specific topic) can join for free.
  • They can link to the store to earn affiliate traffic (visitors sent by partners).
  • They can create backlinks (links from other sites to yours, which help search rankings).
  • Refersion is preferred over loyalty-program apps (apps that reward repeat buyers with points) for referral programs and affiliate tracking.

Refersion: pricing

1 min
  • Refersion requires a paid subscription (a regular monthly fee).
  • Its costs are easily recouped (paid back) through affiliate-generated sales.
  • Refersion is a paid app, not a free one.
  • Niche blogs that join an affiliate program through Refersion do so for free.

Refersion: when to add it (sources disagree)

1 min
  • Older advice: Refersion is the recommended software.
  • Place the affiliate signup link in the website footer and upload banners so affiliates can use them.
  • Older advice: Refersion is the affiliate platform recommended for stores large enough to run an affiliate program.
  • A footer link alone adds no sales.
  • Source A: Refersion is the affiliate program app the source recommends for managing affiliates.
  • But it is not recommended during the first stage of a store.
  • Source A: Affiliate programs are not advisable in the early phase of a new store.
  • Paid placements (paid adverts) on relevant sites usually produce faster results.
  • How to apply: The 2021-22 sources describe how to set up the program.
  • The 2022 and 2023 sources describe when a store is ready for one.
  • Under the 2023 advice, do not start with an affiliate program in a new store's early phase.
  • The sources give no numeric sales threshold (a set number of sales that proves a store is ready).

Refersion: banners and affiliate links

1 min
  • In Refersion, go to Dashboard > Manage > Creatives. Dashboard is the main control panel.
  • This tab shows all banner ads available to your affiliate partners.
  • Partners click a banner to copy an embed code (a short piece of code that shows the banner on another site).
  • Partners paste the embed code into their website's HTML (the code that builds web pages) to load the banner automatically.

Procedure: upload a creative to Refersion

  1. Go to Dashboard.
  2. Click Manage.
  3. Click Creatives.
  4. Click the button to create a new creative.
  5. Upload a banner image file.
  6. Enter the landing page URL (the web address where visitors arrive) where the banner links.
  7. Add a description.
  8. Assign a category if desired.
  9. Save.
  • Refersion automatically appends each partner's unique affiliate code (a code that identifies one partner) to the end of your landing page URL.
  • Refersion personalizes the landing page URL for every affiliate partner by adding their unique affiliate code to the end.
  • You enter the URL once, and the system handles the personalization.

Other referral and creator-payment tools

1 min
  • Referral Candy creates affiliate links for products in a Shopify store.
  • It tracks the sales generated by each creator.
  • It lets you set per-order payouts (a fixed amount for each order) or percentage payouts (a share of each sale).
  • It lets you set tiers (reward levels that grow with sales).

Affiliate-site tools

2 min
  • Hosting and domain: Buy the domain and hosting at Namecheap. The domain is your web address. Hosting is server space that keeps the site online.
  • Namecheap is used to buy the domain and first-year hosting for the affiliate site.
  • At the time of the source, domain registration cost 10 dollars and first-year hosting cost 12 dollars.
  • Platform: Use WordPress (website-building software) with specific themes (design templates) and plugins (add-on tools) for affiliate sites.
  • Shopify is built for taking payments in a store, so it is a poor choice when your visitors are sent to another merchant (another seller).
  • Amazon affiliate store: Shopper Press is a WordPress e-commerce theme that costs around 70 dollars.
  • It can import 20,000 or more Amazon products automatically, with prices, images, and descriptions.
  • It is used to build a branded Amazon affiliate store (a store that sends visitors to Amazon products and earns commission).
  • This Amazon store sits alongside a dropship store (a store that does not stock items; the supplier ships them).
  • Prerequisites: Building an Amazon Associates (Amazon's affiliate program) WordPress store with an imported product catalog (the full list of products) requires working knowledge of WordPress, WordPress themes, and affiliate marketing.
  • Without these basics the store is hard to set up and maintain, so wait until you have them before building one.
  • Banner advertising: BuySellAds offers banner placements (ad spaces) on different websites within many niches.
  • It can be used to buy visibility on niche sites when paid search (ads shown in search results) and social ads are limited.
  • Free consultation as lead bait: Skype (a video-call app) is the video-call tool named for a short free consultation, such as 10 minutes on a specific topic.
  • Limit the slots, because each lead (a person who shows interest in buying) costs you personal time.

Email tools for affiliate sites

  • Entreport: A powerful autoresponder (a tool that sends emails automatically) with membership features (tools for paid, members-only areas) and a high price.
  • It suits membership sites, but it is more than a pure affiliate site needs.
  • Mailchimp: Avoid it for affiliate marketing.
  • Its price is similar to GetResponse (another email tool), but it bans accounts that send affiliate links in their emails.
  • Aweber: Be careful.
  • On about half of accounts it requires a mailing history (past emails sent) before you can disable confirmed opt-in (a rule where each subscriber must click a confirmation email before joining).
  • A new account would ask every subscriber to confirm, which adds friction (extra steps that make people quit).

Finding and verifying partner contacts

1 min
  • hunter.io finds email addresses that belong to a website.
  • It helps when a site has no usable contact email for outreach (reaching out to people).
  • Use it only when the site itself does not offer an address you can use.
  • ZoomInfo is a business-information website that can reveal company contact details when a brand's site cannot be found.
  • Search the brand name plus "Zoominfo" written as one word.
  • The free view of contact details can be used without signing up, and the paid subscription is not required.
  • Sales CRM tools (CRM, or customer relationship management, is software that tracks contacts and deals) can suggest possible email addresses for influencers and public figures.
  • These tools have scraped the internet (collected automatically from public web pages) for contact details.
  • Use them as a supplement (an extra source) to manual searches, not as the only source.
  • D7 Lead Finder collects a list of businesses in a chosen city or country, such as dentists.
  • It pulls listings from Google and business pages.
  • It shows whether a business is running Facebook ads (paid ads on Facebook).
  • It exports the list to an Excel sheet (a spreadsheet file) after about three minutes of processing.

Building a partner target list

3 min

Dream 100 worksheet

  • To get an editable copy of the Dream 100 worksheet (a spreadsheet template for a list of 100 ideal partners), open the template and choose File > Make a copy to save it to your Google Drive (online file storage).
  • For offline work, choose Download and pick Excel, which opens in Microsoft Excel or Apple Numbers (spreadsheet apps).
  • Use these three sheets. CPC means cost per click (what you pay for each click on an ad). Guest posts are articles you write for another site in exchange for a link.
Sheet Columns
Sheet 1, master list Category, Name, URL, Email, Phone number, Guest posts and social shares, Paid ads, Notes, Date contacted
Sheet 2, ad trial Name, URL, Email, Ad types, Date trial requested, Date trial accepted, CPC
Sheet 3, guest posts Name, URL, Email, Date contacted, Notes
  • The master list is the starting point.
  • The guest post and paid ad columns are filled in during research.
  • Use the notes column for every response, follow-up date, and status.

AI-generated drafts

  • ChatGPT (an AI chat tool), or another AI tool, can generate a first draft of partner names, categories, URLs (web addresses), and emails from a prompt (written instructions sent to the AI).
  • Its output often has broken links, wrong or missing emails, and fewer rows than requested.
  • It only seeds the sheet (starts it) and does not replace research.
  • The free version of ChatGPT is enough to run a partner-list prompt that returns names, categories, URLs, and emails in a spreadsheet-ready format.
  • Use the same prompt in any other AI tool you already have, but expect incomplete and unverified output.

Partner-list prompt

I run [store URL]. I'm building a target list of 100 people and companies who could send their followers to my online shop. Candidates include celebrities, websites, blogs, YouTube creators, course sellers, events, Instagram accounts, podcast hosts, and associations whose audiences are interested in buying [niche products]. Find these contacts and enter them in a spreadsheet with this layout: Column A holds the category, chosen from the types above such as celebrity, website, blog, YouTuber, course creator, event, Instagram account, podcast, or association. Column B holds the name of the person or business. Column C holds their website URL, when you can locate one. Column D holds their email address, when you can locate one. If no email turns up, leave the cell empty, and never make up emails or URLs. Aim for 100 rows of people and/or companies. If you can't reach 100, give back as many genuine rows as you can and state the exact number returned.

Procedure: run the partner-list prompt

  1. Replace [store URL] in the prompt with your store's URL.
  2. Replace [niche products] with the products you sell in your niche.
  3. Send the prompt to the AI tool. If it returns two responses, compare them and keep the one with more usable rows.
  4. Copy the chosen rows into the master list sheet.
  5. If the result has fewer than 100 rows, ask the AI to redo the list and include data for 100 people or entities (people or businesses), then compare the counts again.
  6. Keep a partial list as a starting point and fill the gaps through manual research (searching by hand).
  • In Google Sheets (Google's free spreadsheet app), use View > Freeze > Up to row 3 so the header rows (the top rows with column names) stay visible while you scroll down the list.

Influencer and creator partnerships

2 min

Karo

  • Karo (getkaro.com, Shopify app "Carro") is a collaborative commerce network (a network where brands and sellers work together).
  • Its influencer side (the part for social media creators with audiences) finds people in your own customer base and email list who have an audience.
  • Its basic functions can be used for free.
  • Brand partnership features were free when this advice was written, but some elements may be paid.
  • Check the current pricing before using them.
  • Karo is listed in the Shopify App Store (Shopify's app marketplace) as "Carro" (apps.shopify.com/carro).
  • Its website address was given as getcarro.com, while the source called it getkaro.com.
  • Check the address in the app listing before signing up.
  • Karo was built mainly for Shopify but also works with a few other e-commerce platforms (online store systems).
  • Its described setup path, including connecting customers and email lists, assumes a Shopify account.
  • On the Karo homepage, the main buttons are "Get Started" and "Learn More."
  • The Shopify listing button reads "Install On Shopify."
  • In the brand partnership product view, the button "Add To My Store" changes to "Added To My Store" once the product is on your store.

Vetting creators

Vetting means checking that a creator is real and performs well.

  • Tracker is an influencer-marketing platform (software for finding and managing influencers) that costs about $80,000, which is far too expensive for most brands.
  • Many successful brands do without it and track influencer work in spreadsheets.
  • TikTok Business Center lets verified business accounts send direct proposals to creators.
  • The source reported that around 95 percent of creators responded to such proposals.
  • TikTok Creator Tools analytics (statistics about how content performs) show a creator's views and follower data.
  • Asking a creator to share a screenshot of this analytics page verifies their performance.
  • Social Blade is a third-party site (a separate company's site) that shows public statistics for social accounts.
  • It can be used as a rough cross-check (a quick comparison) of a creator's growth.
  • Its accuracy was not confirmed, so treat it as a supporting check.
  • Verify key numbers with the creator's own analytics.

Giveaways and list growth

1 min
  • Gleam and Viral Sweep are contest tools used to grow an email list (your list of subscribers).
  • They integrate contests closely with social media and email lists, and only one of the two is needed.
  • WooRise is an instant giveaway tool for building the giveaway funnel (the path from the giveaway to a sale).
  • Any other instant giveaway tool can serve the same function.
  • Klaviyo (an email marketing platform) holds the giveaway entrant lists (the names of people who entered).
  • Klaviyo tracks orders and revenue with a 30-day attribution window (the time frame in which a sale counts as caused by the giveaway).
  • Klaviyo stores the segments (groups of contacts) used to measure the funnel.
  • ManyChat (a chat-automation tool) automatically sends a direct message (a private message) to every account that comments a set keyword on a post.
  • It is used to deliver links, such as downloads, to commenters without manual replies.

Social channels

1 min

Posting and presence

  • Tailwind (a scheduling tool) schedules social media posts to Instagram, Twitter, and Facebook in advance.
  • A team member scheduled posts for the coming couple of weeks.
  • A free Facebook business page (a public profile for a company) is used as a basic social presence for the store.
  • It is the free version only, with no paid ads required at this stage.
  • A LinkedIn profile only makes sense if the target audience is active there.
  • This is typically the case for B2B (business-to-business, selling to companies) businesses.
  • Consumer-focused stores (stores selling to individual buyers) can skip it.
  • Facebook offers an option to sell directly on the platform.
  • The source recommended sending traffic to your Shopify store instead, as of early 2023.

Facebook groups

  • Facebook groups (online communities inside Facebook) have a magnifying-glass search icon.
  • Before posting a question, search the group for the same question, since members often have already asked it and received an answer.
  • Facebook groups cannot be set to read-only (a mode where members can only read).
  • Require admin approval (a moderator must accept each post) for new posts to stop new questions.
  • Send members to topic-based community channels instead.

Growth caveats

  • Some free-trial social-media growth campaigns (promotions that give free likes and followers during a trial) can produce likes and followers cheaply.
  • These numbers rarely turn into sales.
  • Focus on clicks and purchases, not followers.

Content research

  • vidIQ is a Chrome extension (a small add-on for the Chrome browser) that adds data to YouTube pages.
  • It shows trending videos (videos gaining popularity now) and views per hour.
  • It is used to find topics that are working right now before making a video.
  • Google Sheets can track content ideas: one column for the content idea and the next for the link to the source of inspiration.
  • This is enough to track content research from forums and communities.

Live shopping and shoppable video

3 min

Shoppable video means video that you can buy from directly.

Livecom

  • Livecom 2.0 is a video player that lets marketers add shoppable video to landing pages and product pages.
  • No waiting for the development team (the programmers who build the site) is needed.
  • Its vendor (the company that sells it) says it avoids the usual site speed loss (slowdown) from embedding video (placing video on a page).
  • Livecom live streaming can broadcast one stream at the same time to TikTok Shop, Instagram Live, YouTube, Twitch, X, and any other platform that accepts RTMP streaming (a standard way to send live video to platforms).
  • Viewers can then be sent to the website to shop from the stream.
  • The Tap Cart integration (a connection to a Shopify app for live streams) lets a Shopify store host live streams inside the Tap Cart app.
  • It sends a push notification (an alert on the customer's phone) to customers when the stream starts.
  • This makes the live stream available on the Shopify site, inside the Tap Cart app, and on the connected social channels.

LyveCom story video

  • LyveCom hosts the MP4 files (a common video file type) itself instead of Shopify.
  • The story video is embedded (placed on a page) with a script rather than a YouTube or Vimeo iframe (a frame that loads a page from another site).
  • Because of this, the store is not slowed down.
  • Shoppers can add variants (versions of a product, such as sizes or colours) to the cart.
  • Shoppers can check out through the store's native Shopify checkout (Shopify's built-in payment step).
  • The platform also works with any Shopify theme that accepts a custom HTML or Liquid snippet (a short code piece for Shopify's template language, Liquid).

Procedure: set up a LyveCom story and embed it

  1. Open the LyveCom dashboard.
  2. Create a new story campaign.
  3. Name the story. The name appears as the overlay (text shown on top of the video) on the video (example: Collection Release).
  4. Click Next.
  5. Select the product to be tagged (linked) in the video.
  6. Click Finish.
  7. Upload the MP4 file to the story (no file size limit; keep the video short because of attention spans).
  8. Click Publish story.
  9. Choose the embed method (how it is placed on the page): Floating Widget (a small box that stays on screen) or Embed.
  10. For the widget, open the Widget tab, click Copy the code, and paste the snippet on any page of the website.
  • There is no file size limit for the uploaded MP4, but short videos are recommended to keep a social, app-like experience.
  • Viewers tend to have short attention spans on product videos.
