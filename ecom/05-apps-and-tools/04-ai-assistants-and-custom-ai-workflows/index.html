AI assistants and custom AI workflows

Section 5.4 · 4 of 22 in this chapter · 34 min read

In short

  • Pick one AI chat tool, ChatGPT or Claude, and use it every day.
  • Give the AI real details: your store platform, your product facts, and the exact problem.
  • Check every AI text by hand before you publish it.
  • Start with clear prompts, and ask for three options instead of one guess.
  • Before any email work, check that Klaviyo shows as connected in Customize > Connectors.
More key points
  • For template work, connect Klaviyo through its API, not the Klaviyo MCP connector.
  • Before you run an unfamiliar agent folder, run the security check first, because Full access lets it act without asking you.
  • When an agent keeps making the same mistake, ask it to update agents.md instead of repeating your prompt.
  • AI answers are only as good as their sources, so check every list, number and email before you use it.

Choosing and starting with AI chat tools

2 min
  • ChatGPT is the most widely used AI chat tool. An AI chat tool is a program where you type a question and get an answer. ChatGPT works well for writing copy (sales text), finding marketing ideas, explaining concepts and learning new topics. It is very easy for beginners.
  • Claude is a strong AI chat tool. It is good at long written content, clearly organised answers, analysing information (breaking it down to check it), deep thinking and problem solving. Choose it when a task needs careful analysis or a clearly organised result.
  • Pick one AI chat tool to start, either ChatGPT or Claude. Use it daily instead of overthinking the choice.
  • For long, structured prompts (detailed instructions) that analyse customer reviews, Claude tends to do better than ChatGPT. When the result matters, run the same prompt and inputs (the information you give) in both tools, and keep the better result.
  • Use AI chat as your first stop for any store, marketing or business question. Do this before asking a community or a coach. It is available around the clock.
  • ChatGPT and Gemini (Google's AI chat tool) give equally good brand name suggestions for dropshipping stores. Dropshipping means selling products you do not keep in stock, because the supplier ships them. Choose by personal preference or by current access. The quality does not differ.
  • The free version of ChatGPT has all the functions needed for niche brainstorming. A niche is a narrow market. No paid plan is required. It can expand a broad category into specific product ideas.
  • The free version of ChatGPT is used to create content outlines and bullet points, not finished articles. The output is a starting structure that a person writes out in their own words.
  • One operator (the person who runs the business) reported that ChatGPT is free to use. It keeps earlier conversation context, which means it remembers what was said earlier. So later requests can build on earlier topics.
  • ChatGPT Professional cost about 20 dollars per month at the time of recording (2023). It gives access to browsing (searching the web) and plugins (add-on tools) that can pull data, such as Amazon reviews. Use the base version when you only need copy made from your own inputs.
  • The free plan of Claude has a message limit. It can cut off a long generation in the middle of the output. This happened when an ebook HTML file (a web-page version of a book) was cut short. Retry later, or use a paid plan and generate a full ebook in several steps.
  • Gemini asks for a login before it imports files. Attach the competitor video and exported brand data (files with your brand information) from a logged-in account. File upload in the chat does not work without it.
  • ChatGPT Playground at platform.openai.com/playground is a free-to-use alternative. Use it when regular ChatGPT sign-up is blocked. It handles the same text tasks: product descriptions, ad copy, ad strategies, and preset answers (fixed replies) for live chat.
  • Chat-based research works best with product context. When you ask about any product, describe your current work first. For example, say that you run a Shopify store (an online store software). This gives more relevant output.

Prompting rules and inputs

1 min

Giving the AI the right context

1 min
  • A large language model (LLM) is the AI that writes text. It answers from its training material (what it learned from). Always state the exact sources, definitions and step-by-step method the AI should follow. Otherwise it falls back on safe, generic answers.
  • Give the model as much product information as possible before you ask for specific outputs.
  • When you ask AI for help with a store problem, always state the platform (for example Shopify), the theme (the design template of your store, for example the Elixir theme), the exact element involved, and what happens instead. Without this context, the answer will be generic.
  • When a prompt asks for Amazon listing data (the product page on Amazon), take the title and bullet points from the product's Amazon listing. Then collect 3-5 detailed real customer reviews. The output quality depends on these inputs, so do not skip them.
  • Standard ChatGPT ad writing needs the product facts, the problem, the feeling and the benefit in the prompt.
  • Brand guidelines (rules for your brand's look) given to a chat AI: provide only the cover page as a screenshot and the colour palette. Do not send the full PDF (a document file). The AI may not read PDFs well visually, and too much information can confuse it.
  • Do not upload more than two PDF pages to ChatGPT. Upload only what the AI needs to see, because large PDFs reduce the quality of its output.

Working in threads

1 min

A thread is one continuous chat conversation.

  • ChatGPT keeps earlier messages in the same chat. So follow-up requests can refine earlier output without restating the product or business name. Keep one chat per topic so the context stays relevant.
  • ChatGPT keeps the conversation context. Follow-up requests, such as making text funnier or more emotional, work without repeating the product details. It is useful for copywriting (writing sales text), ad copy, headlines, product descriptions, bullet points, video captions, brand name ideas, and colour palettes or hex codes (colour codes written as letters and numbers).
  • ChatGPT retains context throughout a thread. So you can refer to previous examples and ask for variations.
  • An AI chat assistant works well as a sounding board (someone to think out loud with). Describe your current work and ask for feedback or ideas. This also works through phone voice mode. For each specific task, ask the AI to write a detailed prompt for you, then edit it.

Reusable prompt patterns

2 min
  • Use a short, concrete prompt. It names the problem, the goal and the number of options you want. The AI then proposes several solutions instead of one guess. A pattern is a repeatable recipe: state the problem, state the desired outcome, then ask for three options. CSV is a simple spreadsheet file. Examples: "My export file opens with broken characters; I need a clean CSV for Excel; what are three ways to fix this?" and "My agent keeps skipping the scoring step; I need every candidate scored; what are three changes I could make to the instructions?"
  • Beginner prompt for a stuck problem. It asks for options, then for changes to agents.md (a file of standing instructions for the AI), so the problem does not come back:
I am a beginner and I am stuck on this problem: [describe the problem]. I need this solution: [describe the goal]. What are three options you could give me, with the pros and cons of each? Once I choose one, tell me which lines to add or change in agents.md so the same problem does not happen again.
  • Regularly put these kinds of prompts to an AI tool, and tailor the specifics to the product you sell:
Regularly put these kinds of prompts to an AI tool and tailor the specifics to the product you sell: ask it to break down a dropshipping idea for someone new to the field, ask it to draft a description for a given item, ask it for five marketing angles worth testing on that product, and ask it why your ads might not be turning visitors into buyers. The more of these questions you ask, the more helpful the AI becomes for your business.
  • Using a ready-made prompt from the ready-made prompt collection:
    1. Find the prompt that matches your task, for example the one for store building.
    2. Copy the complete prompt, including all instructions and section names.
    3. Paste it into ChatGPT or Claude as a new chat.
    4. Fill every required input: the Amazon listing title and bullets, the product description, and 3-5 detailed customer reviews. Add your brand name and product name where the prompt asks for them.
    5. Press send and wait for the full output.
    6. Use the sections in the order the prompt generates them.

Checking generated prompts

1 min
  • A generated prompt must make sense before it is run. Read it, check it against your brand and product, and fix anything unclear. If you are unsure how to phrase a prompt, ask Claude to draft it, then review the draft instead of running it blindly.
  • Dictating (speaking out loud while the computer types) your brand, product and process, and having Claude turn that into a prompt, is faster than typing. Check that the resulting prompt matches your brand before running it.

Structure for product page and ad prompts

1 min
  • Structure for using ChatGPT to generate product page copy:
Structure for using ChatGPT to generate product page copy:
1. Provide product category and name.
2. List product benefits found from competitor research.
3. Define your role: "You are a professional copywriter."
4. Ask specific question: "Provide a one-line USP for this product that shows the benefit and differentiates from normal [alternative]." Then iterate on the response.
5. For bullet points: "Provide four benefit-driven bullet points (not features) describing what customers will experience when using this product."
6. For FAQ: "Generate four common questions and answers for our product."
7. For paragraphs: "Provide a two-to-three-line paragraph that describes [specific aspect] in a benefit-driven tone."
8. For rewrites using another product as reference: "I have this copy for another product [paste example]. Generate similar copy for our product [product name]." ChatGPT retains context throughout the thread, so you can refer to previous examples and ask for variations.
  • Bottom-of-funnel (BOF) ad prompts. BOF means the last stage, when a buyer is ready to buy. Ask Claude or ChatGPT for four testimonial (customer quote), free gift or benefit BOF ad prompts. You can also ask for primary text variations (different versions of the main ad text) based on your customer research. You can also analyse a competitor ad. This takes about 20 minutes.
Make me four [testimonial/free gift/benefit] bottom-of-funnel ad prompts for ChatGPT
Based on my customer research, make me primary text variations for a BOF ad.
What makes this ad work? Create a prompt so I can make similar ads for my niche.
  • When giving a generated prompt to an AI, a prompt that asks for an urgency offer (a deal with a deadline) can be built from the product, price point and niche.

Ads, offers and strategy

1 min
  • ChatGPT can write product descriptions, ad copy and ad strategy ideas for an ecommerce store (an online shop). It is the main AI writing tool shown here. OpenAI's Playground (a testing page) is an alternative interface.
  • An AI chat tool can suggest which urgency offer fits a specific product. Give it the product, the price point (the selling price) and the niche. Ask for time-limited offers, such as a free shipping window. Then keep only the ideas that fit the product margin (the profit left after costs).
  • Connecting Claude to the Facebook ad account lets you ask which campaigns are currently above or below break-even ROAS. ROAS means return on ad spend, the money earned for each unit spent on ads. Break-even ROAS is the point where ads neither gain nor lose money. This is a quick way to check what is working, without reviewing every campaign by hand.
  • ChatGPT Professional, with web browsing and a review-reading plugin, can pull customer reviews from a product listing. This helps you find audience language and objections (reasons buyers hesitate).

Custom AI assistants and Claude projects

2 min

What a custom assistant is

  • A custom AI assistant is a general LLM (large language model, an AI trained on huge amounts of text) such as Claude or ChatGPT. You load it with your own instructions, product information, templates and step-by-step processes. At minimum, it can handle any text task. Visuals depend on the platform and the LLM used.
  • In Claude, a project is a saved workspace. It holds the assistant's instructions and reference files. The instructions say how to create particular ad types or how to research ideas. The files hold brand-specific information.
  • The output quality depends on the instructions. Write a large body of detailed instructions, based on a proven system. This keeps the output on the right path. The assistant then produces research documents, scripts and visual assets from the information it was given.
  • A generic LLM without brand instructions and files gives generic research. It uses general sources such as platform guidelines or old top search results. This dilutes your messaging and can lead to a poor creative path.
  • Example kickoff prompt for a market study. Before you send it, upload the product description and the brand's internal strategy documents into the assistant's workspace. This way the study follows your own methodology, not generic sources. Specify that the results arrive in the layout your templates prescribe.
Hello Magnus. First go through the attached product description, and afterwards run a market study for our label.

Files and capacity

  • Reference files for a Claude project can be uploaded as Markdown (.md) or Word (.docx) files. Markdown is a plain-text file with simple formatting marks. The file format affects how easily the assistant reads it. Markdown is generally easier for AI to parse (read and understand), so mixing formats is workable. Plain-text files are still the better default.
  • The Files panel of a Claude project shows a capacity indicator. Capacity is how much of the project's space is in use, shown as a percentage. In the example, about ten brand files used 15% of capacity.
  • For the script prompt to produce a usable script, add five reference files to the Claude project. They are a voice guide, a structure library, a banned word list, an avatar (a profile of your ideal customer) and a psychology lens. Without these files, the script stays generic and you must check it by hand.

Building workflows with AI

A workflow is a fixed series of steps that gets a job done.

  1. Describe the outcome you want in plain language.
  2. Ask Claude to design the workflow that achieves it.
  3. Ask Claude to build it in the workflow tool or as code.
  4. Check the result manually for mistakes and holes in the process before you rely on it.

AI agent folders (Codex and Claude Code)

5 min

Concepts and set-up

  • An agent is an AI that can take actions, not only answer. An agent folder is a folder that holds its instructions.
  • Codex is a desktop app from ChatGPT that can run agent folders on your computer. As of May 2026, it was recommended for beginners over Claude Code. The reason is that you link your existing ChatGPT account, and setup is simpler.
  • Codex and Claude Code can be compared. Claude Code is the folder-instruction tool and uses claude.md. Codex uses agents.md. Both are instruction files. For the email workflow, the two are comparable in level.
  • The agents.md file is the most important file in an agent folder. It holds the standing instructions that shape how the AI behaves in that folder.
  • Agent folders can be downloaded from GitHub. GitHub is an online place to store and share folders. Downloading also makes it possible to deploy and update them later. You then open the downloaded folder locally in Codex.
  • Create one dedicated AI folder on your computer and put agent folders inside it. Anything you drag into that folder structure is visible to the AI. Keep sensitive data, such as credit card details, out of it.
  • Keep one master AI folder on your computer. Place each agent folder inside it as a subfolder. Open each agent folder separately in Codex. Each opening starts its own chat, scoped to that folder.
  • To connect an agent folder, open Codex and go to File > Open folder. Then select the agent folder. Codex starts a new chat scoped to that folder.
  • In the Codex chat window, the access setting is at the bottom. Set it to Full access so the agent can run without asking you for each action. The model is set to 5.5, with reasoning effort at Extra High. Reasoning effort is how hard the AI thinks before it answers. Both settings are shown in the same selector.
  • In the Codex chat box there is a Toggle /Fast option. It makes responses faster. Codex may suggest turning it on based on past use. It increases plan usage. For careful agent work where depth matters more than speed, keep Extra High reasoning.
  • Review and Undo: after an agent edits files, Codex shows a count such as Edited 3 files, with Undo and Review buttons. Review lists each changed file with its line changes. For example, +9 -2 means nine lines added and two removed. Check every change before you keep it. Use Undo to revert.

First start of a downloaded agent folder:

  1. Download the agent folder, for example from GitHub. Place it inside your dedicated AI folder.
  2. Open Codex and go to File > Open folder. Then select the agent folder.
  3. Set access to Full access and the model to 5.5 with Extra High reasoning.
  4. Start a new chat and send the security-check prompt before anything else runs.
  5. Open the handoff document and the startup prompt file yourself and read them, if you want to see them directly. A handoff document is a notes file that explains the setup.
  6. Read the agent's explanation of what will happen. Check that it matches what you want.
  7. Click go to load the agent and start its workflow.
  8. Answer the setup questions the agent asks before it begins searching.

Security-check prompt:

Please explain what is inside this folder and what it is for. It is a project I want to start working on. Check whether anything in it is malicious or could harm my computer or my data. Then explain step by step what will happen when we start, beginning with an analysis of the handoff document and the startup prompt, so I know what I am agreeing to before anything runs. Do not change, send, or delete any files yet.
  • Do not run an unfamiliar agent folder with Full access before you check its contents. It can contain malicious instructions or files. Always read the handoff and startup files. Ask the AI to explain the workflow first.

Setup questions and what agents do

  • The agent's setup questions show what it needs before it searches. They ask for the target role or candidate type. They ask what a strong fit and a disqualifier look like. They ask where proof of work can be found. They ask for example people, companies, websites or keywords to start from. Clear answers make the first pass more useful.
  • Recruiting-style agents are built to search public sources only, unless you explicitly approve something else. They add qualified candidates to a tracking CSV, which is a plain spreadsheet file. Each entry includes evidence, scores, rankings, risks and next steps.
  • The agent must not contact anyone, submit forms, share lists, change permissions or update outside systems. The only exception is an action you explicitly approve. Ask for this approval in writing for each external step.
  • Limit file searches and reads to the agent folder, unless you explicitly ask for a wider search. Writing this rule into agents.md keeps the agent from browsing unrelated files on your computer.

Getting unstuck and improving agents

  • When an agent keeps giving wrong answers or misunderstands the task, change agents.md instead of repeating your prompt. Ask the agent to update agents.md after each repeated error. Its later answers become more accurate.
  • Standing safety prompt for a beginner. It also makes the agent keep agents.md up to date:
I am a beginner. Please always run the security checks before doing any work in this folder, and explain each step in simple language. Add these instructions to agents.md so you follow them in future sessions. Update agents.md whenever the same mistake repeats or when we learn something new in this process.
  • When you get stuck, ask the AI first, before you ask a mentor. For example, ask when a folder asks for an integration that does not exist. Agents can usually propose a workaround or a custom tool.
  • Example: an agent may report that it cannot push files to Google Drive, because no Google Drive integration exists. Ask the AI how to work around this. One option is saving a local file, then uploading it manually. Another is building a small custom tool.
  • An agent folder can be used for YouTube scripts, creative ideas and recruiting. A recruiting scraper (a tool that collects data from websites automatically) can search LinkedIn, YouTube and Twitter for potential clients. Any agent can be used for a new project, as long as its instructions are set up correctly.

AI limits and verification

3 min

How AI produces answers

  • A large language model such as ChatGPT or Claude predicts the most useful next output. It predicts this from the text it was trained on. It does not produce original insight from nothing. Its answers are only as good as its source material.
  • AI output quality depends entirely on its training material. If the sources are wrong, outdated or unproven, the answers will be unreliable. This is true even when they sound confident.
  • When a general AI model draws on many conflicting marketing opinions, it averages them. The result is a watered-down answer that commits to no clear path. Broad marketing questions with no defined source produce this diluted result.
  • AI is trained to avoid mistakes. So when it is unsure which source is best, it falls back on safe, generic answers. Always state the exact sources, definitions and step-by-step method the AI should follow. Do not leave this open.
  • A single trained person can only do one task at a time. Trained AI agents can apply the same proven instructions across many tasks at once. This is the main reason to build a repeatable, well-instructed system for AI.
  • Generative AI works by predictive generation. The model outputs what would be most useful to say next, based on your question and everything it was trained on. Terms such as predictive modelling describe the same process. A different label does not change how the answer is produced.
  • Large language models such as ChatGPT and Claude are trained on mixed text. The mix includes books, blogs, essays, forum and social media posts, Wikipedia, and other web pages. Their answers reflect that mix. So topics with conflicting opinions, like marketing, give inconsistent results.
  • A model trained only on a 300-year-old science book can answer questions from that book well. It cannot propose new experiments or ideas beyond what the book contains. Its output is limited to the scope of its training material.
  • Chat assistants do well on general questions, largely because they can draw on the wider internet. But broad access to sources does not mean the answer is proven for your specific business task. Choose the source material for that task deliberately.

Verification

  • Check every generated list, email or statement before you publish it. AI tools such as ChatGPT and Gemini sometimes produce incorrect answers.
  • ChatGPT is not reliable for current trend data. This includes lists of the top dropshipping products (products sold through a store without holding stock) for the coming period, or exact market numbers. Verify any figures it gives before you use them. Use it mainly for text and ideas.
  • Many prepackaged AI dropshipping tools are thin wrappers. A thin wrapper is a product that only adds a layer on top of a general chat AI such as ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Grok. Such tools are often built on outdated technology. Using the general models directly is usually cheaper and more current.
  • Do not use an unfamiliar agent folder with Full access before checking its contents.

Changing features and limits

  • ChatGPT's web browsing was switched off temporarily. Users had found ways to bypass privacy controls through links. AI tool features such as web search can be removed or changed without notice. Check current availability before you build a workflow on them.
  • Pro plans have character limits. Long documents may need to be shortened.
  • Four AI tools were recommended as examples: ChatGPT Playground, Runway ML, Compose.ai and DALL-E. They were presented as free to use. Free tiers and access terms can change, so verify each platform before you rely on it.
  • Free-plan limits and file import rules change over time. Examples are the Gemini login requirement and the Claude free-plan message cap. Check them on the platform before you plan a large job around them.

Security and sources

  • Download AI apps only from the official site. Look-alike sites are used for phishing, which is when fake sites steal your logins.
  • Limit agent access to the folder you opened, and keep sensitive data out of any folder the AI can read.
