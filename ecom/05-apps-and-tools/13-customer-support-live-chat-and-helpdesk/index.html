Customer support, live chat and helpdesk

Section 5.13 · 13 of 22 in this chapter · 15 min read

In short

  • Use Shopify Inbox for live chat. It is free and simple to set up.
  • Turn chat on in Shopify Inbox, then check your live store in a private browser window.
  • Install the Shopify Inbox phone app so you get alerts and can answer customers fast.
  • Keep customer data in Shopify and Klaviyo. Add a CRM only when your setup stops covering your work.
This section has 11 parts. Tap one to open it.

Choosing a live chat tool

1 min
  • Live chat (a chat box where visitors message you right away) lets visitors message you during the shopping session. You can answer doubts and give instant help, which supports conversion (turning visitors into buyers).
  • For a beginner on Shopify (an online store platform), choose Shopify Inbox over Tidio. Shopify Inbox is simpler to set up. Tidio has more features and automations (tasks that run on their own), but it is more complicated to configure (set up).
  • Shopify Inbox's main advantage over Tidio is phone push notifications (alerts that pop up on your phone). They let the store owner answer customer questions quickly.
  • One 2024 setup uses Shopify Inbox for live chat with customers instead of Tidio.
  • A live chat app can be used from a phone like a text conversation. The owner can answer pre-sale questions (questions asked before buying), such as whether an item is in stock. Put a live chat link on the product page (the page for one item).
  • Shopify live chat is the support channel for store-level problems. Examples are theme (the store's design template) or code settings that affect how the store appears in search results.

Shopify Inbox

5 min

What it is and what it costs

  • Shopify Inbox is Shopify's built-in live chat sales channel (a place where you sell or talk to customers, added to your store). It is free ($0) and included with the store, so no paid live chat app from another company is needed. It is not active by default and must be added as a sales channel.
  • Shopify Inbox is a free live chat app from Shopify. It adds a chat widget (a small chat box on your website) to the storefront (the public shop pages). It is included with the Shopify plan at no extra cost.
  • Shopify Inbox is included with all Shopify plans. It lets customers message you directly from your website in real time.
  • The Shopify Inbox app is free. Install it early so that replies can be sent quickly from a phone.
  • Shopify Inbox works like a live chat. It sends push notifications to a phone whenever a customer asks a question. It is installed in the store and in the Shopify mobile app.
  • Shopify Inbox replaces the need for Tidio for Shopify stores.
  • Shopify Inbox asks for an email address before the chat begins.
  • The chat widget appears on every store page, so visitors can start a conversation at any time. Messages appear in Shopify Admin (the store's back office, where you manage everything). They are pushed to your phone through the mobile app.
  • Shopify Inbox can replace third-party live chat apps.

Install: route A (Apps menu, short version)

  1. Log in to Shopify Admin.
  2. Click Apps.
  3. Search for "Shopify Inbox".
  4. Select the app (free plan).
  5. Click Install.

Install: route B (Apps menu, detailed version)

  1. Log into Shopify Admin.
  2. Click Apps.
  3. Search for "Inbox". The listing shows the label "Free plan available".
  4. Click Install. The app is added to the store.
  5. Optionally pin the "Inbox" entry in the sidebar (the menu on the left side of the admin) so it is easy to find.
  6. Click Inbox in the sidebar and click "Turn on chat".
  7. Customize the widget in the theme customizer (the editor for your store's design). See the App embeds settings (the switches that place apps on your pages).
  8. Click Save.
  9. Reload the storefront to confirm the chat icon is visible.

Install: route C (Sales channels)

  1. Log into the Shopify admin.
  2. In the left navigation, open Sales channels.
  3. Click the plus icon, search for "inbox" and select Shopify Inbox.
  4. Confirm with yes to grant the app access to store data.
  5. Shopify Inbox then appears under Sales channels.

Install it in the Shopify App Store (Shopify's app shop) by searching "Shopify Inbox" and adding it to the store. Then install the Shopify Inbox app on the Shopify mobile app.

Turning chat on

  • Under Sales channels > Shopify Inbox > Online store chat, chat is off by default. Click Manage settings and turn it on to activate the chat on the storefront. Setup takes about five minutes.
  • In Shopify Admin, Inbox > "Turn on chat" opens the theme customizer, where the chat widget is placed on the storefront. The widget does not appear until chat is turned on.

Verify on the live storefront

To verify Shopify Inbox chat on the live storefront (your shop as visitors see it) after saving the settings:

  1. Open a private (incognito) browser window.
  2. Load the live store URL (your shop's web address).
  3. Check that the chat icon appears in the bottom right corner.
  4. If it is missing, wait a few minutes. Setting changes can take time to show on the store.

To test the full chain (the whole path from visitor to your phone):

  1. Open the storefront, click the chat icon and send a test question.
  2. Enter a name and email address when the form appears, and click "Start chat".
  3. Open Shopify Inbox in the admin and confirm the conversation is listed.
  4. Confirm the message arrives as a notification in the mobile app.
  5. Reply from the admin and check that the reply shows in the mobile app as well.

Common mistakes

  • If chat is not switched on in the Inbox app, no chat icon appears on the storefront, so visitors cannot reach you. After switching it on, always check the live storefront to confirm the icon is visible.
  • The admin bar (a strip at the bottom of the storefront) is visible only to logged-in admin users. Testing while logged in shows a view that visitors do not see. Hide the bar, or test in a logged-out browser window to see the real visitor experience.

Mobile app

  • The Shopify Inbox mobile app (for iOS and Android phones) sends push notifications when customers message. It lets you respond from anywhere by logging in with your Shopify credentials (your login details).
  • Shopify Inbox has a separate mobile app for iPhone (Apple App Store) and Android. It is signed in with the same Shopify username and password used for the admin.

To install the mobile app:

  1. Install the Shopify Inbox app on the phone from the Apple App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
  2. Log in with the Shopify username and password.
  3. Confirm that incoming chats arrive as phone notifications and appear in the app.

The mobile app lets the owner answer messages from a phone. The chat box can be turned off in its settings when the owner is unavailable.

Responses, AI and availability

  • Shopify Inbox responses can include text replies, links, discount codes, images, and emojis.
  • Enable instant answers by turning on "Track my order", "shipping details responses", and "return policy responses". The AI (artificial intelligence, software that writes answers) then pulls answers automatically from your store pages.
  • Shopify Magic is Shopify's AI tool built into Shopify Inbox. It can offer automatic instant answers. Examples are contact-info questions (answered from the contact page) and return-policy questions (answered from the return policy page).
  • Set chat availability to 24/7 so visitors can message at any time. They can still leave messages outside business hours. The automatic first reply is optional, and marketing opt-in (agreeing to receive marketing messages) can remain enabled.

Tidio

1 min

Sources disagree on the preferred tool. Recommendations from 2019 to 2021 favour Tidio. The beginner preference for Shopify Inbox appears in a 2024 source. Tidio has more features and automations but is harder to configure. So use Shopify Inbox for simple setup, and Tidio when you need its extra features.

  • Tidio is a live chat option for smaller stores. The source did not recommend the live chat tool its own company uses, since that tool is built for large volume.
  • Tidio is the live chat software recommended for the store. Live chats and inquiries (questions sent in) are an early sign that visitors are engaging with the site.
  • Tidio is a live chat app for Shopify stores. Avoid the native Facebook Messenger chat integration (Facebook's built-in chat link) on Shopify.
  • Tidio has a free plan. Start with it and upgrade only when needed, without using the chatbot (a robot that answers chats by itself).
  • Tidio's free live chat can run alongside Klaviyo (an email tool) for email. Do not place discount codes in the chatbot greeting, because discounts already appear across the site.
  • Tidio is a live chat app that notifies you of incoming chats on mobile and can capture email addresses to import into Shopify.
  • Tidio can route live chat messages to the owner's phone so chats are answered as they arrive. This allows chat support outside office hours.
  • Tidio Live Chat adds a live chat widget to a Shopify store, so visitors can contact you directly while browsing.
  • ChatGPT (an AI chat tool) can generate automatic replies for a live chat widget such as Tidio. This lets a store answer common visitor questions without a human agent being online.

talk.to and tawk.to

1 min
  • talk.to is a Shopify live chat app that lets visitors ask questions on a product page. Other Shopify live chat apps offer similar functions. Chat messages can reach the store owner's phone as a text or Facebook message, so quick replies from a phone are possible.
  • Talk.to (tawk.to) is a live chat app for Shopify that is low-cost and performs well on both chat functionality and page speed (how fast pages load).
  • The tawk.to dashboard (control screen) shows live visitors, page views, chats (answered and missed), engagement, availability and positive sentiment (how happy visitors seem). It reports for today or the last 7 days. Active chats open in a window in the dashboard for day-to-day handling.
  • tawk.to offers paid add-ons for removing branding, screen sharing, video and audio calls, or hiring agents to answer chats for $1 per hour. These are optional and not needed to launch.

Gorgias chat and helpdesk

3 min

Set up the Gorgias chat widget

Gorgias (a customer support tool) can add a chat widget to your store. Set it up like this:

  1. In Settings, open Channels and click Chat.
  2. Click Add chat.
  3. Enter a chat title.
  4. Select the platform type.
  5. Connect a store.
  6. Choose whether to allow live chat messages or only offline messages (messages left when nobody is online).
  7. Click Create and customize.

Appearance

Settings > Chat > Appearance > Installation. Fields (boxes you fill in):

  • Chat title.
  • Language (English (US)).
  • Intro message for business hours (for example "How can we help?") and outside business hours (for example "We'll be back tomorrow").
  • Colors (main color and conversation color), font, company logo, and widget position on the website.

Labels, automated messages and offline capture subjects can be customized to match the brand's tone of voice (the way your brand sounds in writing).

Preferences

Settings > Chat > Preferences. Choose when live chat is available, for example only when agents (support staff) are online. Outside those hours, the widget shows the built quick responses and lets the visitor leave an email address for later contact. Also configure email capture, an autoresponder (an automatic reply email), and forwarding of chat replies to the customer's email.

Installation on Shopify

  • 1-click installation for Shopify adds the widget to the store in one step. It automatically enables Automation Add-on features (an extra feature pack) if available.
  • Placement options are Show on every page, Show on specific pages, or Hide on specific pages.
  • Manual installation via embed code (a copy-paste code snippet) is used for other platforms or specific pages.
  • A store connection is required for Automation Add-on features in chat and for 1-click installation.
  • A single chat widget is enough for most brands. Separate chats can be created for different purposes if needed.

Campaigns

Gorgias chat campaigns (automatic pop-up messages) prompt a message based on the page URL (the page's web address). They can add conditions such as time spent on the page. The example campaign shows a greeting when a visitor arrives on the site. The campaign name, audience (current URL), conditions and message text are set in the campaign editor. The sender is a random agent's first name.

Offer

With a three-month plan, the second and third month of the Automation Add-on are free. For new Gorgias users, an annual plan gives two extra months of the regular platform free.

Gorgias helpdesk

  • Gorgias is the helpdesk (support inbox) used to find customer tickets (single support requests) and emails. It holds saved macros (ready-made reply texts) such as CHARGEBACK - No Ticket. It can also print or save tickets as PDF evidence (proof saved as a file). Any email platform works if it can do the same.
  • Gorgias connects to Shopify so that the customer profile shows customer type (for example VIP), email, phone number, total spent, and the order list. The order list shows payment status (for example PAID) and fulfillment status (for example FULFILLED, meaning the order was packed and shipped). The Shopify integration allows creating orders from within the helpdesk.
  • Gorgias centralizes all customer conversations and customer data in one platform. Integrations (connections) with e-commerce apps keep workflows connected, so support and sales run from a single tool.
  • The Gorgias AI agent (an AI that answers customers) gives instant, personalized replies across the whole customer journey (every step from first visit to after purchase). It chooses the right channel for each shopper at the right moment. It is used for both pre-purchase questions and post-purchase support.
  • The Satisfaction view under Statistics shows a "Latest Surveys" table with the columns Customer, Assignee, Date, Score and Comment. Each row has a "Go to ticket" link. Use the link to open the original conversation behind a low score.

Shared inboxes and helpdesks

1 min
  • Reamaze is a customer communication platform that centralizes all messages in one inbox. It houses an FAQ library (a list of common questions and answers), stores response templates (ready-made replies), provides live chat, and integrates abandoned cart recovery (follow-up messages to people who left items in their cart).
  • Reamaze is customer service software that handles emails, chats and social media messages in one list. It shows who is working on each case. It should be adopted once the team has one to three people, since a shared inbox in Gmail becomes inefficient and loses track of who sent what.
  • Reamaze (spelled "Remaze" in the source) is a help desk inbox connected to Shopify. It gathers email, social media messages and online chat in one place. Agents (support staff) can see order status, change or cancel orders from the inbox, add internal notes (private notes only the team sees) and set follow-up reminders. It also stores reply templates that insert fields such as the customer name or tracking number automatically.
  • Zendesk (a customer support software) is used for internal customer communication. Zendesk Chat is used on many of the stores. It is described as overpriced and outdated for most sellers.
  • Zendesk is used as the customer email interface (the screen where support emails are handled). Customer replies and outgoing support emails can land in spam, so check the spam folder for missing replies. (2024.)
  • Zendesk charges per seat (per user login), and a 20-person team made it expensive. Switching to Help Scout (another support software) cut the monthly support software cost by about $1,000. (2025.)

Answering support requests and saved macros

1 min
  • Shipment progress can be shown to customers through a multi-carrier tracking page (one page that tracks parcels from many shipping companies), such as 17track. The tracking number is entered in the link. Agents attach a screenshot of the current status to the reply, so the customer sees the same information.
  • Reply templates in Reamaze insert fields such as the customer name or tracking number automatically.
  • Gorgias holds saved macros such as CHARGEBACK - No Ticket.

Chargeback documentation

1 min

A chargeback is when a customer's bank takes money back from the store.

  • In Gorgias, find the customer's tickets and emails, apply the saved macro CHARGEBACK - No Ticket where it fits, and print or save the tickets as PDF evidence. Any email platform works if it can do the same.

Call tracking and phone support

1 min
  • Mighty Call (mightycall.com) provides call tracking (a record of calls) and chat monitoring (watching chats). It shows call logs (a list of calls), how many calls were answered and how many chats came in. This lets the owner check a phone or chat team's performance.
  • MightyCall records phone calls and tracks how many calls are answered. This helps verify that a virtual assistant (a remote helper) doing customer service actually answers calls.

Offline and after-hours messages

1 min
  • Set up your online chat tool to take a message when no agent is available, and forward it to your email. The forwarded message should include the visitor's question, their email address and any phone number they gave.
  • Gorgias shows the built quick responses outside chat hours. It lets the visitor leave an email address for later contact.
  • Gorgias intro messages can differ for business hours and outside business hours.
  • Shopify Inbox chat availability can be set to 24/7. Visitors can still leave messages outside business hours.
  • Turn off the Shopify Inbox chat box in its settings when the owner is unavailable.

CRM and when one is warranted

2 min

A CRM (customer relationship management, software that keeps track of your customers) is only needed at some point. Here is when.

Current practice for small stores

  • Keep customer data in Shopify and Klaviyo rather than a separate enterprise CRM (a large business tool) such as Salesforce. Salesforce is not designed for drop-ship stores of this type.
  • A small store can manage customer profiles with Shopify's built-in customer records. It can send all customer email from a business Gmail account without a separate CRM.

Named CRM options

  • Copper is a CRM that integrates with Gmail. It suits a small team that wants a light, easy system. It is one option among several for organizing customer contacts.
  • Gorgeous is a customer-journey tool built for Shopify that offers more than the default Shopify features. It suits larger stores better than new ones, and users' opinions of it vary widely.
  • ActiveCampaign is a light CRM with strong email automation at a reasonable price, and some CRM functions. At the time it was written, it lacked the direct Shopify integration (a direct link to Shopify) that tools such as Klaviyo and Drip offered.
  • Trello boards (online to-do boards) are a simple way to organize customer follow-ups and tasks in a small business. They are useful as a stopgap (a temporary fix) before a full CRM is justified.

When a CRM is warranted

  • A CRM is only needed when the Shopify-plus-Gmail setup stops covering the workflow (the steps of your daily work).
  • Use Trello as a stopgap before a full CRM is justified.
  • Introducing a complex CRM before you have consistent sales adds work without a return. Good CRM tools are hard to set up and need data to be useful. Start with simpler tracking until sales are steady.
  • High-ticket stores (stores selling expensive items) have far fewer orders than typical e-commerce stores, so their customer records grow slowly. A simple tracking system is often enough for a long time, even at high revenue.
