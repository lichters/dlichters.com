Product options, personalization and digital products

Section 5.9 · 9 of 22 in this chapter · 13 min read

In short

  • Shopify's built-in options (choices like size or color) allow only three option types per product. For more, install an app.
  • Bold Product Options Premium costs about $20 a month and has a free trial. Its cheaper version cannot do step-by-step cross-sell.
  • For free text boxes, such as a name on a product, use Global Product Options. It is free.
  • Digital files such as PDFs need a delivery app or a download link. Without one, buyers may never get the file.
This section has 7 parts. Tap one to open it.

Choosing an options app

1 min
  • Shopify has a built-in option system. Options are choices a buyer makes, such as size or color. It allows three option types per product.
  • If a product needs variations that Shopify cannot handle itself, install an app. An app is an add-on program for your store.
  • ** Bold Product Options is the default for any product with variations that Shopify cannot handle itself.
  • Shopify allows up to 100 variants per product. A variant is one version of a product, such as one size and color combination.
  • For more than 100 variants, or for clickable color swatches (small color circles a buyer taps), use an app such as Infinite Product Options or SC Product Options.
  • ** Having many product options is not a problem by itself.

Bold Product Options

2 min

  • Plan 30-60 minutes per week for analysis (checking the numbers) and optimization (improving what works).
  • Bold Product Options adds checkboxes and drop-down menus (lists that open on click) to product pages. Older (2021).
  • Use it to offer upgrades, such as White Glove delivery (a premium service where staff set the item up), on specific products only. Older (2021).
  • Bold Product Options is a Shopify app for creating customization options, such as sizes, colors and add-ons like assembly services.
  • Bold Product Options can be combined with Bold Product Upsell.
  • Bold Product Options is a Shopify app for managing many product options in a store.
  • Bold Product Options is a Shopify app for setting up product options simply, such as sizes, colors or add-on choices.
  • Bold Product Options is a Shopify app for adding custom product options, such as variants or personalization fields, to product pages. Older (2021).
  • Bold Product Options is a Shopify app for product add-ons and options. Add-ons are extra choices a customer can add to a product page. Older (2021).
  • Bold Product Options is used to display add-ons and product options on the product page. Older (2020).

Global Product Options and personalization fields

2 min

Setup procedure A (free app, product scope set per product): Product scope means which products the option applies to.

  1. Install the app from the Shopify App Store (Shopify's shop for apps). Scroll down, click Install app, then click Continue as free.
  2. Open the product you want to personalize, and click Add element (adds a new box to the product).
  3. Choose Text field as the element type (the kind of box). Set the field label (the title above the box). Example: type in your names.
  4. Open advanced options (extra settings) and add a placeholder (grey example text inside the box) or help text (a small note below the box). Show the expected input, for example a list of sample names.
  5. The help text appears below the field.
  6. Click Next.
  7. Choose which products get the option. Pick All if the store sells only one product.
  8. Open the theme editor (the screen where you change how the store looks). Activate the app embed (a switch that turns the app on in the store design), then click save.
  9. Check the product page to confirm the text field appears.

Setup procedure B (option sets, from a second version of the instructions): Option sets are saved groups of options you can reuse.

  1. Install Global Product Options on the Shopify store.
  2. Open Option sets and create a new option set.
  3. Add a text box with a title, for example Name of boyfriend or girlfriend.
  4. Click Show advanced options.
  5. Add either a text placeholder (shown inside the box) or a help text (shown below the box) to explain what to type.
  6. Optionally add an image upload option.
  7. When an order comes in, forward the customer's text to the supplier (the company that makes and ships the item) exactly as typed.

Notes on the two versions:

  • Both procedures set up the same app. Follow the menu names your app version shows.
  • Example for a single-product store: label "Type in your names", with sample names such as Daniel, Sarah, David, Michelle as the help text, so customers see how it works.
  • For that example, set the product scope to All.
  • Then activate the app embed in the theme editor and save.
  • Global Product Options adds custom input fields, such as text fields, to product pages.
  • Customers can enter personalization details, like names, before they buy.
  • Global Product Options is a free Shopify app for personalization, such as a name field on jewelry.

Other personalization tools:

  • Global Variants is a Shopify app that allows product customization, such as choosing a mug handle, color or text, on a product page.
  • Global Variants is used for personalized products rather than standard single-variant listings.
  • A free Shopify text-box or photo-upload customization app lets buyers enter a name, number, message or photo on a personalized product.
  • The seller then forwards the order details to the supplier. The source does not name the app.
  • Bold Product Options adds text fields to product pages so customers can enter engraving or personalization text. Older (2021).
  • The order arrives with that information. The supplier engraves and ships the item, so you do not stock it. Older (2021).

Add-ons, upsells and cross-sells

2 min

An upsell offers a bigger or extra version of a product. A cross-sell offers a related product. AOV (average order value) is the average amount spent per order.

Setting up the offer:

  • Use the Shopify apps Product Accessories and Product Options by Bold Apps to add accessories and product options to product pages.
  • These apps help raise AOV without changing the core product listing.
  • The Shopify apps Product Accessories and Product Options by Bold Apps work well for offering add-ons and product options.
  • Bold Product Options is the app used for cross-sell conditional logic on product pages.
  • Conditional logic is a rule, such as "if X, then show Y." Example: if the customer is on Product A, show Product B. If they add B, show Product C.
  • Conditional logic is what makes the step-by-step cross-sell work.
  • Do not use the cheaper version, which lacks conditional logic.
  • The premium version's 14-day free trial is worth using to test the setup before paying.

Upsell types:

  • Add-ons such as warranties, maintenance plans or accessories can be priced right on the product page. Older (2022).
  • Bold Product Options is a Shopify app that lets you add extras to a product page as a paid add-on.

Supplier-fulfilled and bundled items:

  • Fulfillment means sending the item to the buyer. Here, the supplier ships it.
  • Bold Product Options adds bundled items or variant options to a product. The extra item ships automatically with the main order.
  • It is preferred over manual fulfillment when order volume grows. Older (2019).

Warranties via Bold Upsell (procedure): A warranty is a promise to repair or replace a product.

  1. Create a product named extended warranty.
  2. Link it to the suppliers who provide the warranty.
  3. Add it as an upsell at checkout (the page where the buyer pays). The setup takes about 5 minutes.

Extended warranties with Extend:

  • Extend.com is a Shopify app for selling extended warranties on high-ticket products (expensive items).
  • You earn a commission (a share of each sale) on warranty sales.
  • The app provider handles warranty fulfillment. Multiple Source A members recommended it.

Special cases:

  • Bold Product Options can offer White Gloves delivery on specific products only. Older (2021).
  • Bold Product Options can show shipping options and fixed shipping charges on product pages. Older (2020).

Mistake to avoid:

  • Product option apps such as Bold may become incompatible with split-payment apps. Split-payment apps let customers pay with two cards.
  • Ask both app providers for a compatible integration. Do not assume one will work with the other.

Creating ebooks and digital bonuses

2 min

An ebook is a digital book. A bonus is a free extra given with a purchase.

Tools for writing and layout:

  • Claude (claude.ai, an AI writing tool) can generate a complete, professional ebook in minutes from a single prompt. The store owner does not need to write the ebook manually.
  • Canva (an online design tool) lays out the AI-generated ebook text with a simple ebook template.
  • Canva exports the finished file as a PDF (a standard document file) for upload to Shopify.
  • Canva is used to design ebook covers and the layout of the PDF product.
  • ChatGPT (an AI chat tool) generates ideas, lists and text for ebooks and offers.
  • Give ChatGPT concrete length targets, such as about 1000 words, to control output length.

Prompt for a dog-recipe ebook (use as given, fill in the placeholders):

Create a list of 20 recipes for dogs that support [PROBLEM, e.g. gut health, skin, dental health or joint problems] using everyday ingredients that are easy to buy in a regular supermarket in [COUNTRY]. For each recipe, give a title, an ingredient list with amounts, three to five short preparation steps, and one sentence explaining why the ingredients suit this problem. Write in [LANGUAGE] and keep each recipe under 100 words, with a total length of about 1000 words for the whole list. Do not make medical claims or promise cures, and add a note that owners should check any recipe with a veterinarian.

Sourcing ready-made content:

  • PLR (private label rights) sites are marketplaces that sell ready-made ebooks you may rebrand (change the look and name to your own).
  • PLR ebooks cost about 2 dollars 70 cents each.
  • You can rebrand them with your own logo and sell them or give them away as bonuses.
  • Free ebook websites provide educational ebooks that can be rebranded and offered as a bonus to customers.
  • Fiverr (a site for hiring freelancers) can be used to hire someone to rebrand a free ebook to match the store, for a low cost.

Delivering digital products

3 min

A digital product has no shipping. It is delivered as a file. A physical product is shipped in a box.

Choosing a delivery app:

  • Digital Downloads is a free Shopify app made by Shopify.
  • Digital Downloads delivers an uploaded file to the customer by email immediately after purchase.
  • It can be used to sell a digital product alongside a bundle.
  • Install it by searching for it in the Shopify App Store.
  • Digital Downloads automatically delivers a PDF or digital product after purchase, so it does not have to be emailed manually.
  • Digital Downloads works for a free ebook offered as a bonus with a physical product.
  • Install Digital Downloads only if the store sells digital files. The example store installs it for its digital sales.
  • Digital Downloads has been used to sell and deliver digital files such as audiobooks, artwork and videos.
  • EDP (Easy Digital Products) appeared as an alternative to Digital Downloads in the Shopify App Store search. It has a free plan.
  • If the free delivery workflow needs more options, compare EDP's features and reviews with Digital Downloads.

Setup procedure (Digital Downloads):

  1. Install the app from the Shopify App Store by searching for "digital downloads."
  2. Open the e-book product in Shopify. Create it first if it doesn't exist yet.
  3. Inside the app, open the product, choose Add asset (a file attached to the product), then File.
  4. Upload the PDF.
  5. In the product's Shipping section, switch off "Physical product" so it reads "Not a physical product."
  6. Click Save.
  7. Configure the notification email (the automatic email sent when a digital product is ready for download), plus the email used for updates.
  8. Set how digital products are accessed and downloaded after checkout.
  9. Add the e-book product as the free gift in the Kaching Bundles bar (the bundle offer box in the app).

Settings and plan requirement:

  • The Digital Downloads settings let you write the notification email for a ready digital product.
  • They also let you write the email sent when a purchased digital product has been updated.
  • They let you choose how digital products are accessed and downloaded after checkout.
  • Adjusting the checkout is only possible on a Shopify plan (a paid Shopify subscription level).
  • If the app shows "To customize your checkout, you need to select a Shopify plan," select a Shopify plan first.

Link products:

  • Digital Downloads can create a link product. A link product sells access to externally hosted content, sold alongside the files attached to a product.
  • Use this when the gift is an online course or membership area rather than a downloadable PDF.

Bonuses without a delivery app:

  • Use Google Drive links to PDFs, unlisted YouTube videos (hidden from search, opened only by people with the link), or unlisted pages to deliver free digital bonuses without an app.
  • Paid digital products with automated delivery need a Shopify app built for that purpose.

Delivering a digital bonus after purchase with Klaviyo (an email tool) (procedure):

  1. Create a flow (an automatic email sequence) in Klaviyo, triggered by the purchase of the product.
  2. Add an email containing the access link.
  3. Use a Google Drive link to PDFs, an unlisted YouTube video, or an unlisted page as the link.
  4. Test the flow before going live.

Online classes:

  • ** For a weekly class sold online, create a Shopify digital product with a price.
  • ** Then edit the order confirmation email (the email sent after purchase) with access instructions.
  • ** For a private online class, a private Facebook group with video streaming is an option.

Free gifts and subscriptions (Kaching):

  • Kaching Bundles is a Shopify app that can deliver a free digital gift, such as an ebook, bundled with a product.
  • Upload the finished file to the app to use it as a product bonus.
  • Kaching Subscriptions adds subscription purchases (regular, repeated orders).
  • It works well with products like supplements. For a book store it is not needed at the foundation stage.

Fallbacks and risks:

  • A digital product such as a PDF bundle may not reach customers if no delivery app or order confirmation link is set up.
  • Check whether delivery is automated before selling it. Check support emails for complaints.
  • Alternative 1: place a download link to the PDF in the order confirmation. The buyer can click and download it immediately.
  • Alternative 2: until automation is in place, send each buyer a separate email with the PDF manually.
