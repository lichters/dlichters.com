Advertising platforms, tracking and attribution

Section 5.17 · 17 of 22 in this chapter · 23 min read

In short

  • Run ads from an agency account, not a personal one. A banned personal account is gone for good, and an agency handles support and replacement accounts.
  • Use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) for tracking. The old Universal Analytics is out of use.
  • Link Google Analytics only through the Google and YouTube app. Remove any Google Ads or Merchant Center connection there.
  • Check your numbers before you scale. Compare cheaper tools first, because Triple Whale costs $300 per month.
  • Give each partner or banner link its own tag, so you can see its visitors and sales in Google Analytics.
More key points
  • Let AI tools gather data and make files. Ask a human expert to read the results.
  • Facebook's built-in automated rules are rarely worth the effort. Test any paid tool against its monthly cost.
  • One test found a click fraud app saved no ad money. Compare its savings to its fee in the first month.

Ad Accounts, Ownership and Risk

1 min
  • An ad account is the account that pays for and runs your ads.
  • A personal ad account (opened in your own name) has no dedicated support.
  • If a personal ad account is banned, it is gone.
  • For scaling (raising your ad spend), choose an agency account.
  • The agency handles support and replacement accounts.
  • The Kuro Network client portal (the provider's website where you manage your account) menu lists: Ad Accounts, Request Top-Up, Funds Transfer, Request BM Transfer, Request Unban, Request BM Verification and Support Chat.
  • Meta Business Manager (business.facebook.com) is free.
  • Business Manager holds your Facebook page, Instagram page, pixel and audiences.
  • A pixel is a small tracking code on your store.
  • Audiences are groups of people you target with ads.
  • Create audiences under the menu Audiences.
  • Before a holiday promotion starts, you need a Meta Ads account and a Google Ads account.
  • Both accounts need remarketing audiences built. Remarketing means showing ads again to people who already visited your store.
  • to link Instagram to a Facebook Business account without mixing personal profiles, log in to Facebook Business Manager > Settings > Assets > Add Instagram Account.
  • Select only your business Instagram account.
  • Microsoft Advertising is Bing's paid ad platform.
  • Microsoft Advertising needs its own account.
  • Bing Webmaster Tools is a free Bing tool.
  • Bing Webmaster Tools only reports organic search performance, meaning unpaid search results.
  • Bing offered a start credit (free ad money for new advertisers) of about 75 to 100 USD.
  • Search for the Bing credit offer and sign up through the Microsoft link to claim it.

Google Ads Tools and Setup

1 min
  • Google Ads management tools include Keyword Planner for keyword research (finding the words people search for).
  • Google Ads management tools include Google Ads Settings for setting up campaigns.
  • Google management tools include Google Shopping for product ads that show pictures and prices.
  • Google management tools include AdWords Express, a simplified way to create ads.
  • The native Google Ads dashboard (the built-in reporting screen) is free. It needs no add-on.
  • Supermetrics is an add-on tool. Treat it as paid, and check current pricing before you budget for it.
  • Start with the native dashboard. Add Supermetrics only when you need one combined sheet across several platforms.
  • To create a native Google Ads dashboard:
    1. Open the native Dashboards feature in Google Ads and create a new dashboard. It starts blank.
    2. Add a widget, for example a chart of conversions by device.
    3. Resize the widget as needed.
    4. Add more widgets, such as conversion rate by device, geographic performance by state or a campaign view.
    5. Click Save.
    6. Return to the saved dashboard at any time. Check metrics and bid adjustments there, without going through campaigns.
  • Google Shopping competitor check: open the Google Analytics Content report in the left sidebar. Google Analytics is Google's free website statistics tool.
  • The Content report shows the store pages with the most traffic (visits).
  • Search those top product pages in Google Shopping to see what competitors charge.
  • Microsoft Advertising (Bing) can import Google Ads campaigns. Importing means copying them over.
  • Bing does not group multiple sellers of the same product in one listing.
  • So the price comparison that Google shows does not happen on Bing.

Google Analytics, GA4 and Tracking Foundations

4 min
  • Google Analytics is a tracking tool. It shows which pages visitors view.
  • It shows which pages visitors leave from.
  • It shows how long visitors stay on the website.
  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is the current version of Google Analytics.
  • GA4 tracks website traffic, conversions (completed goals such as sales), and user behavior.
  • GA4 can create remarketing audiences.
  • GA4 replaced Universal Analytics.
  • Universal Analytics (the old version) has been phased out in many accounts. (2023)
  • Stores should use GA4. (2023)
  • Migration from Universal Analytics to GA4 has its own guided step in the analytics setup. Update your analytics setups to GA4. (2023)
  • Shopify needs GA4 connected through the Google channel (Shopify's link to Google services).
  • Google removes the old Universal Analytics connection on July 1.
  • Do not rely on the old Universal Analytics entry for tracking.
  • Conversion Pixel App is a Shopify app.
  • It manages the conversion tracking pixel. A conversion is a completed sale or goal.
  • The pixel links customer purchases to Google Ads campaigns. This is called attribution: matching a sale to the ad that caused it.
  • Google and YouTube app is a free Shopify app. It connects Google Analytics to your store.
  • The free plan is enough for this purpose.
  • The app is now owned by Google LLC.
  • To link Google Analytics to a Shopify store:
    1. Enter the store URL in the Google Analytics setup. If Google recognizes a Shopify store, it offers Install with Shopify. Click Next to see the instructions.
    2. Search the Shopify App Store for the Google and YouTube app, and choose the free plan.
    3. Click Install.
    4. After installation, the app appears under Sales Channels in the Shopify admin.
    5. In Google Analytics, copy the Tag ID of the data stream. A data stream is the flow of visitor data from your store into GA4. The Tag ID is the code that identifies it.
    6. In the Google and YouTube app, follow option one, the Google Analytics connection that uses the guided Shopify video. Do not use option two, because it is more complex.
    7. Paste the Tag ID where the setup asks for it.
    8. Open the app's Settings and confirm that only one Google Analytics property is connected.
  • Connect neither Google Ads nor Google Merchant Center through the Google and YouTube app.
  • That app is meant solely for linking Google Analytics.
  • If Google Ads or Merchant Center appears as connected under that app's Settings, remove the connection.
  • The person who links Google Analytics through the Google and YouTube app needs administrator rights (full access) on that Google Analytics account.
  • Without administrator rights, the guided connection cannot be completed.
  • The Google channel setup offers to turn on Google emails and to manage Merchant Center from there. Merchant Center is Google's tool that holds your product list.
  • Do not activate these during the Google Analytics connection.
  • Set up Merchant Center in its own later step.
  • Use the feed app's integration for the Google tracking tag. A feed app sends your product list to Google and other channels. The Google tracking tag is a small code that sends visitor data to Google. (2023)
  • If tracking fails after moving from Universal Analytics to GA4, contact that app's support through its help button, with a screenshot. (2023)
  • Keep GA4's default user engagement event settings when integrating directly with Shopify. (2023)
  • Changing these settings could affect Shopify analytics. (2023)
  • Default user engagement events count a visitor after about 10 seconds on the site. (2023)
  • The Google Analytics real-time view shows how many people are on the site at a given moment.
  • Open the real-time view during a large influencer post to see the traffic it generates.
  • Check traffic platform by platform in Google Analytics. For example, look at Pinterest traffic under Traffic sources (the report of where visitors come from). (2022)
  • Check whether traffic and sales come from that platform before judging its cost per click (what you pay each time someone clicks your ad). (2022)
  • The Facebook Pixel (Meta's tracking code) should be installed on the store before running paid traffic. Then analytics data builds up in advance.
  • Business Manager at business.facebook.com is free to set up. It is where the pixel and analytics are managed.
  • Meta Pixel Helper is a browser extension (a small add-on for your web browser).
  • Use it to check whether the Meta pixel is installed and firing correctly on a store's pages.
  • Facebook Pixel Helper is a free Chrome extension.
  • It stays in the top-right area of the browser.
  • It shows which Facebook Pixel events fire on any website you open. This lets you examine competitor tracking.
  • Before enabling Microsoft UET tracking (Microsoft's Universal Event Tracking code) through the feed app, check the Shopify store for existing conversion tracking or UET scripts.
  • If you are unsure about an existing script or tag, contact the app's support first.
  • The support link is at the bottom of the setup page, under "Get support".

Meta Ads: Creation, Bulk Building and Testing

1 min

  • It integrates with Meta Ads Manager (Meta's native ad screen).
  • It offers AI-assisted creative generation (AI helps make ad visuals).

  • The platform saves 20-40 hours per month on media management tasks.
  • Pricing: a 40% discount on the first month is available through partnership.
  • The Essential plan is recommended for starting.
  • The In-House Plus plan offers advanced automation, including cross-platform scaling.

    2. Click the blue launch button and select "Select media to use".

    6. Select the launch status: Active (starts spending immediately) or Paused (delays the launch).

Break-Even and Pre-Launch Checks

1 min
  • Dropship Toolkit is a website with a break-even ROAS calculator for dropshipping stores.
  • ROAS means return on ad spend: sales divided by ad spend. Break-even ROAS is the point where you neither make nor lose money.
  • Enter the selling price, the cost of goods (what you pay the supplier) and the fulfillment cost (packing and shipping).
  • The result is the break-even ROAS. Use it before setting scaling thresholds (the numbers at which you raise spend).
  • Use it before running ads to check whether a product can be profitable at a given price.

UTM Tracking for Partners and Banners

1 min

A UTM is a tag added to the end of a link. It tells Google Analytics (a free Google tool that counts visitors and sales) where a visitor came from.

To add a partner or banner link with UTM tracking:

  1. Choose the destination URL (the web address the link opens).
  2. Open a UTM builder (a tool that makes tagged links).
  3. Set utm_source to banner ad, utm_medium to banner, and utm_campaign to the partner URL. utm_source is where the visitor came from. utm_medium is the type of channel. utm_campaign is the campaign name.
  4. Leave the campaign ID empty. The campaign ID is a code number for the campaign. You do not need it here.
  5. Copy the generated link (the finished tagged link) and send it to the partner.
  6. Check the traffic (number of visitors), country and sales of that source in Google Analytics.

Reporting and AI Reporting Limits

3 min
  • Supermetrics is a Google Sheets add-on. An add-on is an extra tool that plugs into Google Sheets (a free online spreadsheet).
  • Supermetrics connects to ad accounts (the accounts where your ads run) such as Google Ads, Bing Ads and Facebook Ads. It pulls their data into a sheet.
  • The data lands in a regular spreadsheet. So you can use Google Sheets formulas (ready-made calculations) and conditional formatting (rules that color cells automatically) on it directly.
  • Manus is an AI agent (an AI tool that carries out tasks on its own).
  • Manus can connect to a Meta ad account (the ad account for Facebook and Instagram ads) through Meta marketing tools.
  • Manus reads the ad-level names (the name of each single ad) and builds a report.
  • Manus uses Python code (a programming language) to process the exported data (data saved from the ad account) and produce the final file.
  • Manus is an AI agent that can connect to a Meta ad account.
  • Before pulling data, Manus can load a Meta Ads analysis skill (a ready-made set of instructions for the AI).
  • When this was written, Meta (Facebook) had just acquired Manus. Its plans and features may change.

Setting up an AI creative-count report in Manus (a creative is one ad design):

  1. Go to Manus and connect the Meta ad account.
  2. Send a prompt (a written instruction to the AI) that names the ad account, describes the report and attaches an example report.
  3. State what to leave out: iterations (small changes to an existing ad) and new concepts (brand-new ad ideas).
  4. State which field to group by: product type, taken from the ad naming convention (the fixed way your ads are named).
  5. Review the delivered report, which can be produced as a spreadsheet.

Prompt for the creative-count report (use as written, replacing the bracketed placeholders):

I need you to look at the ad account [AD ACCOUNT LINK]. I need a report like the attached example that tracks the total number of creatives launched per month per product. Now note that we do not track iterations or new concepts. What I need is the report by product type, which is part of our naming convention at the ad level (for example: Mixed, Oodie, Weighted Blanket, [OTHER PRODUCT TYPES]). Count each ad once in the month it launched, show the monthly totals per product type, and deliver the final report as a spreadsheet.
  • When you ask an AI agent for an ad account report, attach a screenshot or recording of an example report.
  • Or describe the output in exact words. In your words, state what must be counted.
  • In your words, also state what must be excluded (for example iterations or new concepts).
  • In your words, also state which naming field to group by.
  • Conflict note (the sources disagree): Manus can connect to a Meta account and build reports from it.
  • Current AI reporting tools such as Manus are not yet reliable for interpreting ad account results.
  • Use the AI for data gathering and file production.
  • Use human analysis from mentors, group calls or one-on-one coaches to interpret the data.
  • In Excel (Microsoft's spreadsheet program), the status bar (the thin bar at the bottom of the window) shows Average, Count and Sum for a selected range of cells (a group of selected cells).

Click Fraud Protection

1 min

Click fraud means fake clicks on your ads that you still pay for.

  • a separate click fraud app is not necessary.
  • Instead, monitor all ad campaigns.
  • Then exclude Google Display Network sites (websites where Google shows banner ads) that bring in little value or show poor results. Exclude means block your ads from showing there.
  • one operator (one seller) tested a click-fraud protection app for one month.
  • The app did not save any ad money, so it was not recommended.
  • Compare any savings against the monthly fee within the first month before keeping such a tool.
