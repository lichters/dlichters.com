Team workspace, project management and hiring tools

Section 5.3 · 3 of 22 in this chapter · 22 min read

In short

  • Keep all company files in one shared online folder (Google Drive) that you and your assistant can open from anywhere.
  • Before you edit any shared Google file, make your own copy of it.
  • Use one team chat app for internal talk, and keep customer messages in a separate help desk inbox.
  • Write each daily routine as a step-by-step guide (an SOP), so an assistant can follow it without asking you.
  • Give every task card or order a due date, so nothing is forgotten.
More key points
  • Give your helper only the access they need. Never give full admin access.
  • Back up your store with Rewinds before you let any outside worker in.
  • Hubstaff checks work time. Normal work is about 70% mouse, 30% keyboard. 100% on both with no output is a warning sign.
  • A VPN (a tool that changes the country your internet looks like it comes from) helps with research. It can also trigger account security checks.
  • Test any VPN setup with a small budget before you rely on it.

Google Workspace as the shared base

1 min
  • Google Drive (online file storage you can reach from any device) is the central store for company files. These include SOPs (step-by-step written guides), FAQs (lists of common questions with answers), response templates (ready-made replies), passwords and contact information. The whole team can reach it from anywhere.
  • Keep all company files in one organised, shared Drive folder. The owner and any assistant can open it, so the business can be run from anywhere.
  • Google Drive is used for most business tasks and file organisation.
  • Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Hangouts and Gmail together form a cloud business system. "Cloud" means tools that run online instead of on one computer.
  • A cloud folder system such as Dropbox (another online file storage service) can be used alongside Google Drive.
  • Google Sheets is a free, cloud-based spreadsheet (a table of rows and columns) comparable to Microsoft Excel. It needs only a free Google account and is reached at drive.google.com.
  • Google Sheets is stored in Google Drive and used to store and edit business data and to track orders and other records. Automations (apps that do a step on their own when something happens) can update these records.
  • Google Docs (online word processing), Google Drive and Basecamp (a project tool) can hold a project document and SOPs for each supplier, and can be shared with an assistant.
  • Google Voice provides free phone service, and a US phone number can be set up through it.
  • Cloud tools for a location-independent business (one you can run from anywhere) are Google Docs for documents, Dropbox for files, Slack (a team chat app) for team communication and Skype (a calling app) for calls, plus a US phone number through Google Voice.

Chrome profile as the business workspace

1 min
  • A dedicated Google Chrome profile (a separate browser setup with its own logins and saved links) can act as a shared business workspace. It holds bookmarks (saved links) to dashboards (summary screens), spreadsheets, supplier price lists, contracts and account logins, organised by category.
  • Each business or project can have its own browser window, and a new hire can be given the login to see the same setup.
  • Setup procedure:
    1. Create a Google Chrome profile for the business as early as possible.
    2. Bookmark every tool, dashboard and document used during daily work, such as the Shopify dashboard (the control panel of Shopify, the platform that runs an online store) and Slack.
    3. Start using a help desk tool like Remaze (one inbox for all customer messages) and a task board like Asana (an online to-do board), with Trello (a similar board tool) as a possible alternative to Asana.
    4. Once the processes run efficiently, document each one as an SOP.
    5. Write FAQs from past customer emails and from the manufacturer's website (the company that makes the product), rewritten in your own words rather than copied.
    6. Create on-brand response templates (replies that match your brand's voice) for the VA's email replies to reduce mistakes. A VA is a virtual assistant, a remote helper.
    7. Keep one spreadsheet with all account passwords and one with supplier and service contacts, so the VA can answer questions without the owner being the contact point.
    8. When these are in place, the system is ready for hiring.

Shared templates, copies and checklists

1 min
  • Any shared Google document, whether Docs or Sheets, should be copied into your own Drive before you edit it. The shared original cannot be edited by you, and anyone with access to a shared version can see what you enter.
  • Procedure to work in a shared Google Sheets tracker:
    1. Open the shared Google Sheets link.
    2. Click File in the top menu.
    3. Choose Make a copy.
    4. Save the copy in your own Google Drive and work only in that copy.
  • If copying a Google file feels too complex, use the Excel version of the tracker (Microsoft's spreadsheet file) on your desktop instead. Choose whichever format fits your working style.
  • The research tracker exists as a Google Sheets version and a Microsoft Excel version. The Excel file can be downloaded and used on the desktop, on Windows or Mac.
  • Keep your own copy of any shared checklist spreadsheet, either by copying it to your own drive or downloading it as Excel (preferred) or PDF (a fixed-layout document file). Edits then do not affect other users.
  • To reuse a shared Google Doc template (a ready-made document you fill in), choose File > Make a copy. In the Make a copy dialog (the pop-up box) you can keep the same sharing ("Share it with the same people"), copy comments and suggestions, and include resolved comments and suggestions before pressing Make a copy. Edit only your copy, not the shared original.

Team communication

2 min
  • Slack is the internal communication platform for instant team notifications and task assignments. It also sends abandoned cart alerts (messages when a shopper leaves items in the cart without paying) and organises group messages.
  • Slack is used for daily team communication, with separate channels (topic rooms) for work and a casual channel for team chat. Teams split across time zones use it as a shared office.
  • Slack is a team messaging app that can be used free of charge even without employees. It works as a personal notes space and receives instant notifications from automations, so the owner does not need to check email for every event.
  • Slack is a team messaging tool with channels, direct messages and group chats. It is used for internal team communication, including reporting outages (times when the store or a service stops working) and status updates.
  • Slack is used for daily VA communication instead of email, because it allows near-instant replies and constant availability. It keeps VAs engaged and makes it easy to check progress during the day.
  • Slack is a team chat tool for communicating with remote team members and virtual assistants.
  • Slack is used only for internal communication, one-on-one and in group channels, and keeps a searchable message history. Outside communication with customers stays in the help desk. Slack is preferred over Asana messaging, which was slow and gave poor notifications, and over Skype or Facebook Messenger for team chat.
  • Keep the channels separate: customer messages go through the help desk, order and task management runs in Asana, and internal team talk runs in Slack. This makes it clear where each kind of work lives.
  • Use WhatsApp (a messaging app on phones) as the main direct channel for quick questions from the team. Slack and Discord (a community chat app with topic rooms) are alternatives.
  • Source positions differ on the quick-question channel: one source uses WhatsApp for quick team questions, while another uses Slack for all internal talk with a searchable history. WhatsApp fits fast one-line questions; Slack fits internal work that must stay searchable and separate from customer messages.
  • Google Hangouts (Google's chat and video tool) can be used to chat with a candidate, and for team chat that appears inside Gmail (Google's email). It was also used for face-to-face (in person or on video) team meetings.
  • Skype calls that the assistant can listen to let the assistant learn the owner's tone when speaking with customers.

Discord

1 min
  • Discord is the community platform because other platforms were full of distractions and could not guarantee that members would see each other's posts. The space is dedicated to one community, where members ask questions, answer them and share wins.
  • Discord works like an instant messaging service. The server (the whole Discord space) is split into sections and channels in the sidebar, each for a different topic or program stage.
  • Stage channels: questions about the first stage (named Zero) go in its chat thread, and questions about niche selection (choosing a narrow group of products or buyers) go in the channel named 'One Niche Selection'. Search previous posts first, since the answer may already exist.
  • The 'Social' channel, at the bottom of the channel list, is a free chat where members connect with each other in real time (live, at the same time) on any topic.
  • Private or personal questions go by email to the source's own site Questions that would help the whole community go to the matching section of the Discord, where the coach or other members answer them.
  • Discord can be used in a web browser, in a mobile browser, or through the Discord mobile app, which keeps the community accessible on the phone at all times.
  • Procedure to join the members-only Discord community (a group only members can enter):
    1. Open the join link provided below the page content, which opens Discord.
    2. Enter the same email address you use to log into the program's member area.
    3. Submit it so the membership can be verified and access granted.
  • A resources channel in the program Discord holds tools, supplier lists, ad creative examples (sample ad images and videos) and research templates. A Meta ads guide (how to run ads on Facebook and Instagram, both owned by Meta) built for the Source D system sits alongside it.

Facebook group as a working channel

1 min
  • A Facebook group can serve as a working channel between mastermind calls (group meetings where members help each other) for quick questions and answers, for sharing problems and asking for fast help.
  • The private, members-only Facebook group is used to post questions, share what is and is not working, and get quick help from the host and other members. Members can also ask the host directly there.

Coaching portal

1 min
  • Top menu: Dashboard (returns to the entry screen from any page), Group Coaching Schedule, Facebook Group, Coaching Recordings. Personal menu: My Courses (returns to the dashboard), Settings (basic platform changes), Log Out.
  • The Group Coaching Schedule is a Google Sheet showing the current calendar month's coaching times, with a registration link per call. It is updated on the first day of every calendar month.
  • Registration for a group coaching call in the schedule is required to attend, because the Zoom link (the online meeting link) for the call is only sent after you register. Registering is the only way to access a session.
  • Recordings of all group coaching sessions are available to every member, so missed sessions can be watched later. Recordings are usually uploaded within one to two days after the session.
  • Program guides give step-by-step implementation guidance (how-to steps for putting something into action) for action items assigned during coaching calls, enabling independent execution of recommendations.
  • Members who are stuck can email the program's support team at support@dropshipbreakthrough.com. Questions can also be posted in the members-only Facebook group, where other members can answer quickly.

Trello

3 min
  • Trello (an online task board made of cards and lists) is used for order flows (the steps from order through fulfilment (packing and sending out the product), shipping and invoicing (the bill)), so the status of any order is visible on one board.
  • Trello is also used for projects: a team member picks a card, works on it and moves it to completed, which removes status-check messages. The free version was enough for the example business.
  • Trello is a free task-management tool for to-dos for each business. It lists clients and prospects (people who might buy) to contact and colour-codes progress: red for not done, yellow for in progress, green for done.
  • Each store or project gets its own Trello board.
  • Trello is used to manage tasks: each task is placed in a category list, assigned to a team member and given a deadline, so the team can complete it overnight for review. Sort tasks into lists that match your workflow (the steps your work goes through) before assigning them.
  • Trello is a visual board system for quotes, orders, projects and product uploads, with cards, lists, labels (coloured tags), comments, attachments and due dates. Due dates make cards turn red when overdue (past the deadline) and can send email notifications. Cards can be dragged between lists to show progress.
  • Set a due date on each follow-up or order card so the team gets notified before and when it is due. Overdue cards appear in red on the board. Enable email notifications so reminders reach you outside the board.
  • Trello's free plan at the time of the source allowed five boards with a small team of about three members plus the owner. The author was unsure whether this was truly free, so check the current plan limits before relying on them. Trello and Asana were reported to cost about $10 per user per month with a minimum number of seats.
  • Zapier (a tool that links apps together) can connect Trello to Shopify or other tools so that actions in one app trigger (start) updates in another. It was not yet working reliably for the author, so connect it step by step and test each trigger. Automations of this kind were the next planned step after manual boards (boards updated by hand).
  • Trello is a task board tool for organising projects and tracking work across a remote business.
  • Procedure to set up a store project board in Trello:
    1. Create a board in Trello for the store project.
    2. Create a big task card, for example Store creation, and list the sub-tasks (smaller steps inside it): create Shopify account, install apps, prepare payment providers, marketing, and product research.
    3. Add labels such as Not done, In progress and Done to show progress, using the pen icon (the edit button) to set the label text and colour.
    4. Give each card a deadline.
    5. Open Share and enter the email address of the assistant to invite them to the board.
    6. Open Members and select the assistant so everyone knows who owns which task.
    7. Use the filter (a tool that hides everything except what you pick) to show only the cards assigned to you.
    8. Attach any PDF (a fixed-layout document file) or product criteria to the card if needed.

Asana

1 min
  • Asana (an online task board) is the core business tool for hiring, task management, reporting and team collaboration. Use it for the job application pipeline (the steps each applicant goes through), candidate tracking, onboarding tasks (steps to get a new hire started), order tracking (each order = task), daily and weekly reporting, and automatic performance dashboards.
  • Asana cost: free at first, then $10 per user per month for premium features with reports and advanced automation.
  • Asana cost, conflicting source: the Reports tab is available in the paid plan only, at $10 per user per month.
  • Asana is used to track order status through fulfilment stages and assign tasks to team members. Orders appear as cards on a board with status columns.
  • Asana is a similar project board tool that also offers a list view for tasks and sorting by due date. Choose between Asana and Trello based on whether you want board-style moves or list views. Both can handle the same operational boards.
  • Asana tasks support due dates and extra detail fields, and personal projects in Asana are free, so it can also serve as a personal to-do list.
  • Asana Reports tab: an automatic live dashboard shows task completion by person, open tasks remaining, and trend over time (4 weeks, 7 days, yesterday). Use it to see team performance and workload distribution (how tasks are split across people) at a glance. Available in the paid plan only ($10 per user per month).

ClickUp and Basecamp

1 min
  • ClickUp (an online task manager) can serve as the central task manager, with one list per area of responsibility, a calendar view that shows upcoming deadlines and calls, and filters or grouping by priority to work through tasks in order. Notion (a note and to-do app) is an alternative for to-do lists. Keep all open tasks in one system so they do not spread across notes and memory.
  • In a ClickUp task description, type / to insert tables, templates, bulleted lists, headings or embedded videos. Use this to keep the description as a dynamic (interactive) page instead of plain text.
  • In ClickUp, open the Everything view and group tasks by Priority to see all open work across lists in priority order. Save this grouped view and use it whenever free time appears.
  • Basecamp (a project tool) is useful for breaking up tasks across many people with due dates, but it is not needed until the business has a decent-sized team.

Loom (screen recording)

  • Loom is a free screen recording tool available as a desktop app or browser extension (a small add-on for your browser). It records screen, microphone and camera for instructional videos.
  • Loom is a free Chrome extension for recording screen, voice and optional webcam (a camera on your computer) to document a process such as uploading products, for training a virtual assistant.
  • Loom records your screen and microphone so you can show a team member how to do a task.
  • Loom (free version), Google Meet (Google's video call app) or QuickTime (Apple's built-in Mac recording tool) can record your screen with microphone audio so that you can document how to perform a task.
  • Loom is a free screen recording tool that gives a shareable link. Use it to record how a product should be uploaded so a freelancer (a self-employed worker you hire per job) can follow it.
  • Loom is a screen recording tool that records the screen and voice, useful for making quick tutorial or explanation videos.
  • Loom is a free Chrome extension that records the screen, optionally with a webcam and microphone. It produces a video link or embed code (a code snippet that shows the video on a web page) that can be placed in a step-by-step guide.
  • Loom is a free screen recording tool that adds a webcam bubble (a small camera circle on the screen). Short Loom videos can be used to answer customer or lead questions (people interested in buying) more personally than email text.
  • Loom is a free screen and video recording tool, available as a Chrome extension or as a desktop app from loom.com. It works well for answering questions with a short visual explanation instead of long text.

  • A short screen recording tool such as Loom can show a store funnel (the path a shopper takes from ad to purchase) to an outside expert, who can spot obvious errors the owner missed. A five-minute walkthrough of the website and funnel is enough for this.

QuickTime (Mac screen recording)

1 min
  • On a Mac, QuickTime is a simple way to record a screen-based process for the team. Press Command + Shift + 5 to start screen recording.

Hiring pipeline tools

Upwork

1 min
  • Upwork (a freelance marketplace) and onlinejobs.ph are used to hire hourly freelancers for product uploads and other tasks. Fiverr is not recommended for product upload work.
  • Upwork is used to hire Shopify developers (people who build custom store features) for custom product tools or small theme changes (changes to the store's look). Bidders (freelancers who offer a price for the job) are usually available quickly. (2021)
  • Upwork is the platform for one-off projects such as uploading products or hiring a virtual assistant. (2021)
  • Upwork is used to hire freelancers for Shopify developer work, product uploads and site design, while virtual assistants can be hired from online-jobs.ph. (2020)
  • Upwork lets you post a project and receive bids from writers and specialists. Run test projects with several writers before choosing one for ongoing work. (2021)

  • Upwork and Fiverr are freelance marketplaces where a store owner can find first virtual assistants. The rate of bad hires there is high, so check the assistant's track record and consider a trial period before committing.

Fiverr

1 min
  • Fiverr (a freelance marketplace) is pay-per-job, so a single task such as an upload or small edit can be bought without a subscription, with prices around $10 per task. Its freelancer pool covers a very wide range of skills, and quality varies strongly.
  • Fiverr is a marketplace for hiring freelancers, used to outsource (hand work to an outside person) store builds, product pages, or purchasing pre-warmed Facebook ad accounts (accounts that have already been used a little, so the platform trusts them more).
  • Source positions on Fiverr differ. Fiverr is not recommended for product upload work, while a single upload or small edit can be bought there for about $10 per task. Use Fiverr for one-off tasks with a checked result, and use Upwork or onlinejobs.ph for recurring upload work.

Virtual assistant tools

1 min
  • A virtual assistant (VA, a remote helper who works from their own computer) can use Hootsuite (a tool for posting to social media) from one dashboard (one control screen).
  • Hootsuite publishes the same product pictures to all connected social media accounts.
  • Give the VA access only to Hootsuite. Posting stays in one place, and you avoid sharing account logins.
  • Google Drive (online file storage), Google Sheets (an online spreadsheet) and Asana (a task list tool) together give the VA an order and task view.
  • Customer messages stay in the help desk (the support inbox).

Monitoring and activity checks

1 min
  • Hubstaff is a time tracking tool. It shows mouse and keyboard activity and time spent.
  • Normal product listing work (the product pages in your store) shows 70% mouse and 30% keyboard.
  • Abnormal work shows 100% keyboard and 100% mouse with no output. This is likely fake time tracking or off-task behaviour (work that is not part of the job).
  • Use Hubstaff to detect fake time tracking. For example, someone plays videos or uses software to fake activity.
  • Hubstaff is not used to pay per second. It is used for activity validation (checking that the activity is real).

Access, backups and handover

1 min
  • Give the virtual assistant limited access to the Shopify admin panel (the back office of your Shopify online store, a platform for running shops).
  • Limit access to the parts needed to see order status (where an order is) and enter tracking information (the shipping code).
  • Do not give full admin access. Use the same limited-access approach used for phone support staff.
  • Rewinds is a Shopify app (an add-on program) for backing up your store data before granting contractors (outside workers) access. (2024)

Research VPNs

2 min

  • Use it to see the search results and shopping results for the United States, if Google shows results for your own country instead.
  • Any reputable VPN works, free or paid. (2020)
  • A VPN or proxy (a middleman connection that also changes your location) set to another country shows Google search results as local shoppers see them.
  • Use it to check foreign competitors (other sellers) and Shopping ads (paid product ads in Google Shopping) from outside that country. (2024)
  • A VPN lets you see Google Shopping results as if you were in another country.
  • This helps with price research where Google Shopping is not available locally.
  • Proton VPN is free or costs about $10 per month. (2021)
  • ExpressVPN and similar VPN services change the apparent country of the connection.
  • Use them to see country-specific search results, such as when the Google Shopping tab is missing in the research country. (2022)
  • ExpressVPN is a tool for connecting to the internet securely when you work remotely from different countries.
  • ExpressVPN can show your Google Ads or store as your target country sees them, while you are abroad (in another country).
  • Geo-targeted ads (ads shown only in chosen countries) will not show to people outside that country.
  • Use it carefully. A VPN can trigger account security checks.
  • NordVPN was named as the VPN to use on a desktop (a computer) to open a US-region TikTok Ads Manager (TikTok's control panel for paid ads) from the UK.
  • Reports on this setup were mixed. They described it as prone to issues (likely to have problems).
  • Test it with a small budget before you rely on it.
  • A VPN subscription (a paid plan) can be used to log in with an IP address (an internet address) from the target country.
  • Target countries include Australia, New Zealand, the UK or an EU country (European Union).
  • Use this before opening TikTok Ads Manager or TikTok Business Center (TikTok's business account hub).
  • A yearly NordVPN plan was cited at about $1 to $2.
  • Turn the VPN on before starting the session (your work on the site), not afterward.
  • A VPN can be used to set an Instagram account to a target country, for example the US. This way, organic reels (short videos that reach people without paid ads) reach that audience.
