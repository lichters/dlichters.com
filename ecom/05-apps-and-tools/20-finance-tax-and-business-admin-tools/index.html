Finance, tax and business admin tools

Section 5.20 · 20 of 22 in this chapter · 15 min read

In short

  • Check each product's profit before you spend money on ads.
  • Track the profit of every sale. A simple spreadsheet is enough at first.
  • Start with a free spreadsheet for your books. Move to Xero only when orders grow a lot.
  • Look up sales tax for each US state. TaxJar can do this for you.

Break-even and margin checks before ads

2 min
  • The Dropship Toolkit calculator takes a product cost and a selling price.
  • It shows the profit margin (the share of each sale you keep) and the break-even ROAS.
  • ROAS means return on ad spend, the money you get back for each dollar spent on ads.
  • Break-even means you neither make nor lose money.
  • Use it to check a product before running ads.
  • A dropshipping break-even calculator takes two inputs. Dropshipping means you sell products that a supplier ships for you.
  • The first input is COGS, which stands for cost of goods sold: the product cost plus shipping to the customer.
  • The second input is the selling price.
  • It returns the break-even ROAS for the product. Use it before launching ads.
  • A calculator such as Drop Ship Toolkit computes the break-even ROAS from the COGS and selling price you enter.
  • This lets you compare products you are considering, quickly.
  • The Ecom Margin Calculator has three input fields.
    • LISTING PRICE: the price the product shows on the store.
    • COGS + SHIPPING: the total cost per unit.
    • AD SPEND PER ORDER: optional.
  • Its outputs are GROSS MARGIN, PRODUCT MARGIN, PRODUCT ROI, TARGET ROAS and TARGET CPA.
  • ROI means return on investment: the profit compared with the money you put in.
  • TARGET CPA means cost per acquisition: the most you can spend on ads to win one buyer.
  • It also shows a tier badge, a label that rates the product: Elite, Sweet Spot, Workable or Insufficient. It adds a short status message.
  • A Sweet Spot result means the product is ready to scale, which means spending more on ads once it works.
  • An Insufficient result means the product needs work.
  • An Insufficient result suggests a bundle (several products sold together as one offer) or a higher-margin product (one that keeps more of each sale).
  • An Insufficient result also shows the hint: "Low margin? Try adding a bundle offer."
  • The source does not describe the status messages for the Elite and Workable tiers.
  • The Source D margin spreadsheet is named KPIs Margin Net Profit BE ROAS. KPI means key performance indicator, a key number that tracks how well something works.
  • For each product, it calculates:
    • product cost
    • credit card fees
    • merchant account fees (fees charged by the account that takes your payments)
    • shipping costs
    • fulfilment cost per unit (the cost to pack and send one item)
    • total cost of product plus fulfilment
    • percentage cost
    • expected gross profit per order
    • gross margin
    • net profit (what is left after all costs)
    • break-even ROAS
    • target ROAS at 10%, 15% and 20% margins
  • Use the margin spreadsheet for each market and quantity row.
  • Check that the selling price meets the margin target.

Pricing procedure for one product in the margin spreadsheet

1 min
  1. Open the margin spreadsheet and create your own copy with File > Make a copy. This copy is yours to change, and the original stays unchanged.
  2. Confirm that the market row is set to USA with quantity 1.
  3. Get the USA product cost from the supplier.
  4. Enter only the two input cells: the product cost and the selling price (AOV, which means average order value, the average amount one customer spends per order). Do not touch any cell marked in red.
  5. Adjust the selling price until the gross profit margin shows 70%.
  6. If 70% is not reachable, accept 69%, 68% or 67% as the minimum.

Profit trackers

2 min
  • Google Sheets is a free online spreadsheet.
  • Use it to track product costs, prices and profits if you do not use a dedicated app (an app made only for this job).
  • Enter prices by hand, then compare them over time.
  • A profit tracking spreadsheet records the net profit for each sale.
  • Google Sheets, Excel or Numbers (Apple's spreadsheet app) can be used.
  • Google Sheets is a free tool for tracking profit per sale. It can also use a sales automation, which is a set of rules that does a task by itself.
  • Put the profit formula (the calculation) in the first cell of each column. A cell is one box in the sheet.
  • Then drag it down to fill the column.
    1. Use the spread column for MAP minus wholesale. MAP means minimum advertised price, and wholesale means the price you buy at. The spread is the gap between them.
    2. Use a later column for spread minus shipping.
  • The Order Profit Tracker is a Google Sheets spreadsheet.
  • It has a Profit Per Order tab (a sheet inside the file) and a Setup tab.
  • Colored cells calculate by themselves.
  • Only the white, uncolored cells are filled in by hand.
  • To get your own copy of the Order Profit Tracker:
    1. Open the shared link. It gives read-only or view-only access, which means you can look but not change.
    2. Go to File > Make a copy and save it to your Google Drive (Google's online file storage).
  • A Shopify backend app can store the wholesale cost on each product. Backend means the admin area that only you see.
  • So margin is tracked in the back end while shoppers see nothing.
  • B-Profit is installed as follows:
    1. Open the recommended apps section, which links to the B-Profit listing in the Shopify App Store.
    2. Click Install.
    3. After creating an account, the B-Profit dashboard (your main screen with the numbers) opens.
    4. It shows the profit numbers once setup is finished.
  • Before you connect Facebook, Instagram and Klaviyo accounts (Klaviyo is an email tool) to BeProfit, pause any active ad blocker on the BeProfit website. An ad blocker is a browser add-on that hides ads.
  • Otherwise the account connection steps may not load correctly.

Bookkeeping and accounting

2 min

Bookkeeping means recording every money coming in and going out. Choose the tool by stage (how far along the business is) and order volume (how many orders you get). Xero is online accounting software.

Stage Tool Ids
Brand-new store, low volume Spreadsheet (Google Sheets, Excel or Numbers)
Early stage, no high transaction volume Google Sheets (free, less sophisticated)
Growing volume, many transactions Xero
  • Use Excel to track profit and loss from the beginning. Profit and loss means what you earned minus what you spent.
  • Shopify (the software that runs your online store) does not know your expenses.
  • So Excel or a third-party bookkeeping service (an outside company that keeps your books) fills that gap.
  • The first years were run on Excel with well over $5 million in sales before switching.
  • Google Sheets can serve as basic accounting and reconciliation software for early-stage businesses. Reconciliation means matching your records with your bank statement.
  • Google Sheets without high transaction volume is free but less sophisticated than dedicated accounting software. A transaction is one sale or payment.
  • Google Sheets can handle profit tracking at an early stage.
  • It was used for over $10 million in sales before switching to Xero. (2023)
  • The source gives a different switch point for Excel above.
  • Xero provides centralized bookkeeping, meaning all your books in one place, with access for accountants, bookkeepers and business owners.
  • Xero costs approximately £25/month.
  • Xero is superior to Google Sheets for businesses with significant transaction volume.
  • Xero is a common accounting platform for e-commerce businesses.
  • Xero is a bookkeeping and accounting tool that pulls in transaction data.
  • It is not required at the start. A spreadsheet is enough while order volume is low.
  • Xero is the accounting software used by the bookkeeper.
  • A brand-new store does not need Xero or QuickBooks (another accounting software).
  • Once money flows through the business, bookkeeping software makes accountant review cheaper. An accountant review means an accountant checks your books.
  • Xero is the example given, and QuickBooks is an alternative.
  • In the source's setup, operating subscriptions (monthly tools that run the business) such as Shopify, Grasshopper and Bonjoro are charged to the Capital One card together with supplier orders.
  • Keeping these subscriptions on one card keeps operating expenses in one statement.
  • A Google Sheets allocation chart can model the Profit First system. Profit First is a way to split your money on purpose into separate accounts.
    1. Enter the sales for a period, the COGS percentage, and the percentage for each account.
    2. The sheet calculates the COGS amount, real revenue (sales minus product cost), and each account's amount.
    3. Check that the percentage column totals 100% before using it.
  • The Profit First planning model is built in Google Sheets.
  • So the owner can share the allocations and staff planning tabs with the team.
  • It is edited and viewed in a browser, so no separate software is needed.

Sales tax with TaxJar

2 min

Sales tax is the tax added to a sale. In the US, each state sets its own rules.

  • TaxJar is a Shopify app for automatic tax calculation and filing. Filing means sending the tax reports to the state.
  • Set it up once, and it handles tax in all registered states (states where you are signed up to collect tax). (2023)
  • TaxJar is used to manage sales tax across several US states.
  • Other tax apps with good reviews in the Shopify app store can also be used. (2024)
  • To find it, search for TaxJar in the Shopify app store. (2023)
  • TaxJar is a Shopify app that calculates state sales tax.
  • It tracks sales thresholds (sales levels that trigger a rule) across states.
  • So you know when to register in another state. (2023)
  • TaxJar is a provider of e-commerce tax and law information.
  • It has a large resource section of guides, videos and blog posts.
  • Its app connects to Shopify and can collect and remit sales tax. Collect means charging it at checkout. Remit means sending it to the state.
  • Using the app is optional.
  • TaxJar offers a Shopify app and a resource center with detailed sales tax information.
  • Use it to understand sales tax rules, nexus thresholds and collection requirements. Nexus means a legal connection to a state that makes you collect its tax. (2025)
  • TaxJar offers free resources on e-commerce sales tax and a Shopify app for tax calculation.
  • Use its resources to look up exact nexus thresholds for each state.
  • TaxJar provides sales tax and nexus information, including a resource center and an app for Shopify.
  • It is used to check sales thresholds per state.
  • TaxJar provides current sales tax information by US state.
  • It is a reliable resource for nexus thresholds.
  • Check it before assuming how much tax to collect in each state.
  • TaxJar is recommended for sales tax handling in the US and integrates with Shopify.
  • Economic nexus thresholds for each state are on its guide. Economic nexus means a state can require tax collection based on sales alone, not on an office.
  • These are commonly 100,000 dollars in sales or 200 transactions per year, depending on the state.
  • TaxJar is a Shopify app and service that calculates, collects and remits sales tax for states where the business has to collect it.
  • TaxJar is a resource for looking up state-by-state sales tax rules.
  • This includes whether shipping charges are taxable.
  • Check the state rules before setting shipping tax.
  • TaxJar is a website and Shopify app that collects sales tax and also files it for you.
  • It is the option for a fully hands-off tax service on a Shopify store.
  • The two sources differ on how optional TaxJar is.
  • One describes the app as optional. (2020)
  • The other calls it the fully hands-off option. (2018)
  • Choose the hands-off app if you want the filing handled for you.
  • Use the resources alone if you only need to look up rules.

Payment processors and checkout financing

1 min

A payment processor is the company that moves card money from the customer to you. Financing means letting customers pay over time.

  • Shopify Payments is the preferred payment processor where it is supported in the seller's country.
  • Check Shopify's supported-countries list. It included roughly 15 to 20 countries at the time.
  • Shopify Payments support includes many European countries. (2023)
  • The two sources give different country counts.
  • So check the current list before relying on either.
  • 2Checkout is a payment processor.
  • It can be used as a fallback (a backup option) when Shopify Payments is not available.
  • PayPal can be used as a secondary payment method.
  • It is not the main one. (2023)
  • PayPal Credit can be enabled on a store that already accepts PayPal.
  • This makes financing simple to add.
  • It is one of several financing options for high-ticket orders (expensive orders).
  • Shopify's built-in financing through Shop Pay Installments and Klarna (a pay-later service) is available with Shopify Payments. (2024)
  • Use it so customers can split payments for expensive products. (2024)
  • Affirm is a pay-over-time financing provider.
  • It integrates with Shopify and shows the financing option on product pages next to the Buy It Now button (the button that buys at once).
  • It can be offered as an alternative to PayPal Credit for customers who prefer financing.
  • Currency conversion apps from the Shopify App Store show prices in the local currency.
  • They do this based on the visitor's IP address, a number that shows where a visitor connects from.
  • Shopify also planned a built-in version for fall 2018.
  • Shopify Payments accepts the converted payments.

Business registration and virtual mailing addresses

1 min
  • LegalZoom is used to form an LLC. An LLC is a limited liability company, a business type that keeps your personal money separate.
  • LegalZoom gives a virtual forwarding address, a mail address that forwards your post to you.
  • Some suppliers accept this virtual forwarding address as a business address.
  • Stripe Atlas is a service that can set up a US business entity (a legal company in the US).
  • It can also set up a US bank account for non-US residents.
  • The account integrates with Shopify.
  • It is a workable route for non-US sellers who need a US entity.

Due diligence and valuation

1 min

Due diligence is a buyer's check of a business before buying it. Valuation is an estimate of what a business is worth.

  • Shopify analytics (the store's built-in traffic and sales reports) are useful.
  • Buyers still want Google Analytics for store traffic data.
  • Empire Flippers, a business-sale broker, requires Google Analytics for every listing.
  • Buyers check backlinks (links from other sites to yours) for spam and manipulation.
  • Moz Open Site Explorer (written as "boz.com open site explorer" in the source) shows backlinks to your site.
  • This includes links you built yourself.
  • Centurica publishes due diligence reports that buyers can order on a site.
  • A seller can order the basic report six months out, meaning six months before selling.
  • This finds weak spots so you can fix them first.
  • The Empire Flippers online valuation tool takes the inputs below.
  • It gives a range that the broker compares with previously sold businesses. A broker is a person who helps sell businesses.

Each input changes the valuation. A multiple is the price factor a buyer uses, based on earnings.

Input Effect on valuation
Seller hours per week (seller only, not team) Fewer hours are more valuable: 5 hours is better than 50
Company start date Older company is more valuable
Average monthly revenue Bigger businesses usually get higher multiples
Average monthly expenses Profit margin affects valuation
Number of products More products mean lower risk
Traffic Minor factor

Annual review and goals

1 min
  • The annual review spreadsheet has four tabs (sheets inside one file): Business Questions, Business Answers, Personal Questions and Personal Answers.
  • Each question in a Questions tab has its matching answer area in the corresponding Answers tab.
  • Write answers only in the Answers tabs.
  • Use the Numbers file on Mac or the Excel file on Windows.
  • The Course B Goals and Rocks Tracker is a Google Sheets template. A template is a ready-made file you copy and fill in.
  • It puts the 10-year picture, 3-year picture and 1-year plan on one page as a roadmap.
  • It can be downloaded to Excel or CSV (a plain table file) for those who work on desktop or do not use Google Drive.
  • To set up your own goals tracker:
    1. Do not edit the shared template directly.
    2. In Google Sheets, click File and then Make a copy, which places a copy in your Google Drive.
    3. Rename the copy to your business name.
    4. Alternatively, download the template as Microsoft Excel or CSV and edit it on your desktop.
