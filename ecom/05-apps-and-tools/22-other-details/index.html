Learning tools

  • Video Speed is a free Google Chrome extension (a small add-on for the Chrome web browser). It changes the playback speed of any recording, faster or slower. Install it to watch teaching material at your own pace.
  • Kajabi (an online platform for selling and hosting teaching material) can show a student a list of items to go through. It can mark each item as done. It can show mentors exactly what each student has completed.
  • Kajabi is useful for tracking progress in a training or mentoring program.

Setup and official tool sources

  • Use the official websites for your tools: zapier.com for Zapier (a tool that connects apps automatically), trello.com for Trello (a task board), and Google Sheets through Google Drive (online spreadsheets).
  • Slack (a team chat app) and Shopify (a platform for building an online shop) are used through their own sites and apps.
  • A basic laptop is enough to run an online store.
  • This includes a Chromebook (a cheap, simple laptop that works mostly in the web browser).
  • Heavy software is not needed. Large product spreadsheets can be handled in Google Sheets online.
  • The Shopify App Store offers apps (add-on tools) that add features to a store.
  • A store credit (money kept on your account that you can spend in the store), for example $50, can be spent there when offered.

Shopify apps and store features

  • Reviews and pop-up apps are updated in this text.
  • The store setup section was rebuilt for current Shopify interfaces (the screens you click through).
  • Shopify apps can lock parts of a store behind customer accounts (login accounts). One example is a dealer section with different prices and products.
  • Shopify is not the right platform for a content membership site (a site where people pay for access to content).

B2B and trade customers

  • B2B means selling to companies. Shopify plug-ins (add-on tools) can show hidden trade-only products and prices.
  • Only logged-in, approved trade customers can see those hidden products and prices.
  • Use them to offer business buyers better deals without showing those prices to the public.
  • B2B request-a-quote setup, in four steps. A quote is a price offer made on request (2023):
    1. Add a "request a quote" link in the header menu (the top bar of the website).
    2. Create a quote page with logos of past business customers.
    3. Add a contact form asking for name, phone, email, items of interest and quantities per item.
    4. Add a homepage section with the same business logos near the bottom of the page.

Offer and customer planning

  • The CURVE Offer Builder is a simple spreadsheet. It records all nine offer elements in one place.
  • The nine elements are: marketing messages, product, price, shipping, USP package (unique selling point, what makes the offer different), risk reversal (a promise that takes the buyer's risk away, such as a money-back guarantee), customer service, trust factors, and bonuses.
  • The Offer Builder also has a separate marketing-message section. Complete it after the offer structure is set.
  • The customer avatar sheet has one main sheet and three side-by-side avatar columns. An avatar is a made-up profile of one typical buyer. Each column holds a full set of customer questions.
  • Its layout, row by row. A subreddit is a topic forum on Reddit. Quora, LinkedIn and Twitter are social sites:

  • The avatar spreadsheet has space for three customer avatars. You can keep distinct buyer profiles side by side.
  • This allows separate profiles for, for example, a female and a male customer group. It also works for B2B and B2C buyers. B2C means selling to private people.

Product map

  • Keep the product map (a list of all your products and their details) as a spreadsheet. Do not use a PDF or a separate document per product.
  • A PDF needs a new file for every product and is harder to edit.
  • A spreadsheet with one tab per product keeps all products in one file.

Email, orders and tracking apps

  • A wishlist or gift registry app (a list where people save items, or where guests buy gifts) should cost at most about $15 per month.
  • Paying $99 per month for a registry app is excessive.
  • Klaviyo (an email marketing tool) and AfterShip (an order tracking tool) both have setup documentation and responsive support teams.
  • Follow their step-by-step setup guides before contacting support.

Booking tools for service businesses

  • Fresha is a free booking app for service businesses such as barbershops and salons.
  • Clients can choose a service, pick a barber and book an appointment online.
  • Fresha can be connected to Google.

Troubleshooting apps and store errors

  • When an SEO (search engine optimization, getting found on Google) or store app shows errors that cannot be fixed directly, follow four steps:
    1. Copy the exact error message.
    2. Search for that message together with the platform name, for example Shopify. Include forums and the Shopify community.
    3. Look for fixes documented by users who had the same error.
    4. If no fix turns up, have a specialist handle it.
  • To troubleshoot a technical problem in a Shopify app or store setup, follow three steps:
    1. Search Google for the exact product name or the exact error message.
    2. Search Reddit for the same problem. People who cannot get help from official support often post there.
    3. Contact general support only if these searches do not solve the issue. Common problems are usually documented by others.

Backups

  • Rewind Backups is used to back up (save a copy of) store data and code.
  • Backups are not strictly necessary but are recommended (2024).

Tools that need verification

  • StoreTasker, Niche Verification and Stubgroup were named as tools in the October 2024 coaching call.
  • The source gives no usage details for them.
  • Check each one's current function before relying on it (2024).

Product claims and compliance

  • A claim is a statement about what a product does. Laboratory testing or expert review can verify product claims before they are advertised.
  • Verifying a claim protects it from being challenged. It also lets the claim be stated with exact figures.
  • Unverified claims can cause an ad to be refused by reputable media.

Work setup and cost of living

  • Replace car ownership with ride-hailing (Uber, a car service you book on your phone) and monthly Airbnb stays (short-term rented homes).
  • This avoids car costs such as maintenance, smog checks (emissions tests) and washing.
  • It is a practical way to cut responsibilities while living abroad.
