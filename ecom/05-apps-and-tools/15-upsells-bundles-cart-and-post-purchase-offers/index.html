Upsells, bundles, cart and post-Purchase offers

Section 5.15 · 15 of 22 in this chapter · 45 min read

In short

  • Add offer apps (bundles, upsells, cross-sells) only after your store has more than one product, and compare apps before you pick one.
  • Use countdown timers and urgency messages only when there is a real deadline, such as a delivery date before Christmas.
  • Test every offer on a phone before you publish it. Mobile is the most important view.
  • Turn a discount on in Shopify and activate it before you test. A widget that shows a discount with no discount in the cart looks like a bug.
  • Check the app's current price and billing terms inside the app before you go live.
More key points
  • Use Rebuy Smart Cart, an app that replaces your store's cart page, and build new carts as version 2.
  • Keep Use Shopify Functions switched on, so free-gift steps in the progress bar really give a discount.
  • Pick post-purchase tools by budget. Start with one simple offer after checkout.
  • Install bundle apps by checking whether you must paste code or can avoid it.
  • Below about 20,000 USD in monthly sales, keep Shopify's own checkout.
  • Upsells and cross-sells (extra offers that add to the order) can raise what each customer spends. Check each app's price and free trial before you pay.
  • Free gift apps can clash with other cart apps. Test them together before you send visitors to the store.
  • Post-purchase offers appear after payment and do not change the first order.
  • Old Smart Cart discounts stay in the list when you switch carts. Delete them by hand.

Choosing the offer stack

2 min
  • A bundle sells several products together as one deal.
  • A quantity break gives a lower price when the buyer takes more units.
  • An upsell offers a bigger or better version of a product.
  • A cross-sell offers a related extra item.
  • These offers are usually built with dedicated apps, which are add-ons for your online store.
  • Several apps exist for each job, so compare them before you choose one.
  • Bundle and upsell apps are an option once the store has more than one product.
  • Complementary-bundle apps (apps that bundle items that go together) work best when the store has a strong product that can be sold as an upsell.
  • Older advice: add post-purchase apps (offers shown after checkout) and cross-sell apps only once the store grows.
  • Add them when average order value (AOV, the average money spent per order) starts to matter.
  • They are not needed on day one.
  • Older advice: do not use urgency timers (countdown clocks that push a buyer to act now) or urgency apps unless there is a specific real deadline, such as a delivery date before Christmas.
  • The sources give different timer values for different setups.
  • Kaching Cart (a Shopify app that controls the cart) and OCU (a one-click upsell setup) use a 3-minute timer.
  • A 20-minute deadline appears as an example in post-purchase SMS (text message) and email text.
  • Use any timer only with a real deadline, as the older rule states.
  • For free-gift content (a free item given with a purchase), use ChatGPT (an AI writing tool) to brainstorm bundle names, offer copy (sales text) and persuasive framing before you build offers in the store.
  • For free-gift content, use ChatGPT to create instructional eBooks, email sequences, guides and digital resources.
  • They cost nothing to produce.
  • They add perceived value (what the buyer thinks the item is worth) to higher-tier bundles.
  • A native Shopify discount (built into Shopify, no extra app) can apply automatically when a customer adds 2 or 3 units.
  • The discount appears without codes or third-party apps (outside apps).
  • This removes friction (extra steps that stop a buyer) when a customer decides to buy several items.
  • Planning helper: the BSGR Product Upsell Planner (a spreadsheet in Google Sheets) can be exported via File > Download as.xlsx,.ods,.pdf,.html (zipped),.csv (current sheet only) or.tsv (current sheet only).

Bundle Bear

3 min

What it does

  • Bundle Bear is an app that creates product bundles, volume discounts (lower price per item when buying more) and free-gift offers in Shopify.
  • Offer types, as the app describes them:
    • Volume discount: more items, bigger discount.
    • Mix and match: discount for different products bought together.
    • Cart goal: discount when the cart reaches a set value, e.g. $50.
  • Bundle and volume discount are the two most-used types.
  • Volume discount example: buy 2 and save 10%, buy 3 and save 15%.
  • Bundle option for two complementary products (products that go together): save $10 when buying both, or save $20 when buying two items.
  • Free-gift bundle: set the second product as a free gift and select which product is the gift.
  • Bundle link with no discount: shows that both items can be bought together.
  • Example offer from one store: buy two, get 20% off plus a free gift.
  • Discount formats differ by source.
  • Fixed-dollar amounts ($10 or $20) are used for the two-item bundle.
  • Percentages (10% and 15%) are used for volume tiers (levels of discount by quantity).
  • Use the format the offer type requires.
  • The example setup used the Bundle and Volume discount options.
  • Advanced settings include a must-choose-option rule (the customer must pick an option), country limits, start and end dates and rounded prices (prices that end in a set amount).
  • Economics reported in the source: a 40 to 50 percent bundle take rate (share of orders that use the bundle).
  • The same source reports roughly $40 extra profit per order.
  • Treat these as reported results, not guarantees.

Setup procedure

  1. Install Bundle Bear from the Shopify App Store (Shopify's shop for apps) and click Install app.
  2. Click Create offer.
  3. Choose the offer type. Bundle and volume discount are the two most-used types.
  4. For a bundle, select the first product and the second product.
    • Choose the discount: save 10 when buying both items, or save 20 when buying two items.
    • Or set the second product as a free gift and select which product is the gift.
    • Or choose no discount to show that both items can be bought together.
  5. Open advanced settings.
    • Choose whether the customer must choose an option.
    • Limit the offer to a country.
    • Set a start date and end date.
    • Enable Round discounted prices with.99 as the ending.
  6. Save the offer.
  7. Open Design to change alignment, colors, and the button color to match your store.
    • Change the text if the ads target a country with a different language.
  8. Click save.
  9. Open Embeds (switches for app features) in the theme editor (the store's design editor).
    • Check the installed apps.
    • Switch Bundle Bear on.
  10. Open the product section, click Add block, and choose Bundle Bear.
    • Drag it below the buy buttons.
  11. Check the layout on a mobile phone, then save.
  • Offer options: click Add option to add tiers (levels).
  • Advanced settings include custom badges (small labels), start and end time for urgency, and rounding of discounted prices (e.g. to 50 cents or per dollar).
  • Click save and activate.
  • Placement by source (sources differ, so test them):

(step 10), Below the buy buttons, not below the discount Theme block placement via Embeds and Add block

  • Check the layout on a phone before publishing.

Rebuy dynamic bundles

3 min
  • Rebuy is a Shopify app for product recommendations.
  • A dynamic bundle fills itself with products picked by rules or AI.

Creating the bundle widget

  1. In Rebuy, go to Widgets > Product Page > New Widget. (A widget is a small feature box shown on a page.)
  2. Scroll to Dynamic Bundle, click Create new, name it Dynamic Bundle Offer and confirm with Create it!
  • The default data source rule for a new dynamic bundle is "if anything, return recommended AI".
  • A data source is the rule list that decides which products appear.
  • This rule compares the main product with products other customers bought together with it.
  • A purchase-based recommendation rule works well as a starting point for bundles because it needs no manual product picking.
  • It relies on what customers actually buy alongside the main product.
  • To create or edit a dynamic bundle, go to the product page tile in the Rebuy app and select the widget.
  • If none exists, create the dynamic bundle widget there first, then open its Data Sources page from the widget.

Widget ID

  • The widget ID is the widget's number.
  • It appears in the widget header (e.g. Dynamic Bundle Offer (ID: 117326)).
  • It also appears as the number in the edit page address, /widgets/id//edit.
  • Copy this number for the Shopify app block field or the manual div (a block of code you paste into the theme).

Installing with Shopify App Blocks

An app block is a ready-made piece you place on a page without writing code.

  1. Copy the widget ID shown in the widget header (e.g. Dynamic Bundle Offer (ID: 117326)).
  2. Click Install (top right) in the Rebuy widget editor, then click add block to my online store.
  3. In Shopify's theme customizer, open the product page template.
  4. Add a block (new section or existing section) and choose Rebuy Widget under APP BLOCKS.
  5. Paste the widget ID into the Widget ID field.
  6. Click Save.
  7. Open a product page to confirm the bundle shows.
  • The Rebuy Widget app block has these fields:
    • Enable Widget (on)
    • Widget ID
    • Enable Product ID: lets the widget use the product ID to return products based on the data source.
    • Enable Collection ID: lets the widget use the collection ID for the same purpose.
  • App blocks only appear in themes built for Shopify Online Store 2.0 (the current store design system).
  • Use the manual code method if the app block cannot be installed.

Installing manually in theme files

A theme is the set of design files that controls how your store looks. Liquid is Shopify's code language.

  1. In the widget editor, open Placement, expand Manual installation and copy the widget div.
  2. Open the theme's three-dot menu and click Edit Code.
  3. Open layout/theme.liquid (the main page layout file).
  4. Add the line RenderRebuyExtensions to theme.liquid.
    • Check the exact Liquid call against Rebuy's install guide.
  5. Create a new file snippets/rebuyExtensions.liquid. (A snippet is a small reusable code file.)
  6. In that file, write an if product block that contains the copied widget div and close it with end if.
  7. Save, open a product page and scroll down to check the widget.

Editor layout

  • Widget editor tabs: General, Language, Data Source, Discount, Timer, Layout, Placement and Images.
  • Preview switches between Desktop and Mobile views.
  • Live Mode is available.
  • Install and Save sit at the top right.

Rebuy data sources for bundles

3 min

Setting up a data source

  1. Open the Widgets section in Rebuy and select the dynamic bundle widget (create one first if none exists).
  2. Open the widget's Data Sources page.
  3. Click Add Rule and choose the rule type: Product Tags, Collection or Specific Products.
  4. Set the condition:
    • For tags, use Contains Any and enter the tag.
    • For a collection, select the collection.
    • For specific products, select the products.
  5. Enter the rule title that shoppers will see.
  6. If the bundle should show a discount, set Widget Discount to Percentage and enter the value.
  7. Turn on Exit if Matched if rules below this one should be ignored when it matches.
  8. Click Save and confirm the message that the data source was saved.
  9. Create the matching automatic discount in Shopify (see the Shopify procedure) and test the bundle on the storefront.
Rule type Use it to Example Condition used
Product Tags Pull in every product that carries a shared tag Tea products Contains Any + tag name (tea)
Collection Pull in all items of one collection Jewelry collection Collection selected
Specific Products Build a fixed set around one product Supercharge drink plus accessory products Individually selected products
  • Tag rule example: Product Tags, Contains Any, value tea makes every tea product appear as bundle items on a tea product page.
  • The shopper picks which teas to include before adding the bundle to the cart.
  • Collection rule example: a jewelry collection fills the bundle with options from that collection.
  • Adding the bundle puts all selected items in the cart at once.
  • This reduces shopper fatigue (tiredness from too many choices) on large ranges.
  • Specific-products rule example: a hand-picked set, such as a main product plus complementary accessories, suits a single high-margin product (one that earns a big profit per sale) that should always be sold with the same add-ons.
  • The rule editor shows the rule type, a condition field with operators such as Contains Any, a title field, a sort option (Smart Sort or Recommended (AI)) and the Exit if Matched toggle.
  • Further buttons are Add Before, Add After, Duplicate, Remove and Collapse.
  • Save the data source with Save.
  • Rules are evaluated in order.
  • When Exit if Matched is enabled on a rule, every rule after it is ignored if that rule matches.
  • Put narrow or specific rules above broad ones so the broad rule does not swallow them.
  • Each rule's conditions can be joined with AND or OR.
  • Add rules at a position with Add Before or Add After.
  • Use connectors to combine several tags or products in one rule instead of building many separate rules.
  • Under the data source's filter settings, Filter out of stock products and Filter out of stock variants keep unavailable items out of the bundle. (A variant is a version of a product, such as a size or color.)
  • An Add Exclusion filter option removes specific items from the input.
  • The data source name is only visible internally, so use a name that makes the bundle purpose clear to your team.
  • A Clear Cache button (it empties saved data) on the data source page helps if the widget does not update after changes.
  • Mistake: testing before the Shopify automatic discount is active shows the widget discount but no discount in the cart, which looks like a bug.
  • Create and activate the Shopify discount first, then test in a cleared cart.
  • Bundle items are added to the cart as separate products, not as one bundle SKU (stock code for one item).
  • So there is no separate bundle inventory.
  • Stock is managed per item as usual.
  • Shopify automatic discounts apply without a code.
  • They are the method used to make a Rebuy widget discount real.
  • They can combine with other active discounts if that option is enabled.
  • Rebuy on a product page lets shoppers tick several products and variants and bundle them together.
  • When this builder adds too many clicks, replace it with ready-made bundles that carry a fixed extra discount.

ReBuy as a platform

1 min
  • ReBuy is a Shopify app that uses AI to personalize product recommendations across the shopping journey (the path from first visit to checkout).
  • Stated goals: higher average order value (AOV), conversion rate (share of visitors who buy) and customer lifetime value (total money one customer spends over time).
  • According to the vendor (the company that sells the app), ReBuy can be set up on a Shopify store in minutes without coding or developer skills.
  • Treat this as a vendor claim and verify it on your own store.
  • Custom rules can be stacked on top of each other to control which products appear where and how often.
  • This supports a deliberate merchandising strategy (a planned choice of what to show) instead of default recommendations.
  • ReBuy connects directly to other tools in the store's tech stack (the set of apps and tools a store uses).
  • It works with several third-party integrations (links to outside apps).
  • Check the integration list against your own apps before installing.

Bold Sales Motivator (threshold display)

A threshold is a set amount that unlocks a reward.

  • Bold Sales Motivator shows a shopper how close they are to a free bonus, for example in a spend X to get X offer.
  • The threshold is visible, which encourages the shopper to reach it.
  • It shows how far shoppers are from a cart threshold as they add items.
  • Use it for offers such as a free gift over 1,000 dollars.
  • Turn it off as soon as the promotion ends.

Cart drawers and cart-stage upsells

3 min

Cart drawers

A cart drawer is a cart panel that slides out over the page.

  • Slide Cart (Slide Cart Drawer) slides the cart out over the current page.
  • Its cart function is free, while its upsell function is paid.
  • The cart drawer opens on the same page as the product.
  • Customers do not need to go to a separate cart page and press back.
  • Cart and checkout feel faster and more fluid.
  • Monster Cart Upsell is a cart app that shows complementary product offers and slide-out cart blocks.
  • It works best when the store has several complementary products.
  • Upcart automates upsell prompts at checkout when the cart value reaches set thresholds.
  • It encourages customers to add items or upgrade to higher bundles, which increases AOV.
  • After a customer clicks add to cart, a product recommendation app can show related items on the cart step to raise AOV.
  • Use it alongside a post-purchase upsell page, not instead of one.
  • The app named for the post-purchase upsell is AfterSell.

Free-gift threshold messages

  • Shopify apps can show a cart message that says how much more is needed to unlock a free gift.
  • Search the Shopify App Store for free gift threshold apps.
  • Monster App adds free gift offers to orders, but its usage fees are high.
  • Operators (people who run the store) may look for a theme with built-in free gift options to replace it.

Kaching Cart

  • Kaching Cart is a Shopify app that controls the cart and checkout entry point (where checkout starts).
  • It adds urgency messages, timers, promotion progress bars, upsells and trust badges (small logos that signal safety).
  • Cart settings sections in the left menu: General, Top bar, Timer, Promotions progress bar, A/B test (testing two versions to see which works better), Edit translations, Product upsell, Products in cart, Side product upsell, Footer, Discount, Sticky Cart and Kaching Upsell.
  • Make sure Cart is enabled is on.
  • Cart mode is set to Previewing items in this setup.
  • Cart is enabled must be switched on before any other cart setting takes effect.
  • Side product upsell: on mobile, the side upsell appears inside the cart drawer and can be dragged into position.
  • Options include Hide upsell if product already in cart, Automatically remove when the cart is empty, Automatically add the upsell product and Show Saved N badge.
  • Upsell toggles (used for shipping protection, an optional insurance add-on for delivery): set Display mode to Standard in checkout button.
  • Fill Text when is not in the cart with ((upsell_toggle_price)) ((upsell_toggle_title)).
  • Fill Text when is in the cart with Includes ((upsell_toggle_price)) ((upsell_toggle_title)).
  • The add-on then appears as a toggle (an on/off switch) next to the checkout button.

Kaching Cart setup sequence

  1. Open Apps > Kaching Cart > Cart settings and confirm Cart is enabled.
  2. Under Top bar, enter a warning message about stock scarcity (running low on items).
  3. Under Timer, set the countdown to 3 minutes and turn on Reset timer when products are added to cart.
  4. Under Promotions progress bar, set the threshold, the reward and any tiers.
  5. Under Trust badges, upload the Trustpilot badge image (Trustpilot is a review site) and set it to show under the checkout button.
  6. Switch off Shipping protection and Footer while testing a product.
  7. Go to Settings > Checkout > Editor > Header > Logo and upload the Trustpilot-formatted logo.
  8. Go to Online Store > Themes > Edit default theme content, search for checkout, find Checkout alternative payment method banner and enter a low-stock message.

Rebuy Smart Cart

10 min

What it is

  • Rebuy Smart Cart is an app that builds a new cart for your online store. The cart is the page where shoppers check what they bought.
  • It is conversion-focused. Conversion means the share of visitors who actually buy, so the cart is designed to raise that share.
  • It replaces the default cart of a Shopify theme. Shopify is the online store software, and a theme is the store's design template.
  • It can be a full-page cart or a slide-out cart. A slide-out cart opens from the side of the screen.
  • It adds features your theme may lack:
    • personalized recommendations (product suggestions picked for each shopper)
    • progress bars (bars that show how close a shopper is to a reward)
    • upsells (offers of a bigger or better version of what they chose)
    • subscription options (repeat delivery purchases)
  • The recommendations block is powered by Rebuy data sources and its AI recommendation engine. The AI engine is software that picks products.
  • You, the merchant, define the rules that decide which products appear.
  • The cart also contains a tiered progress bar and an announcement bar (a banner message at the top).
  • Recommendations can be adjusted in the Rebuy admin, which is Rebuy's control panel.
  • The in-cart cross-sell widget is a box that suggests extra products. It recommends products that are already in the cart.
  • It is created automatically with each new smart cart.
  • It uses Rebuy's AI technology by default.
  • A new smart cart automatically gets a cross-sell widget.

Versions

  • Rebuy no longer lets you create new v1 Smart Carts. "v1" means the old version.
  • Existing v1 carts are marked with a Legacy label, which means old.
  • You can still duplicate a v1 cart and edit the copy.
  • A new cart built from scratch will be v2.
  • The new cart starts as v2.

Creating a Smart Cart (v2)

  1. Open Smart Cart in the left navigation, under Merchandising Widgets.
  2. Click New Smart Cart. The new cart starts as v2.
  3. Choose Single Column or Double Column as the layout.
  4. Click Create.
  5. Configure the cart from top to bottom.
  6. Use the preview to check the mobile and desktop views.
  7. Toggle Live only when the cart is ready. The Live toggle is the switch that shows the cart to real shoppers.
  • Older procedure, same flow with more detail:
    1. In the Rebuy admin, open Smart Cart in the left menu and click New Smart Cart.
    2. Give the smart cart a name.
    3. Choose Single Column or Double Column layout and click Create.
    4. Work through the editor's five sections: Fixed Top Bar, Header, Goal Box, Body, Footer.
    5. In the Fixed Top Bar, adjust the cart title and the login button.
    6. In the Header, click Add and select Announcement Bar.
    7. In the Goal Box, click Add and select Tiered Progress Bar.
    8. In the Body, click into the Cart Items block for its advanced options.
    9. In the Footer, click Add and select Discount Code Input.
    10. Drag any draggable component to reposition it.
    11. Confirm all changes are saved using the top-right control.
    12. Switch the toggle to make the smart cart live.
    13. Test the cart on the storefront.
  • The layout is Single Column or Double Column. You name the cart before you create it.
  • The Smart Cart list shows all drafts. Each draft has Preview, Live toggle and Duplicate options.
  • Use Duplicate to copy an existing cart instead of building a new one.

Post-purchase flows: how they work

1 min
  • In Rebuy, upsells are offers shown right after a customer accepts an earlier offer. Downsells (backup offers, usually cheaper) are shown after the customer declines one. Both live inside a post-purchase flow (the full sequence of offers after payment), and the flow pulls in widgets (the boxes that hold the actual offers).
  • In the Rebuy admin, open the Post Purchase section in the left menu (the side list of pages). It has two areas.
  • Post Purchase Flows is where the complete upsell and downsell sequences are built.
  • Post Purchase Widgets is where the individual offers are created and then referenced (linked) inside flows.
  • The Rebuy admin left menu lists Dashboard, Widgets, Products, Landing Pages, Data Sources, Reports and Integrations, plus a Post Purchase section. Under Post Purchase, the three areas are Post Purchase Flows, Post Purchase Widgets and Post Purchase Analytics. Help Docs sits under Support.
  • Each flow in the Post Purchase Flows list shows a status of LIVE or NOT LIVE. Only a flow marked LIVE is shown to customers (live means real customers see it). Test and draft flows stay NOT LIVE until you switch them on.

Procedure: build a post-purchase flow

1 min
  1. Open the Rebuy admin and go to Post Purchase > Post Purchase Flows.
  2. Open an existing flow or create a new one.
  3. Set the initial offer, the first widget shown to every customer who reaches the checkout confirmation page (the page after payment).
  4. Add a downsell branch (a side path) that appears when the initial offer is declined.
  5. Add an upsell branch that appears when the initial offer is accepted.
  6. Chain (link) further upsells and downsells off any accepted or declined offer to create multi-layer flows (flows with several levels).

When offers appear

1 min
  • The initial offer is shown in the Shopify checkout as soon as the customer clicks the Pay Now button. Widget settings and data sources (where the store gets its product data) decide which exact offer each customer sees.
  • Rebuy flows show offers only on the checkout confirmation page. They add revenue without changing the customer's original order. Keep upsell and downsell offers separate in the flow so the customer always sees the next logical step (the next offer that makes sense).

Example: BOGO initial offer and downsell

1 min
  • A simple initial offer can be a buy-one-get-one deal (BOGO) with 25% off the second product. Its rule (the condition that triggers the offer) can trigger when the cart contains the input product. It is set up as the first widget in the flow.
  • Downsell setup: after a customer declines the 25% BOGO initial offer, show a second BOGO offer at 50% off with the message "Last chance, grab it 50% off". This second offer is a downsell because it appears only after a decline.
