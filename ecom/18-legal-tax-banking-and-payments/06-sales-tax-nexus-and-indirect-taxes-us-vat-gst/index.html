Sales tax, nexus and indirect taxes (US, VAT, GST)

Section 18.6 · 6 of 24 in this chapter · 30 min read

In short

  • A state can only require sales tax from you after you have a real link to it, called nexus. Usually that means passing that state's sales limit, called a threshold.
  • Charge sales tax only in states where you are registered and actually owe it.
  • Most new small stores need a tax form only in their home state at first. Check each state's rules before you sell into it.
  • Set up Shopify's tax settings only after you know where you owe tax. Ask a tax professional before you act.
  • Sales tax (the tax added to each sale) that you collect is not your money. Send it to the state or country that charged it.
More key points
  • Collect sales tax only where the law says you must. Pay it everywhere it applies.
  • Start with your home state. Sign up there and collect tax only on orders from there.
  • Check the exact rules with an accountant before you act, because they change and vary by place.

Nexus: Which States Require Sales Tax

4 min
  • Sales tax nexus is a legal link between your business and a state. It decides where you must collect and remit (send to the state) sales tax. It depends on your business activity and where your customers are.
  • Nexus is a link to another state that obliges you to collect sales tax there. An obligation is something the law requires you to do. A threshold is a sales limit that each state sets. You get nexus by reaching that threshold. Having one customer in a state does not create it.
  • Nexus is created by too many local employees, office locations, or other significant business activities. Consult your 3PL (a third-party logistics company that stores and ships your products) and a local tax specialist. Their goal is to avoid unintended tax liability (tax you owe without planning for it).
  • A business with a physical presence in a state can have nexus there. Physical presence means an office, employees, or ownership in that state. You can have nexus there even below sales thresholds.
  • Sales tax duties follow where your business entity (the legal company itself) has a physical presence.
  • Orders shipped into that state then require tax collection.
  • Competitors in no-sales-tax states may look cheaper to buyers in that state. This alone is not a reason to stop advertising there.
  • Sales tax is based on the shipping address, not the customer's billing address. Collect the tax for the state where the order is delivered.
  • Collect sales tax only where you are legally required to collect it. But pay it wherever it applies.
  • Services that claim to offer free sales tax either pay it themselves or operate in states where collection is not required.
  • Collect sales tax only in states where you have crossed the nexus threshold.
  • Sales tax you collect belongs to the state. So you cannot keep tax you were never required to collect.
  • Most states with no nexus require no collection. Competitors not collecting tax may simply be below the threshold.
  • Not charging sales tax in states where the store has no nexus is normal for small online stores. Customers in those states do not expect to pay it.
  • Sales tax obligations start only when a store has nexus in a state.
  • Sales tax is charged on orders shipped to a state if that state has sales tax. The charge applies to orders in the state where the business is registered (officially signed up with the state's tax office).
  • Do not charge tax in a state where the business has no registration or nexus.
  • A sales tax certificate is the state's official sales tax registration. It gives you permission to collect tax there. If you hold one for one state, only that state's orders need tax collection.
  • Do not assume your home state has no sales tax. The home state is the state where your business is based. Verify its current rules before you conclude you owe nothing.
  • Check your business's tax obligations early, before the first sales.
  • Sales tax or VAT rules depend on where the business is registered and where its customers are located. VAT (value-added tax) is a sales tax used in many countries outside the US.
  • Operating in many states creates sales tax nexus obligations. With nexus in 16 states, a business may need to track sales tax across all of them. Review rules regularly, because they change.
  • A business in one state only needs to register for sales tax in another state if it has nexus there (2024).
  • Check nexus with an official resource before registering.
  • This is general information, not tax advice (2024).
  • Sales tax is usually charged only in the state where the business is located, because thresholds in other states are high.
  • Some states have no sales tax. Sellers below a state's threshold do not need to report or pay there. Report means filing a form that lists your sales.
  • Federal income tax is the national tax on your profit. It is based on overall net profit, which is what remains after costs. It does not require state-by-state registration.
  • Register for sales tax only in states where you have nexus.
  • A non-US seller (a seller based outside the US) with third-party suppliers in other countries usually does not have US nexus.
  • That seller is unlikely to exceed the sales thresholds in the first years.
  • Confirm this with a tax provider for your situation.
  • Whether a non-US company must charge sales tax depends on how the business is structured.
  • Confirm the setup with an accountant before deciding.
  • Each state sets its own nexus rules. Check them before charging tax on orders shipped to that state (2023).
  • Advice from a tax professional is needed before acting (2023).

Economic Nexus Thresholds

3 min

Core rule

  • Economic nexus is a link based on your sales volume (how much you sell), not on your office or staff.
  • Once sales into a state cross that state's economic nexus threshold, you must collect and remit sales tax there.
  • Thresholds are set per state. They are usually based on revenue, which is the total money from your sales.
  • Check the current figure for each state where you ship before acting,.
  • Track revenue by state each year (2025).
  • Most early-stage (new) stores do not reach nexus thresholds. Still, check the nexus levels as sales grow (2023).
  • In most states, a business under $100,000 in annual revenue has no nexus outside its home state. So it needs a sales tax certificate only in its home state (2023).
  • Thresholds are typically high. So most new sellers need a certificate only in their home state.
  • They register elsewhere when sales there reach that state's thresholds.
  • This usually happens after several years of growth.
  • Thresholds differ by state. Only collect and report tax where you are obligated to do so. Report means filing a form that lists your sales.

Nexus notification procedure

Shopify is the online store software you sell through.

  3. Configure the tax settings in Shopify so orders shipping to that state are charged sales tax. To configure means to set up.
  4. Repeat these steps for each state where you reach the threshold.

Certificate basics

Resale certificates

  • Nexus forms only when you exceed your state's yearly revenue threshold, which varies by state (2024).

  • Example: a business registered in New York needs a New York certificate from the start.
  • It needs a New Jersey certificate once it sells over $100,000 per year into New Jersey (2025).
  • If a supplier charges you sales tax but you have not reached the sales threshold (nexus) in that state, provide your resale certificate.
  • You should not owe sales tax (2024).
