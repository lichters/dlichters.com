Ad platform compliance and account setup

Section 18.18 · 18 of 24 in this chapter · 16 min read

In short

  • Check the Google and Meta rules before you run ads or take payments.
  • Use one real company and one matching set of contact details everywhere.
  • Keep a valid payment card on file, and pay every bill on time.
  • Never try to get around a ban. Appeal the right way instead.
This section has 16 parts. Tap one to open it.

Compliance checklist before traffic

1 min
  • Review the compliance checklist (a list of rules your store must follow) before you launch any paid traffic. Traffic means visitors sent to your store. Do not start Google, Bing or Facebook campaigns until this review is done.
  • Running Google, Bing or Facebook campaigns before the review can risk account problems on the ad platforms.
  • The compliance topic covers the legal and platform rules a dropshipping store must follow before launch. Dropshipping means a supplier ships the products to your customers.
  • Check these rules before you run ads or take payments.
  • Review the Google Ads compliance policies (Google's written rules for ads) before launch.
  • Policy issues cause most of the problems at first launch.
  • Fix any issue before you turn on ads.
  • The checklist covers the most common reasons Google suspends Merchant Center or Google Ads accounts. A suspension is a temporary block.
  • The biggest reason is misrepresentation, which means giving false or misleading information.
  • Setting up a parent LLC and a separate brand name (DBA) before you start is generally acceptable. An LLC is a US company type. A DBA is a second business name.
  • Neither one by itself causes Google account problems.

Store requirements

1 min
  • Put a privacy policy on the website. A privacy policy is a page that explains how you handle customer data. Meta's terms of service expect one.
  • Include a complete privacy policy on the site before you set up Google Merchant Center. Merchant Center is Google's tool that holds your product list.
  • The store website must show a phone number, a contact us page and a privacy page. These must be visible to customers.
  • Missing any of these is a common cause of a Merchant Center suspension.
  • Use the same contact information on the store as you registered in Google Merchant Center.
  • Phone, email and contact page options together can be enough. This is true even if no virtual address (a mailing address service, not a real office) is shown. (2023.)
  • Footer social links (the icons at the bottom of the site) pointed to Shopify's social pages or platform home pages. Shopify is the store software you use. They did not point to your own profiles.
  • In one case, these links led to a Merchant Center suspension. (2023.)
  • Replace them with your own profiles, or remove them.
  • Then resubmit for review.
  • A financing or payment-option tab is typically not a reason for suspension. (2023.)
  • Financing options are common on stores without issues. (2023.)
  • Each store's ad campaigns should match the store's selling region and currency. A campaign is one set of ads with its own budget and target.
  • Keeping region, currency and traffic aligned avoids confusion about prices and shipping.

Google Merchant Center requirements

1 min
  • Merchant Center first runs an automatic bot-based review. A bot is software that checks the store without a person.
  • It flags problems for a manual review, which means a person checks them.
  • Tax setup is mandatory for the United States. See the tax section below.

Google Ads billing setup

2 min

Setup sequence

  1. Select the billing country, time zone and currency.
  2. Check the currency carefully before you submit the billing information.
  3. It cannot be changed later.
  4. Customer info: choose Individual or Business.
  5. For a business, enter the business name and business address.
  6. Create a payments profile. Select Individual or Organization, then add a valid credit card.
  7. A temporary $50 authorization (a hold on the card) may appear.
  8. Payment method: choose automatic payments and enter a credit card.
  9. Automatic billing means you do not pay invoices by hand each cycle. It works like a phone bill.
  10. Decline Google Ads strategist support. Strategists are sales representatives, not strategic advisors.
  11. You can add multiple payment methods later.
  12. The billing page shows the account information and payment methods in one place.

Alternative steps

  1. Open Tools and settings (the cogwheel, top right). Then go to Billing, then Billing info.
  2. Enter the account name, the business address and the credit or debit card that will pay for the ads.
  3. Campaigns can be built before billing is added.
  4. Ads cannot run until billing details exist.

Rules and states

  • The Google Ads billing country must match the country where the business operates. It is not the country where the owner lives.
  • For a US business, select United States. For a UK business, select United Kingdom.
  • A new ad account shows no payment methods and no recent spending.
  • It shows an outstanding balance (money still owed) of $0.00. This stays until you add a payment method in Billing & payments.
  • Keep a valid payment card on file in Google Ads.
  • A declined bill can lock the account. Reopening it may require contacting Google directly. (2025.)
  • A credit, such as a 500 dollar Google credit, usually appears on its own after the account processes it. (2024.)
  • If it does not appear, contact Google Ads support. (2024.)
  • To restart a business in a niche (a narrow product market) with suspended Google accounts, call the Google Ads policy support number shown in your account. (2023.)
  • Ask for a live review. (2023.)

Google Merchant Center tax settings (US)

2 min
  • Tax setup in Merchant Center is mandatory for the United States.
  • For sellers without a US business address, Google proposes a default tax setup. You can save it without entering US sales tax rates.
  • Tax can also be submitted through the product feed (the file that carries your product list).
  • Tax details in Google Merchant Center must match the tax rates set in Shopify settings exactly.
  • For a US-based dropshipping store, copy the tax configuration used in Shopify into Google Merchant Center's tax setting.
  • The state where the business is registered decides which tax nexus applies. Nexus is the link to a state that makes you collect tax there.
  • In the setup described, no nexus is configured for all states. (2023 or undated.)

Sales tax steps

  1. Go to Merchant Center, then Tax settings, then Sales tax.
  2. Turn on custom sales tax only for the states where the business must collect tax.
  3. Enter each state's rate as a percentage. Example: 7 for a 7% rate.
  4. Turn off all other states, then click Save.
  5. Check the rates with a tax advisor before saving.

Which setup applies

Situation What to set Ids
No US business address Google's default tax setup can be saved without US rates, or tax can be submitted through the product feed.
Business must collect tax in some states Custom sales tax on only for those states, rate as a percentage, all other states off, then Save.
Shopify-matched setup, business registered in a state Copy the Shopify tax configuration; the registration state determines the nexus.
  • US sales tax for Shopping ads (Google's product ads) applies in all states.
  • Buy on Google requires collecting and remitting (sending to the state) tax in Florida, Kansas and Missouri.
  • Whether a seller must collect tax depends on the state where the business is registered.
  • The sample US setup asks "Is shipping and handling taxable?" The answer shown is No.
  • The link "Need more tax options? Switch to advanced settings" opens the advanced tax configuration.

Google suspension appeals

1 min

Inventory mistake

Inventory means the stock you have available to sell.

  1. Correct the inventory. Restock the item, or remove products that will not be in stock from the product feed.
  2. Make sure the feed no longer lists items customers cannot buy.
  3. Request a review from inside Google Merchant Center.
  4. If the review is denied, contact Google Ads support by phone or live chat.
  5. You must be logged in to reach that support.

Misrepresentation (2023)

  • A misrepresentation suspension can be appealed by showing corrected changes.
  • The appeal should list the changes made.
  • It should explain what was wrong.
  • It should cite (point to) Google's policy documentation.
  1. Identify what was not compliant in the store.
  2. Make the required changes.
  3. Review Google's full policy documentation and confirm the store complies.
  4. Submit the changes with a clear explanation and links to the policy pages.

Timing and resubmission (2023)

  • Google appeals about a Merchant Center suspension usually get a response in one to two weeks.
  • A failed appeal can be submitted again.
  • Fix the cause before you appeal, so the first appeal succeeds.

Meta Business Manager verification

2 min

Business Manager is Meta's tool for running ads and managing business assets.

Documents and profile

  • Verify the Business Manager with proper documentation.
  • The documents are a company registration certificate, a certificate of incorporation, a business address and contact details.
  • Fill out all required fields in the business profile.
  • Add the business documents, such as a certificate of incorporation and business address.
  • Complete all contact details on the business page.
  • An unverified or incomplete business profile can look like a scam to Meta.
  • Documents that support verification include LLC or Ltd registration papers. Ltd is a UK company type.
  • A business registered in one country can still run ads that target other countries.
  • Verification only checks that a legitimate business is advertising.
  • Verification needs a registered business with complete business details.
  • Verifying it significantly reduces the chance that the ad account is banned or restricted.
  • Entering registered business details builds trust with Meta.
  • It lowers the chance of bans.
  • A beginner without a registered business may use personal information at first.
  • A proper business setup is recommended.

One company, several accounts

  • Register one company and use it to verify all accounts, including Facebook and PayPal.
  • The company name does not need to match the product or the store domain. A domain is your store's web address.
  • One company can verify several connected accounts, such as PayPal, a Facebook account and Shopify Payments. Shopify Payments is Shopify's built-in payment service.
  • The company name does not have to match the store names.
  • For every new store, the domain must be verified again in the ad account.

Location and country of registration (sources differ)

  • The business location does not matter for verification.
  • Verification needs to show that the business is registered and legitimate.
  • For verifying a US business, the ad account asks for the document matching the country where the business is registered.
  • Examples: a Corporation document from Companies House (the UK company registry) or HMRC (the UK tax office), or a GST certificate in Australia. GST is Australia's sales tax.
  • Have the registration certificate ready. Otherwise Facebook will ask why it is missing.
  • Apply both rules. The business location and the ads' target countries do not limit verification.
  • But the document you submit must match the country where the business is registered.

Account access

  • Use an email address you check regularly for the Business Manager.
  • Verification codes and one-time passwords (single-use login codes) are sent there.
  • Losing access to that inbox can lock you out of the account.
  • Facebook accounts can be banned for reasons outside the seller's control.
  • One reason is ownership verification issues with business documents.
  • One seller lost an account a week after launching a winning product.
  • The cause was conflicting document requirements from Facebook.

Meta ad policy and rejections

1 min

Policy training

  • To find the free Facebook Advertising Policy Blueprint training, go to the Policy, Data Privacy and Ads section of Meta's advertising resources.
  • The beginner training takes about 15 to 20 minutes.
  • It gives a certificate on completion.
  • Keep the certificate to show in appeals that the advertiser knows Meta's policies.

Rejected ad appeal

A rejected ad is an ad Meta has refused to run.

  1. Read the rejection reason and the review points Facebook gives.
  2. Remove or rephrase any flagged claim, skin exposure or comparison visual. Flagged means marked by the review. A comparison visual is a picture that compares products.
  3. Submit an appeal if the ad did not break a rule.
  4. Appeals often succeed for ads with no real issue.
  5. Relaunch the adjusted version after the appeal.

Other policy rules

  • Ad platforms can detect a creative that was previously used in a suspended account. A creative is the ad itself: its picture, video or text.
  • Reusing it may get it taken down.
  • Resolve the policy issue at the root.
  • Do not rely on re-uploading creatives.
  • Meta allows fictional characters on Facebook pages only if the character is clearly a brand persona. A persona is a made-up identity for a brand.
  • The character must not impersonate a real, identifiable person.
  • Standard ad policy still applies to every ad run from the page. (2026.)

Meta billing and payment setup

1 min
  • Add payment info to the new ad account.
  • Select the country first.
  • Then choose a payment method: debit card, credit card, PayPal or online banking.
  • Enter the details.
  • The account is ready to run ads once payment info is added.
  • Keep a valid credit card on file with Meta.
  • Pay all invoices.
  • Unpaid balances are another reason ad accounts get restricted.
  • Wise is flagged and declined by Facebook, because Wise is not a bank. Wise is an online money service.
  • Avoid using Wise to pay Facebook ads.
  • Paying Facebook in a local currency, such as CAD, is allowed.
  • It makes results harder to track, because the local currency is weaker.
  • Many marketers track metrics (the numbers that measure results) in USD for comparison.

Late payments and restricted accounts

1 min
  • Late or unpaid ad account bills are a common reason for ad account disabling (switching off).
  • Repeated overdue payments, around 3-4 times, can make Meta treat the account as suspicious.
  • So pay due amounts on time.
  • Pay Facebook ad account bills on time.
  • Late payment can trigger spend limits, such as a daily cap of 50 USD.
  • Logging into a Facebook ad account from a different personal account can cause Facebook to restrict the ad account's automatic payments.
  • Switching between accounts can cause the same restriction.
  • This leads to failed payments.
  • Failed payments can cap the daily budget and freeze ad delivery.

Recovering a restricted account after a failed payment

  1. Pay the open balance through Facebook support chat. A support agent opens the payment for you.
  2. Make small advance payments to rebuild payment history.
  3. Confirm that the spending limit and daily budget have been restored.
  • After a payment failure, a Facebook account's spending limit can be cut to a low amount.
  • One seller's limit went from 300 to 50 USD.
  • Support may only promise an increase without applying it.
  • So check the limit yourself after the payment is cleared.

Microsoft Ads

1 min

Microsoft Ads is Microsoft's search ad platform.

  • If a new account is automatically suspended for "egregious violations" (serious rule breaks), file an appeal as instructed by Microsoft support.
  • Do not create duplicate accounts or try different credentials.
  • Duplicate accounts are flagged as a violation of Microsoft's policy.
  • Wait for the appeal to be approved and the account reinstated (turned back on).

TikTok payments

2 min

Account and tax requirements

  • TikTok payments may not ask for a billing address.
  • If one is requested, a US address is required.
  • US virtual cards (card numbers made for online payments) or PayPal can be used as payment methods in that case.
  • Owning an LLC alone may not be enough for a non-US seller to open a TikTok Shop.
  • A tax identification number is often required.
  • Check the exact tax ID requirement for your country with a local tax advisor before applying.
  • A verified TikTok Business Center account usually requires a business account plus legitimate business documents. Business Center is TikTok's business tool.
  • It often needs an EIN, which is a US tax number for businesses.
  • Receiving proposals requires no verification.
  • Starting proposals (sending offers) does require verification.
  • You need a bank account or a payment method such as PayPal to pay creators. Creators are people who make content for your brand.
  • Confirm first whether TikTok takes a commission on Business Center deals.
  • This was not verified.

  • The sources differ on the default.
  • Automatic is recommended in one place, while manual is recommended for scaling (growing your ad spend).
  • Use automatic for ordinary spend.
  • Use manual when budgets are high or campaigns are scaling.

Linking accounts

  • Linking an account may prompt a switch from a personal to a business account.
  • One person reported that copyrighted sounds (music or audio protected by copyright) stop being usable after such a switch.
  • This was described as a risk and was not verified.
  • Check the current platform rules before linking.

New ad account payment setup

1 min
  • Make the first four payments with a debit card on a new ad account.
  • Early credit card use on a new account was linked to flagged behavior. Flagged means marked as risky.
  • Credit cards are better used later, for their billing window. A billing window is the time between a purchase and the bill's due date.
  • Use a new bank account and payment method dedicated to each business.
  • A card tied to a business address in a different country than the business operates in can be flagged as potentially fraudulent.
  • The same goes for a card whose time zone and country differ from the Google account settings.
  • Cards already flagged elsewhere or with past failed payments can also trigger suspension.
  • The customer-facing billing descriptor (the statement name on the card) must be entered together with a number field.
  • Do not use a personal phone number there.
  • Customers may call that number heavily if the product goes viral (spreads fast online).
  • Use a separate business or alternate number.
  • For Ads > Billing, enter the business details and click Save.
  • Then add a credit card, debit card or virtual card.
  • PayPal is not accepted as a payment method.

Agency accounts

1 min

An agency account is an ad account run through a third-party agency.

  • Setting up an agency account costs $400+ per month.
  • It is named as one recovery option for a banned Business Manager.
  • With an agency ad account, you do not connect your own payment card.
  • You top up the account by bank transfer or crypto (digital currency) instead.
  • ROAS Agency accepts funding by international bank transfer or crypto only. ROAS means return on ad spend, the sales earned for each dollar spent on ads.
  • Top-ups are arranged through WhatsApp with the agency contact.
  • The account is ready for ads only after the first top-up has been made. (2026.)
  • Agency accounts are listed as a workaround (a way around a problem) for bans.
  • The same source advises against circumventing (getting around) platform bans.
  • It advises against doing this with agency accounts, new accounts or VPN workarounds.

VPN and account safety

1 min

A VPN is a tool that changes your apparent location.

  • A VPN is only needed when researching search results from a different country.
  • It is not required for normal business operation.
  • In one case, a TikTok ad account suspension was linked to using a VPN.
  • Check your connection and login setup before managing ads.
  • Once the cause is found and removed, the ads account can be run again without the same problem.
  • Circumventing platform bans (Google, Facebook, TikTok) using agency accounts, new accounts or VPN workarounds is not recommended.
  • The source advised against it in general.
  • Keep one consistent login and contact setup for each account.
  • Do not switch between personal accounts when logging into an ad account.

Bans and recovery

1 min

Facebook business manager

  • If the business is banned and ID-verified, account recovery is difficult.
  • The options named in the source are: using a friend's account, setting up an agency account ($400+ per month), or using third-party unlock services (Fiverr, a freelance marketplace website).
  • The same source advises against circumventing bans in general.
  • When a Facebook Business Manager account is permanently banned, the source describes this procedure:
    1. Contact a Fiverr seller who specializes in business manager rental.
    2. Request new account access.
    3. Set up ads in the new account.
  • Following a correct process for launching ads and setting up the Business Manager reduces the ban risk.
  • A banned account can be recovered by following a specific appeal process.
  • Several people who followed the process reported few bans.

Other causes

  • Reusing a creative from a suspended account can get it taken down.
  • Resolve the root policy issue instead.
  • Suspensions for circumventing systems are often linked to an earlier account.
  • Call the Google Ads policy support number to request a live review. (2023.)
