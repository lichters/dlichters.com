Forming the entity and registering the business

Section 18.2 · 2 of 24 in this chapter · 15 min read

In short

  • Pick an LLC (a company type that keeps business money apart from your own) unless you have almost nothing to lose.
  • Get a free EIN (a US tax number for your business) directly from the IRS website. Do not pay anyone for it.
  • Form the LLC first. The EIN application needs the LLC name.
  • Get a sales tax certificate (a state permit to collect sales tax) only if your state has sales tax.
  • Decline every upsell (an extra paid add-on offered at checkout). The basic LLC costs only the state fee.
This section has 19 parts. Tap one to open it.

Order of registration

1 min
  • Choose the legal form first: sole proprietor (one person runs the business, with no separate company), LLC, or S-Corp (a company type with its own tax rules).
  • A sales tax certificate is needed only where the seller must collect sales tax.
  • Form the LLC before you apply for the EIN. The EIN application uses the LLC name.
  • Register early, even before the business earns money.
  • The LLC, EIN and any sales tax certificate will be needed anyway. Doing it early avoids later delays.
  • It also makes the business look more professional to suppliers (companies that sell you products).
  • The business name and the store name can differ.
  • A business name is often generic. It is not tied to one niche (one specific product topic).
  • Do not set up ad accounts (the accounts used to run online ads) until supplier products are on the store.
  • While you wait for the EIN or company paperwork, work on supplier research and understanding your niche.

Full procedure: US LLC with EIN and sales tax certificate

1 min
  1. Decide between a sole proprietorship (one person, no separate company) and an LLC. Choose an LLC unless there is almost nothing to lose.
  2. Choose a generic LLC name that is not the store name.
  3. Form the LLC online in the state where you are located. Use LegalZoom's $0 basic option (an online service that files company papers) plus the state filing fee (the government fee, no more than $97). Decline all upsell offers.
  4. Confirm with LegalZoom, or the chosen service, that the LLC name is available and belongs to you.
  5. Apply for an EIN on the IRS website (the US federal tax office) using the "Apply for an EIN" option. Answer the questions. This usually takes 5 to 10 minutes. You receive a 9-digit number.
  6. Only if your state has sales tax: search Google for "[your state] sales tax certificate", open the state's.gov sales and use tax registration page, choose to register online, and enter the LLC name.
  7. Download the PDF that shows your sales tax certificate number. If none is offered, the certificate is sent by mail.
  8. Use the LLC name, EIN and sales tax certificate when registering with suppliers.

Step-by-step details

1 min
  • The EIN can usually be requested at the same time as the LLC, or right after the state approves it.
  • The exact timing depends on the filing service (the company that files papers for you).
  • The EIN is a unique tax number for the business.
  • It is needed for Stripe (an online payment service), Mercury (an online business bank) and most platforms.
  • Use the EIN as the business tax number for Stripe, Mercury and other platforms.
  • Open the business bank accounts only after the LLC and EIN are in place.
  • Entity (the legal company), tax ID (a number used for taxes) and bank setup should be done before supplier applications.
  • Suppliers ask for the business name, business structure, registration and tax numbers.
  • Missing these can delay progress by about a week while government registrations are processed.
  • Keep the EIN and sales tax certificate under the LLC's name, not the store name.

Choosing the legal form

1 min
  • Sole proprietor or LLC: choose an LLC unless there is almost nothing to lose.
  • Before the EIN and sales tax certificate, get advice from an accountant (a tax expert) on the structure that fits you.
  • Track every business expense from the start. This way, all deductible costs (costs that lower your tax) can be written off.
  • Describe the business as "online retail" or similar.
  • No physical location is required in the formation documents.
  • An S-corp can be set up with an accountant after the EIN and LLC are in place.
  • Many owners set up the EIN, LLC and S-corp as a package.
  • Converting a sole proprietorship to an LLC involves a DBA (a second business name, "doing business as"), an EIN and bank accounts. Consult a CPA (certified public accountant, a licensed tax expert) or the state where the DBA was registered. (2024)
  • One call did not give the steps. (2024)

Forming an LLC: DIY, services and fees

4 min
  • Forming an LLC means filing articles of organization (the main paper that creates the LLC) with your state.
  • This is a formal registration step, separate from any federal number.
  • It can usually be done without a paid service. Many online ads push paid helpers.
  • You can form a business entity yourself.
  • Paying someone is optional. Many owners pay only after the business is making money.
  • An LLC and EIN can be set up directly online for little cost. (2023)
  • Third-party formation services (companies that do the paperwork for you) often charge about $99. Then they upsell extra services. (2023)
  • An LLC filed online through LegalZoom costs about $99 and is optional.
  • It keeps business and personal funds separated from the start.
  • Do not pay a service just to obtain a document you can file yourself.
  • Compare quotes (price offers) before paying.
  • A service that forms LLCs for a monthly fee charges only the state filing fee.
  • Typical formation services add a markup (an extra price on top) of roughly $100 to $150.
  • Forming a business entity directly through a local government website typically costs about $100 to $300 at most.
  • Paying third-party middlemen to form a legal entity abroad can be far more expensive than the official government registration website.
  • Use the government portal whenever it is available.
  • Use the official state website to apply for a business entity or the state's reseller certificate (a document that lets you buy goods without paying sales tax, because you resell them). (2023)
  • A virtual address (a mailing address that is not your home or office) can be used where needed for business documents. (2023)
  • Formation services usually handle the state filing for you.
  • Confirm with the service what is included.

Naming the entity

1 min
  • Choose a general business name that does not tie you to a specific niche, for example "AM Stores LLC". (2023)
  • All domain names (web addresses) for different niches can be owned by the same LLC. (2023)
  • When contacting suppliers, reference the LLC name, not individual niches. (2023)
  • Confirm that the LLC name is available and belongs to you before you apply for the EIN.

Obtaining the EIN

1 min
  • The EIN is free when requested directly from irs.gov during IRS business hours.
  • Have it ready for suppliers.
  • Apply on the IRS website, not through paid third-party sites.
  • A formation service may charge extra for the EIN as an add-on.
  • Add it when placing the LLC order if you use the service.
  • The EIN can usually be requested at the same time as the LLC or right after the state approves it.

Sales tax certificate (state level)

2 min
  • A sales tax certificate is needed only in the state where the business is registered.
  • It is needed only if that state has sales tax.
  • There is no single federal source.
  • It is obtained through the state's government website.
  • States that charge sales tax require a state sales tax certificate.
  • Get it early rather than late.
  • Depending on your state, you may need a sales tax certificate.
  • It can be applied for online and usually arrives within a few days.
  • Not every supplier asks for it at the start.
  • Search for the state name with "sales tax certificate" or "sales tax permit" to see whether your state requires one.
  • Use only the official.gov website.
  • Commercial.com sites offer to file the same registration for a fee. It can be done directly and quickly online.
  • After online registration, the state usually provides a downloadable PDF with the certificate number.
  • If no PDF is provided, the state mails the certificate.
  • Example, North Carolina: the sales and use tax registration page has an online form that asks for the LLC name.
  • There is no fee for the certificate of registration.
  • To get a certificate: search Google for your state's name with "sales tax certificate". Open the state's official online form and fill it out. (2024)
  • It usually costs $0 and is often issued online within about a day. (2024)
  • In the US, sales tax permits are issued by each state.
  • Many states let you apply online through the state government's website.
  • The sales tax certificate is only required in cases where the seller must collect sales tax.
  • Check your state at taxjar.com/states (a site that lists each state's tax rules). Click your home state and read the registration FAQ (frequently asked questions). This shows whether you need a sales tax certificate there.
  • After getting a seller's permit (the state's sales tax permit) in your home state, register separately for any sales and use tax obligations.
  • Also register separately for payroll (paying employees) if you will pay employees.
  • These registrations are separate from the seller's permit itself.
  • Apply for a sales tax certificate once the niche is picked.
  • It can often be done online in a short time, depending on the state.

Business licenses and state-specific rules

1 min
  • Business license and permit requirements differ by state in the US.
  • Look up the rules for the state where you operate. Do not copy another state's steps.
  • A business license may be requested by suppliers. (2023)
  • If the home state issues no license, ask the supplier whether an alternative form can be used instead. (2023)
  • Business license filing services can charge very different fees for the same filing.
  • One quote was $250 and another was $100 (2020 example).

California and out-of-state entities

1 min
  • If you live in California but incorporate in another state (for example a Wyoming S-Corp), California requires you to register as a foreign business entity (a company from another state, registered in California). (2024)
  • California has the strictest state tax requirements. (2024)
  • Consult the Source A Discord community (an online chat group) for specific steps. This varies by situation. (2024)

S-corp election

1 min
  • The S-corp election (IRS Form 2553, the tax form that chooses S-corp status) can be filed early. (2025)
  • It has no effect until the business has revenue (money coming in from sales). (2025)
  • When to file is a personal choice. (2025)
  • An S-corp can be set up with an accountant after the EIN and LLC are in place.
  • Converting a sole proprietorship to an S-corp means these updates:
    • Update the legal name and tax details with Shopify (the online store software).
    • Update the legal details with each supplier account.
    • Update the financial accounts that hold the business's money.
    • Keep a list of who has confirmed the change and who is still pending.
    • Some accounts respond slowly.

Banking and payments timing

1 min
  • Open bank accounts after the LLC and EIN are in place.
  • Identify which banking options you will use.
  • Open the business accounts early.
  • Before the store goes live, open a business checking account, a business savings account and a business credit card. (2025)
  • Do this in that order, after registration and EIN. (2025)
  • Set up a business PayPal account (an online payment account) linked to the business bank account.
  • Ask PayPal support to enable auto sweep (automatic transfer of your PayPal money into your bank).
  • Use the EIN as the business tax number for Stripe and Mercury.

Non-US equivalents (Australia)

1 min
  • In Australia, a sole trader (one person running the business) needs an ABN (Australian Business Number).
  • A company needs an ACN (Australian Company Number).
  • Other countries have an equivalent business number. It identifies the business for tax purposes.
  • Do business in the country where you live.
  • If that is not possible, do it in the US.
  • Registering as a sole trader and getting an ABN online costs about 36 Australian dollars.
  • Forming a company with an accountant's help typically costs between 1,500 and 2,000 Australian dollars.
  • An Australian sole trader needs an ABN. It is obtained through a short online form and issued quickly.
  • A company gets an ACN from its accountant when it is registered.
  • Both numbers may be required on supplier documents.
  • A sole trader can begin trading as soon as the ABN is issued online.
  • No further registration is needed before the first sales.
  • Company formation usually takes longer and involves an accountant.
  • Government registrations can take one to two weeks to process.
  • Start them early. Have your business name, company name, company number and tax ID ready when you contact suppliers.
  • In Australia, a.com.au domain (an Australian web address ending) requires a registered Australian business, identified by an ABN.
  • Buying an ABN is one route to obtain that domain.

Legal and banking setup checklist (Australia, in order)

  1. Decide the country where you will do business.
  2. Start the process to form the business entity (the legal company) now. For a sole trader, use the government registration page for your country (in Australia, apply online for an ABN). For a company, work through the setup with an accountant.
  3. Obtain the tax ID required in your country (in Australia, an ABN or an ACN).
  4. Start looking for a business accountant now, even if you are not using one yet. Ask people you know for recommendations.
  5. Identify which banking options you will use and open the business accounts now.

Tax setup checklist (US e-commerce)

1 min
  1. Decide which business structure fits you (sole proprietor, LLC or S-Corp). Get an accountant's advice on it.
  2. Track every business expense from the start, so all deductible costs can be written off.
  3. Form the business type you chose.
  4. Get a free EIN directly from irs.gov during IRS business hours. Have it ready for suppliers.
  5. Check your home state at taxjar.com/states to see whether you need a sales tax certificate there.

Case: US company (Wyoming LLC, reported)

1 min
  • Formation cost around 300 to 400 USD. Formation time was about 2 to 3 weeks.
  • Filing by a CPA cost about 200 USD at most. It took under 3 to 4 days.
  • Stripe payouts (money Stripe sends to your bank) came within 1 to 2 days.
  • The first year reached about 300,000 USD in revenue. Zero taxes were reported as paid under that structure.
