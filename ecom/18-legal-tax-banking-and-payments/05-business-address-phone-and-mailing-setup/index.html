Business address, phone and mailing setup

Section 18.5 · 5 of 24 in this chapter · 12 min read

In short

  • Keep your home address off public company records and your website. A UPS Store or virtual mailbox address does this.
  • Use the same address everywhere: company papers, bank, Shopify and Google.
  • Check first: payment services such as Shopify Payments and Stripe may refuse virtual addresses.
  • Expect customers to call to check that you are a real business.

Business Address Options

1 min

Address Types Compared

2 min
  • A UPS Store mailbox gives you a business address with a suite number (a unit number, like an apartment number) instead of a P.O. Box (a mailbox at the post office). Suppliers see it as more professional. The cost is about $320 USD per year.
  • A mailbox suite (a numbered mailbox unit) at a UPS Store costs about $100 to $150 per year. It keeps your home address off the public record.
  • A non-US seller who needs a US address can use a UPS Store location set up remotely (from another country). The cheapest option is about $20 per quarter (every three months), depending on the location.
  • A virtual business address is the recommended option over a P.O. Box (2021 advice).
  • A virtual mailbox is a rented mailbox at a real street address. It has a suite number.
  • It receives mail and either forwards it to you (sends it on to you) or holds it for pickup.
  • A P.O. Box can be used for business mail, alongside a separate address on the website.
  • Business address options are: a P.O. Box, a UPS Store-style address with a suite number, or a virtual mailbox. Many LLC (limited liability company, a company type that keeps personal and business money apart) formation services also sell an address for a yearly fee.
  • A virtual address from some providers looks like a street address followed by a mailbox number, such as "#406". Use the full address line exactly as the provider lists it, on forms and on the website.
  • Mailbox services such as PakMail can provide a business address when a UPS Store does not respond. Check their terms for business address use before signing up.
  • Earth Class Mail receives, scans (makes digital copies of) and forwards a business's physical mail. It can support a US address for a business without a physical office.
  • PakMail receives parcels and letters at a US address and forwards them to the owner.
  • UPS Store addresses provide mail forwarding and a professional business location without maintaining physical premises (without renting your own office or shop).
  • A virtual office address in another city, a registered company address, or a correspondence address (an address used only for letters) are all options for account verification (proving who you are to a platform).
  • One seller reported about $20 for a virtual address and about $120 per year for a Swiss address service.
  • Whether such addresses are accepted depends on the country and the platform's rules.

Anytime Mailbox (US)

1 min
  • Anytime Mailbox (anytimemailbox.com) lists US locations by state and city. Browse the state, then pick a city or address and select a plan.
  • The basic US plan costs US$ 9.99 per month.
  • The site advertises rates starting at US$ 5.99 per month across 1,127 locations.
  • It gives a real street address for a registered business address (the official address on company papers).
  • It includes mail forwarding, check deposit (paying in paper checks for you), and open-and-scan mail (they open your mail and scan it).
  • The lowest-cost plan is enough because the address is not used as a mail service.

Gordon Executive Centre

1 min
  • Gordon Executive Centre has a one-month notice period (the time you must warn before leaving) after the initial 1, 3 or 6 month term.
  • Renewals are automatic. You are billed again for the same number of months you signed up for.
  • A Gordon business address includes up to five names, such as a company name, a trading name (the name you trade under) and the names of two principals (owners or directors).
  • In this example, that is four names.
  • Each additional name costs 10 per month.
  • Or a block of five names costs 35 per month, plus GST (goods and services tax, a sales tax added on top).

Australia

1 min
  • Companies in Australia are registered federally (at national level), not per state.
  • The virtual address can be in any city in the country.
  • It is usually placed in a capital city, such as the Sydney CBD (central business district) or Brisbane.
  • Australian virtual business addresses typically cost AUD 30 to 40 per month.
  • National providers with offices in every city often cost around AUD 50 per month.
  • These figures are in local currency context and must be verified before use.
  • To find a cheap Australian virtual address:
    1. Search for "virtual mailing address" plus the city name.
    2. Compare the local providers.
    3. Pick one with a location in your own state. This avoids time-zone confusion (different clock times) and customers asking whether you are in the wrong city.

Choosing Between Home and Virtual Address

1 min
  • A home address can also be the business address. Running a business from a non-business premises (a place that is not a business site) is acceptable. The choice between home and virtual address is personal.
  • A home address on supplier agreements (contracts with suppliers) is acceptable if customers do not send mail to the business. Changing it is optional in that case. [L963.124]
  • Do not use a home address as the business registration address. Registrations are published online and can be found by searching the business name.
  • A P.O. Box or business address can be used as the LLC address to keep your home address off documents.
  • Use a virtual business address so your home address does not appear on the store. A mailbox at a UPS Store does this.
  • Use a business address (such as a UPS Store suite address) on your website, supplier forms and documents instead of your home address. This is for privacy and professionalism.
  • Do not advertise the address as a physical retail location or invite customers to visit.
  • Digital nomads (people who work while travelling) who need a US business address can choose a tax-friendly state such as Texas, Delaware, Nevada or Florida. They can use a UPS Store address in that state.
  • In the UK, a company's registered office (official address) can be changed at Companies House (the UK company register) when a new one is set up.
  • The speaker recommended a dedicated office address rather than a personal address. This keeps private information out of public records.

Matching Addresses Across Entity (Legal Company), Bank, Shopify and Merchant Center (Google's Product Listing Tool)

2 min
  • Use the virtual address in the business bank account as well if the LLC is linked to it. Keeping them synchronized (always matching) avoids mismatches. [L963.123]
  • Update your business bank account with the new address once you have one. This way the bank records match your business details. [L696.010]
  • A UPS Store or virtual address can be the business address for an LLC and for Shopify. [L971.069]
  • The address must be consistent across all business documents. [L971.069]
  • A virtual address may cause problems with Google Business Profile (your free business listing on Google). [L971.069]
  • Do not use a registered address in a different state or city from where you actually operate.
  • Customers and suppliers may ask questions. Calls may come in at inconvenient hours across time zones.
  • Choose a provider in the same state.
  • In the US, the virtual address should be in the state where the company is registered. A Nevada LLC should use a Nevada address.
  • This avoids supplier questions caused by mismatched registration and postal addresses (where mail is delivered).
  • If the business operates from outside the US but is based in the US, the company is often formed in a state such as Wyoming.
  • The registered business address should be in that same state.
  • Address and tax settings: a PMB (Private Mailbox, a mailbox rented at a shipping store) or mail-forwarding address may cause Merchant Center to identify the store as being in a different region.
  • Contact Merchant Center support to correct country settings. (2024 advice.) [L856.082]
  • Remove an old mailing address from business accounts once it is no longer used.
  • Replace it with the current business address the store uses. [L857.065]
  • An online formation service can form an LLC and provide a virtual address.
  • A mailbox service at a UPS Store can serve as a business address. [L468.150]
  • The address and phone number entered during Shopify sign-up are used only for the Shopify account and billing (what Shopify charges you).
  • Customers do not see them.
  • The phone number can be a mobile or a business number.
  • Google account setup asks for two contact details.
  • A support email (the address customers write to for help) with a business address is a common combination.

Verification Risks for Payments

1 min
  • A virtual business address is often not accepted by Shopify Payments (Shopify's built-in payment service) or Stripe (a payment service) for verification (proof that your business is real).
  • Stripe may require a physical address that proves the company exists.
  • Buying a virtual address from an unknown online provider can block payment setup.
  • Shopify may ask for proof of address during payment verification.
  • Before choosing an address service, confirm the provider supplies documents that payment platforms accept.
  • Check this before setting up Shopify Payments or Stripe.
  • Setting up a company in a country (for example the US or UK) and using its address to verify Shopify Payments, Stripe or PayPal is a common path.
  • Check the address type and provider with a qualified professional, because payment verification rules can change.
  • If a supplier rejects a UPS or P.O. Box address as a business address, ask whether the supplier accepts a P.O. Box or a virtual forwarding address.
  • Some suppliers and states may require a registered address. Confirm with a qualified advisor. [L854.089]

Google Ads and Website Address Requirements

1 min
  • A business address shown on the website is needed for suppliers and some advertising platforms.
  • Google Ads (Google's paid advertising service) requires a visible postal address on the site before it approves advertising.
  • A virtual office address is acceptable if you do not want to use your home address.
  • A virtual address is used only as a registered business and contact address.
  • Expected mail volume is near zero, so the provider's service quality matters little.
  • The address should look professional and exist as a real street address.
  • Pick the most basic plan and sign up before moving on to the next setup step.
  • The full setup procedure:
    1. Decide whether to use the home address or a virtual office address.
    2. If using a virtual address in the US, pick the state where the company is registered. Then find a location there on anytimemailbox.com.
    3. If in Australia, search for a virtual address provider in your own state, preferably in the capital city.
    4. Select the most basic plan and sign up before moving on to the next setup step.

Business Phone Numbers

1 min
  • A landline (a phone wired to a wall) or mobile number can both be used as a business contact number on Shopify.
  • There is no preference or requirement. [L245.067]
  • Phone numbers on the website build trust without overloading calls.
  • A visible phone number in the header (the top of every page) increases conversion rates (the share of visitors who buy) and buyer confidence, especially for expensive products. [L268.078]
  • Most incoming calls are customers checking that you are a legitimate business before ordering. They are not complaints or rejections. [L268.078]
  • Do not expect to be on the phone all day. [L268.078]
  • Phone number alternatives for business addresses:
    • Google Voice and Skype offer inexpensive local or toll-free (free for the caller) numbers.
    • Mighty Call provides professional toll-free numbers with a 50 percent discount for the first three months (code provided with partnership deal). [L243.023]
  • A virtual business phone number rings through an app on an existing phone.
  • No second device is needed, and it supports outbound calls (calls you make).
  • Use a local business address such as a UPS Store address and skip e-fax (faxes sent over the internet, with no fax machine).
  • A fax number is rarely needed for a dropshipping store (you sell products, and a supplier ships them to customers).

Business Email

1 min
  • Use a business email for corporate communications (official company messages) and for linking bank accounts and tools, not a personal email.
  • This lets the business be sold cleanly later.

Google Workspace (Paid Business Email and Apps) and Microsoft Subscriptions

1 min
  • Enable two-step verification (a login that also asks for a code sent to your phone) on your Google Workspace account. This protects against unauthorized access. [L119.010]
  • Google Workspace Business Starter Plan costs $7 per month. [L125.002]
  • Google Workspace DNS setup: allow up to 72 hours for DNS propagation. DNS is the internet's address book, and propagation is the time changes take to spread across it. [L272.045]
  • If setup still fails after 72 hours, retry or check DNS settings. [L272.045]
  • Microsoft Office subscription costs $19 to $29 per month. [L218.010]
  • Do not buy yearly plans, because you may need to cancel the tool if requirements change. [L218.010]
