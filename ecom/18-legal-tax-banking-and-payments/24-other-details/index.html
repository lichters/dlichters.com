Playbook › 18. Legal, tax, banking and payments

Other details

Section 18.24 · 24 of 24 in this chapter · 10 min read

In short

  • Add extra tracking only if you need it. Shopify plus Google Analytics is enough for most stores.
  • Check your country's rules with a local accountant or lawyer. This text is general information, not legal, tax or financial advice.
  • Use Shopify's free SSL for your domain. SSL is the certificate that makes your site secure.
  • Get fresh shipping quotes often. Do not rely on one carrier.
  • Read each tool's terms before you share logins or pay through a third party.

Tracking and analytics

1 min
  • Extra tracking is optional: Add campaign URL options and tracking templates only if you rely on third-party affiliate tracking, UTM builders or custom tracking software.
    • A campaign URL option is an extra tag added to a web link.
    • A UTM builder is a tool that creates these tagged links.
  • Google Analytics with Shopify is enough: If you use Google Analytics connected to Shopify's built-in tracking, you already have enough performance data.
  • You do not need tracking templates: Tracking templates are not needed in that setup.
  • Most stores need no more: Most ecommerce stores get complete tracking through these platforms alone.
  • GA4 setup deadline: GA4 is the current version of Google Analytics. Google sets the default deadline for GA4 setup.
  • Old version stopped: Universal Analytics, the old version, stopped working in July 2023.

Company, tax and compliance

1 min
  • Check compliance regularly: Treat regulatory and platform-policy compliance as a standing check, not an afterthought. Regulatory compliance means following the law. Platform policy means the rules of the site you sell on.
  • Compliance came up most in January 2026: It was the one topic raised in a January 2026 coaching call.
  • Business and personal tax: Business tax and personal tax guidance is covered separately.
  • Resale and state registration: The resale and state registration steps are in the starter basics.
  • Rules depend on your country: Payment, banking and company rules depend heavily on the country and on local tax law.
  • Ask a local expert: Check any country-specific decision on company formation or tax with a local accountant or lawyer.
  • Not professional advice: This is general information only. It is not legal, tax, investment or financial advice.
  • You carry the risk: Each owner must evaluate the risks and consult a professional for their own situation.
  • Check EU stores first: Before selling into the EU (the European Union), look at EU stores that sell the same products.
  • Check shipping policies: Read the shipping policies of those stores. Older advice from 2021.
  • Check every EU country: Confirm whether they ship to every EU country.
  • Check taxes by country: Confirm whether shipping and taxes differ across countries before you commit.
  • Supplier name on packaging is normal: In drop shipping, where the supplier ships each order for you, a supplier's name on the package is normal. Older advice from 2021.
  • No need to hide it: You do not need to hide the supplier's name.
  • Buy direct at wholesale: You may buy a supplier's product for yourself or a family member directly from the supplier at wholesale price, the trade price.
  • No store needed: This does not need to go through your Shopify store.
  • Not unethical: It is not unethical.

Payments and banking

2 min
  • Shopify Payments is Shopify's built-in way to take card payments.
  • Proof of address: Provide a utility bill (a bill for power, water or internet) or any mail that proves you own the address. Older advice from 2022.
  • Outside the US with a US address: Provide a contract with the address company or mail from a supplier.
  • Payment details: These can include a bank account for transfers through TransferWise (an online money transfer service) or a PayPal account email. Older advice from 2021 to 2022.
  • Keep them in the form: Save them in the standardized questionnaire responses, the standard form answers, so they are easy to find later.
  • Online-only banks such as Mercury: Shopify Payments may not accept them. Shopify support may also be unsure. Older advice from 2021.
  • Test the bank: Connect the account and see whether it is accepted.
  • Ask other sellers: Check community groups for other sellers' experiences with that bank.
  • Accepted methods: Credit card, debit card and PayPal are accepted for Shopify subscriptions.
  • Change later: You can update the payment method later, for example when a business credit card is available.
  • Billed through Shopify: Autoresponder subscriptions, the automatic emails sent after a trigger, are billed through the Shopify account. Older advice from 2021 to 2022.
  • Charge timing: The charge appears on the Shopify bill after the trial ends. A trial is a free test period.
  • Card needed for Gemini API: Gemini API is Google's AI service, used through code and a key. Usage is billed through a Google Cloud billing account that needs a credit or debit card.
  • Key owner pays: Charges go to the account linked to the key's project, the group that holds the work.
  • Shared tools: If a tool runs on someone else's key, their account pays. So every user should enter their own key.
  • Shared logins break rules: Sharing one login across several people usually breaches the terms of service, the rules you agree to, of paid software.
  • Examples: ChatGPT Plus, Canva Pro or Helium 10 are examples of paid software like this.
  • Risk of closure: The provider can suspend or close the account.
  • Read the terms first: Read each tool's terms before you depend on shared access.
  • Buying through Whop: The bundle is sold through Whop, a third-party checkout platform, using an affiliate-tracked sign-up link. That link records who sent the buyer.
  • Seller is not the maker: The seller is therefore not the software vendors themselves.
  • Before paying, check:
    • who the seller is,
    • The refund terms,
    • whether the subscription renews automatically, and at what price.

Shipping costs and carriers

2 min
  • Open your own account: Open a UPS or FedEx account online. It is free.
  • Give your account number: Give the supplier your account number so they can ship and bill it to you.
  • You pay the bill: You then pay the shipping bill.
  • Cut costs on big or heavy products: Older advice from 2021.
    1. Ask for a discount: Ask the supplier whether they can give a shipping discount through their own carrier accounts.
    2. Get several quotes: Get quotes from several freight brokers, middlemen who find carriers for large loads, for recent shipments.
    3. Compare the quotes: They can differ by hundreds of dollars.
    4. Pick the cheapest reliable option: Use the cheapest reliable option.
  • Fixed rates waste money: Pricing a large item with a fixed carrier rate can waste a lot of money over time.
  • Get quotes often: Get shipping quotes regularly.
  • Avoid one carrier: Avoid relying on one carrier.
  • UPS can be costly: UPS quotes for large, heavy items can be very high. Older advice from 2021.
  • Check with the carrier: Products that are unusually costly to ship or need special handling should be checked with the carrier, the shipping company. Older advice from 2022.
  • Standard approach: The standard approach is standard shipping with insurance, not a separate cost line.
  • Smart SEO shipping settings: Smart SEO is the app where these shipping settings live.
    1. Turn it on: Turn on the shipping details option.
    2. Enter the country code: Enter the country code for the country you ship to. A country code is a short code for a country.
    3. Enter minimum handling days: Handling days are the days needed to get an order ready to ship. For example, enter 0 if some products ship the same day.
    4. Enter maximum handling days: For example, enter 3 if orders cannot ship on weekends.
    5. Enter minimum transit days: Transit days are the days the parcel takes to arrive. For example, enter 1 to 5 days.
    6. Choose the price type: Choose minimum, maximum or fixed amount.
    7. Enter the amount: For a fixed amount, enter the currency (USD in the example) and the rate. For free shipping, enter a rate of 0.
    8. Save: Leave the setting turned on and save.

Shopify store setup: domains, SSL and licences

2 min
  • Connect your domain: Older advice from 2021 to 2022.
    1. Open Domains: In the Shopify admin, the back office, go to Online Store > Domains.
    2. Click Connect: Click Connect existing domain.
    3. Enter your domain: Enter the domain name you bought, without https:// (the secure web prefix). Then click Next.
    4. Choose the connection type: If the registrar, the company where you bought the domain, is a major one such as GoDaddy, choose Connect automatically. Shopify then asks you to authorize the connection in the registrar's window. Then click Connect and close it.
    5. Let Shopify verify: Shopify verifies the connection and changes the domain's DNS settings, the records that point your domain to your store. Verification can take a minute or two.
    6. Use manual connection for small registrars: For a smaller registrar that Shopify does not connect to automatically, choose Connect manually. Then follow the instructions Shopify shows to change the DNS settings.
    7. Wait for propagation: Once the domain shows as connected, wait for propagation, the time the change takes to spread across the internet. It can take up to 24 hours.
  • Automatic detection: Shopify detects and connects domains from major registrars such as GoDaddy automatically. Older advice from 2021 to 2022.
  • Manual for smaller registrars: Smaller registrars require a manual DNS connection.
  • Free SSL: SSL is the security certificate that makes a site secure. Shopify provides a free SSL certificate for connected custom domains, meaning domains you own.
  • No purchase needed: You do not need to buy one separately.
  • Pending for about 30 minutes: The status shows SSL pending for a short period, around 30 minutes. Older advice from 2021 to 2022.
  • HTTPS becomes active: After that, HTTPS, the secure version of the web address, is active.
  • HTTPS is essential: HTTPS is essential for e-commerce, meaning online selling. Older advice from 2021 to 2022.
  • Not secure while pending: While the SSL status is pending, the site shows as not secure in the browser until the certificate is issued.
  • One licence per store: One Shrine licence per store is enough. Shrine is a paid store theme, a design template.
  • No extra licences: Buying more licences for a single store is unnecessary.
  • You are not locked in: Shopify does not lock your store code.
  • Export and move: You can export all products as a CSV file, a spreadsheet file, and download image assets, the store's pictures and files, to move them elsewhere.
  • Rebuild is not one click: A full rebuild on another platform is not a one-click process. Older advice from 2021.

Software costs and subscriptions

1 min
  • Shopify is the one must-have: Shopify is the only paid software that is a must-have at the start. Advice from 2023.
  • Canva free version: Canva, an online design tool, has a free version that is enough for design.
  • Stripe Atlas: Stripe Atlas, a Stripe service for company setup, can help a founder operating from abroad set up a US company. Advice from 2023.
  • Klaviyo free tier: Klaviyo, an email marketing tool, offers a free tier, a free plan.
  • Klaviyo billing starts: Billing starts once the contact list grows past a set size.
  • Check contacts early: Check the contact count against the pricing before the list grows. Advice from 2023.
  • Canva cost: Canva's subscription cost about 11 GBP (roughly 14 USD) at the time of recording.
  • What it included: It included a library of stock images, audio and stock videos, ready-made media that would otherwise cost extra.
  • Check the price: Check the current price and the licence terms before relying on this figure.

Marketplace fees and paid promotion

1 min
  • Amazon Associates rates: Amazon Associates is Amazon's affiliate program, where affiliates, people who send buyers, earn commission. Rates range from about 4.5 to 8.5 percent.
  • Rate depends on volume: The rate depends on the number of transactions, meaning sales sent, per month.
  • Low volume, low rate: Lower-volume affiliates earn the lower rate.
  • Commissions stay small: Commissions stay small until the volume grows.
  • eBay commission depends on category: Commission depends on the product category, the type of item.
  • Example category: The example category had about 7 percent commission.
  • Payment fees on top: Payment processor fees, companies that handle card payments, such as PayPal's, come on top of this.
  • Calculate before pricing: Calculate the full fee per category before you set prices.
  • Coins for paid boosts: Coins are the currency for paid boosts. They can start at $5.
  • Usual starting level: $10 per day for the first three days is the usual starting level.
  • Avoid overspending: Avoid overspending on paid boosts. Heavy spending defeats the organic purpose, meaning free, unpaid reach.

Health insurance abroad

1 min
  • Compare total yearly cost: Compare the total annual cost of a plan, not just the monthly premium. A premium is the regular fee for the plan.
  • What total cost includes: Total annual cost means the premium plus the copay or deductible. A copay is a fixed fee per visit. A deductible is the amount you pay before insurance starts paying.
  • Example plan: One example US plan at about 500 USD per month carried a 7,000 USD annual copay.
  • Insurance for big emergencies: Many long-term travellers buy travel insurance for large catastrophic events, very costly emergencies. An example is helicopter evacuation from a hike.
  • Cash for routine care: They pay cash for routine care.
  • Private care can be affordable: Private care abroad can be affordable.
  • Example visit: An emergency visit with ultrasound, a scan using sound waves, in Thailand cost about 120 USD in cash.
  • SafetyWing option: SafetyWing offers health and travel cover built for digital nomads, people who work remotely while travelling, and expats, people living abroad.
  • Without a home plan: It is an alternative for people who need coverage without a home-country plan.
