EIN, tax IDs and supplier paperwork

Section 18.3 · 3 of 24 in this chapter · 22 min read

In short

  • Get a free US company tax number, the EIN, from the IRS first. Use only the official IRS website and never pay a website for it.
  • Get a state sales tax certificate only if your state has sales tax and suppliers ask for one.
  • Keep a W-9 form ready for suppliers who accept it.
  • Do your paperwork before you contact suppliers, where you can.
  • Rules differ by state and by supplier, so ask each supplier what it requires.
More key points
  • Fill out a W-9 (a US tax form) when a supplier asks for one.
  • Get an EIN (a free US tax number for your business) before you contact suppliers.
  • Ask each supplier what they need, instead of guessing.
  • Check the MAP rule (the lowest price you may show) before you offer coupons.

Decision order: what to get and when

1 min
  1. Get the EIN from the IRS first. It is the minimum you need before you contact suppliers.
  2. Get a sales tax certificate if your state has sales tax and suppliers ask for one.
  3. Keep a W-9 ready for suppliers that accept one. A W-9 is an IRS form that gives a supplier your tax details.
  4. Apply before you contact suppliers, where possible. Some suppliers require these documents and others do not.
  5. If you are close to approval, have the documents ready within one to two weeks. Do not wait and contact the supplier again later.

The core numbers and what they are

1 min

EIN (federal tax ID)

1 min
  • An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is a 9-digit number the IRS issues to a business.
  • It works like a Social Security number for the company.
  • You give it to suppliers instead of your personal SSN.
  • The format is two digits, a dash and seven digits.
  • A tax ID in that format is the same kind of number as an EIN.
  • It is a federal tax ID, which means a US national tax number.
  • It is free from the IRS.
  • It is needed for tax reporting and for many business tasks in the US.
  • A US business typically needs an EIN for tax filings.
  • A US business also needs an EIN to open a business bank account.
  • An EIN is needed to open a business bank account, apply for business licenses, file business taxes and fill in most supplier application forms.
  • It keeps business tax and accounting records separate from personal records.
  • Do not use your Social Security number in place of an EIN.
  • Using the EIN keeps business and personal finances separate.
  • US business suppliers require an EIN.
  • Many suppliers ask for an EIN during approval.
  • Suppliers prefer an EIN over a personal SSN.
  • Suppliers may use the EIN and the sales tax ID (your state's sales tax number) as interchangeable terms, meaning the same thing, when they request a tax ID. See Conflicts below.

Sales tax certificate, resale certificate and related names

1 min
  • A sales tax certificate (a state permit for buying without paying sales tax when you resell) is the same thing as a reseller permit.
  • A sales tax certificate and a resale certificate are the same document, not two separate ones.
  • They can be obtained online, state by state.
  • The process is typically fast and automated.
  • A sales tax permit and a reseller certificate are the same thing.
  • They are based on the EIN and cover collecting and remitting sales tax (collecting sales tax from buyers and paying it to the state).
  • Suppliers asking for either one are requesting the same document.
  • In the US, a reseller permit and a sales tax certificate are the same document.
  • In many states, a sales tax license and a resale certificate are the same document.
  • Suppliers may ask for either one.
  • A resale certificate and a sales tax ID refer to the same thing.
  • Use them interchangeably when registering for sales tax.
  • A reseller number is essentially a sales tax ID.
  • Some suppliers ask for it, and depending on your state you may need one.
  • A seller's permit is also called a sales tax permit, sales tax license or reseller's permit.
  • A business license, sales tax certificate and sales tax registration number usually refer to the same thing.
  • The name varies by state.
  • A supplier's request for a business license number generally means the same thing as a reseller permit or sales tax ID.
  • Names differ by state. Search for the state name plus "sales tax certificate" or "resale certificate."

W-9 (form, not a number)

1 min
  • The W-9 is an IRS form that a US business uses to request the taxpayer identification number of a US-based supplier or contractor.
  • It collects this information in a standard format.
  • It applies only when the business pays US-based payees (the people or companies you pay).
  • A W-9 is usually not needed, but fill it out if a supplier asks for it.

ITIN

1 min
  • An ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) is a tax ID for individuals, meaning people rather than companies.
  • It is for people who are not US citizens or residents and need to file US taxes.
  • Use it when you are a non-US resident starting a US business.
  • An ITIN is used only for non-US citizens filing taxes without a Social Security number.
  • For non-US residents, an ITIN works similarly to an EIN.
  • Suppliers may ask for one or the other.
  • Ask each supplier what they require.
  • Check forums and communities for answers.

VAT and corporate tax numbers

1 min
  • VAT is specific to Europe. (2024 advice)
  • An EIN is specific to the US. (2024 advice)
  • A VAT number is enough for European business. (2024 advice)
  • A corporate tax number is not the same as a VAT number.
  • Suppliers may ask for a specific one before approving (authorizing) a store.
  • Ask which number a supplier requires before you submit the application. This avoids delays.

Getting your EIN

1 min

Free application through the IRS

1 min
  • An EIN is free on the official IRS website (irs.gov).
  • Many third-party sites (outside companies, not the government) charge for the same EIN. Use only official sources.
  • A third-party service is not needed.
  • Some companies charge about $200 or more for the same free filing.
  • Paid services that advertise EIN setup are unnecessary for US residents.
  • Non-residents may need paid help to obtain one.
  • Paid helpers are not needed for this simple process.
  • Many online ads charge fees to do these steps for you.

Procedure: free EIN in the US

  1. Search for "EIN number" on Google.
  2. Do not click the paid results marked as ads. These companies charge a fee for setting up an EIN.
  3. Click the first organic result (the normal, unpaid result), which is the IRS site at irs.gov.
  4. Apply for the EIN online and answer the questions the IRS asks. No paid helper is needed.
  5. Expect to receive the EIN typically within about 15 minutes.

Procedure: apply for an EIN

  1. Use only the IRS website, not paid third-party services.
  2. If you live outside the US, use the international applicant route, which requires a phone call.
  3. Check the IRS phone hours before calling.

Procedure: apply for an EIN

  1. Go to the IRS website and apply for an employer identification number (EIN) for free.
  2. The application takes about five minutes.
  3. Do not pay a third-party service for the EIN. Some companies charge about $200 or more for the same free filing.
  4. Use the EIN instead of your social security number when applying for supplier accounts.

Sole proprietors

1 min
  • A sole proprietor (one person running a business, with no company registered) can contact suppliers once the store is built.
  • No legal entity (a registered company or legal form) is needed for this.
  • A US business without a Social Security number can get an EIN from the IRS website.
  • Get an EIN even as a sole proprietor.
  • It is only needed for businesses in the US.
  • A sole proprietor without employees does not strictly need an EIN.
  • They can obtain one or use the owner's SSN instead.
  • A sole proprietor can be approved by some US suppliers, according to reports from store owners.
  • This was not verified.

Reusing an existing EIN

1 min
  • An existing EIN from a previous business may be reusable for a new store.
  • The business type on the EIN may need updating.
  • Verify with a tax professional (an accountant or tax advisor) before reuse.
  • Reusing an old EIN from a business that never started is probably allowed.
  • Verify it with a lawyer or a legal forum before you do.

Who applies and how the form is filled

1 min
  • An EIN can be applied for directly by the company.
  • The title used on the form (president, CEO) is a matter of how you describe yourself.
  • A partnership (two or more owners) with several officers (company leaders with official titles) requires more care.

Why the EIN matters for pacing

Getting a state certificate

2 min

Procedure: sales tax certificate (general)

  1. Search Google for "[Your State] sales tax certificate." Use only official.gov websites (government web addresses ending in.gov). Do not pay third-party sites.
  2. Complete online registration if your state offers it. Most do, and it is often free.
  3. You will receive a downloadable PDF (a file you can print) or a physical certificate by mail.

Procedure: resale or sales tax certificate (2023 advice)

  1. Go to your state's.gov website.
  2. Search for "[Your State] resale certificate" or "[Your State] sales tax certificate."
  3. Complete the online form (typically about 5 minutes).
  4. Receive your certificate number.
  5. Provide this number to suppliers to purchase at wholesale (buying at the trade price) without sales tax.

Procedure: resale certificate (2025 advice)

  1. Search Google for the registration state plus sales tax certificate or resale certificate.
  2. Open the official.gov page of that state.
  3. Complete the online form. Many states give a PDF immediately. Some mail it, which can take about a week.
  4. Get it before supplier outreach, because some suppliers require it and others do not.

Procedure: sales tax certificate online

  • In a state with sales tax, search for the state name plus sales tax certificate or reseller certificate.
  • Then apply through the state's online form.

Procedure: sales tax ID after your EIN

  • Get a sales tax ID after your EIN, as early as practical, because many suppliers ask for it.
  • The setup is usually done online. States without sales tax do not need one.

Procedure: when a supplier asks for a resale number and the state has no certificate

  1. Search for an exemption form (a form showing you are not charged sales tax) on the official state government website.
  2. If none is listed, call or email the state's contact number and ask for a document stating you are exempt.
  3. Send that document to the supplier.

Procedure: resale certificate in a state with no registration yet

  1. Ask the accountant for the exact name of the state sales and use tax form (the form for reporting sales tax).
  2. Search that form name online to check whether it can be filed online.
  3. If it is available online, file it there instead of visiting a city or county office.

W-9 forms

1 min

A W-9 is a US tax form that gives your tax details to a business. IRS means the US tax agency.

When and how to use a W-9

  • Fill out a W-9 when a supplier asks for one.
  • Give a W-9 if requested, or your sales tax certificate (a state document with your sales tax number), when purchasing for resale (buying items to sell again). (2025 advice)
  • Offer a W-9 instead of applying for resale permits in states where you have no nexus (a legal tie to a state, such as an office or enough sales there). Send it together with your home-state resale certificate (a state form that proves you buy to resell). (2025 advice)
  • A D&B (Dun & Bradstreet, a company that gives business ID numbers) number is not necessary for the business.

Procedure: handling a W-9 request from a large business customer

  1. Keep a blank Form W-9 PDF in the PDF files section of the corporate information board.
  2. When a customer asks for it, print the form.
  3. Fill in the business name and the tax information.
  4. Send the completed form to the customer.
  5. Keep the file ready because large companies request it repeatedly.

What to tell suppliers

2 min

Suppliers are the companies that provide the products you sell.

Before you contact suppliers

  • Ask each supplier directly what they require before assuming a requirement.
  • Get the EIN (Employer Identification Number, a free US tax number for a business from the IRS) before contacting suppliers.
  • The EIN is the minimum to have before contacting suppliers.
  • Get a resale certificate before supplier outreach, since some suppliers require it.
  • EIN numbers and resale certificate numbers are needed for supplier applications. (2023 advice)
  • D-U-N-S numbers (business ID numbers from Dun & Bradstreet) are not required for this model. This model means dropshipping, where the supplier ships each order straight to the customer. (2023 advice)
  • Ask which number a supplier requires: a corporate tax number (the tax ID of a company) or a VAT number (value added tax, a sales tax used in many countries outside the US), before submitting the application.

What to give the supplier

  • Provide the official LLC name (limited liability company, a business type that separates your personal money from business debts) and the business address where the LLC is registered. Do not give a private mailing address.
  • The company name and EIN go on supplier approval forms, which usually ask for company details in a one-page form.
  • The company name is not shown to customers.
  • One LLC can run several stores.
  • When calling suppliers, use the store name.
  • On approval forms, list the store names you will sell under, next to the corporation's legal name.
  • Provide a resale certificate for the state where your business operates when a supplier asks for one. (2023 advice)
  • EIN numbers and resale certificate numbers are needed for supplier applications. (2023 advice)

Procedure: when a supplier asks for a state tax certificate (a state document with your sales tax number) and your state does not issue one (2024 advice)

  1. Contact the supplier, asking for their tax certificate requirement.
  2. If they ask for a state tax certificate, start with your business registration state (e.g., Delaware).
  3. If your state does not issue a sales tax certificate (e.g., Delaware), provide a W9 form from the IRS instead.
  4. Tell the supplier: "I am a Delaware business; please see the attached W9."
  5. Ask if the W9 is acceptable.
  6. If not, follow up with any documents your state requires.

Credit, references and business details

1 min
  • Suppliers typically request credit (letting you pay later, after you get the goods) and banking information as part of the retailer approval process.
  • Use a business credit card or debit card for authorization, not bank account details. This keeps privacy and control.
  • Leave the business references (other companies that vouch for you) blank if you are not applying for credit.
  • Use cash or a credit card only, since no credit terms are needed in the dropshipping model. (2023 advice)
  • Drop shipping stores do not require credit for supplier approval.
  • A low credit score (a number that shows how well you pay debts) caused by past insolvency or bankruptcy (failing to pay your debts) does not block working with suppliers.
  • D&B numbers are not required for this model.

Supplier agreements and dropship policy

1 min
  • For high-ticket items (expensive products, $5,000+), you can request a supplier agreement (a written deal between you and the supplier).
  • Search "dropshipping supplier agreement" on Google, or check competitor sites (other stores selling similar products) for templates to adapt.
  • The Source A material does not provide standard agreement forms.
  • You do not need your own dropship policy (a written rules document for dropshipping). (2024 advice)
  • Suppliers that ask for one are rare. (2024 advice)
  • A simple agreement from a template is enough. (2024 advice)

Conflict: says high-ticket suppliers may ask for a supplier agreement, while says a dropship policy is rarely requested. Use a template agreement for high-ticket suppliers, and a simple agreement otherwise.

MAP, MSRP and coupons

1 min

Definitions

  • On a supplier price sheet (the list of prices the supplier gives you), MSRP is the manufacturer's suggested retail price (the price the maker recommends), while MAP is the minimum advertised price (the lowest price you may show a product at). (2023 advice)
  • Check the case pack size (how many units come in one set), since the wholesale cost (the price you pay the supplier) may cover a set of several units rather than one. (2023 advice)
  • Some markets have no legally enforceable MAP (no law makes the rule binding), but suppliers may follow unwritten rules.
  • Discounting can reduce their supply.
  • In the UK, undercutting (selling below) a supplier's price can cause the supply to dry up, because of damage to the supplier's reputation with other retailers.

Coupons and MAP compliance

  • MAP compliance (following the MAP rule) is judged by the price displayed on the store, not by the price after a coupon (a discount code).
  • A coupon at checkout (the payment step) is usually allowed.
  • A displayed price below MAP is a violation (breaking the rule) unless the supplier agreement permits it.
  • Check the supplier's MAP agreement before offering coupon codes.
  • A 20 dollar coupon on a 200 dollar MAP product is allowed as long as the product page still shows 200 dollars.
