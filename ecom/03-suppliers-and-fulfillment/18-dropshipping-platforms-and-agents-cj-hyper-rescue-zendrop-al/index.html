Playbook › 3. Suppliers and fulfillment

Dropshipping platforms and agents (CJ, hyper rescue, zendrop, AliExpress)

Section 3.18 · 18 of 22 in this chapter · 37 min read

In short

  • Start with CJ Dropshipping as your first supplier. A supplier is the company that ships your orders.
  • Do not use AliExpress as your long-term supplier. It is slow and hard to control. Use it only to test.
  • Before you sell a product, check for 4.6 stars or higher and at least 50 reviews.
  • Order one sample of the product yourself before you promote it.
  • Once sales are high, move toward better suppliers or your own agents.
More key points
  • Ask a live person for a lower price. Listed prices are often higher than what you can get.
  • Check delivery times first. Slow shipping can hurt your sales and your ads.
  • Start with a fulfillment company (a company that packs and ships orders for you) while sales are low.
  • Switch to a private supplier only when you reach about 10 orders per day.
  • Start with an easy supplier like CJ Dropshipping to test products.
  • Once sales are steady, switch to a faster or cheaper supplier.
  • Before switching, stop your ads on the date the old supplier stops getting orders.
  • Check that every paid order is marked as shipped before you switch.
  • Order and test a product yourself before you advertise it.

Quality check before scaling a product (procedure)

1 min
  1. On AliExpress, look for a listing with a rating of 4.6 or higher and at least 50 reviews.
  2. If a listing has fewer than 50 reviews, treat it as doubtful. Look for another listing of the same product.
  3. On Amazon, look for a rating of 4 or higher.

AliExpress quality check (procedure)

1 min
  1. Search for the product term.
  2. Find a listing with at least a 4.6 star rating (out of 5) and at least 100 orders.
  3. Avoid listings with high order counts but a rating below 4.6.
  4. Record the AliExpress average rating in a column of your research sheet.
  5. Use that rating to rank products.

CJ Dropshipping: listing a product in Shopify (procedure)

1 min
  1. Search for the product on CJ Dropshipping, or search by keyword to find similar listings.
  2. Open the listing and select the variant (color and style). The variant changes the product price.
  3. Check the product price, the shipping cost and the total cost for the target country.
  4. Compare shipping options. Pick the one with the best balance of low cost and reasonable delivery time (for example 7 to 15 days).
  5. Choose the List button, not Connect, for the product you want to add to the store.
  6. Set the store name, product type, collection (a group of products), vendor (the supplier label) and a target price (the selling price in the store). For example, set 39.99 USD. The vendor can be, for example, 'cj'.
  7. Click List it now.
  8. Wait 1 to 2 minutes for the product to appear in the Shopify store.
  9. Edit product title, images, descriptions and prices in Shopify as needed.
  • On CJ Drop Shipping, the Connect option adds the product to the store with images, videos and descriptions that you can edit.
  • The List option is used when you add the product with a target price directly through the listing screen.
  • CJ Dropshipping has a very large catalog (its full list of products), so most products can be found and listed directly.
  • CJ Drop Shipping has a very large catalog. So most products found on AliExpress can also be found there.
  • Products can be connected to a Shopify store within minutes.
  • CJ Dropshipping has a trending products page with product categories. It is convenient for browsing dropshipping products.
  • Its delivery times can be slow unless you pay for faster options.

1 min
  • Supplier pricing does not automatically decrease in quality.

  • Automated replies rarely offer discounts, so get a live agent first.

  • Platforms and agents such as CJ Dropshipping and Hyper Rescue often list higher prices to new dropshippers.

  • Negotiate by live chat and expect lower prices than the listed price.

Setting up a private supplier (procedure)

1 min
