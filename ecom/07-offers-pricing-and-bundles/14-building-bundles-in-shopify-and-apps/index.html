Playbook › 7. Offers, pricing and bundles

Building bundles in Shopify and apps

Section 7.14 · 14 of 28 in this chapter · 27 min read

In short

  • Never run Monster Card and bundle offers at the same time. They clash.
  • Use a Shopify automatic discount (a discount that applies by itself, with no code) for "buy X, get Y" offers.
  • Check the math. 20% off 69.99 USD is 55.99 USD, not 59.99 USD. Confirm the number before you publish.
  • Keep the widget percentage and the Shopify discount identical, or the customer sees two different savings.
  • Set the minimum item count to the full bundle size, so the discount only works on a full bundle.
More key points
  • Discount tiers only work with Shopify Functions (Shopify's built-in discount engine) turned on. Turning it off removes them.
  • Each tier unlocks at a cart total called the unlock price.
  • Sources disagree on the tier-type list, so check your admin before you build.
  • Only one progress bar shows to each visitor. Give every bar its own countries.
  • Bundles can go on an existing product page or on their own page.
This section has 15 parts. Tap one to open it.

Choose the offer type before you build

1 min
  • Bundle offers do better than Monster Card for average order value (the average amount one customer spends per order).
  • Monster Card is an offer app (a tool that shows extra products to buyers).
  • Never run them together. Monster Card and bundle tactics do not work at the same time.
  • Bundle apps (add-on tools that build bundles) work better for selling more of the same product.
  • Monster Card works better when you have different items that go well together (complementary items) to offer.
  • Choose by your specific goal.
  • Use an automatic discount instead of a discount code for a buy-X-get-Y offer (buy X items, get Y free or cheaper).
  • The discount applies in the cart. The customer enters nothing.
  • The offer stays identical at every step, from the ad to checkout.
  • Bundle builders (tools that create bundles) with quantity discounts usually need a paid app.
  • Built-in bundle features in a theme (the design template of your store) can replace several monthly app costs.
  • One bundle app was estimated to cost about as much per month as the full $249 theme price over roughly six months.
  • Compare total app costs with a one-time theme price to judge the savings.
  • Common conversion features (features that turn visitors into buyers) are: bundle builders, quizzes, upsells (offering a bigger or better version), cross-sells (offering a related product), free shipping options, and discounts for adding more items.
  • They were built into a theme because they were seen converting well across many brand projects.

Shopify automatic discounts (no bundle app needed)

2 min
  • Shopify is the online store software used here. An automatic discount applies by itself at checkout.
  • Go to Discounts, click Create discount, then choose Automatic discount.
  • Set a rule such as "buy 2 and get 20% off".
  • Show a banner message on the product page or in the cart.
  • Example: 2 products at 34.99 USD each (69.99 USD) become 59.99 USD at checkout.
  • Apps such as Bundle Bear are optional. You do not need one at the start.
  • Source check: 20% off 69.99 USD is 55.99 USD, not 59.99 USD. The example shows about 14% off.
  • Confirm the discount value in the Shopify rule before you publish the banner.
  • Set up the automatic quantity discount: Discounts > Create discount > automatic discount.
  • Tell the customer on the product page, for example "20% off when two items are added to the cart".
  • This works without a bundle or upsell app.

Create an automatic product discount for a bundle

  1. Go to Discounts and create a new discount.
  2. Select Amount off products.
  3. Set the method to Automatic.
  4. Enter a Title. Customers see this title in the cart and at checkout.
  5. Set Discount value to Percentage and enter the value (for example 10%).
  6. Under Applies to, choose Specific collections and select the collection (a group of products) that holds all bundle items.
  7. Set a minimum purchase requirement of 4 items, so the discount applies only to a full bundle.
  8. Decide whether the discount may combine with other discounts.
  9. Set the start date under Active dates and, if needed, an end date.
  10. Save, then check the Performance section (the page that shows how often the discount was used).
  • The automatic discount summary should read Amount off products, Method Automatic and Details For Online Store.
  • The summary also lists the percentage and the minimum item count.
  • The discount must be active before you test it. Otherwise the cart will not show it.
  • Set the Shopify minimum purchase quantity to the number of items in the bundle.
  • The discount triggers only when the full bundle is in the cart.
  • A minimum below the bundle size gives the discount on partial sets.
  • Formula: Bundle price after discount = list total x (1 - discount %). The list total is the full price before any discount.
  • Example: a list total of $37.00 with a 10% discount gives $37.00 x (1 - 0.10) = $33.30, a saving of $3.70.
  • If the widget discount percentage and the Shopify discount value differ, the shopper sees one saving on the bundle. At checkout they get another.
  • Keep both values identical for each bundle.
  • In Shopify, a quantity-based discount can apply when two items are in the cart, so the customer sees the saving at once.
  • The example amount is 10, but its unit is not stated.
  • Confirm whether it is a percentage or a fixed amount in your store before you publish.

Bundle Bear

3 min

Tool description and offer types

  • Bundle Bear is a Shopify app (an add-on you install from the Shopify App Store) for volume discount offers, such as buy two and get 40% off.
  • In Bundle Bear, the Bundle and Volume discount offer types are the most practical choices for most stores.
  • A bundle can offer a fixed saving (a set amount off) on two complementary products.
  • Or it can make the second product a free gift with the first.
  • Bundle Bear can combine two different products into one deal, such as a cap with a free t-shirt.
  • It can run buy-one-get-one-free offers.
  • Each bundle can use the brand color and brand images.
  • Performance can be checked in the analytics (the statistics page).
  • Bundle Bear offer types are:
    • Bundle with another product.
    • Volume discount (the more bought, the less paid).
    • Mix and match (a discount for different products).
    • Cart goal (a discount above a cart value, such as $50).

Create a bundle offer in Bundle Bear

  1. Install Bundle Bear from the Shopify App Store and click Install app.
  2. Click Create offer and choose Bundle.
  3. Select the first and second product.
  4. Choose the saving. Options:
    • A fixed amount off for both items (for example $10 or $20).
    • The second product as a free gift.
    • No discount, to show that the products are sold together.
  5. Under advanced settings, set a start and end date for urgency (a deadline that pushes people to buy). Round prices and choose 0.99 as the ending.
  6. Click save.

Create a volume discount in Bundle Bear

  1. Install Bundle Bear from the Shopify App Store.
  2. Click Create an offer and select Volume discount.
  3. Select the specific product and search for it.
  4. Add tiers (price levels by quantity), for example Buy 1 and save 10%, Buy 2 and save 15%, Buy 3 and save 20%. Customize the text for each tier.
  5. Save. The offer appears just below the product.

You can also set brand color and images, create buy-one-get-one or free-gift offers, and check bundle analytics.

Create a Bundle Bear volume discount (second procedure)

  1. Install Bundle Bear from the Shopify App Store and click Install app.
  2. Create an offer and name it, for example "offer one".
  3. Choose the offer type: bundle with another product, volume discount, mix and match, or cart goal.
  4. For this example, choose volume discount, then pick all products or specific products.
  5. Before you set the discount, calculate the fulfillment cost per unit (what it costs you to pack and ship one item) with your supplier.
  6. Add the discount tiers, then click save and activate.
  7. In the theme editor (the page where you change how your store looks), add the Bundle Bear block to the product page. It usually goes below the quantity selector (the box where the customer picks how many).
  • A bundle offer can use a label such as "Get my deal" instead of "Add to cart" or "Buy now".
  • The label and savings text can be edited in the bundle app.

Tier values and examples

  • Typical volume-discount tiers are buy 2 for 10% off and buy 3 for 15% off.
  • A cheaper alternative to Bundle Bear is the Sketching Bundle app.
  • Tier values differ between sources.
  • One source uses Buy 1 save 10%, Buy 2 save 15%, Buy 3 save 20%.
  • Another uses 10% for 2 items, 15% for 3 items and 20% for 4 items.
  • Both are examples. Calculate your fulfillment cost per unit before you choose the tiers.
  • Worked example: 10% off for 2 items, 15% off for 3 items and 20% off for 4 items.
  • Two items were priced at about $201 instead of about $223. So one item cost about $100 instead of about $112.

Advanced settings and rounding

  • In Bundle Bear advanced settings, you can add custom badges (small labels on the offer).
  • You can set a start and end time for urgency.
  • You can round discounted prices to 50 cents or $1. The example rounded prices to 50 cents.
  • For discounted prices, enable rounding and choose the .99 ending. For example, show a bundle price as 49.99 instead of 50.00.
  • Set this in the advanced settings of the offer.
  • Rounding values differ between sources.
  • Use the .99 ending for charm pricing (prices that end in.99 to look lower).
  • Use 50 cents or $1 when you want whole-amount rounding. The example in used 50 cents.

Kaching Bundles

8 min

Kaching Bundles is a Shopify app that builds bundle deals. Terms used below:

  • Bar: one row of the offer, for example "Buy Two".
  • Block title: the heading above the offer.
  • Fixed price: a set total you type in, instead of a percentage.
  • Variant: a version of a product, such as a size or color.

Set up a quantity-break bundle in Kaching Bundles (single-product example)

Quantity break means the price per item drops when the customer buys more.

  1. Search "Kaching Bundles" in the Shopify App Store, install it, and approve the billing permissions (the app's charges).
  2. Open the app and create a new bundle deal.
  3. Select the product to bundle. In this example it is one product, a single supplement.
  4. Enter a name for the bundle. Only you can see it.
  5. Set the Block title to a free-shipping threshold (the minimum order for free shipping). The threshold must be above the one-item price and between the one-item and two-item totals.
    • Example: "Free Shipping for orders above $25" for a $19.95 product.
  6. Set the Discount name shown in cart and checkout to "Bundle & Save".
  7. Leave all other settings at default except Price rounding.
  8. Enable Price rounding and choose 95 or 99 cents.
  9. If the product has no variants, turn off "Let customers choose different variants for each item".
  10. Set up Bar #1 (Buy One):
    • Set quantity to 1 and keep Full price.
    • Title "Buy One". Delete the subtitle.
    • In the price line, insert the variable for new price per item, followed by "/ bottle".
    • In the label, type "SAVE" followed by the variable for saved total.
    • Badge style "Simple", badge text "Great Start!".
  11. Set up Bar #2 (Buy Two):
    • Set quantity to 2 and choose Fixed price with a total of 29.99.
    • Title "Buy Two".
    • Subtitle and label identical to each other.
    • Badge style "Most Popular".
    • Optionally select it by default.
  12. Set up Bar #3 (Buy Three):
    • Set quantity to 3 and choose Fixed price with a total of 38.95.
    • Title "Buy Three".
    • Badge text "Best Value".
  13. Add a free e-book gift to Bar #2 and Bar #3 (and to Bar #4 if you add one).
  14. Open Style and change colors, fonts and corner radius (the roundness of the corners) to your brand.
  15. Click Save as draft, check the preview, then click Publish.

Set up a bundle deal in Kaching Bundles (generic version)

  1. Search Kaching Bundles in the Shopify App Store, install it and approve the billing permissions.
  2. Open the app, go to Bundle deals, then click Create bundle deal.
  3. Under the product selection, choose the product that the bundle is built around.
  4. In Settings, enter:
    • A name, visible only to you.
    • A block title that carries the free shipping message, for example "Free shipping for orders above $25".
    • The discount name shown in cart and checkout, for example "Bundle & Save".
  5. Keep all other settings at default except Price rounding.
  6. Choose the deal type. Quantity breaks for the same product is the usual default.
  7. Set up Bar #1 Buy One: Quantity 1, Full price, title "Buy One", delete the subtitle, Badge style "Simple", badge text "Great Start!", label "SAVE" with the saved total variable, and the price line with the new price per item followed by "/Bottle".
  8. Set up Bar #2 Buy Two: Quantity 2, Fixed price with a total of 29.99, title "Buy Two", subtitle and label identical to the title, Badge style "Most Popular", optionally "Selected by default".
  9. Set up Bar #3 Buy Three: Quantity 3, Fixed price with the chosen total, title "Buy Three", Badge style "Best Value".
  10. Add a free gift to bars 2 and 3: Add gift, Single gift, rename it "Free e-book", and select the e-book product.
  11. In Settings, turn off the variant selector if the product has no variants.
  12. In Style, change colors, font sizes and corner radius to the brand.
  13. Use Preview to check the product and the country (for example United States). Confirm the prices display correctly.
  14. Click Save as draft, then Publish. A Setup A/B test option (a test of two versions to see which sells better) is available before publishing.

Pricing and fixed totals

  • Use Fixed price for bundle bars instead of percentage discounts.
  • You can type the exact total. The per-unit price is then easy to show.
  • Example: a fixed total of 29.99 for two bottles displays as about 14.98 per bottle.
  • Source check: 29.99 divided by 2 is 14.995. So the exact per-bottle figure is about 14.995, and the example displays 14.98.
  • Use the price shown in your Preview.
  • Reference bundle prices from the example, for a $19.95 single bottle:
    • Buy Two at a fixed total of $29.99 (about $14.98 per bottle, compare-at $40.00).
    • Buy Three at a fixed total of $38.95 (about $12.98 per bottle, compare-at $60.00).
    • Price rounding set to 95 shows prices ending in.95.
  • The compare-at price is the old, crossed-out price.
  • Formula for the SAVE label: Saved amount = compare-at price of the bundle minus the bundle total.
  • Example: a compare-at price of $40.00 and a bundle total of $29.95 shows SAVE $10.05.
  • Pricing settings: in Settings > Pricing, the toggle "Update theme product price" sits next to Price rounding and the other price display options.
  • Turn it on only if the theme should show the bundle-adjusted price on the product page.
  • Confirm the result in Preview before you publish.

Variables for labels and price lines

  • The bracket button in the label and price fields opens an "Add variable" menu. It offers preset maths.

  • The variables are:

    • New unit price
    • Saved $ per unit
    • Original unit price
    • Unit quantity
    • Buy quantity
    • Got quantity
    • Discount
    • Saved percentage
    • Saved $ total
    • Product title
    • New total price
    • New price per item
    • Original total price

  • Use New price per item with the unit label for the bar price line.

  • Use Saved $ total in the SAVE label.

Bars and badges

  • Bar settings include: Add upsell, Add gift, Add personalisation, Add highlights, Show as Sold out, Show quantity selector and Selected by default.
  • Each bar can carry its own title, subtitle, label, badge style and badge text.
  • Badge styles in the example bundle:
    • Simple for the single-unit bar.
    • Most Popular for the two-unit bar.
    • Best Value for the three-unit bar.
  • Badge text is free to set, for example "Great Start!", "Most Popular" and "Best Value".
  • Bundle with a free gift: a free gift on the two-unit and three-unit bars gives the customer both a discount and extra value for buying more.
  • This helps move them from one unit to at least two.
  • Use the same gift logic on any higher-quantity bar.

Gifts

  • Gifts added to a bundle receive an automatic 100% discount, so the free item costs nothing at checkout.
  • Add the free gift to the Buy Two and Buy Three bars.
  • If a Buy Four bar exists, add a gift to it too. A buy-four bar needs its own gift.
  • Gift options in a bar: Add gift offers "Single gift" or "Multiple gifts selector".
  • Toggles for gifts:
    • Show original price.
    • Include in compare-at price. Leave this on if the free gift's value should count toward the savings shown.
    • Apply only for subscriptions. Use this to limit a gift to subscription orders.

Variants and product selection

  • Leaving the variant selector on for a product with no variants adds an unnecessary step for the customer.
  • Turn it off in the settings when the product has no variants.
  • Variant options in Kaching Bundles:
    • "Show variant selection for single quantity deal bar"
    • "Hide theme variant picker"
    • "Hide unavailable variant options"
    • "Don't update other products when a variant is selected"
    • "Set default variants"
    • "Add swatches"
  • For a product with no variants, turn off "Let customers choose different variants for each item", so the selector does not appear.
  • Each bar selects its products via "All products", "Selected products" or "Selected collections", with a search filter.
  • A counter shows how many products are selected out of the allowed maximum (200 in the example).

Visibility, schedule and states

  • Visibility and activity settings: Markets (the countries and regions you sell to) set to All.
  • Also set Exclude B2B customers (business buyers), and keep the checkbox "Apply discount only via bundle widget" as shown in the default setup.
  • Set Active dates (Start date, optional End date) only if the bundle has a limited run.
  • Active dates: the Other settings > Active dates section has Start date, Set end date and Start time fields.
  • The start time is shown with the store's time zone (the example store showed GMT+9:30).
  • Set both a start and an end date for limited-time bundles.
  • Check the time zone before you rely on the schedule.
  • Bundle deal states in the Kaching Bundles list are: Active, Inactive and A/B test archive.
  • Use Save as draft to keep changes unpublished.
  • Use Publish to make the bundle live.
  • Mistake to avoid: leaving the variant selector on for a product with no variants.

Bundle widgets on the product page (Rebuy and general)

3 min

A widget is a small box added to a page that runs a tool. Rebuy is a Shopify app for product widgets.

Principles

  • A dynamic bundle lets customers add several related products to the cart with one click.
  • This shortens the path to a larger basket (the set of items in one order) on the product page.
  • A dynamic bundle widget lets shoppers change the set by selecting or removing items first.
  • Suggesting frequently bought together items, complete-the-look sets or curated product bundles reduces decision fatigue (the tiredness that comes from too many choices).
  • It also raises average order value.
  • Bundle offers on the product page can be built with the Kaching Bundles app.
  • Use quantity tiers, for example buy 2 or buy 3 with a percentage off.
  • Use gift-style tiers, for example buy 3 and give 2 to someone.
  • Place the bundle widget above the Add to Cart button, so the offer is seen before purchase.
  • Use the same offer set across product pages and the cart, so the customer sees a consistent deal.
  • Bundle apps can build buy-one-get-one-free, buy-X-get-Y-free and volume-discount offers.

Example data source (demo)

A data source is the list of products the widget reads from.

  • In the demo bundle, the Rebuy Mug ($10.00, White), T-shirt ($25.00, White / XS) and Bold Brew ($10.00, 1lb) add up to a total price of $45.00.
  • The data source preview lists recommended items under the label "Frequently Bought Together".
  • It shows their individual prices and a combined total.
  • The total is shown above the Add to Cart button before any discount is applied.
  • The bundle's total price is calculated from the included products.
  • Discount settings sit in a separate Discount tab in the widget editor. You can adjust them without changing the product list.

Discount values in the widget

  • A Widget Discount only shows the saving on the bundle.
  • It does not reduce the price in the cart.
  • Real discounting requires either a Shopify Scripts discount (custom discount code, Shopify Plus only; Plus is Shopify's top paid plan) or a Shopify automatic discount.
  • To set a discount per rule: open the rule, choose Widget Discount, select Percentage and enter the value.
  • Each rule can have its own percentage.
  • Rule titles should state the saving, so the shopper sees the discount in the bundle heading.
  • Setting one discount for a whole widget: if all bundles of one widget share the same discount, set it once in Widget Settings under the Discount field (for example 10% or 15%).
  • Use rule-level Widget Discount percentages on the data source only when different bundles need different savings.
  • Keep each percentage identical to the matching Shopify automatic discount.
  • Each bundle must be its own Shopify product, so bundle counts grow quickly.
  • Example: five accessories with one bundle per accessory means at least five bundles.
  • Every added combination multiplies that number.
  • Keep the set small and limited to the best-selling accessories, so shoppers are not overwhelmed.

Rebuy merchandising tools

  • Merchandising means how products are shown and grouped in the store.
  • ReBuy merchandising widgets include personalized product carousels (sliding rows of products), product add-ons (small extra items added to an order) and dynamic bundles.
  • Add-ons and bundles are the offer types to use when raising order value on product pages and in the cart.

Page-builder embeds (PageFly and others)

1 min

A page builder is an app that lets you design store pages without writing code. PageFly is one example. An embed is a small piece of code you paste into a page to add a feature. HTML is the basic code behind web pages. Liquid is Shopify's template code.

  • Add a bundle app to PageFly pages. Examples: White Bundle or BundleBear. A bundle is several products sold together as one offer.
    1. Find the HTML/Liquid section in PageFly.
    2. Copy the embed code from the bundle app.
    3. Place the code in the HTML section, right after the "Add to Cart" button. This button puts a product in the cart.
    4. The app widget (a small box from the app) appears on the product page.
  • Different apps use different code formats.
  • Read the app's documentation (its official instructions).

Rebuy Smart Cart: tiered progress bar and discount tiers

1 min

Rebuy Smart Cart is a cart app. A cart is the basket where customers see what they bought. A tiered progress bar is a bar that shows reward levels. Each level is a tier. A tier unlocks its reward once the cart reaches a set amount.

Bar options and types

1 min
  • Smart Cart goal box > Tiered Progress Bar > Configure Tiers. A goal box is the area that shows the target.
  • Each tier has three fields:
    • Choose Tier Type: Free Shipping, free products, order discount, or decorative only (a tier that shows but gives nothing).
    • Unlock Price: the amount in dollars that unlocks the tier.
    • Add New Tier button: adds another tier.
  • You can add a maximum of four tiers.
  • Advanced Bar Settings > Use Shopify Functions (Recommended) is on by default.
  • The Free Gifts section has a Gift Area Title field.
  • Two separate sections change the look of the bar:
    • Configure Icon changes the bar's icon.
    • Configure Labels & Text changes the wording.
  • Sources disagree on the tier-type list.
  • names "decorative only" as a type.
  • and name Fixed Price.
  • Check the tier-type list in your admin before you build.
  • A tiered progress bar supports up to 4 tiers.
  • The tier types are Free Shipping, Free Product, Order Discount and Fixed Price.
  • With Shopify Functions, a tiered progress bar can turn full-priced products into free gifts once the cart reaches a tier.
  • Each tier can be set to a free gift, free shipping, an order discount, or a decorative stage.
  • Only one tiered progress bar shows to a visitor at a time.
  • You can build several bars with different country limits.
  • Example: one bar offers free shipping and a free gift in the US. Another offers only the free gift in Canada.
  • Each progress bar must have its countries set. Without them, it shows to all visitors.
  • Currency settings follow the country codes (two-letter country codes) assigned to each bar.

Setup procedure

1 min
  • Set up a tiered progress bar:
    1. In the cart editor, click Add Component to Header and choose Tiered Progress Bar. Place it in the header or goal box area.
    2. Set the countries where the bar should appear. Use the country field with autocomplete (suggestions as you type). This replaces the old two-letter ISO code entry. ISO codes are standard country codes.
    3. Add a tier and choose its type: Free Shipping, Free Product, Order Discount, or Fixed Price.
    4. Set the threshold amount for that tier. The threshold is the cart amount that unlocks it.
    5. For Free Product, choose the product and the variant option. A variant is one version of a product, such as a size or color. Then set the unlock price.
    6. Turn on Use Shopify Functions if the tier should be applied as a discount automatically.
    7. Set a custom tier label and icon size if needed.
    8. Repeat for additional tiers.
    9. Save the cart.

Free gifts and line items

1 min
  • Line item mode is an on/off switch (a toggle) for free gifts.
  • Turn it on to add the free gift to the cart as a normal row (a line item).
  • Without it, the gift shows in the free gift area instead.
  • This saves space and avoids a complex workaround.
  • For a Free Product tier, you can select a single variant or all variants of a product.
  • When several variants are selected, the customer picks one from a dropdown (a menu list).
  • With line item mode on, the chosen variant appears as a line item in the cart.
  • Any product used in a free product tier must be active, in stock and available for sale on the online store.
  • Otherwise, the reward cannot be applied at checkout (the payment step).
  • Free Product tier example: a free product is added automatically when the cart reaches a $100 unlock price.
  • The product is a single-variant item in the demo.
  • Use a custom tier label to describe the reward generically, such as "savings" or "money off."
  • Do not name the free product or the discount amount.
  • Icon size can also be set for each tier.
  • Selectable gift with purchase (the customer picks the free gift) is not a native option on non-Plus stores at the time of the webinar.
  • Native means built into Shopify itself. Plus is Shopify's higher-priced plan.
  • It needs extensive workarounds, a third-party app (made by another company) that can run Shopify Functions discounts, or a cross-sell widget (a small box that suggests extra products).

Discount tiers (Shopify Functions)

2 min
  • Discount tiers can only be powered by Shopify Functions.
  • When you add a discount tier, the Use Shopify Functions toggle is hidden.
  • If you switch that setting off, the discount tier disappears for the user.
  • The step 6 wording in the setup procedure presents Use Shopify Functions as a choice.
  • For discount tiers, keep it on, because says discount tiers depend on it.
  • Path to add a discount tier: Tiered Progress Bar > Configure Tiers > Add New Tier.
  • For a discount tier, choose Order Discount Type: Percentage or fixed amount.
  • Set Order Discount Amount from Total.
  • Set the Unlock Price, which is the cart total that unlocks the tier.
  • Order Discount tier example: spend $500 and get 25% off the order.
  • Fixed Price tier example: spend $500 and get $50 off the order.
  • Both are applied through Shopify Functions.
  • Example pair:
    • A fixed $50 off tier, unlocked at a $500 cart total.
    • A percentage tier of 20% off, unlocked at a $1000 cart total.
  • Set the Order Discount Text of each tier to match its discount, such as "20% OFF."
  • By default, Functions discounts can combine with product, shipping and order discount types.
  • In the tested cart, only the largest qualifying order discount was applied.
  • With several tiers reached, the biggest one wins.
  • With a $50 fixed tier at $500 and a 20% tier at $1000, a cart of about $1000 or more receives only the 20% discount.
  • That is because 20% is greater than the $50 fixed amount.
  • The checkout shows only that order discount.
  • Remaining-amount label: a placeholder is a code word the app fills in with a live value.
  • With a $500 unlock price, a cart subtotal of $499.99 shows the remaining-amount label as one cent away from the reward.
  • The remaining-amount placeholder always shows the gap between the current cart total and the next unlock price.
  • So customers see exactly how little they still need to add.
  • Two product tiers:
    • Tier 1: a $50 unlock price for one free product.
    • Tier 2: a $100 unlock price for a second free product.
  • When both tiers are unlocked, checkout shows both products fully discounted.
  • The product discount text comes only from Tier 1.
  • So keep the Tier 1 text generic so it fits both rewards.

Subscriptions and Buy More Save More

2 min
  • Switch to Subscription (Cart Items > Switch to Subscription) lets customers upgrade one-time products to subscriptions in the cart.
  • A subscription is a repeat delivery the customer signs up for.
  • It is useful when a store sells both one-time and subscription versions of a product.
  • Buy More Save More (Cart Items > Buy More Save More) is a tiered discount.
  • It encourages customers to buy more units of a product to unlock a bigger discount.
  • Set up subscription upgrades in the cart:
    1. Integrate your selling plan provider, such as Recharge, in the Rebuy admin under the Integrations tab. A selling plan provider is the app that runs subscription billing.
    2. Open the cart body and toggle subscriptions on for the eligible line items.
    3. Customers who add a one-time version will see the option to switch to a subscription.
    4. Optionally, turn on Disable downgrade to stop customers switching back from subscription to one-time.
    5. Edit the switch-to-subscription labels to fit your brand.
  • The Disable downgrade toggle stops customers from switching a subscription back to a one-time purchase.
  • Leave it off if you want to keep the option available.
  • A step back can keep the journey moving.
  • Switch to subscription works across multiple selling plans for the same product.
  • If the delivery frequencies share a uniform option, such as switching a 6-week item to a 4-week option, both items can be delivered together.
  • Buy More Save More is set up in the cart with editable language.
  • At the time of the webinar, it had no Shopify Function equivalent.
  • So it is set up with Shopify automatic discounts or a third-party app instead.
  • It was largely a Shopify Plus feature.
  • At the time of the webinar, Buy More Save More in Rebuy Smart Cart had no Shopify Function equivalent.
  • It had to be built with Shopify automatic discounts or a third-party app.
  • Rebuy planned a Shopify Functions integration for this offer as its next one.
  • But it did not expect it before the end of that year.
  • ReBuy's smart cart shows a tiered progress bar.
  • It can switch the buyer to subscription options.
  • Both elements can be fully customized to the brand.
  • So the tiers and subscription offer can be set per store.

Cart progress bars (Kaching Cart)

1 min
  • Go to Kaching Cart > Cart settings > Promotions progress bar.
  • Set Reward type to Order discount, and Discount type to Percentage.
  • Set a Min price in USD for each tier. This is the smallest cart total that unlocks it.
  • Use "Add tier" for more tiers.
  • Optionally, exclude specific products from being counted, or celebrate unlocks with confetti.
  • Set the promotion threshold above the price a customer must reach for buying two products.
  • So a 5 percent discount requires buying two items.
  • The example threshold is 50 dollars.
  • The progress bar can hold several reward tiers, for example a 5 percent discount and a higher 15 percent tier.
  • Each tier needs its own threshold above the previous one.
  • Promotion progress bar texts. App placeholders are kept as shown:
Default text: Add ((progress_needed)) to unlock ((next_promo_label))
Unlocked text: ((unlocked_promo_title)) Unlocked!
All promotions unlocked text: all rewards are unlocked
Promo discount text: #1 - ((promo_discount_amount)) discount
Discount text: ((promo_discount_amount)) discount

Bundles and promotions without an app

1 min
  • Run a bundle promotion without an app.
    1. During the promotion window (the time the offer runs), edit the existing product page.
    2. Describe the bundle in the title or description, for example "standing desk plus chair."
    3. Restore the normal product page after the window ends.
  • Create a bundle as a separate product page, or temporarily change an existing page.
  • Then remove it when the promotion ends.
  • Orders from several suppliers (the companies that send you products) are not automated.
  • So each order has to be sent out as it arrives.
  • A bundle can be added as a selectable option on the existing product page.
  • An extra description explains what the upgraded package includes.
  • Ads then keep sending the same visitors to the same page.
  • Alternatively, the bundle can be its own product page and feed item. A feed item is a product entry that ad platforms read.
  • Use the same product title plus a suffix (extra words at the end).
  • This way it still shows for the standard product's search terms.
  • Whichever option you choose, the price changes with the selection, near the add-to-cart button.
