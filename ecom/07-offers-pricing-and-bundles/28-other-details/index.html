Domains and store costs

1 min
  • A domain is your web address, like yourshop.com. Standard domain pricing is $16-$20 per year.
  • Skip premium domains (names with a very high price) that cost hundreds or thousands of dollars, however appealing the name.

Shopify plans and trial offers

3 min

Shopify is a platform for building online stores. Its prices and offers change over time. Check the current figures on the Shopify site before signing up.

  • Standard recommended plan for a new store (USA, 2020 to 2026, as shown at that time): $39 per month when paid monthly, or $29 per month when paid for a full year in advance. A plan is a price package with set features.
  • Signup offer at that time: a 3-day free trial (a test period with no charge) plus a first month for $1 on select plans.
  • Promotion in July 2025: a trial of 3 days free, followed by 3 months for $3 USD. This may have changed since.
  • Store credit: Shopify gave new stores a $50 credit for the Shopify App Store (its shop of add-on apps) at that time. It was shown at the top of the store setup screen. The bonus may not always be offered.
  • Payment: the trial needs no credit card to start. A payment method is needed to subscribe (sign up to pay) after the trial.
  • Contracts: Shopify plans are pay-as-you-go (you pay each month and can stop) with no contract. You can change or cancel the plan at any time before the trial ends, so no charge is made.
  • Cancel-before date: this is 3 days after the trial was created. Cancelling before this date means no charge is made.

Signup flow (setting)

  1. Enter your email in the free-trial field and click Start free trial.
  2. On the quiz screen, click Skip all.
  3. Choose the business location. It defaults to the current location. Change it to the country where the business will operate, then click Next.
  4. Create a password for a new account, or enter the existing password for an existing account, then click Login.

Trial signup procedure

  1. Open the Shopify trial link from the page that gives the offer, so the offer is applied.
  2. Enter an email address and click Start free trial. The email can be personal and changed later. No store name is needed at this point.
  3. On the quiz screen, click Skip all. It does not affect the account.
  4. Select the business location. It defaults to the current country. Change it to the country where you plan to do business, then click Next.
  5. If the email already has a Shopify account, enter the existing password. Otherwise, create a new password. Click Login.
  6. Wait for the store setup process to finish.
  7. Check whether the $50 Shopify App Store credit and the $1 first-month offer are shown at the top of the screen. The credit is not guaranteed and may not appear.
  8. Click Select a plan (available at the top or further down the page).
  9. Choose the Basic plan.
  10. Enter a credit or debit card, or choose PayPal.
  11. Check that the $50 app store credit is included and that the amount due is $1.
  12. Click Subscribe.

Trial and price timeline (USA, 2020 to 2026; check current offers before signing up)

  1. Days 1 to 3: the free trial runs with no charge. No credit card is needed to start it.
  2. Before the cancel-before date shown on the plan page (3 days after the trial was created): select a plan, or cancel to avoid any charge. The plan can be changed or cancelled at any time. There are no contracts.
  3. First paid month: $1 on the Basic plan, the plan the offer applies to.
  4. After the first month: $39 per month when paid monthly, or $29 per month when paid yearly in advance.

Agency and outsourcing costs

2 min

Defining the partner relationship

  • Define the partner relationship you want before negotiating contract terms. A partner relationship is how long and how closely an outside firm works with you. Decide whether you need:
    • short-term tactical support (help for a specific task or short period),
    • a long-term scaling partner (a firm that helps you grow over time), or
    • a transition vendor (an outside helper, with a planned move to in-house staff within a set timeframe).
  • The relationship type shapes contract length, flexibility requirements, and training obligations (who must provide training).

Matching cost to scope

  • Cost structure must reflect the scope of work (how much work you pay for).
  • Managing only paid media (paid ads, such as pulling levers, meaning adjusting settings, in ad accounts) costs less than full agency-of-record responsibility (one firm handles all your marketing). Full responsibility covers strategy, creative sourcing (finding ad images and videos), landing pages (the pages ads send people to), email setup, and analytics (tracking results).
  • Do not pay for breadth you do not need.

Compensation structure

  • Base compensation (a fixed fee) lets the agency budget for labor costs, even if you reduce ad spend for profitability reasons.
  • Percentage-of-spend compensation (a fee based on how much ad money you spend) makes agency income volatile and creates resistance to spending discipline (pushback when you try to spend less).
  • Rule of thumb: the base fee covers labor, and incentives (bonuses for results) cover upside (extra gains).

Hiring a virtual assistant

  • When you are completely new to hiring a virtual assistant (a remote helper who works from another place) and only want to test a candidate, start with an hourly rate (pay per hour worked).
  • Once you have found a good candidate, switch to a fixed monthly price, typically between 300 and 500 USD per month for Filipino virtual assistants.
  • Offer changes can be delegated (handed to them) by stating the new offer directly, for example switching the offer to buy one get one free.

Logo design on Fiverr

Fiverr is an online marketplace for freelance services.

  • Fiverr logo design services start at approximately 53 AUD (Australian dollars, roughly 40 USD) for basic packages. Set your budget maximum at 100 USD. Above that, the investment is not justified for a startup brand.
  • Fiverr organizes each designer's logo service into three package tiers:
    • Basic: the lowest price.
    • Standard: the mid-tier.
    • Premium: the highest price, with extra features.
    • You can upgrade for source file access (the editable design files) or additional deliverables (extra items delivered).
  • Fiverr logo designers typically include up to 5 revisions (rounds of changes) in their packages. You can request design changes up to five times until you reach the final version you want to accept.

Products, variants and inventory

1 min

Variants

  • Set up variants (versions of one product, such as colors and sizes) in the product's variant options. For example, switch a color to black and add a size range.
  • For an early version with one color, a simple single-variant product (one version, no options) is enough. Plan variants before launch.

Pre-orders and uneven demand

  • For product variants with uneven demand (some sell much more than others), use a pre-order (customers buy before the stock arrives) for low-volume variants.
  • This reduces inventory risk (the danger of holding stock you cannot sell) and lets you market all variants without holding slow-moving stock (items that sell slowly).

Backorders

  • Accept backorders (customers order now and get the item later) for products with low current stock.
  • Tell the customer the expected delivery timeline and let them choose to wait.
  • This converts sales you would otherwise lose by removing the product or marking it out of stock.
  • Source age: older advice (2022).

Economic timing and inventory planning

  • A current economic recession does not meaningfully reduce Q4 sales (the last three months of the year), because consumer spending shifts to credit and installment plans (paying in parts).
  • Credit crises (times when lending tightens) typically strike 6-12 months after high spending periods.
  • Plan inventory (the stock you keep) conservatively from mid-2024 onward.
  • Consider shifting to high-ticket products (expensive items) aimed at wealthy buyers, who are less affected by economic cycles (the ups and downs of the economy).

Reviews and trust

1 min

Incentives for video and image reviews

  • Video and image reviews need a significant incentive (a reward for doing it), because they are more demanding for the customer and worth much more to the business.
  • Examples of incentives:
    • a larger rebate (money back) on the reviewed order,
    • a cash-back payment,
    • a $100 Amazon gift card, or
    • another product sold at cost price, including delivery.

Supplier reviews

  • When a store has high volume (many orders) but very few reviews, ask suppliers for reviews. Use them alongside store reviews to build trust.

Review schema (Judge.me)

Judge.me is a review app for Shopify. Schema is hidden code that tells search engines what a product is.

  • Product reviews from Judge.me can be linked into product schema, so star ratings appear next to products in search results.
  • This requires the Pro plan (a paid Judge.me package) and Judge.me installed on the store.

Reciprocity and gifts

1 min

Reciprocation

  • Reciprocation is the urge to pay back a kindness. Give a valuable, unrequested gift first (one the person did not ask for). The recipient feels obliged to respond with a purchase.
  • The gift must be given without being requested. It should be valuable enough to feel thoughtful, not a cheap logo item.

Example: moving service

  • A moving-service business sends gift certificates to new neighbors on behalf of local restaurants and auto shops. Examples are a free pizza, a free entree, or a free oil change.
  • The gift introduces the business and starts the reciprocation effect. Satisfied recipients often return and become long-term customers.

Common mistake

  • A reciprocity gift only creates a sense of obligation when the recipient did not ask for it and it feels valuable.
  • A gift given in exchange for an action the customer already took is a thank-you, not a reciprocity trigger (the thing that starts the pay-back urge).
  • A service provider can offer something like 30 minutes of free time, with no obligation, to create this effect.

Upsells, anchors and entry products

1 min

Add-on upsell template

Use this only for an add-on (an extra item sold with the main one) that completes or speeds up [PRODUCT NAME].

Thanks for choosing [PRODUCT NAME]. Would you like to add [UPSELL NAME] to this order for [UPSELL PRICE]? It [SPECIFIC BENEFIT, e.g. gets you to your first result faster or supplies the part [PRODUCT NAME] needs to work fully], and it ships with your current order. Add it with one click, or continue without it.

Price anchoring for high-ticket services

An anchor is a first, higher price that makes the next price look fair.

  • Start from the price you feel comfortable quoting, then test a number roughly three times higher.
  • If the higher figure still sounds credible when a prospect (a possible customer) hears it, use it as your opening anchor for negotiation.

Entry products

An entry product is a low-priced first purchase.

  • A low-priced digital template (a ready-made file you sell online) can be the first purchase for a buyer who later pays a much higher fee for a workshop. This works if the template solves an early step of the same process.
  • Choose the entry product so that it demonstrates your method. This makes the higher-priced offer the natural next step.

Case study: lemonade stand

1 min

Starting point: a lemonade stand run by a ten-year-old. Typical neighborhood stands brought in $10 to $15 per day and charged 10 to 25 cents per cup.

  1. Phase one (sourcing, meaning finding supplies): lemons were sourced at no cost, which kept cost of goods (what each item costs you) very low.
  2. Phase two (product range): homemade cookies were packaged in packs of 1 and 3. Premium golf balls were collected and resold.
  3. Phase three (location): the stand was placed on a busy street where regular cars and golf carts passed.
  4. Phase four (pricing and pitch, meaning the sales message):
    • Lemonade: $1 for the first cup, 50 cents for a second.
    • Cookies: 1 for $1, or 3 for $2.
    • Golf balls: regular at 50 cents, premium at $2.50.
    • The pitch offered cookies with each drink, and golf balls to golfers who had lost balls. This matched a visible need.

Result: sales reached $63 in one day, about 4 to 6 times the usual stand income. $58 was left after paying a helper $5.

Lessons: small mistakes reduced results. These included missing samples (free tastes), not adjusting pricing, and having a weak sign.

Ad budgets and promotion

2 min

Daily budget guidance

  • Beginners starting with dropshipping (selling products that suppliers ship directly to customers) should start around $25 per day, not $500.
  • A $500 daily budget fits established brands that already have proven organic success (sales that come free, not from paid ads) and reinvest profits into high-quality, trend-aligned content.
  • The expert says $10 per day is the lowest workable level, but $25 has performed better. TikTok's own recommendation of $100 is not considered necessary.

TikTok minimum budget

  • The minimum daily budget on TikTok is $25.
  • The expert kept the daily budget at $25 and set the ad-level budget (the budget for one single ad) higher, up to $50, so the ad could spend more.
  • The budget setup was described inconsistently. Verify the budget fields (the boxes where you type amounts) in Ads Manager (TikTok's ad control panel) before launching.

Promoting a video with coins

Coins are TikTok's in-app currency, used to pay for promotion.

  1. Open the video, tap the three dots at the bottom, and choose Promote.
  2. Under the destination, choose Website visits (instead of the default profile option) to send traffic (visitors) to your store.
  3. Paste the store link, tap Shop now as the button, then tap Next.
  4. Choose the audience (the group of people who see your ad). The default is TikTok's choice, but the author prefers Custom with minors (people under 18) removed. The common selection is:
    • ages 18-24, 25-34, and 35-44,
    • gender set to match the product (all, female, or male),
    • The broad interest option kept.
  5. Tap Save, then Next, then set the budget.

Creator video pricing on Billow

Billow is a service for hiring creators (people who make videos for brands).

  • On Billow, the total price updates after every change to the order. This includes the number of creators, the video type, and the video length.
  • The $99 base price shown for one creator and a 15-second video is only the starting point.
  • Check the final price after each adjustment before clicking Next step.

Order value and revenue benchmarks

1 min

Revenue is total sales money before costs are paid.

  • A $10,000 daily revenue screenshot is usually a 24-hour total from several bundled orders (products sold together), not one product.
  • It can also be a high-ticket niche product (an expensive product for a small market), or a small business ordering several units (pieces) of a $2,000 item.
  • Source age: older advice (2022).
  • The average order value (the average amount spent per order) in the coach's stores in 2020 was about 1,000 USD, aimed at upper-middle-class buyers. This is a 2020 benchmark (a reference point).

Charitable promises

1 min
  • A charitable promise (a pledge to give money to charity) based on net profit (money left after all costs) is clearer than one based on revenue.
  • A pledge of 10 percent of revenue would be far too large for most stores, so the wording must say net profit.

Done-for-you store builds

1 min

A done-for-you store build is a store that someone else builds for you.

  • A done-for-you store build can typically be claimed within 12 months of the purchase date.
  • Keep the purchase email with the claim instructions. If it cannot be found, contact the provider's support address.

Marketplace limits (eBay)

1 min

eBay is an online marketplace where many sellers list items.

  • A new eBay account is initially limited to 3,500 GBP (British pounds) in listed sales value (the total price of items for sale).
  • The limit can be increased after contacting customer service.
  • One seller's limit rose to 70,000 GBP after a few calls within about two weeks.
