Playbook › 11. TikTok and short video

Campaign setup: objectives, targeting and placements

Section 11.15 · 15 of 22 in this chapter · 8 min read

In short

  • Start broad. In the first test, use no interests.
  • Run ads on TikTok only. Remove Global App Bundle and Pangle, which are other apps where TikTok can show ads.
  • Build five ad groups: two open, two with stacked interests, and one with hashtags.
  • Let the test run for two days before you judge it.
  • Check each audience. If it has fewer than 8 million people, turn on targeting expansion.

Setup order for a website conversion test campaign

2 min
  1. Open the TikTok ad account (the account that pays for and runs your ads). Go to Campaigns (the list of your tests; one campaign is one test with its own budget) and click Create.
  2. Choose Custom mode instead of Simplified mode (the shorter setup with fewer options).
  3. Under Conversions, select Website conversions as the advertising objective (the goal of the campaign; a conversion is an action you want, such as a sale). Click Continue.
  4. Name the ad group after the interest being tested. An ad group is a set of ads that share one audience and settings. An interest is a topic a person has shown they like. If no interests are used, name it "broad". Then continue.
  5. Select the website pixel you created, or create a new one. A pixel is a small tracking code on your store that reports sales back to TikTok.
  6. Set the optimization event (the action TikTok tries to get more of) to Complete Payment.
  7. Under Placements (where the ad appears), remove Global App Bundle and Pangle so only TikTok remains. These are networks of other apps.
  8. Under Demographics and location (who sees the ad: age, gender and country), choose the target countries.
  9. Set gender and age (all ages from 18+). Leave spending power (how much people usually spend) and audience include/exclude (adding or blocking groups of people) unchanged. Then turn on targeting expansion (lets TikTok reach beyond your choices).
  10. Under Budget and schedule, go back to the campaign level. Turn on Campaign budget optimization (TikTok spreads your budget across ad groups). Set the daily campaign budget (the amount spent per day). Example: 50 dollars.
  11. Click Continue. Set the start date to the next day at 6 a.m. or midnight, then click Next.
  12. Name the ad. Leave Spark Ads off. Spark Ads are ads that run through an existing TikTok post. Enter the store name in the ad sign (the name shown on the ad) and upload the logo.
  13. Upload the video. Add the ad text with a simple call to action (a short line that tells people what to do next). Click Continue.
  14. Add the product page link. Click Submit to publish.
  15. After publishing, click the duplicate button (copy button) on the ad group four times to get five ad groups in total.
  16. Let the campaign run for two days.

Entry points and objective

1 min
  • Two entry paths lead to the same settings.
  • Path A: click Create an ad, choose Website conversions, then select Custom mode instead of Simplified. The full set of options then appears.
  • Path B: go to Campaigns and click Create, then select Custom mode.
  • Select Website conversions again inside Custom mode.
  • In the ad group setup, after you choose the Website conversions objective and name the ad group, the optimization location is set to the website.
  • Then the store's pixel is selected in the same section. Only after that is the optimization event chosen.

Placements

1 min
  • Remove all placements except TikTok, so the ad runs only on TikTok.
  • Remove Global App Bundle and Pangle.

Countries and language

1 min
  • The countries you can target depend on the country where the TikTok ad account is registered. Only options for that market appear.
  • German-speaking markets: target Switzerland, Germany and Austria.
  • English-speaking markets: target the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and the UK.
  • Keep the language setting broad. Users may have their phones set to a different language than the one they speak.

Age and gender

1 min
  • Age for broad testing: select all age ranges from 18+.
  • Age for open targeting (no interests chosen, so the ad can reach anyone in your filters), default: use 18+.
  • Age for open targeting, larger audience: if the 25+ audience exceeds about 10 million, use 25+. That group buys more.
  • Age for open targeting, 18 to 24 group: it often gives strong engagement (likes, comments and shares) and clicks.
  • But the 18 to 24 group often gives weaker purchase conversion.
  • The two age rules apply at different times.
  • The broad-testing setup in this handbook uses all ages from 18+.
  • The open-targeting rule uses 18+ as the default.
  • It switches to 25+ only when the 25+ audience is above 10 million.
  • Gender: choose based on the product. If most buyers are female, select female.
  • Let targeting expansion reach other genders where TikTok sees potential.
  • Age and gender are the only filters kept on broad setups. Filter both by product.

Targeting strategy

2 min
  • Broad targeting works better on TikTok than specific interest targeting. The reason is that the algorithm (the system that picks who sees your ad) is newer and still learning.
  • Let the creative (the video and text) decide which customers it reaches. Do not pick interests first.
  • TikTok has always used very broad targeting. The creative and the message must carry the audience fit, not the targeting settings.
  • Testing on TikTok can reach a younger audience than Facebook.
  • Interest targeting (choosing topics people like) used to perform well on TikTok. It now works less well.
  • The recommended setup is completely broad targeting with no interest filters.
  • A broad audience works well only for products with very wide mass-market appeal (a product most kinds of people want).
  • Otherwise, use targeted interests.
  • Start broad with no interests in the first test. Do not narrow to specific interests from the beginning.
  • Open targeting is usually the cheapest audience on TikTok. The platform can show ads to anyone.
  • Stacked interests (several interests combined in one ad group) usually have higher CPMs (the cost to show the ad 1,000 times). The reason is that they are narrower.
  • Treat stacked interests as a second layer. Use them to find a different pocket (group) of buyers.
  • For purchase intention, turn on the TikTok "Purchase intention" option inside interest targeting.
  • Then select broad interests under it.
  • This option reaches users who have shown purchase behavior, meaning they have bought or shown they buy things.
  • It is meant to attract higher-intent buyers (people more ready to buy) than plain interest targeting.
  • Automated targeting (TikTok picks the audience itself) can work for broad, relatable products that appeal to many people. Examples: simple life-hack or fitness products.
  • Test it as an alternative. Keep it secondary to the open and interest structure for most products.
  • Household income (the income level of a household) and interest targeting were left at default in the setup shown here.
  • They can be tested, but they were not used as a standard setting.
  • Spending power and audience include/exclude stay at their defaults for broad testing.

Audience size and expansion

1 min
Situation Rule Ids
Interest or open audience, target size At least about 8 to 9 million people
Audience under about 4 to 5 million Usually shows clearly high CPMs
Interest audience below 8 million Turn on targeting expansion to widen toward about 10 million
Open or broad, no interests or custom audiences (groups built from your own lists) selected Targeting expansion applies
US broad open audience (18+, all genders, no interests) Estimated 150 to 200 million reachable people
  • Smaller audiences tend to produce expensive CPMs. They are also hard to scale (grow spending without the cost rising).
  • Expansion lets TikTok reach beyond the chosen interests when the audience is small.
  • Small budgets reach only a small group of the broad US audience.
  • This is why separate campaigns are used to find the right group.

Test campaign structure

1 min
  • Use 5 to 6 ad groups: two open-targeting ad groups (18+ with no interests), two stacked-interest ad groups, and one hashtag ad group.
  • Split related interests across two stacked-interest ad groups with roughly five interests in each.
  • Keep hashtags in a separate third ad group.
  • Before launching, check that each combined audience reaches at least 8 to 10 million people.
  • Duplicate a finished ad group and change only the audience. This keeps the creatives identical across groups.
  • The setup order produces five ad groups in total by duplicating the first one four times.

Hashtag audiences

1 min
  • Some TikTok hashtags (words starting with #) do not appear as targeting options in TikTok Ads Manager (TikTok's tool for running ads). This happens even when they have hundreds of millions of views.
  • Hashtags tied to a brand or a specific product are likely to be excluded.
  • A hashtag may need to exist for some time before it becomes available.
  • The two rules differ in emphasis. The hashtag procedure asks for hashtags that describe the product and its niche (a small, specific market). The availability rule warns that brand- or product-specific hashtags are likely to be missing.
  • Use niche-level hashtags and check each one for availability.

Procedure:

  1. Choose hashtags that describe the product and its niche.
  2. Search each hashtag on TikTok without ads. Note the ones that appear often in recent videos.
  3. Keep the frequently used hashtags. Add them to the hashtag ad group.
  4. Check the audience size. Turn on targeting expansion if it is below 8 million.

Budget and schedule

1 min
  • Set the budget with Campaign budget optimization turned on, at campaign level.
  • Example daily campaign budget: 50 dollars.
  • Start date: set it to the next day at 6 a.m. or at midnight. Then click Next.
  • Default run hours: start the ad group schedule (the times the ad is allowed to run) the next day at midnight and run all day.
  • Or run from 7 a.m. to midnight.
  • Narrow the hours only when past data shows that traffic (visitors to your store) arrives at certain times without producing sales.
  • Ad schedules can be set to run at chosen times instead of running continuously.
  • Let the campaign run for two days before judging it.

Naming

1 min
  • Name each ad group after the interest it tests. Results can then be traced to that interest.
  • If no interest is used, name it "broad".
  • Give each video ad a sequential name (numbered in order), such as "add 1" for the first video.
  • This lets you identify every creative later when comparing results within an ad group.

Ad configuration

1 min
  • Name the ad. Leave Spark Ads off.
  • The option "use TikTok account to deliver Spark Ads" is left off for this test structure.
  • Enter the store name in the ad sign. Then upload the logo.
  • Upload the video. Add the ad text with a simple call to action. Click Continue.
  • Destination (where people go after they click): under the link field, add the product page URL (the web address of the product page).
  • Then click Submit to publish the campaign.

Comment filters

1 min
  • Comment filters (settings that control which comments appear on an ad) can be set on ads in TikTok Ads Manager.
  • Use these settings when you run your own ads.
