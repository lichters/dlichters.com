Playbook › 11. TikTok and short video

Coins, spark ads and paid boosting of organic videos

Section 11.14 · 14 of 22 in this chapter · 12 min read

In short

  • Test a product with free organic TikTok posts first. Check sales over 3 to 7 days.
  • Boost only a video that already works. Keep the paid boost to 3 days.
  • Buy coins on desktop, not the phone. Phone coins cost about 31% more.
  • Keep paid Spark Ads on a separate account. Never link your organic account.
This section has 12 parts. Tap one to open it.

Choosing between organic testing and paid boosting

2 min
  • Test the product with organic content first. Organic means free posts, not paid ads. Create organic TikTok videos and measure how the product performs over 3 to 7 days.
  • Boost only a video that is already performing. A video with strong engagement (likes, comments, shares, watch time) and many views can become a Spark Ad. A Spark Ad is a paid ad that runs from that organic video's link (URL).
  • Spark Ads usually beat ads made from scratch. They use real engagement signals (proof that people actually respond).
  • A two-day test with a small budget shows whether a product gets sales. It also shows whether the product will probably get organic sales once views come in.
  • Coins help a new account's video reach the right audience faster, so organic reach can follow. Coins are TikTok's paid boost currency, bought with money. The paid part lets TikTok learn the audience early. The organic part can be much larger.
  • Coins are most useful once your content format already works. They are also most useful when you run multiple accounts on the same product.
  • For a product that already has proven sales, start a new TikTok account for it. Spend on coins only during the first few days, so TikTok finds the right audience faster. Limit boosts to that window to avoid overspending.

Procedure: accelerate organic reach for a product that already sells

  1. Choose a product that has already produced proven sales.
  2. Start a new TikTok account for it.
  3. Spend on coins (paid boosts) during only the first few days so TikTok finds the right audience faster.
  4. Use the new account advantage during that window.
  5. Do not overspend on coins. Limit the boosts to the first few days.

Example (one case, not a guaranteed result): A product that had already passed six-figure sales was pushed on a new account with paid coins. The new account's video went viral with millions of views within three days. This suggests the early paid boost can compound with the new account advantage.

Timing: when to boost

1 min
  • Spark Ads on a viral organic video (one that spread very fast) are usually run after the viral effect has passed. Run them once the video has slowed down. The video then carries social proof (it already shows that people like it).
  • Organic reach on TikTok is inconsistent. Videos can reach very high views in the first hour and then stop. Whether running ads stops organic growth is unproven, so test it yourself.
  • Some videos go viral repeatedly. One reported video gained another 5 million views every few months. Such videos are good Spark Ad candidates (good choices to run as ads) after their viral phase has passed.
  • If a viral video's views start declining, wait a day or two, then add coins to it.
  • Use coins or Spark Ads on a video that is growing but not yet getting sales. Do this only after the audience has been checked.
  • A Reach campaign (an ad goal that shows a video to as many people as possible) can kick-start a new account. It puts a test video in front of many people in the target audience (the group you want to sell to). If those viewers watch to the end and engage, average watch time (how long people watch on average) rises. The platform then distributes the video to larger groups. Use this to speed up organic growth during testing.

Procedure: Spark Ads trigger for a test video

  1. Pick the best organic video from the first days.
  2. If it reaches roughly 1,000 views on day two or three, set up a Spark Ads campaign for it.

Buying coins

1 min

Where and how to buy

  • Buy coins on desktop (a computer), not on the phone. Desktop is cheaper and gives more coins per dollar. Promote the video afterward from the phone app.
  • Coins bought on the phone cost about 31% more for the same amount.
  • Coins bought on the TikTok desktop version cost about 31% less than on mobile.
  • Price points cited:
    • 1,000 coins cost about 10 USD and fund about 2 days of promotion. A thousand coins is a good starting amount.
    • On desktop, $14.80 buys 1,400 coins. This is described as roughly three days of testing.
  • TikTok sometimes sends notifications with coin promo deals of around 20% off. Check these before buying at full price.
  • If you use a VPN (a tool that changes your internet location), buying coins on desktop may cause problems. Try the purchase in the phone browser instead. Avoid the in-app purchase, which costs about 31% more.

Procedure: buy coins on desktop

  1. Log in to TikTok on desktop using the QR code login (you scan a code with your phone to sign in).
  2. Open the coin purchase page and select the 1,400-coin package for $14.80.
  3. Complete the purchase. The coins are added to your account automatically.

Login tip: Log into your TikTok account on desktop. Use the QR code login for faster sign-in if needed.

Return to the app: Buy the coins on desktop. Then return to the phone app, where the coins boost prompt (the pop-up that starts a boost) appears automatically.

Coins: setup, objective and audience

1 min

Procedure: coins setup, in this order

  1. Buy coins on the desktop version of TikTok, which is about 31% cheaper than mobile.
  2. Go back to the phone app. The coins prompt appears automatically.
  3. Choose the goal: More video views, More website visits, or More followers.
  4. Choose the audience: TikTok's automatic targeting (the platform picks people for you), or Custom (you pick).
  5. For Custom, select interests, the age range, and gender (male, female, or both).
  6. Choose the button call-to-action (CTA, the button text people tap), such as Shop now. Then enter the website URL.
  7. Set the daily amount and number of days. For example, $10 per day for 4 days = $40 total. Check the estimated click range shown before confirming.

Objective

  • Choose the coins promotion objective (the goal you pick for the boost) before setting the budget. Website visits send paid traffic (paid visitors) to your store. Video views only increase reach.
  • Use website visits once the store is ready to take visitors.

Age targeting

  • When targeting coins by age, exclude 13 to 17 year olds because they rarely buy.
  • Typical adult ranges are 18 to 45 or 18 to 54, combined with a gender choice.
  • You can target audiences aged 18 and up when boosting with coins.

Review and link rules

  • TikTok reviews coin-boosted videos before promotion.
  • Campaigns aimed at views face fewer restrictions than campaigns that add a website link. Keep links out of boosts when possible.
  • Views-based objectives are more lenient than traffic objectives that include a link.

Audience finding through comments

Procedure

  1. Read the comments from people who say they want to buy the product.
  2. Open those commenters' profiles. Look at their photos and videos to identify who they are, for example a mom account.
  3. Find other accounts that look similar. This defines the group you want to reach.
  4. Set the age range and audience of your coins or Spark Ads to match that group.

Coins: budget, duration and music

1 min
  • The coins promotion budget can be as low as 5 USD per day per video.
  • The typical plan is 10 USD per day on one video for 3 days. For example, that is 30 USD of coins spread over three days.
  • Running coins longer than 3 days is not recommended.
  • The setup example shows a 4-day run ($10 per day for 4 days = $40 total).
  • Use the 3-day plan as the recommended limit.
  • The 4-day example is only an illustration of the setup screens.
  • A coins boost runs over a 24-hour window from its start time. It spreads the budget across that period.
  • Coins only work on videos with original or licensed music. They do not work with copyrighted trending sounds (popular songs you do not have rights to).

Coins: what they deliver

1 min
  • Paid TikTok views cost roughly 2,000 USD per 1 million views at the time discussed. At that rate, 8 million views would cost at least 16,000 USD in ads.
  • This makes organic reach valuable for products with limited budgets.
  • In the cited test, a $100 coins budget generated about 100,000 views. That is roughly a fifth of the Spark Ads result at the same spend.
  • Coins deliver views more slowly, over a roughly 24-hour window. They showed no visible harm to organic reach.

  • Choose Reach when the goal is views or an organic push (a push to get more free views).

Spark Ads: how they work

1 min
  • Spark Ads run an existing organic video directly from the connected TikTok account. No new video needs to be produced.
  • Spark Ads boost an existing organic post so it runs as an ad from your own account.
  • When viewers tap through, they see your profile and earlier videos. A link on the ad lets them buy directly.
  • Spark Ads run under the account's own profile. Viewers can see the followers and buy directly from the ad with a shop-now button.
  • This avoids sending viewers to the profile to find the link.
  • Spark Ads can run without a TikTok pixel (a tracking code that records sales from ads) or a Shopify store link (the connection to your Shopify online shop).
  • The reason is that the ad uses the authorization code of the organic video.
  • A pixel can be added later if conversion tracking is needed.
  • One seller skipped the pixel for low-budget Spark Ad tests.
  • Spark Ads deliver views fast. They can slightly reduce organic reach at times.

Identifying a Spark Ad

  • To tell a Spark Ad (a boosted organic post) from a copied ad, open the account behind the ad.
  • A Spark Ad links to the real account that posted the video.
  • A copied ad runs under a different name with no genuine TikTok presence.

Spark Ads: authorization codes and authorized posts

2 min
  • Two routes appear in the sources.
  • Route one is linking. Linking the account in Business Settings (TikTok's account settings page) makes it selectable as an identity (the name and logo shown on the ad) in ad groups. An ad group is a set of ads that share one budget and audience.
  • Route two is the authorized post. It runs a Spark Ad without linking the account.
  • One source warns never to click the option to link your account for Spark Ads.
  • Choose the authorized-post route when you want to keep the organic account unlinked.

Connecting an account (linking route)

  • To use an organic TikTok account for Spark Ads, connect it in the business settings of TikTok Ads Manager (TikTok's ad control panel).
  • Once connected, it appears in the identity list when you set up an ad group.
  • You can choose it instead of a brand name and logo.

Generating a Spark Ad code

  1. In the TikTok app, open the Profile and go to Creator tools (the menu of extra tools for creators).
  2. Scroll down to Settings, add settings, and enable the ad settings.
  3. Open a video that is not copyrighted, and tap the three dots (the "more" menu).
  4. Slide the bottom menu (Analytics, Save video, Pin, Promote) to the left.
  5. Tap Add settings, then Add authorization.
  6. Generate the code.
  7. In TikTok Ads Manager, choose the option to deliver content through Spark Ads. Paste the code. The account is recognized without logging in.

Authorized TikTok Post method, without linking the account

  1. In the TikTok app, open Creator tools and turn on Ad settings.
  2. Open the video you want to run. Confirm the music is not copyrighted, otherwise it cannot run.
  3. Tap the three dots on the video, choose Ad settings, then choose Generate code.
  4. Send the code to yourself and copy it.
  5. In TikTok Ads Manager, open the Authorized TikTok Post option. Paste the code into the field, then search.
  6. Your account should appear. Select the option Use authorized account, not the option to link your account.
  7. Under Add creative (the video or image in the ad), choose TikTok post and select the video that appears.
  8. Choose whether to duplicate the post, and edit how it appears on the For You page (TikTok's main feed).
  9. Enter the product page URL (the web address of the product) so viewers who click go directly to the product.
  10. Choose the call to action. Standard is Shop now. Dynamic lets TikTok choose.
  11. Scroll down and click Submit.

Upload without the Spark option

  • If you upload a video without selecting the option to use a TikTok account for Spark Ads, the ad runs as a regular TikTok ad.
  • In that case your organic account does not appear on the ad.

Spark Ads: volume and budget

1 min

Procedure: scale one winning video into many ad groups

  1. Pick the best organic video from the first days.
  2. If it reaches roughly 1,000 views on day two or three, set up a Spark Ads campaign for it.
  3. Create one ad group containing only that video, with a $25 budget.
  4. Set the payment method to manual (you add money in advance). Load $25 in advance so the ad spends within minutes.
  5. Duplicate the ad group to create 20 to 30 copies, each with a $25 budget.
  6. Do not add more money to the same video.
  • Pouring repeated Spark Ads money into one video tends to suppress its later organic reach. Organic distribution usually picks up after two to three days.
  • Spend on a video once and let it run. Do not repeatedly top it up.
  • For Spark Ads volume, set up 20 ad groups with one creative in each.
  • With a $100 spend, the cited test generated about 500,000 views. The approach pushes the post into distribution.
  • One operator used Spark Ads on a product with about 50 million views per week.
  • A 50 USD test on Spark Ads was run, then stopped after a few days.

Spark Ads and organic reach

1 min
  • Sellers disagree on whether Spark Ads reduce organic reach.
  • One advertiser reported that they do.
  • Another saw an organic boost after a small spend ended.
  • Opinions were mixed and based on small samples.
  • Test Spark Ads on a separate ads account. Watch the organic views of the main account before scaling spend.
  • If you run both paid Spark Ads and organic content, keep them on two separate TikTok accounts.
  • One account handles the paid ads. The other stays purely organic.
  • Never click the option to link your TikTok account to deliver Spark Ads.
  • Once linked, the organic account should be considered lost. The platform tends to limit organic views on accounts used for paid promotion.
  • A seller's other videos can show whether a viral result is repeatable.
  • If they show no sign of the paid-boost pattern, the reach likely came from organic posting alone.

Judging viral results and sales

1 min
  • Spark Ads are a possible reason when sales arrive in short bursts despite huge view counts.
  • Judging them unlikely just because an account has many followers is weak evidence.
  • Compare the timing of sales with the timing of the views instead.
