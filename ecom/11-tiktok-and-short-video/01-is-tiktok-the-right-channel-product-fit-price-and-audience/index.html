Is TikTok the right channel? product fit, price and audience

Section 11.1 · 1 of 22 in this chapter · 8 min read

In short

  • Set up your TikTok account after the store is ready. Pay for TikTok ads only after a product already makes profit.
  • Use a niche store that sells one product, priced about 15 to 40 USD.
  • Free social posts do not bring sales. Paid ads do.
  • If you cannot make new ad videos steadily, start with Facebook ads, not TikTok.
  • Products that people eat or drink often fail on TikTok, even when they go viral.
This section has 9 parts. Tap one to open it.

Decide Whether TikTok Fits Your Store

1 min
  • Set up the TikTok account as its own step in the store checklist. Test TikTok ads (paid videos on TikTok) only after the store is ready.
  • Run a niche store (a shop about one narrow topic) that sells one product. General stores do not perform on TikTok ads, and niche stores selling one product perform best.
  • Do not expect organic social growth (free, unpaid reach) to produce sales. One store grew to about 1,500 to 1,600 Instagram followers and over 2,000 TikTok followers within 30 days with zero sales, while paid advertising drives sales.
  • Pay for ads only after a product has sales and profit to reinvest.
  • In the early stage with small budgets, do not split spend onto TikTok ads. Put the budget into Google and Meta (the company behind Facebook and Instagram ads), which deliver most of the results for this model.
  • In the team's experience, TikTok ads had not been tested for high-ticket drop-shipping (selling expensive items, where the supplier ships each order straight to the customer). So the team focused its time on Google and Facebook.
  • Short-form video (short vertical videos) on Instagram Reels and TikTok was not used for e-commerce marketing (selling online) at that time. So budget went into Google and Facebook first. (Source A; two identical entries are merged)

Account Setup and Ban Risk

1 min
  • Ban risk means the chance the platform shuts your account down. TikTok ad accounts are generally easier to open and carry less ban risk than Meta accounts.
  • TikTok has a low barrier to account creation (it is easy to sign up), fewer ban issues than Meta, and better support, with human replies to tickets (messages to support).
  • Facebook has more targeting power (control over exactly who sees your ad) than TikTok. So the creative (the ad video or image) and audience drive results differently on each platform.

Audience and Targeting

1 min
  • Most TikTok traffic (visitors) comes from users aged about 21 to 30, so products should appeal to young buyers.
  • Open targeting (showing ads without narrow filters) works best on TikTok. Narrow targeting of buyers aged 30 to 40 plus is less effective, because the algorithm (TikTok's system that picks who sees each ad) does not target them well.
  • TikTok uses broad audiences (wide groups, few filters) where the creative is the main variable. Facebook can find buyers through better targeting once the account is safe.
  • Products for an older audience are better tested on Facebook. Products for a younger audience should be researched directly on TikTok.
  • Widen product ideas beyond one audience. A bottle opener aimed only at beer drinkers over 21 is narrow. Showing soda or other beverage bottles can reach more age groups.

Product Types That Tend to Sell

1 min
  • Unique, attractive or shiny-object (eye-catching, trendy) products tend to sell on TikTok.
  • Life-hack tools (small gadgets that solve everyday problems) sell well, as do niches such as jewelry, beauty and women-focused products.
  • Broader products tend to work better on TikTok. Competition (how many sellers already offer it) matters for a specific product.
  • A product that already won on Facebook can be tested on TikTok. The TikTok audience has grown a lot since the earlier winning period. A product that sold well in an earlier year may still have an unreached TikTok audience that has never seen it.
  • A proven product can be tested on TikTok, which was a new platform in the case, because a new platform brings a new audience. Test the same product's hook (the first seconds that stop people scrolling) and creative there before dropping it.
  • A product that struggles on Facebook may do well on TikTok, which can pick up the slack.

Product Types That Tend to Fail

1 min

High-ticket products (expensive items)

  • For high-ticket products above about $200, return on ad spend (ROAS, money earned back for each dollar spent on ads) is usually too low to justify the budget.
  • TikTok ads are not recommended for cold traffic (people who have never heard of your store) to a website. They reach people who may not buy high-ticket products.
  • He did not put organic effort into TikTok for higher-ticket products.

Print-on-demand products (items printed only after an order comes in)

  • For print-on-demand products, TikTok ads were reported to bring little success compared with other channels.
  • Organic TikTok reach (how many people see free posts) is hard to achieve for print-on-demand products, and it has been slowing down in general. Plan to spend on paid ads rather than rely only on organic short video.

Older-audience and practical comfort products

  • Practical comfort products that sell well as Facebook ads often underperform as TikTok ads. Test such products on Meta first before adding TikTok to the plan.
  • Products that target an older audience are better tested on Facebook.

Ingestible and finished goods (items people eat or drink, and other finished items)

  • Some brands found that TikTok ads, including Spark Ads (ads that boost a real post), and Facebook ads did not become profitable for certain products, such as beverages and some finished goods. For those products, creator-led content (videos made by people with their own audience) was the way to keep sales going.
  • Buyers hesitate over products that go into food or drinks, even with a decent website. (See the case below.)

Sequencing for Beginners

1 min
  • Two sources disagree on where beginners should start:
    • TikTok is beginner friendly, with fewer ban issues than Meta, better support, and a low barrier to account creation.
    • Beginners should master Facebook ads first before running TikTok. TikTok ads die out fast, so the account needs a constant stream of new creatives, which is hard to produce well.
  • The stated constraint in the second view decides which applies. If the team cannot produce a steady stream of new creatives, start with Facebook.
  • Creative lifespan (how long an ad keeps working before people tire of it) on TikTok is about one week. So scaling (growing spend and sales) depends on a steady process for producing new creatives.
  • Run Facebook and TikTok in parallel (at the same time) for the same product to see which platform performs better, because results differ by product.
  • A product that works on TikTok may not work on Facebook, and vice versa (the other way round), because the audiences are different.
  • In 2023, TikTok ads were a newer channel with less saturation (too many sellers competing for the same buyers). Cost per click (what one click costs), click-through rate (share of viewers who click), and conversions (buyers) were cheaper than on many other paid channels. But that advantage erodes as more advertisers join. (2023 conditions)
  • Retargeting (showing ads again to people who already visited your store) on TikTok can work, but it adds an extra platform to manage. Meta and Google remarketing (the same idea under another name) usually earn more profit.

Testing Winners Across Platforms

1 min

Procedure: use TikTok to find winning creatives before paying for them on Facebook.

  1. Test several videos as TikTok organic posts (free posts).
  2. Find the video that goes viral (spreads very fast).
  3. Use that video in Facebook ads, cut to the Facebook formats if needed.

Running one video on Facebook and posting one on TikTok without testing is not worth it.

  • Trending products from TikTok can be tested on Facebook with a different creative. The product stays the same, and only the ad creative changes.
  • Test the product on both channels. TikTok can open a separate marketing stream even when a product works on only one platform.
  • For a product that already works on TikTok, TikTok's own ad platform may perform better than Meta ads for the same product.

Case: Viral Video, Few Sales

1 min
  • Product: a ceramic ice cube.
  • Hook: a video asked whether the cube makes drinks taste like metal. Then it showed the ceramic cube, so viewers stayed to find out what it was.
  • Result: about 15 million views and only about 25 sales.
  • Cause of low sales: buyers hesitate over products that go into food or drinks, even with a decent website.
  • Comment section: comments were left unfiltered (not checked or hidden), so complaints dominated it.
  • Competitor: a competitor who copied the video frame by frame also went viral. But the unfiltered negative comments damaged that account.
