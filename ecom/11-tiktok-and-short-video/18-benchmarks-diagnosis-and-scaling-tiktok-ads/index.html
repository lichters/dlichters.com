Benchmarks, diagnosis and scaling TikTok ads

Section 11.18 · 18 of 22 in this chapter · 5 min read

In short

  • Strong video ads come first. Scaling means spending more on what already works, and it only works when the ads are strong.
  • Only scale when all four checks pass: cost per click, cost per 1,000 views, click rate and store sales rate.
  • Check your ad claims against the platform's rules before you scale, not after the first sales.
  • On TikTok ads alone, plan a second channel or new ad strategy before you go past about $1,000 per day.

Creative First: The Precondition for Scaling

  • TikTok (the short-video app) depends heavily on the creative, meaning the video ad itself. It depends much less on the scaling strategy.
  • Self-scaling means the campaign raises its own budget step by step. CBO means one budget for the whole campaign. ABO means a separate budget for each ad group.
  • Self-scaling, CBO and ABO work mainly because the creatives are strong, not because of the method itself.
  • Before scaling, secure high-quality creatives.
  • You can film original content.
  • Or you can replicate competitor content in your own style. Do not copy it directly.
  • Running the same ads that already work for a successful product gives no advantage.
  • A test has to differ from the product's original winning approach. It must differ in angle (the hook or message), platform or funnel (the path from ad to purchase).
  • Scale by making more distinct video creatives, not by simply raising budget.
  • Raising budget too fast with too few creatives stops results quickly.
  • Lower competition is also a reason to focus on creatives.

Benchmarks: CPM, CPC, CTR and Conversion

These are the key numbers:

  • CPM is the cost per 1,000 views.
  • CPC is the cost per click.
  • CTR (click-through rate) is the share of viewers who click.
  • Conversion rate is the share of visitors who buy.
Metric Reference or target Context Source

CPC scale trigger Below $1 Part of the scale-readiness gate

  • Use 1.5% as the bar for scaling decisions.
  • Expect that many ads will sit below it.
  • Accept about 0.8% when CPM is low and the store converts well.

Diagnosing Add-to-Cart Without Sales

  • Add-to-cart means a shopper puts an item in their basket.
  • When TikTok traffic gives an add-to-cart rate of about 10% to 12% but almost no purchases, the audience is often the problem, not the page.
  • Excluding a younger group that browses without buying (for example 18 to 24) can lower add-to-cart volume.
  • The same exclusion can raise the purchase conversion rate.
  • Judge audiences by purchase conversion, not by the number of add-to-carts.

Scale-Readiness Gate

Start scaling when all of the following hold:

  1. CPC is below $1.
  2. CPM is below $10.
  3. CTR is above 1.5%.
  4. The store is making sales with a conversion rate above 1.5% to 2%.
  • Before scaling, check the claims in the ads against platform policy (the platform's own rules).
  • Do not wait for the first sales to find out.

Scaling Methods

CBO scaling procedure

  1. Launch a CBO campaign at a $250-$300 budget.
  2. Create 4 ad groups (also called ad sets, which are groups of ads with their own audience and settings).
  3. Place your best-performing creative in each ad group.
  4. Set up automated rules (settings the app runs on its own) to manage performance.
  5. If profitable (you earn more than you spend), scale by self-scaling, for example 250 → 350 → 500 → 700.
  6. Verify profitability after each increase.

Same-day budget increase procedure

  1. On a day when an ad set performs well, raise its budget that same day.
  2. Check results within about three to four hours.
  3. If results stay good, keep the increased budget and continue.

Duplication

  • To scale an ad that works, duplicate (copy) the ad set into a CBO campaign.
  • Use a budget of $51 instead of $50.
  • The author's experience was that a $51 budget bids better against competitors. A bid is the price you offer to win an ad spot.
  • Open targeting (no interest filter, a broad audience) usually performs well on TikTok.
  • When a particular interest is working, duplicate it to reach the right audience.
  • Duplicate winning creatives as well.

Cost cap

  • A cost cap (the highest price per result you allow) works well for scaling TikTok ads.
  • Use it once a winning creative and audience have been identified.

Targeting for new CBOs

  • Targeting means choosing who sees the ad.
  • Scaling can mean launching new CBOs with the same broad targeting.
  • Alternatively, test interest targeting (choosing audiences by their interests) by filling the ad group audience instead of leaving it blank.
  • Sources differ on the default. says open targeting usually performs well. recommends testing interests.
  • Use open targeting as the default.
  • Test interests as a separate step.

New countries

  • When ads meet the metric targets and the market is narrow, such as only the US, test additional countries.
  • Examples are Canada, Australia and New Zealand.
  • You can also test entirely different markets.

Spend Ceilings

  • Spend on TikTok ads alone reached about $1,000 per day in the source.
  • Going higher became difficult without a different strategy.
  • Products priced below $60 held up better at higher spend than products priced above $60.
  • Plan a second channel or a new creative strategy before exceeding about $1,000 per day.
  • In another source, the same level appears in euros: about 1,000 euros per day, consistently, for winning products in a niche (a narrow market group).
  • That source notes that the niche limited this scale.
  • Read the two figures as roughly the same size. They are in different currencies.
  • The dollar figure applies to the US-dollar context.
  • The euro figure applies to the euro context of its source.

Multi-Account Fallback

  • A fallback is a backup plan.
  • Running several TikTok ad accounts in parallel lets a seller move spend to a backup account when one is restricted (limited or blocked by the platform).
  • This is a fallback for account bans (permanent blocks).
  • It is not an officially allowed way around policy violations.
  • So the underlying policy issue still has to be fixed.
  • On TikTok, a strong product with strong creatives tends to perform even on a new ad account.
  • Weaker products depend heavily on the standing and history of the account (how it has performed before).
  • When testing across accounts, judge the product and creative, since these are the most consistent variables (the things that stay steady and change results most).

Ad Restrictions and Prohibited Claims

  • TikTok can flag (mark) and ban ads or products that make before-and-after claims or weight-loss claims.
  • A claim is a promise the ad makes.
  • This often happens within a week or two of launch.
  • Check the claims against platform policy before scaling, not after the first sales arrive.
  • If an account is restricted, the backup-account fallback applies.
  • The policy issue must still be fixed.
