TikTok ads account setup, pixel and tracking

Section 11.13 · 13 of 22 in this chapter · 7 min read

In short

  • One country, one ad account: each country you sell to usually needs its own TikTok ad account, made in that country.
  • Use a computer, not a phone: the phone version of the ad tool (Ads Manager) does not work for this setup.
  • Workarounds are risky: a VPN (a tool that makes your location look like another country), random phone numbers and new devices can get accounts flagged or shut down.
  • Replace the pixel often: a pixel (a small tracking code that records visits and sales) wears out, so change it every 3 to 7 days.
This section has 10 parts. Tap one to open it.

Account Types and Target Countries

1 min
  • Personal ad accounts showed higher CPMs (cost per 1,000 views) than agency accounts running the same ad setup. The source thinks this is likely due to trust factors (signs that TikTok sees the account as trustworthy).
  • Each target country (the country you want to sell to) usually needs its own TikTok ad account, registered in that market. An account from one country usually cannot target people in another. Plan separate accounts for Australia, New Zealand, the UK or European markets before you launch tests there.
  • TikTok Ads Manager (TikTok's tool for running ads) only lets advertisers target their own country. A UK advertiser who wants US ads has to use a VPN on a computer to reach a US-region ads account. This setup was described as unreliable and prone to problems. Use it only for ads, not for organic accounts (free accounts that post without paid ads).
  • Run ad activity from a desktop computer, not the phone. The phone version of Ads Manager does not work for this setup. Keeping ad work on desktop also keeps the phone separate from the ad account.

Browser, Email and Payment Settings

1 min
  • Create and run new TikTok ad accounts in Safari (Apple's browser) or another privacy-focused browser (one that blocks tracking), not Google Chrome. The source saw more account suspensions when using Chrome with extensions (add-ons). Use a different browser for each new account you set up.
  • Use a new email address for each new TikTok ad account, not one that has already run ads on TikTok. In the source's experience, TikTok sent verification codes (one-time codes that prove it is you) to the email address, not by text message.
  • Browser advice differs by source and case:
Source Browser When it applies Ids
Safari or another privacy-focused browser, not Chrome with extensions Each new account in its own browser General account creation
Brave instead of Google Chrome Different from your usual browser Self-created account after the old one is blocked
Google Chrome, fully quit and reopened Chrome, with any TikTok sales channel (the link between TikTok and your store) removed first US targeting from a blocked location such as India

Brave is a browser that blocks tracking.

Creating an Account Yourself

1 min

  • Use this procedure to create a new TikTok ad account yourself when the old one is blocked:

    1. Connect a VPN set to the target country, such as the United States.
    2. Log in with a different browser from your usual one, such as Brave instead of Google Chrome.
    3. Create a new TikTok ad account from that browser session.

  • Use TikTok sales channel in the US by:

    1. Delete the sales channel integration (the connection between TikTok and your store).
    2. Clear the browser cache (saved website files) or use incognito mode (a private window that keeps no history).
    3. Open NordVPN and connect to a US server (a computer located in the United States).
    4. Download and add TikTok sales channel again.
    5. Create a new ad account.

    This workaround allows non-US users to run ads to US audiences.

  • Creating a TikTok ads account for US targeting when the business is based in India or another blocked location:

    1. Add TikTok as a sales channel in Shopify (a platform for running an online store).
    2. Create a new advertising account.
    3. Enter a US phone number.
    4. Connect a PayPal account for payment.

    The VPN must be set to the US. Chrome must be fully quit and reopened, with any TikTok sales channel removed first.

Creating an Account Through a Provider

1 min
  • Litfix.com is a provider (a company that sells this service). It creates TikTok ad accounts in a chosen country using your email and payment details, then sends you the login. It is used to get an ad account in a target market, such as the United States, Europe or Australia, without setting one up yourself.
  • A TikTok ad account created by a provider in a chosen country, such as the US, can be logged into from your own location without a VPN, because the country is fixed when the account is created. Providers usually sell separate accounts per market, such as US, Europe or Australia, so each new target market needs its own account. A self-created account instead requires logging in through a VPN set to the target country.
  • For TikTok ads from a country where you cannot create ad accounts, a third-party service (an outside company) can create a TikTok ad account for a chosen country (US, Australia, or EU, where the whole of Europe can be targeted). Choose ad account currency US dollars and pay via automatic payment (the card is charged on its own).
  • Third-party account creation steps:
    1. Open the third-party TikTok ad account service.
    2. Choose the ad account type: US, Australia, or EU.
    3. Enter a new email address (a new Gmail account per ad account is recommended, since a password is required).
    4. Enter the store URL (the web address of your shop).
    5. Enter a phone number to receive an SMS (a text message).
    6. Select the currency (US dollars) and the payment method.
    7. Pay; the account is confirmed by email within two days.

Business Documents

1 min
  • For a US TikTok ad account, the operator reported that few strict business documents were needed, and the store name could be entered as the business name. Requirements for Australian accounts were not confirmed, so check which business fields are mandatory (must be filled in) before creating the account.

Ads Manager Setup with Shopify

1 min

  • Creating a TikTok Ads account and connecting it to a Shopify store with a pixel (a small tracking code that records visits and sales), in order:

    1. Go to ads.tiktok.com and click Create.
    2. Fill in the personal information and accept the policy.
    3. Choose the payment method: Manual Payment (top up the ads account with a budget, meaning add money to it in advance) or Automatic Payment (card is charged automatically when a threshold, a set amount, is reached). Automatic Payment is the recommended choice.
    4. Click Go to Ads Manager.
    5. Optionally use the Dashboard (summary page) to review settings and the business overview.
    6. Change the business or user settings via the account icon at the top right (User Settings: email, username, password, notifications).
    7. Open Tools > Events > Manage Web Events.
    8. Click Create Pixel and choose Partner Setup, then Next.
    9. Select Shopify as platform, open the Shopify app and install the TikTok app in the store.
    10. Scroll down, check that everything is done and click Add Sales Channel.
    11. Click Setup Now and connect the business account via Connect Business Manager (TikTok's tool for managing business accounts).
    12. Set Data Sharing to Maximum (how much visitor data the pixel sends), check that the pixel is shown as created, then click Confirm.
    13. Click Finish Setup.
    14. Wait for the review, which takes up to 24 hours.
    15. When activated, the status shows 'TikTok Marketing' as Active.

  • In TikTok Ads Manager, the account icon at the top right opens User Settings, where you can edit the email address, username, password and notifications. If the account holds several business accounts, the business can be switched from the same top-right area.

Shopify and Pixel Connection

1 min
  • Install the TikTok Pixel Helper Shopify app and set up TikTok as a sales channel, which connects the pixel automatically. Then connect the pixel to the store and to the TikTok Business Manager ad account.
  • Sources disagree on linking the ads account to the Shopify store:
Position Advice When it applies Ids
Link the account Add TikTok as a sales channel and connect the pixel, as in the setup steps Standard path for connecting the pixel to Shopify
Do not link the account One seller saw a sudden decline in views (how often people see your ad) after linking, then kept accounts unlinked, creating a second account when a link was needed Experience of one seller; weigh it against the standard path

Pixel Creation and Renewal

1 min
  • When testing TikTok ads with a new product, create a new pixel for each product. Even for running products, change the pixel every three to seven days, because TikTok pixels wear out (stop working well) after a few days.
  • Connecting pixels to a store is no longer reliable. The strategy (plan) of not connecting the pixel is considered outdated and does not work as well as it used to.
  • A new pixel gets a grace period (a short window when early traffic is cheaper), so performance can look better for a while. Renewing pixels every day is not a sustainable approach (you cannot keep it up over time).

Backup and Multiple Accounts

1 min
  • For TikTok ads, several ad accounts can be run from the same registered company setup by creating a new account with a different email address. The Shopify connection is changed in the same way, and the business entity (the legal company itself) does not need to change.

Ban Risks

1 min
  • TikTok ad accounts created from a foreign location via a new device and VPN can get flagged (marked as suspicious). The phone-verification workaround (a way around TikTok's phone check) with random US numbers or US number services at 2-3 USD per month also carries a risk of account restriction.
  • Logging into a TikTok ad account from a country different from the one it was set up in can cause shadow bans (hidden limits on your ads, with no clear warning) and suspensions again. Use a VPN in the target country or a provider-set account, matching the login location as closely as possible.
  • A prepaid card (a card loaded with money in advance) from the target country can be used to pay for ads from a country where the store is located. The source mentions this as a workaround but notes it is not recommended.
