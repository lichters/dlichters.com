Playbook › 11. TikTok and short video

Replicating, remixing and refreshing viral content

Section 11.8 · 8 of 22 in this chapter · 9 min read

In short

  • Copy what already works first. Use the winning idea and format of a proven ad or video.
  • Never repost another creator's video. Rebuild the idea with your own footage.
  • Change the file itself. Even a small trim counts, because identical re-uploads get skipped.
  • Test every change against the original. Keep what sells and drop what does not.

Principles of Replicating and Remixing

1 min
  • Copy the winning angle and format first. The angle is the main idea of an ad.
  • Change the angle only later. Do this after the product shows traction (clear signs of growing interest) and converts well (turns viewers into buyers).
  • Copying a competitor's video closely can still go viral. A viral video spreads very widely. Some sellers copy a successful video even frame by frame (each moment exactly).
  • This suggests that video structure drives results more than timing alone.
  • A viral video can still work with weak quality. It may have poor sharpness (how clear the picture is) and weak audio.
  • Keep the concept and improve the quality. Use a clearer image and better sound to beat it.
  • Do not repost other creators' videos. TikTok increasingly detects reused content, and your account can suffer.
  • Recreate the concept with your own footage. Footage means the raw video you film.
  • Study the top videos of a successful account closely. Look at frame changes, splits (screen divided into parts), sounds, transitions (cuts between shots), recording style and editing pace.
  • Recreate the structure with your own product. Do not copy the content itself.
  • Use competitor accounts in the same niche (small market category) as inspiration.
  • Borrow the theme or structure, then improve it or change it entirely.
  • Study a proven viral video from another account. Then recreate its structure in your own way.
  • Use the same type of music, hook and context. A hook is the first few seconds that grab attention.
  • Copying the core format while filming your own version is the approach that produced the first sales.
  • Spend regular time on TikTok. Watch which videos go viral and which sounds they use.
  • Replicate those sounds and formats in your own videos.

Remixing a Winning Ad or Viral Video

2 min

Remixing a Winning Ad

  1. Start from the winning ad's angle and script. A script is the written text that is spoken or shown.
  2. Open with a big benefit shot. For example, a phone that sticks anywhere.
  3. Find footage that shows each benefit. Then cut it into short clips.
  4. Adjust clip audio and volume as needed.
  5. Add text in a TikTok-style layout. Use the font Open Sans, scale it down (make it smaller) to fit, and set the text background to white at 100% opacity (solid, not see-through).
  • One clip is rarely enough. Gather a full set of footage before you cut.
  • This prevents a copy that looks exactly like the original.
  • Change the clip every 2-3 seconds when remixing competitor videos on TikTok.
  • This helps the video not get flagged as a duplicate (an exact copy).
  • Mixing hooks from several winning videos into one new edit is a common method.

Replicating a Viral TikTok Video

  • Keep the same filming style and footage approach.
  • Make slight variations of the spoken text or on-screen words.
  • Cut any weak moments. For example, shorten a 10-second video to 8 seconds.
  • Judge each variation against the original.
  • If a variation performs worse, work out whether the words, the angle or the length caused it.
  • Do not repost the same video.
  • Make a new variation of the same style. Use the same filming approach, but different wording or a different cut.
  • Reuse trending sounds that worked on your best video.

Repurposing Viral Footage into a Facebook Ad

Repurposing means reusing existing content in a new way.

  1. Write a script with ChatGPT (an AI writing tool). Base it on the footage and the product's problem and benefits.
  2. Record a voiceover, or generate one with ElevenLabs (an AI voice tool). A voiceover is a voice that talks over the video.
  3. Cut the footage to match the voiceover. Then add on-screen text.
  4. Test the cut as a Facebook ad against a version with a fresh opening.

Copying a Viral Product's Video Approach

  1. Identify the viral video and its trending sound while the trend is still active.
  2. Copy the video's format, structure, and sound closely. Do not invent a new angle.
  3. Make sure your product link is working before you publish.
  4. Add a clear call to action (a message telling viewers what to do) to the video text and description.
  5. Add urgency (a reason to act now) to the video text and description.
  6. Post while the sound is still trending, ideally within days of the viral peak (the highest point of views).

Recreating Your Product Cleanly

  • Learn the structure of successful competitor videos.
  • Rebuild it with your own footage, so the content is yours even when the format is copied.
  • Remove watermarks. A watermark is a logo or name shown on the video.
  • Create original footage. Reused clips with watermarks can signal low quality and lead to lower distribution (fewer people seeing the video).

Content Mix and Testing Volume

1 min

4-5 Two or three proven styles, one or two variations, and one new risky angle
  • Keep testing new angles constantly.
  • The same content gets harder to keep relevant over time.

Trends, Sounds and Hashtags

1 min
  • Trend-based videos are easy to produce. They are worth doing even if the trend does not last.
  • Only do this when the product fits naturally into the trend format. An example is listing benefits over a trending sound on a product-style video.
  • Use trends to reach new audiences from the For You Page (TikTok's main feed of suggested videos).
  • Put your product into a current video trend, filter, or trending sound. Do this instead of relying only on your own content format.
  • A trend can be used without showing your face.
  • Put the product into a current video trend, a filter or a trending sound.
  • Keep the product presentation within normal limits. Avoid anything too revealing.

Organic Replication of a Viral Video

Organic means free reach, not paid ads.

  1. Use the same trending sounds and the same hashtags (words starting with #) as the viral video.
  2. Use a very similar text-to-speech voiceover. This is a computer-made voice that reads the script.
  3. Upload two to three more similar videos after the viral one.
  4. Leave at least 2 hours between uploads.
  5. Vary posting times, including unusual hours such as 2 am. This lets different time zones and regions see the content.

Reusing Your Own Old Videos (Repurposing)

1 min

Eligibility Rules

  • Only repurpose on an established account. It must have been posting for several weeks or months and have a large library (collection) of videos.
  • Do not repurpose on a new account.
  • Only consider re-uploading a video that is at least about one month old.
  • Only do this if the video previously performed well.
  • Use repurposing sparingly. Repeating it too often stops it from working.

Procedure

To repurpose an existing video:

  1. Take a video that already performed.
  2. Change the hook or the opening.
  3. Slightly alter the video file itself.
  4. Re-upload it, so TikTok does not treat it as spam.

File Differences

  • A re-uploaded video must differ in its file content. A small trim of one second or a changed frame is enough. This is true even if it looks the same to the viewer.
  • Re-uploading the identical file is risky.
  • A viewer who has already seen the video will swipe past it.
  • Viewers may swipe past a video on a large account when it reappears. This can lower its performance.
  • Avoid identical re-uploads, even when no restriction is triggered.

Changing the Fingerprint

A fingerprint is the file's unique signature that a platform can recognize.

  • Speed up the video by 1.01x to 1.05x (1% to 5% faster) in a video editor or speed tool. This is for re-uploading it as an ad.
  • Swap the hook of one video onto the body of another. Combined with the speed change, this creates variants that TikTok may treat as new videos.
  • This can lower CPM (cost per 1,000 views).

Metadata and Meta Refresh

1 min

Metadata is hidden information inside a video file, such as its size, timing and coding (the way the file is compressed).

  • The aim of a meta refresh is to make TikTok treat a re-uploaded ad as new content.
  • A meta refresh gives the ad fresh reach. Reach means how many people see it.
  • A metadata-changing method can refresh a copied video. This makes the platform treat it as a new upload.
  • In one test, a third-party website was used for this. A third-party website means a tool made by another company.
  • The effect lasted only about one day.
  • Metadata that can be changed includes coding, dimensions, length, timing, and playback speed. Dimensions means the width and height of the video.
  • Changing these helps the platform treat a reused video as a new ad.

Meta Refresh Procedure

  1. Start with the video at its original 1080 x 1920 (9:16) dimension. 9:16 is the tall, vertical phone format.
  2. Change the dimension to 1920 x 1080 (16:9). 16:9 is the wide, horizontal screen format. Use an editor such as CapCut, Sony Vegas Pro or Premiere Pro.
  3. Export the video, which means saving the finished file, and re-import it into the editor.
  4. Change the dimension back to 1080 x 1920 (9:16).
  5. Change the coding and other metadata of the video, such as speed or timing.
  6. Export the final video and use it for the new ad.

Results Benchmark

A benchmark is a standard to compare against. CTR (click-through rate) is the share of viewers who click.

  • After using the meta refresh on competitor videos, a CTR of 1.52 percent was reached on TikTok. This is very high.
  • A CTR of about 0.7 to 0.8 percent is considered good on TikTok.
  • The 1.52 percent is a single test result.
  • The general benchmark is 0.7 to 0.8 percent.

Downloaders, Watermark Removal and Editing Tools

1 min
  • Snap Tick (snaptick.app) and SnapTik are web tools. They download TikTok videos without the TikTok watermark.
  • Post the clean file on Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts. It will have no visible TikTok branding.
  • Use the downloader to get a clean file before re-uploading.
  • The watermark suppresses reach on other platforms. Suppress means hold back.
  • To reuse existing TikTok content, download the source video with SnapTik.
  • Then edit it into your own custom ad in CapCut. CapCut is also available as a mobile app for editing on the phone.

Video Replies

1 min

A video reply is a new video that answers a viewer's comment.

  • Pick a high-liked comment as the hook.
  • The selected comment appears in the top left corner of the reply video. There it works as a strong hook.
  • Video replies can compound reach. Compound means the effect grows on itself.
  • Each reply can ride the momentum of the earlier viral video. Momentum means the speed already built up.
  • The top comments on a new reply supply topics for the next replies in the same chain.

Case: Replication That Produced the Breakthrough

1 min
  • A dropshipper started about 20 months earlier. A dropshipper sells products without holding stock; the supplier ships them.
  • He first doubted that dropshipping could be profitable, until a friend began making sales.
  • He tested 6 to 7 products before finding a hit.
  • For the hit, he noticed another account getting tens of millions of views within a day.
  • He bought one unit on Amazon. It arrived the same day.
  • He filmed many videos that copied that account's format, music and hook. He used his own execution.
  • He posted 4 to 5 videos per day for two weeks.
  • He was about to stop just before the breakthrough.
  • He gained 1,000 followers in 15 days, then 10,000.
  • He reached about 20,000 followers, as one video reached roughly 10 to 15 million views.
  • That one video earned about $1,000 within 2 hours.
  • The same product kept getting about 4 million views per day for roughly a month.
  • The overall path from the first dropshipping attempt to the win took about a year.

Step-by-Step Procedures

1 min

Competitor Analysis to Recreation

  1. Find the largest accounts selling the product.
  2. Collect their most viral videos.
  3. Note the hooks, transitions, and formats such as ASMR (soft, tingling sounds in videos).
  4. Remove dull moments and shorten each video.
  5. Recreate the best videos using your own footage. Improve the weak parts.

Further Details

  • Reusing the same video clip across several posts hurts reach. This also applies to a second account.
  • Posts with identical clips tend to get very few views.
  • Use fresh footage for every new video.
