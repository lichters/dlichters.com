Accounts per Phone and Device Pool

Three-account cap and its exceptions

Setup Limit When it applies Source
Testing on one device Maximum of three TikTok accounts per device Default rule for testing. Use two or more devices when running several accounts.
Practical limit to avoid detection About three accounts on one phone, with no engagement between them Given as the practical limit for avoiding detection.
Organizational limit Up to three accounts per device Recommended for organization.
Observed higher count Up to seven accounts on one device Reported in the lesson, where the presenter ran up to seven. The device limit is not a hard rule if you do not engage across accounts.
Seven logged in at once Seven accounts logged in on one phone One seller uploaded to only some of them. Separate devices are safer and easier to manage.
  • Going beyond three accounts on one phone is considered a bad idea.
  • Several accounts on one phone is common and was reported without a clear ban.
  • Accounts can be shared across family members' phones as an additional device pool. Each device should still stay within the three-account cap.
  • A separate phone is not a guaranteed fix for shadow bans. Use an extra phone only if you already have one. Do not buy one for this purpose.

Separate Devices for Ad and Organic Accounts

  • When you run an ad account alongside an organic account for the same product, use a separate phone for each. This keeps ad activity from putting the organic account at risk.
  • This recommendation applies only if you already have the extra phone. See.

Cross-Account Engagement

  • Do not log into a second account on the same phone to comment or like your own store account. Cross-account engagement on one device can hurt the account.
  • Do not engage with the accounts from each other.
  • Each new product test should run on its own fresh account. The same Wi-Fi may be shared across accounts, but an account must not be engaged from a device that is used for another account.

New Accounts and Content Reuse

  • After about two weeks of consistent posting, the advantage of a new account can fade. Start a third account only after checking that the first two still perform.
  • Repurpose the same content on new accounts before creating new content.
  • Case: multi-account growth on separate accounts
    • Starting point: a first account started gaining traction.
    • About 48 hours later: a second brand-new account was created. It took roughly three days to gain traction and go viral.
    • About a month later: a third new account was created.
    • Result: the first and second accounts each reached 100,000 followers in the same period. The third reached 100,000 within a month. Each account reached different people, and the second account became the largest.
  • Number on organic posting with the router setup: one account per phone is typical. The lesson's example recommended one account on the reset phone.

Resetting a Device for a New Product Test

  • Testing a new product on a device requires a full factory reset and new accounts. The platform would otherwise link the new product to the old account footprint. Keep the data backed up before resetting.
  • Procedure to move a device to a new product test, or to clear a suspected device or account limit:
    1. Back up the phone's data to the cloud, but do not include TikTok account information in the backup.
    2. Perform a full factory reset of the phone.
    3. Create new TikTok accounts for the new product and post at least three to five completely different pieces of content on them.

Diagnosing Device or IP Limits

  • If new social accounts are suspended within minutes of posting the same product videos, the device or IP address is more likely the cause than the video content.
  • Test this by posting from a different phone, tablet, or network, or by posting an unrelated video from the same account.

Router and VPN Setup

  • A VPN can make an account lose reach or be shadowbanned on TikTok. Test with a split setup: one account with a VPN and one without. Skip the VPN if you do not need it for your location.
  • A VPN is generally not needed to go viral from a first-world country. It may help only in certain third-world locations, and some sellers there have still gone viral without one.
  • A VPN that is only switched on in the phone's VPN app does not work for organic TikTok content. The VPN must run on the router so the device's connection is consistently routed.
  • Procedure: TikTok VPN setup to avoid shadowban (router method)
    1. Test whether the VPN works on the router before the phone is changed. The VPN must be connected to the router, not only in the VPN app on the phone.
    2. Set up the VPN on the router. NordVPN was used. A router setup guide is available from the provider.
    3. Choose a rotating VPN so the phone stays permanently connected and the algorithm consistently sees the chosen country.
    4. Once the router works, factory reset the phone, wipe it completely, and then set it up again.
    5. When backing up the phone, do not restore Instagram and TikTok data. Log back in manually instead.
    6. Check whether the account still struggles. Only create a new account if the old account still has problems.
    7. Connect the phone to Wi-Fi via the router after the reset.

Posting from the Target Country

  • TikTok organic accounts are not run through a VPN. Instead, content is posted in the target country so that TikTok first tests it there and then moves it to the US content pool if it performs well.
  • Store and account setup in the creator's own country is recommended, since VPN use for other regions can be frustrating. Start the first account locally, then expand to other regions once you have results.
  • Using a VPN to upload videos from a different region can cause shadow bans, because the platform detects the mismatch in IP location. Upload from the country where the account is intended to operate. Do not rely on a VPN to reach other regions.

Reaching US Viewers from Abroad

  • To reach US viewers, upload at US times, use US hashtags, use US trending sounds, and stay consistent.
  • Expect US reach only after videos get roughly 100,000 to 200,000 views, and preferably over 1 million views.
  • To reach a US audience from outside the US, post English content with hashtags and music that are popular in the US instead of changing IP addresses. Reach then depends on the content's relevance and viewership. Videos from the seller's own location may still reach the UK first before spreading.
  • Do not use a VPN to make TikTok treat you as a US user. A VPN-driven spike was followed by a shadow ban that wiped out views. Posting from outside the US without a VPN still reached viewers, since US viewers make up a large share of TikTok traffic.

Country Availability

  • At the time of the lesson (2023), TikTok was accessible in Pakistan, so TikTok ads could be run from there, and a VPN was an option. TikTok was banned in India. Check the current status in each country before running ads.
  • TikTok is blocked in India, so organic accounts cannot be opened from there. Instead, content creators from the UK and US were hired to run the accounts.
