YouTube shorts, snapchat, Pinterest and other short-Video channels

Section 11.20 · 20 of 22 in this chapter · 6 min read

In short

  • Post the same short video on several platforms. One weak result on one platform does not mean the video failed.
  • Each platform has its own audience. Check results on each one.
  • Pick the platform list by task. Use five platforms for cross-posting, or the core three plus Snapchat for reusing a video.
  • Simple videos can work. A person, a logo, or music is enough. You do not need professional production.
  • The 2022 YouTube ad steps may be out of date.

Choosing platforms for short video

  • Snapchat is an app for photos and videos. It works for very niche audiences, such as the French market. A niche is a narrow topic or group.
  • Snapchat is weaker for worldwide targeting.
  • Pinterest, a site for saving visual ideas, can be tried when a product does very well.
  • Each platform has a different audience. A video can do well on one and poorly on another.
  • Each app has its own short video feed: Reels (Instagram and Facebook), Shorts (YouTube), and Spotlight (Snapchat). TikTok is a short video app.
  • Two sources give different platform lists for short video. Use the list that matches your task. Cross-posting means posting one video on several platforms. Repurposing means reusing a video you already made.

  • The same video can perform very differently by platform. One clip had weak reach on TikTok. Reach means how many people see it.
  • That same clip reached hundreds of thousands of views on YouTube Shorts and Snapchat Spotlight.
  • UGC-style videos show a person talking or holding the product. UGC means user-generated content, made to look like a real customer's video.
  • UGC-style videos perform well on TikTok and Snapchat.

Multi-platform posting routine

  1. Choose the platform set. Use the five cross-posting platforms or the core three plus Snapchat for repurposing.
  2. Make the video in UGC style, with a person talking or holding the product.
  3. Post the same content on each chosen platform.
  4. Do not drop the content after a weak first result on one platform. Keep posting it across platforms.
  5. On Snapchat, post Spotlight content every day.
  6. For Facebook, a social network, advertise the same product with new creatives. Creatives are the ad images and videos.

YouTube Shorts strategy

  1. Post product videos, tutorials (step-by-step how-to videos), unboxings (opening a box on camera), and reviews.
  2. Use descriptive titles that say clearly what the video shows.
  3. Also use keyword-rich descriptions. Keywords are the words people type into search.
  4. No professional production is needed. Even simple videos with logos, music, and supplier footage rank in Google search results. Rank means they appear high in results. A supplier is the company that makes or ships your products.
  5. Leverage user-generated content (UGC): ask suppliers for videos and add your own branding. Branding means your name, logo, and style.
  6. Optimize for keywords to rank in both YouTube search and Google search. Optimize means improve.

YouTube Shorts ads

The following procedure is from 2022. It is older advice. Placement rules, which set where an ad appears, may have changed since then.

  1. Upload the vertical video to YouTube. Vertical means tall, like a phone screen.
  2. Create a YouTube ad campaign with the Video views objective. A campaign is a set of ads with one goal. The objective is that goal.
  3. Use the video URL as the ad. A URL is the web address of the video.
  • There is no manual Shorts placement. You cannot pick the Shorts spot yourself.
  • A vertical video under 60 seconds automatically qualifies for Shorts placements.
  • Run it alongside a Facebook version, at the same time.

YouTube Shorts viewer and creator features

  • Custom thumbnails (the preview picture you pick) for Shorts are fully rolled out to all YouTube Partner Program creators. The Partner Program is YouTube's program for creators who earn money.
  • The earlier version was unstable during testing.
  • The Like and Dislike buttons were replaced by a single Heart icon. An icon is a small symbol.
  • Viewers can choose Not Interested or Don't Recommend Channel instead of the old thumbs-down button.
  • Clear Screen is a viewer setting that hides all on-screen icons, for watching without distraction.
  • Clear Screen launched in the week of 29 June 2026. One tap hides icons and captions.
  • Because viewers watch without overlays (icons and text on top of the video), this can affect how engagement is measured. Engagement means likes, comments, and watch time.
  • Viewers can watch at 2x playback speed, which means double speed. They press and hold anywhere on a Short.
  • This faster option may change how long viewers spend watching each video.

Snapchat Spotlight and Now Playing

  • Now Playing is on Snap Map, Snapchat's map of friends.
  • Users can opt in, meaning choose to join, to share their real-time Spotify listening activity. Spotify is a music app. Real-time means live, as it happens.
  • Friends can then discover songs through that activity.
  • In Now Playing, tapping a track opens Spotlight videos that use the same sound.
  • Songs can also be saved to Spotify directly from Spotlight videos and Sound Pages. Sound Pages are Snapchat's pages for a song.
  • Snapchat reports that creators who post Spotlight content every day grow followers about 10 times faster than creators who post now and then.
  • This number comes from the platform itself.

Snapchat Snap Map sharing

  • When you add a Snap to Snap Map, the share screen lists "My Story" and "Best Friends" as audience options. A Snap is a photo or video sent on Snapchat.
  • "My Story" is seen by all your friends. "Best Friends" is a short list you pick.
  • Choose the audience before sending, because the two options reach different viewers.

TikTok feature updates

  • Creators can now upload original songs directly to TikTok.
  • Before this update, that was possible only through a Spotify or TikTok representative. A representative is a person who works for the company.
  • This is useful for a jingle, a short catchy tune, or a sound tied to a paid campaign, an ad run you pay for.
  • Photo posts, called carousels (sets of photos you swipe through), can be created, published, and scheduled from the TikTok desktop web (TikTok on a computer browser) or app.
  • They are not only from mobile.
  • Use this to plan photo content in batches (many at once) on a computer.
  • TikTok's feature list for this update names a "Songs of Live" option for uploading original songs for TikTok Live. TikTok Live means live video streams.
  • Live content can be auto-clipped (cut automatically) into shareable LIVE Gifts, TikTok's name for these clips.
  • Check the exact label in your own app before relying on it.

Pinterest with Autopin

  • Autopin.co is a tool that does Pinterest tasks for you automatically.
  • It can pin content, send board invites (requests to join your boards), and follow relevant users.
  • Use it to grow Pinterest followers and bring recurring traffic (repeat visitors) to your website.
  • Do this without daily manual effort.
  • Pinterest strategy. A pin is a saved image that links to a web page. A board is a folder of pins.
    1. Create pins linking to your blog articles and product pages.
    2. Organize pins into boards by category.
    3. Use Autopin.co to automate pinning schedules, follower growth, and board invitations.
    4. Build backlinks, which are links from other websites that point to yours, by adding your website URL to pins. Approach this carefully to avoid policy violations, which means breaking the platform's rules.
    5. This is particularly effective for home renovation, home products, beauty, fashion, and sports niches.

Reddit

Reddit is a discussion site. A subreddit is a topic group on Reddit. Karma is a score of points you earn when other users value your posts.

  1. Build subreddit karma by participating in relevant communities.
  2. Once you have sufficient karma (enough points) or reputation (your standing in the community), create your own subreddit focused on your niche.
  3. Post all blog content, new products, and updates to your subreddit.
  4. Each post generates backlinks and traffic to your site. Traffic means visitors.
  5. Engage with the community to grow followers. Engage means take part, reply, and interact.
