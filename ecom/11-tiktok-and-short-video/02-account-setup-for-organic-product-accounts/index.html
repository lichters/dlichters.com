Account setup for organic product accounts

Section 11.2 · 2 of 22 in this chapter · 6 min read

In short

  • Sign up with a dedicated email, not a phone number or Apple login, and keep the account on Personal, not Business.
  • Turn on comment keyword filtering, and turn off "Suggest your account to others" to protect your store.
  • A website link needs about 1,000 followers. Before that, use an Instagram link, which has no follower minimum.
  • Never put a website link in the bio.

Email and Sign-Up

  • Register the account with an email address, not a phone number, Apple ID (Apple's login account), or Apple Pay (Apple's payment wallet) login, because each of those can only be linked to one account.
  • Use a dedicated email, meaning one used only for this, so that store and account matters stay separate.
  • Use a Gmail address (Google's email service) that matches the email of the Shopify store (online shop software), not a personal email, so the business accounts stay organized.
  • For an organic account (one that grows without paid ads), an ordinary email address is enough. A US phone number (a phone number from the United States) is not required.
  • Avoid numbered variants of an address (the same name with added numbers), suspicious phone numbers, and changing IP addresses (the internet address of your connection). These can lead to shadow banning (TikTok quietly hiding your videos from others without telling you). Focus on legitimate content instead.

Username

  • Choose a short username with plain words and no symbols, since symbols look unprofessional. Match it to the brand or store name.
  • Check username availability (whether the name is still free) before creating the Shopify domain (the store's web address, such as brand.com).
  • If the username is taken, add .com or.co (web address endings) to the end, or add shop before or after the brand name. These variants are often still free.
  • A TikTok username can be changed after 30 days, so a poor choice at setup is not permanent.

Personal or Business Account

  • Keep the account as a Personal account (the standard TikTok type). Do not switch to a Business account (the type made for companies).
  • Business accounts lose access to trending and cooperative sounds (popular music clips and shared audio others can use with you), get less reach (fewer people see your posts), and cannot post a link without 1,000 followers.
  • Business accounts run into copyright blocks (TikTok blocking a video because the music belongs to someone else) when posting music. The trending sound library (the collection of songs TikTok allows) is restricted for them. This is a main reason to stay on a personal account for product videos.

Profile Picture, Display Name, and Bio

  • Set the profile picture (the small image at the top of the profile) to a high-quality product image, a product logo, or the brand name.
  • Set the display name (the name shown in large letters, not the username) to the store domain, such as brand.com.
  • Write a bio (short text about the account) of 2 to 3 lines with emojis (small picture symbols).
  • Place an emoji at both ends of each bio line. This format was used on TikTok product accounts and read well in the profile. Keep each line short so the slogan, offer, and link pointer (words that point people to the link) stay readable on a phone screen.
  • Do not put a website link in the bio. The bio is not a link location.

Links and the 1,000-Follower Threshold

Link type Requirement Source
Website link in the profile link tab (the spot on the profile for a clickable link) About 1,000 followers. The link option appears only after this number of followers, so a TikTok Shop (the store inside TikTok) is a practical starting point until then.
External website link before 1,000 followers May be restricted. One seller avoided it and used Instagram instead, noting that the risk is not fully known.
Instagram link (social link, a link to another social account) No minimum follower requirement applied as of this writing.
Any link on a Business account Cannot be posted without 1,000 followers.
  • A store can be built later, once the link function is available.
  • To add links, tap Edit profile (the screen for changing profile details). The fields for a website link and other links (such as Instagram and YouTube) are located there.
  • Logging into Instagram from TikTok syncs (connects automatically) the Instagram link. It then shows next to the Follow button.

Adding an Instagram link:

  1. Open Edit profile on the account.
  2. Scroll down to Social.
  3. Choose Instagram.
  4. Link the brand Instagram account, which should look similar to the TikTok account.
  5. An Instagram icon appears on the profile. It opens the Instagram page with its products and a shop-now button (a button that takes people straight to buying).

Comment Filters

Turning on keyword filtering:

  1. Tap Settings at the top right of the profile tab.
  2. Open Comments.
  3. Switch on Filter keywords (hides comments that contain the words on your list).
  4. Keep the Filter all comments option off. It forces manual approval (you must approve each comment yourself before it shows) of nearly every comment, and it lowers engagement (likes, replies, and other interaction).

Filter keywords:

  • Add these keywords to hide comments that reveal dropshipping (selling products that a supplier ships directly to the customer) or hurt trust: dropshipping, amazon, aliexpress, CJ dropshipping (a supplier name), cheaper, china, scam, shopify, dropshipper, fake, and don't buy.
  • Expand the list for each product, using negative words (words that signal complaints or bad experiences) that appear in comments on that product.

Privacy and Suggestion Settings

  • Turn off every option under Suggest your account to others (a setting that lets TikTok show your account to other people). This stops people who receive a link or interact with you from seeing which account you run.
  • New accounts come with these options enabled by default (already switched on), so check them at setup.

Upload Quality Settings

Enabling high-quality uploads:

  1. On the post screen (where you upload a video), tap More options (the last option in the bottom row).
  2. Turn on Allow high quality uploads.
  3. Do this before posting or drafting (saving a video without publishing it).
  • Record in 4K at 60 fps (4K is a very sharp picture size, and 60 fps means 60 frames per second for smooth motion). If 4K or storage (space on your phone) is not available, 1080p (a standard HD picture size) is sufficient.

Mobile versus Desktop

  • Do product research and account setup on the TikTok mobile app (the phone app), not desktop (a computer).
  • Desktop has far fewer filters and editing options. It is mainly useful for buying coins (TikTok's paid in-app currency) or looking at some analytics (statistics about your videos).

Desktop upload procedure:

  1. Log in to TikTok in a desktop browser (a web browser on a computer) with the account.
  2. Upload the video from the computer.
  3. Choose the cover (the still image shown for the video), set public or private, and run the copyright check (TikTok's scan for music or video owned by someone else).
  4. Add the music inside the editing tool before uploading, because the desktop upload does not allow adding music.
  5. Add the hashtags (words starting with #) in the caption (the text under the video).

Setup Checklist for a New Product TikTok Account

Complete these steps in this order:

  1. Sign up with an email address: a Gmail that matches the Shopify store email, not a phone number or Apple ID login.
  2. Choose a short username with plain words and no symbols that matches the brand. If it is taken, try adding .com or.co, or shop before or after the brand name.
  3. Keep the account on Personal, not Business.
  4. Set the profile picture to a high-quality product image, product logo, or brand name.
  5. Set the display name (not the username) to the store domain, such as brand.com.
  6. Write a 2 to 3 line bio with emojis.
  7. Turn off every option under Suggest your account to others.
  8. Turn on Filter keywords under Comments in Settings, and add the blocked keyword list.
  9. Turn on Allow high quality uploads under More options on the post screen.
  10. Draft at least 3 to 5 days of videos (save them without posting) before you post.
  11. Add a website link only after 1,000 followers, under Edit profile.
