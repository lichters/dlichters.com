Shadowbans, restrictions and account health

Section 11.12 · 12 of 22 in this chapter · 5 min read

In short

  • A sudden drop in views is the first sign of a restriction (TikTok limiting how many people see your videos).
  • Never delete a video. Set it to private (only you can see it) instead.
  • An ordinary restriction usually lasts a few days. A shadow ban (TikTok quietly hiding your videos) can take up to about two weeks.
  • Most accounts are removed after many violations or reports, not after one mistake.

Symptoms of a Restriction

  • A restriction shows up as a sudden drop in views compared with your recent videos.
  • Several uploads in a row stay at a similar, very low level.
  • Example: one video gets 5,000 views, then repeated uploads get near 80 views. This is a strong sign of restriction.
  • Judge restriction by views compared with follower count (the number of people who follow your account), not by one low result.
  • Views in the thousands on a small account can be normal.
  • An account with about 100,000 followers that gets only a few hundred views is likely restricted.

Diagnosis Before Assuming a Shadow Ban

  1. Check whether the problem is platform-wide. Analytics (TikTok's statistics page) and Creator Fund (TikTok's creator payment program) issues can affect many accounts, not only yours. Before assuming a shadow ban, check whether other accounts report the same delays or problems.
  2. Check the account setup and VPN use (a VPN is a tool that hides your location online) before blaming the content. This applies to a new account whose videos get almost no views.
  3. If a video stays in review (TikTok's check before a video goes live) for a long time, check its upload status with the Copy link step (a button that copies the video's web address) before assuming it failed.

Causes

  • A glitching VPN or other account-level problem can get a new TikTok account shadow banned.
  • Removing several videos in one day may trigger a restriction.
  • Videos from new accounts can be shadow banned or removed quickly if they contain risky content.
  • Posting a private copy, then publishing the same video again from the same account, is risky. TikTok may see the duplication as a problem.
  • Competitors can file reports against successful accounts.
  • Enough reports can lead to flags (warning marks on your account) even without a violation.
  • Most account removals happen after multiple violations or reports, not after a single incident.

Durations

The cases below give different times. Read them together.

Source Duration When it applies
Restriction in general Usually one to two days; the longest observed lasted about two weeks. Ordinary restriction
Shadow ban in the described cases Typically about 14 days before it lifts. Shadow ban; the length varies with the content posted during that time.
Several videos removed in one day Views stayed very low until the account recovered after about one to two weeks of waiting and re-uploading. Case of mass removal
  • Expect a few days for an ordinary restriction, and plan for up to about two weeks.

Recovery

When an account seems shadow banned:

  1. Keep posting about once a day on that account.
  2. Run a new account in parallel.
  3. Expect the shadow ban to usually lift after about 14 days in the cases described. The duration depended on the content being posted.
  • After mass removal, wait about one to two weeks, then re-upload videos.
  • A single restriction usually recovers within a short period after a successful appeal (a request asking TikTok to review and reverse its decision).
  • Recovering from a shadow ban can cost weeks of work.

Appeals and Review

  • Appeals are typically resolved in about 24 hours. Sometimes it takes three to four days when TikTok is backed up.
  • A video removed at least once may cause later uploads to go into review more often.

Reports and Violations

  • Videos that break the Community Guidelines (TikTok's rules on what is allowed) are not pushed to the For You Page (TikTok's main feed of suggested videos). They are shown only to the creator's own followers.
  • Repeated violations can lead to a permanent ban (the account is closed for good). Removing an account means losing its followers and income.
  • Enough reports can lead to flags even without a violation.

Guidelines and Risky Content

  • Keep the first videos of a new account safe. Avoid borderline jokes until the account is stable.
  • Check every video against the Community Guidelines before posting. Videos that break the rules lose their reach.

Deletion Rules

  • Never delete a video. TikTok treats deleted videos as failed.
  • If a video is bad or has an editing mistake, set it to private instead.
  • Do not publish the same video again from the same account after posting a private copy. TikTok may see the duplication as a problem.
  • If an account stops performing, test the same content on a new account. This is the safer option.
  • Removing several videos in one day may trigger a restriction.
  • Do not delete accounts that have brand, business or personal value right away.

Keeping or Abandoning Accounts

  • A restricted account with thousands of followers can be kept.
  • For product accounts, a new account is often easier to make go viral (spread widely) than reviving an old one.
  • A large follower count builds credibility (trust) with shoppers. An account with tens of thousands of followers is worth keeping as the main brand account, even when its organic views (views from normal, unpaid reach) drop.
  • Shoppers who click a Spark Ad (a TikTok ad that boosts an existing post) often look at the profile first. That is why the follower count matters.

Account Roles and Separation

  • Keep a separate account for paid ads and for testing new products.
  • Keep the personal account separate from ad and test work. This protects the main account and keeps the work organized.
  • The sources use "main account" in two senses. Keep both roles apart.
  • In, "main account" means the high-follower brand account that you keep.
  • In, "main account" means the account kept personal and separate from ads and tests.

Platform Rule Changes

  • Platform rules and TikTok features change often. Check the current rules before relying on any setting or program.
  • Changes are a recurring reason setups fail.

Risk Principles

  • Keep the risk of account bans low. A ban can wipe out the views and progress of accounts on the same device.
  • Avoid the mistakes that trigger shadow bans. Recovering from one can cost weeks of work.
