Playbook › 6. Conversion optimization

Live chat, forms and customer contact

Section 6.17 · 17 of 22 in this chapter · 9 min read

In short

  • Answer shoppers fast. Live chat helps visitors become buyers.
  • Start with the free Shopify Inbox. Add Tidio only if you need more.
  • Ask for name and email only if you cannot reply within one or two minutes.
  • Show your phone number in three places: header, footer and contact page.
  • In the first sales phase, reply to every inquiry yourself.
This section has 13 parts. Tap one to open it.

Why live chat and fast answers matter

1 min
  • Live chat is a chat box on your store where visitors type to you. It removes friction, meaning small obstacles that slow a buyer down, during the buying decision.
  • Customers can ask questions instantly.
  • Live chat helps you close sales in real time.
  • It is an easier option than contact forms or email.
  • Live chat leads to extra sales. Shoppers get answers in real time instead of waiting hours for an email reply.
  • For a store built this way, live chat is almost a standard feature.
  • Live chat lets a visitor with a question get an answer while still on the site.
  • Without live chat, the visitor must search for an email address, fill in a contact form and wait.
  • Answering in near real time can close sales that would otherwise be lost.
  • So live chat matters from the early stage of a store.
  • A live chat button on the store lets shoppers ask last-minute questions on product pages.
  • This recovers sales that would otherwise be lost.
  • Real-time answers at the moment of doubt are the core benefit.
  • Shoppers expect fast answers and fast delivery.
  • A visible way to get instant responses helps you convert browsers into buyers. To convert means turning people who only look into people who buy.
  • Competitors offering next-day delivery make slow answers and 2-3 day shipping a conversion risk.
  • A live chat widget lets visitors ask questions and get answers immediately while browsing. A widget is a small chat box or button on the page.
  • The example is the Tidio Live Chat app, a chat app you add to your store.
  • This turns browsers into buyers, since shoppers expect instant answers and fast delivery.

Setting up Tidio

1 min
  1. Install Tidio from the Shopify App Store, the shop inside Shopify where you add apps.
  2. Turn on the automations, which are rules that run by themselves. This lets the chat answer basic questions such as pricing and delivery times automatically.
  3. Customize the widget appearance.
  4. Under Chatbot, a tool that gives automatic replies, turn on the Welcome series under Increase sales. This is a set of automatic welcome messages, so visitors receive a welcome message.
  5. Under Increase sales, open Cart abandoned and use a template. Cart abandoned means a shopper left items in the cart without buying.
  6. Set the discount in the abandoned cart flow. The example changes 15 to 10 percent. Accept, activate, choose a coupon (a discount code) such as WELCOME10 with a value of 10, and save.

Chat widget design and placement

1 min
  • In the theme customizer (the editor where you change how the store looks), open the App embeds section (the place where apps add things to the store).
  • In that section you can change the chat widget.
  • Options include the widget color, text color, button color, and the icon.
  • Icon choices are a chat bubble, a smiley face or a hand wave.
  • Set the position to bottom right, and the lowest vertical position.
  • Pick the icon and colors that fit the site. No single option is better.
  • A widget color that contrasts with the site's palette makes the chat icon easier to notice.
  • That increases the chance of a click.
  • Icon style is mostly a matter of brand fit and taste.
  • Once chat is active, the chat icon appears on every page of the storefront (the part of your store shoppers see).
  • So visitors can start a conversation from any page.
  • chat widgets must not open as a large bubble that covers more than a third of the screen on mobile.
  • Configure them to open only after the visitor has read part of the page or has stayed on the page for a set time.

Chat greeting

1 min
  • The greeting appears next to the chat icon.
  • You can edit it in the widget settings. A preview updates as you change the text.
  • The default text is a generic greeting that invites questions and promises help.
  • Formula for a chat welcome message:
    • Name the store.
    • Invite the visitor to ask a specific question.
    • Add a short reassurance of a quick reply.
    • Keep it to one or two short sentences.
  • This makes it easy to read in a small widget.
  • It gives the visitor a clear reason to click.
  • Example 1:
Welcome to [Store Name]! Ask us anything about sizes, shipping or your order.
  • Example 2:
Hi, this is [Store Name]. Have a question before you buy? Send it over and we will reply shortly.

Capturing customer details in chat

1 min

Two sources give different rules for when to ask for contact details. Use the rule that matches your response speed.

  • Email before chat (Shopify Inbox): Shopify Inbox asks for the customer's email before chat. This is an email gate, a step that blocks the chat until the email is typed.
  • Keeping the email field is acceptable. People who ask buying questions usually give their email.
  • The trade-off is fewer chat starts.
  • Capture when you cannot reply quickly: Live chat should capture the customer's name and email if you cannot reply within a minute or two. This lets you follow up later.
  • No capture when you reply quickly: If you can respond quickly from your phone, this capture is not necessary.
  • Which to use: Keep the email gate if chat starts matter less than lead quality (how likely a lead is to buy). A lead is a person who showed interest.
  • Use the name and email capture only when a reply within a minute or two is not realistic.
  • Drop it when you can answer from your phone in that window.

Recommendations in chat

1 min
  • Personalized product recommendations are suggestions based on what a shopper says they need, such as skin type.
  • They can raise the conversion rate, the share of visitors who buy.
  • They can also create upsell opportunities. An upsell means offering a more expensive or extra item.
  • An AI agent (an AI helper that talks to shoppers) can suggest a fitting product directly in the conversation.

Response speed

1 min
  • Answer live chat, phone calls and emails quickly.
  • A slow reply can lose a sale worth $1,000 or more in net profit over a month. Net profit is the money left after all costs.
  • Customer service responsiveness affects conversions.
  • When customers call or email with questions and get no reply, they abandon the purchase.
  • So monitor inquiries and respond quickly.
  • Early on, a store's conversion rate is driven by manual follow-up with every inquiry.
  • Even one or two extra sales from inquiries can put a young store in profit.
  • In the first sales phase, handle every inbound contact manually, meaning yourself.
  • Answer the phone quickly, call back missed calls, and reply to every inquiry with real effort.
  • This personal touch drives high conversion for a new store.
  • Make the response fast and convenient. Even an interested reader will procrastinate when responding costs effort.
  • Offer a toll-free number, a number customers can call for free.
  • Consider staffing it around the clock.
  • Consider fax, website or email response options.

Quote forms for trade and bulk buyers

1 min
  • A quote is a price offer. Trade buyers are business customers, and bulk orders are large orders.
  • Custom quote forms built with Wufoo or Typeform (online form builders) trigger Zapier (a tool that connects apps automatically).
  • Zapier sends a private Slack message (a team chat app) to the sales team.
  • It also auto-fills a Google Sheets quote tracker (a free online spreadsheet).
  • Respond to inquiries within minutes to maximize quote-to-order conversion.
  • Quote reply template:
Hi [customer name],

Thank you so much for your inquiry. We work with many [customer type, e.g. interior designers] and we are more than happy to provide custom quotes for your business.

I am the owner of [store name]. Please reply to this email with the products you are interested in and the quantities, and I will get back to you with a quote.

Best regards,
[your name]
[store name]
  • Trade program welcome template:
Welcome to our trade program. We love working with [customer type]. If you ever need special orders or bulk orders, please email [trade email alias, e.g. trade@yourstore.com] or call [phone number].

Phone numbers on the site

1 min
  • Showing a phone number on the store tends to raise the conversion rate.
  • This happens even when visitors never call.
  • It signals that the business can be reached.
  • It also reduces doubt before buying.
  • Add a phone number on the store's contact page.
  • Customers rarely call. But the number builds trust.
  • The few who call are more likely to buy.
  • Place the business phone number in three spots:
    • The header (the top of every page), which shows it on every page;
    • The footer (the bottom of every page), for visitors who look for contact details;
    • The contact page, for visitors who look for contact details.
  • Set a voicemail message, a recorded message for missed calls.
  • It should thank the caller, say you are away or helping another customer, and ask for a name, email address and inquiry.
  • Even if you rarely answer the phone, the number builds trust and makes visitors more likely to buy.

Contact page

1 min
  • A contact page should show several ways to reach the business.
  • These include an email address, a phone number, and a physical address, alongside the contact form.
  • This gives customers more than one option to get help.
  • The Contact Us page needs its Template suffix (a setting that picks the page layout) set to 'page.contact' instead of the default 'page'.
  • Only then does it display a contact form.
  • The form shows fields for Name, Email, Phone Number, Message, and a SEND button.
  • Steps to add the contact form to the Contact Us page:
    1. Open the Contact Us page under Online Store > Pages.
    2. Scroll to the Template section and open the Template suffix dropdown, which shows 'page' by default.
    3. Select 'page.contact'.
    4. Click Save.
    5. Click View page to check that the contact form appears.

Mid-page "We are here to help" section

1 min
  • Build a mid-page help section, a block in the middle of the page, with this structure:
    • a heading that says the business is ready to help customers reach their outcome;
    • a line naming the outcome;
    • contact details: phone, email and live chat;
    • one button linking to the contact page.
  • Example:
Ready to plan your next move?
[one line naming the goal]
Phone: [phone number] | Email: [email address] | Live chat
[Talk to our team]

Personal video follow-up

1 min
  • A personal video lets a lead see a face and hear a voice behind the brand.
  • This makes a reply more likely than a generic reminder email.
  • Most competitors do not send such videos. So this method also helps the store stand out.
