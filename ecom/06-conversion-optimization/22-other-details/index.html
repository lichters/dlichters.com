Foundations for conversion

  • Market knowledge, content development (writing your store's text and pages), and customer understanding feed directly into conversion rate optimization (CRO, getting more visitors to buy). Outdated basics therefore weaken every later marketing and sales effort.
  • A small store with good suppliers (the companies that make or send your products) and steady traffic (regular visitors) can make money before organic traffic (free visitors from search results) builds. Organic traffic grows over time as Google (the search engine) recognizes the site as high quality.

Sales channels and store setup

  • A sales channel is a place where you sell besides your own website. The Shop sales channel can be added to Shopify sales channels. It rarely brings in much business, but adding it does no harm.

Product images in Search product ads

  • Search product ads are Google ads that show your products in search results. Image extensions (extra images added to these ads) have an approval rate (the share Google accepts) of approximately 50%, with no clear rejection reason.
  • Upload product images anyway, because approved images generate more clicks.

SEO titles and SKUs

  • SEO (search engine optimization, making pages easy for Google to find) uses the SEO title, the title shown in Google results. A SKU (stock keeping unit, your product's own code number) is fine to add to it, and it helps SEO.
  • The product title follows what the top competitors (other shops selling the same thing) put in their titles.
  • Products without variants (versions, such as different sizes or colors) show the SKU in the SEO title. Variant products do not include the SKU.

Ad funnel

A funnel is the path a visitor follows from first click to purchase.

  1. Run targeted Google Shopping ads (paid ads with a product photo and price, shown in Google shopping results) to product pages optimized for trust.
  2. Use dynamic Facebook remarketing ads (ads that automatically show each visitor the products they looked at, to bring them back) and email marketing to bring back visitors who left without buying.
  3. Optimize lead value (how much each customer is worth to you) by earning more from every sale once a customer purchases.
  4. Repeat to maximize revenue per customer.

Affiliate banners in Refersion

An affiliate is a partner who sends buyers for a share of the sales. Refersion is software for running affiliate programs. A banner is an image ad that partners place on their sites.

  • When uploading a banner creative (the banner image file) to Refersion, set the landing page URL (the web address visitors open after clicking) to match the banner type.
    • General business-wide banner: link to the homepage.
    • Product-specific banner: link to the specific product page.
  • This ensures visitors land on the appropriate destination, where they find details about the offer.

Trade buyer capture (2024)

A trade buyer is a professional who buys for clients, such as an interior designer. Trade discounts are lower prices for professionals. An exit-intent overlay is a pop-up that appears when a visitor is about to leave. The footer menu is the set of links at the bottom of every page.

  1. Create a page with a name such as "interior designer discounts" or "to the trade", and link it from the homepage and the footer menu.
  2. Add an exit-intent overlay that asks whether the visitor is an interior designer and collects their email for trade discounts.
  3. Send the captured emails a personal reply that offers custom quotes (price offers made for each request).
  4. Create a separate trade contact page with its own contact form (a form visitors fill in to reach you). The form collects name, email, phone, products of interest, and quantity.

Returns benchmark

A return rate is the share of sold items that customers send back. A benchmark is a target to measure against.

  • Aim for a return rate under 1 percent.

Case: high Facebook CTR, no sales

CTR (click-through rate) is the share of people who click an ad after seeing it. Facebook is Meta's ad platform.

  • Starting point: A store with a high Facebook CTR but no sales.
  • Changes: Changes were made to the store, including the store adjustments covered in this section.
  • Result: Within a couple of hours, the store received two sales.
