Business email, phone number and contact identity

Section 4.3 · 3 of 20 in this chapter · 13 min read

In short

  • Use an email on your own domain (your web address, such as yourbrand.com), not a Gmail address, for your store and suppliers.
  • Put a UPS Store address (a shipping shop that rents numbered mailboxes) on the website, never your home address.
  • Connect email with Third-party email hosting in Shopify, not email forwarding.
  • Add the DNS records (settings that tell the internet where your email goes) and send a test email.
  • Show a real business phone number on the header, footer and contact page, never a fake "555" number.
This section has 17 parts. Tap one to open it.

Which addresses and roles you need

1 min
  • Set up three business addresses on your domain (your web address, such as yourbrand.com): sales@yourdomain for supplier outreach, support@yourdomain or returns@yourdomain for customer inquiries, and yourfirstname@yourdomain for personal business use.
  • Alternatively, set up sales@yourstore.com for customer inquiries, returns@yourstore.com for managing returns, and yourname@yourstore.com for personal business use. The two sources differ on the inquiry address: suggests support@ or returns@, and uses sales@.
  • Set up business addresses such as info, support and sales at your domain, and use them for customer service and supplier contact.
  • Use the same role address (info@ or sales@ on your domain) for Shopify, apps and social profiles so logins stay consistent. Do not use a no-reply address (an address that cannot receive replies) for newsletters, because replies from customers are valuable.
  • Use the store domain (your shop's web address) for customer-facing emails, such as orders@yourstore.com, because customers expect order confirmations from the store. Use the company domain (your company's web address) for corporate communication, bank links and similar business accounts.
  • Enter a store email on the store's own domain (for example info@yourdomain) under Settings > General > Store contact details. Do not use a Gmail address (Google's personal email service) as the store email, because a domain-based address looks more professional and is more trusted.
  • Use a separate business email address, not a personal one, across all Google platforms: Gmail, Google Analytics (a tool that counts website visitors), Search Console (Google's tool that shows how your site appears in search), Google Ads (Google's paid ads) and Merchant Center (Google's product listing tool). This keeps data in one place and makes handing over the business easier later.
  • A new Shopify store (the software that runs your online store) defaults its store email and phone number to the ones used at sign-up. You can swap these for dedicated business email and phone later, once they exist.

Company name and store domain

1 min
  • The owning entity (the company that owns the stores) can use a generic company name, such as an LLC (a company type that keeps business debts separate from your personal ones) or an S corporation (another company type for small businesses), that owns several stores in related niches. The entity name does not need to match a store domain, and changing an existing store domain is unnecessary. (2025)
  • An older rule (2021) says the store domain and company name should not match. Name the company something generic (e.g. LJ Stores LLC) so that additional stores can be opened under it without confusion.
  • Enter your business information rather than your personal name in the Shopify store information and settings. Your personal name can stay as well, but the business information should be the primary entry. (2023)

Business address

1 min
  • Do not put a home address on the website. The options are no address, or a UPS Store address, which costs about $200 to $250 per year and works as a business address.
  • A UPS Store address shows a suite number (a numbered unit, such as "Suite 123") instead of a PO box, which looks more professional in an email signature and on a website. A PO box (a mailbox at the post office) is a cheaper alternative but looks less professional.
  • Use a professional business address (UPS Store with suite number) rather than a USPS PO Box (a mailbox at the US Postal Service, the US post office) on business documents and supplier agreements (written deals with suppliers). It conveys greater professionalism and legitimacy. (2023)
  • Use a business address such as a UPS Store mailbox instead of your home address on the website.
  • Use a commercial address such as a UPS Store address rather than a home address or a PO box. This is the practice used across the owner's businesses, though the reason for the policy is not confirmed.
  • Enter the business address in the Shopify store settings alongside your other business details.
  • Adding a physical business address to the contact page is optional, but it adds a level of trust for shoppers who want to send mail.
  • The business address on the Contact Us page is another contact method. It is useful for Google and not required, but it must be a business address, not your home address. (2023)
  • Some competitor stores (other shops selling similar products) list a private home address on their website, which exposes the owner's personal information. Do not copy that practice. If you list an address at all, use a business address on the contact page.

Personal details and second phone line

1 min
  • Entering your personal phone number in a Shopify store or SMS sign-up (a sign-up for text messages) can lead customers to call you directly. A second, dedicated number is safer for store contact fields.

Google Workspace sign-up

1 min

Google Workspace is a paid Google service that gives you business email on your own domain. It used to be called G Suite.

  1. Go to the Google Workspace sign-up page.
  2. Scroll down to locate the Business Starter Plan ($7/month).
  3. Click 'Get Started' under the $7/month option.
  4. Complete account setup.
  • Google Workspace can be set up through a meta tag (a short code placed in the hidden top part of a website's page) or through DNS records (DNS, or Domain Name System, is the set of settings that tell the internet where your email and website live) to verify the domain (prove you own it). The meta tag method is the first choice. (2023)
  • Enter the domain without https:// and without www, only the name and extension such as yourbrand.com. (https:// is the secure web prefix; www is the "www." start of many web addresses.) If the domain was bought through Shopify, keep Shopify as the domain host (the company where your domain is registered) so the DNS can be edited there. For a domain bought elsewhere, choose that registrar (the company that sells domains) from the list and edit its DNS there instead.
  • Domain verification may not be required if Shopify automatically recognises the domain.

Connecting Google Workspace to a Shopify domain

2 min

These steps combine three versions of the same connection. Each step keeps the details of all sources that describe it.

  1. On the Google Workspace domain host screen, keep Shopify as the domain host if the domain was bought through Shopify; otherwise select the actual registrar from the list. Click Continue.
  2. In Google Workspace, hover over the clipboard icon next to the Text value field (the box that holds the code) and click it to copy the unique verification code.
  3. In Shopify Admin (the back office where you run your store), click the Settings icon in the bottom left corner, then click Domains.
  4. Click the primary domain of the online store (your main web address). It must be the same domain added to Google Workspace.
  5. Scroll down past Email forwarding (which sends mail to another inbox and should not be used) to the section Third-party email hosting (a real email service, such as Google, that runs your domain's mail), and click Switch to email hosting.
  6. Leave the email hosting provider as G Suite. This is the old name for Google Workspace, which Shopify still shows.
  7. Paste the copied code into the Text value field and click Save. The provider now shows G Suite.
  8. At the top of the page, click Domain settings, then click Edit DNS settings (the page where you edit your domain's records).
  9. Click Add custom record and select MX record (the DNS entry that tells the internet where your email goes).
  10. Set Name to @ (the at symbol, which means your main domain), set Points to to smtp.google.com (Google's mail server address), and set Priority to 1 (the number that decides which mail server is tried first; 1 is first). Click Confirm.
  11. Scroll down and check that the MX record list shows smtp.google.com with priority 1.
  12. Back in Google Workspace, tick the box to confirm the code was updated on the domain host, click Confirm, and leave the page open while DNS verification runs. Verification can complete almost instantly.
  13. When verification finishes, Gmail is ready for the domain. Confirm verification in Google Workspace and wait for confirmation.
  • The MX record for Google Workspace must be Type MX, Name @, Points to smtp.google.com, Priority 1. Without this record, incoming email is not routed to Gmail.
  • Short version: open the DNS settings of the domain, add the MX records that Google Workspace provides for the domain, and wait for the change to take effect (to start working) before expecting email to work.

Mistake to avoid: forwarding instead of email hosting

1 min
  • Choosing Email forwarding instead of Third-party email hosting in Shopify does not connect Google Workspace. Use the Switch to email hosting option for the Google connection.
  • The "Create a forwarding email" option under Email forwarding only forwards to an existing inbox. Use the Third-party email hosting section so Google receives and sends mail for the domain, and paste the verification code into its Text value field.
  • Shopify's free forwarding can still serve as a receiving address. A contact address on the store's domain can be forwarded to your private inbox, so customers see a branded address (an address with your own name in it) while you read mail in your usual account.
  • Example: a contact address such as contact@yourstorename.com can be forwarded to a private inbox with Shopify's free forwarding.

Source D procedure: test the domain email

1 min

The Source D procedure uses the menu Settings > Domains > Email forwarding and hosting.

  1. Choose Third-party email hosting.
  2. Connect a Google Workspace account.
  3. Follow the Google Workspace steps to add the DNS records to the domain.
  4. Check that the domain's email works by sending a test email from it. Shopify's domain settings page shows the DNS records and nameservers (the servers that hold your domain's DNS records) used for this.

Authentication: DMARC, DKIM and TXT records

1 min
  • Shopify requires DMARC (a rule that tells email providers your mail is real) so that outgoing emails do not go to spam. You must authenticate the domain and add a DMARC record. (2025)
  • To add DMARC on Namecheap (a company that sells domains):
    1. Go to Namecheap DNS records.
    2. Get the DMARC code from Shopify: log into your account and go to settings.
    3. If the code is not visible, contact Shopify support via live chat (instant chat with support) for the DNS record code.
    4. Add the code as a DNS record in Namecheap.
    5. Shopify will verify, and emails will no longer show the Shopify domain in the 'from' line (the sender name shown on an email).
  • When transferring (moving) a Google Domain to Shopify, configure the TXT records properly (TXT records are DNS entries that hold short text, used for proof), including a DKIM record (DKIM is a digital signature that proves an email really came from you) in the DNS settings. Without this, order confirmation emails fail to send to customers even though they are triggered in Shopify.

Switching the store to business email

1 min
  • After the professional addresses are working, replace older personal email addresses in the Shopify store settings and anywhere else they appear with the new business addresses. This keeps store notifications and customer contact consistent with the domain. Do this once the Google Workspace setup is complete.
  • Set up the mailbox before the store goes live. A support email in the footer (the bottom strip of every page) only works once a real mailbox for that address exists.
  • Shopify contact form messages are delivered by email to the address set in the store's Shopify account. Check that address in the Shopify settings to make sure inquiries reach you. (2023)

Replacing placeholders in policies and admin

1 min

A placeholder is dummy text that stands in until you add the real one.

  1. In Shopify Admin, replace all placeholder emails with your professional business email addresses.
  2. Update your store footer to display your professional email.
  3. Update the privacy policy, terms of service and returns policy pages with your business email. (A privacy policy explains how you handle customer data; terms of service are the rules for buying from you.)
  4. Update your contact page with your professional email address.
  • Terms of service and demo store placeholder details (such as phone numbers) are not required before supplier contact. (A demo store is a test store with sample content.) Placeholder data such as '555' numbers (fake US phone numbers starting with 555) looks unprofessional and should be replaced with a real phone number or an email address. (2024)
  • Use the same store and business contact details everywhere: the Shopify account, Google Merchant Center and the website's contact page. Mismatched details can cause account reviews or suspensions (accounts being shut down).

Getting a business phone number

1 min
  • Secure a business phone number before building the website, so the number can be placed on the site from the start. Setting it up early keeps the contact details consistent across the store and supplier communication.
  • Add the business phone number to the store only after the phone app signup is finished, because the number is issued at the end of that signup. Before that, there is no number to enter in the Shopify header, footer or contact page.
  • The phone number can come from Google Voice (a phone number service from Google) or OpenPhone (a business phone app).
  • Use a dedicated business phone number, such as a toll-free 800 number (a number that is free for the caller) in the US or a 1300 number in Australia.

Voicemail

1 min

Voicemail is a recorded message box for calls you miss.

  1. Set up a phone number on the store.
  2. Route calls to voicemail after two rings.
  3. Record a voicemail greeting (the message callers hear first) that asks for name, phone number, email address and the reason for the call.
  4. Review the voicemails to learn what customers are asking.

Adding the phone number to the header

1 min

The header is the top strip of every page. A theme is the design settings of your store.

Locations: Online Store > Edit theme > Header, in the field phone number / email address.

  1. In the Shopify admin, click Online Store. Click Edit theme (in the theme customizer, the visual editor for your theme, click Customize).
  2. Click Header. In the left navigation, or click the header in the preview (a live view of your page), then zoom out so the settings panel on the right is visible.
  3. In the field labelled phone number / email address, which initially holds an email address, delete the email address and type the business phone number. The preview updates in real time.
  4. Click Save.
  • Replace the email address in the header with your business phone number.
  • The header contact details (contact text, hours of operation, phone and email) are set under Header > Contact details. Set the hours to the times you can actually answer customer service, and leave the phone field blank until you have a number.
  • Promo header (a small extra bar at the top of the page): set the small promo header element to a heading "Call us today", enter the business phone number, link it to the contact page if wanted, and set the icon to a black cell phone icon.

Adding the phone number to the footer

1 min

Locations: Online Store > Edit theme > Footer menu > Contact us section. The phone field sits directly above the email address. Save after changes.

  1. Scroll down in the theme editor to the footer menu.
  2. Open the Contact us section. It may show an email address and a filler phone number (fake text that fills a space).
  3. Click the section so the WYSIWYG editor (a text box that looks like the final page while you type) opens on the right.
  4. Type the business phone number directly above the email address, so the footer shows two contact methods.
  5. Click Save.
  • Replace the filler phone number with the real business number rather than leaving filler text visible to customers.

Adding the phone number to the contact page

1 min
  1. In the Shopify backend, go to Online Store and then Pages.
  2. Open the Contact us page.
  3. In the WYSIWYG editor, add a section that reads "call us at", followed by the business phone number.
  4. Click Save.

Contact methods on the site

1 min
  • A contact page should offer at least two ways to reach the business, such as a business email and a contact form (a box visitors fill in to send you a message). A phone number or a virtual address (a mailing address that is not your home) can be added as extra methods. (2023)
  • Include multiple ways to contact the business: a contact page with email, phone and contact form, plus email and phone in the footer. A single email address is not enough.
  • Show a phone number in the homepage contact area when one is available. An email address alone is acceptable, but a phone number adds more trust. (2024)
  • If no dedicated contact exists for a supplier form (a contact form from a supplier), use 'support' or a store email address in the contact fields. (2023)
