Store name, domain purchase and domain connection

Section 4.2 · 2 of 20 in this chapter · 33 min read

In short

  • A domain is your store's web address, and it must be unique to your store. Choose a short, easy name that ends in .com for a US store.
  • Check that the name is free before you commit. Also check its past history and that it is not a registered brand owned by someone else.
  • Expect to pay about $6 to $16 per year. Never pay $500 or more for a "premium" name.
  • Set the domain as the primary domain so visitors land on your store.
  • Your own domain name should be the main address customers see. Set it as primary.
More key points
  • A domain bought elsewhere needs changes at the company where you bought it. Then you wait, often for a long time.
  • Keep auto-renew and domain privacy on. Sources disagree on when to turn auto-renew on.
  • Never use the default myshopify.com address as your public address.
  • Change one setting at a time, and save a copy of your theme before editing.

Naming the store and the domain

2 min
  • A domain is the web address of your online store. Customers type it to reach the store.
  • Every store needs its own unique domain. Example: the source's own site
  • The domain is the main web address people type to reach the store.
  • Check that the name is available and affordable before you commit to it.
  • Choose the domain before you invest in the store build or the logo.
  • Check availability first. The name you want is often already taken.
  • Check availability before you finalise brand or logo work.
  • The .com version of a good name is often unavailable. The.com is the ending after the dot, called the extension.
  • If it is taken, try variations of your product or brand name.
  • Keep trying until you find one that is free and fits the brand.
  • Business name and domain: choose the business name first.
  • Then check whether the matching domain is free before you commit.
  • Brainstorm five to ten name ideas.
  • A backup is ready if your favourite domain is taken.
  • Keep the store name and the domain consistent.
  • A business name does not have to match the domain. It can stay broad enough for several businesses.
  • Even so, keep the domain and the store as close as possible, ideally the same.
  • A custom domain should match your Shopify store name, so customers can find and trust it. Shopify is the software that runs your online store.
  • The niche keyword is optional. A niche keyword is a word that names your market, such as "yoga."
  • Include it if it is available and inexpensive.
  • A creative brand name that fits the theme is equally acceptable.
  • A store name does not need to list every category.
  • Buyers who search for a specific product still buy from a store whose name does not mention it.
  • The meaning of the domain matters little, as long as visitors can remember it.
  • Choose a simple, memorable name.
  • The domain can be kept while the brand name changes.
  • One example reused a basic name after a niche rename.
  • For testing, start with a broad, neutral brand name.
  • Use only a few basic branding elements. Branding means your name, logo and look.
  • The offer and the ad creatives decide the first sales more than the logo does. The offer is what you sell and the deal you give. Creatives are your ad images and videos.
  • For unrelated products, a general name such as a female first name works for testing.
  • Add full branding once a product has its first sales.

Practical name rules

1 min
  • Use .com, with no dashes or hyphens, and three words or fewer.
  • The name should be easy to remember and easy to type.
  • Do not use numbers or dashes.
  • Numbers and dashes make the name harder to remember and harder to tell to others.
  • Keep the name short and easy to remember, with no hyphens.
  • Avoid unusual hyphens.
  • Naming is free-form. But the name should be something customers remember.
  • If the.com is unavailable, avoid a shortcut name with an odd ending.
  • A clear form is better, such as shop.brandname.com or brandname.com.
  • Check that the name reads the same when spoken aloud.
  • Use a generic company or LLC name rather than one tied to a single store. An LLC is a registered US company type.
  • This lets several related stores in the same niche share it.
  • Google requires that an LLC's name is not used as a website.
  • Build one website per sales store.
  • The LLC itself needs no website.

Naming procedure

1 min
  1. Brainstorm 5 to 10 business name ideas that identify the market and stay within three words.
  2. Check each idea against Google searches for your product keywords. This confirms it is unique in the market and not a close copy of a competitor's name.
  3. Pick the idea that feels right to you and whose matching domain is available.
  4. Confirm the domain is affordable, then buy it so the business name and domain match exactly.

Domain-selection procedure

1 min
  1. Choose a short, memorable domain with no hyphens that ends in.com.
  2. Check that the name reads the same when spoken aloud.
  3. Check the US trademark registry for conflicts before buying. A trademark is a legally registered brand name. The US trademark registry is the official US list of them.
  4. Buy the domain through the Shopify admin, or through an outside registrar such as Namecheap at about $9 per year. The Shopify admin is the back office of your store. A registrar is a company that sells domains.
  5. Link the domain to the store before contacting suppliers.

  • Google gives preference to domains that carry the local country designator. This is the country code in the ending, such as.au.
  • A local extension is trusted more by local buyers than a.com.
  • Example:.ch in Switzerland or.com.au in Australia.
  • A country-specific extension can be chosen instead of.com when you target one specific country.
  • .com is the first choice for a store domain because it is the most widely accepted.
  • .co is the second most common extension for e-commerce stores.
  • A .com is recommended over.store or similar extensions.
  • Customers type.com by default.
  • For a US store, do not use.net,.store or.org.
  • Customers who come back later will type.com out of habit. They may land on another site or on none at all.
  • For sales to US customers, do not use a.co.uk domain.
  • Shoppers may hesitate to order from a store that appears not to ship to them.
  • In the US, only a .com is needed.
  • In Australia, .com.au is preferred.
  • Buying both.com and.com.au is possible but optional.
  • Use .com even when you sell in only one country.
  • Shoppers remember and type.com by default.
  • A .us domain is not recommended.
  • Meta Ad Library shows the domain to viewers. Meta is the company behind Facebook and Instagram. The Ad Library is a public archive of their ads.
  • A .us or.store domain can make a pre-lander or advertorial look like a direct sales page. A pre-lander is a page shown before your store. An advertorial is an ad written like an article.
  • This can reduce trust.
  • Example: a store that sources (buys) and sells only within one country used that country's extension.
  • Example: .com.au for Australia, as in an early home lighting store.

When the preferred name is taken

1 min
  • When the local extension of a name is taken, the search also suggests.net,.org,.online or.us.
  • For a store aimed at one country, keep the local extension.
  • Change the name instead of switching to a generic extension.
  • When the .com is already taken, do not switch to a different-looking extension.
  • Change the store name, or buy the.com if the owner is willing.
  • A.shop address causes customers to mistrust email from a mismatched domain. A mismatched domain is one that does not match your store name.

Availability

1 min
  • Search the name in the store's domain search, or at the registrar, with the correct local extension.
  • If the result says available, add it to the cart.
  • In Shopify's Buy new domain search, the default results are suggestions.
  • You can type a specific name you already chose straight into the search field.
  • Delete any "http://" prefix and trailing slash before searching. The prefix is the start of the address. The trailing slash is the slash at the end.
  • Then confirm the result says the domain is available.
  • If a candidate domain's website cannot be reached, that usually means nobody is using it. A candidate is a name you are considering.
  • Still confirm availability in the store's own domain search.
  • An unreachable site does not prove the name can be bought.
  • A message that the domain is taken means someone already owns it.
  • You cannot buy it for that name. Do not force that name.
  • Search the next backup name the same way. Repeat until one shows as available with the correct extension.
  • AI chat tools cannot check real-time domain availability.
  • Their suggestions may already be registered.
  • Taken names often belong to someone else's site. Others are parked, meaning unused and held for sale, or listed as premium, meaning sold at a high price.
  • Avoid buying domains that other people hold to sell at high prices.
  • Choose a name that is actually available after brainstorming.
  • Do not buy domain names for resale as a business idea.
  • Many are held for thousands of dollars, and this is unnecessary.

Trademark

1 min
  • Before buying, search the US trademark registry for conflicts.
  • If a store name or domain may infringe a trademark, search the US trademark registry first. To infringe means to break the rights of a brand's owner.
  • If it does, pick a new domain.
  • Link the new domain to the existing Shopify store.
  • This keeps all products and settings.

History checks (procedure)

1 min
  1. Pick the available domain you like best.
  2. Google "site:" followed by the domain name. "site:" is a Google search command that lists pages on one domain. Zero results are normal for a new name.
  3. Open the Google Transparency Report. Enter the domain in the check site status field. The Google Transparency Report shows whether Google has flagged a site as unsafe. "No data available" is normal.
  4. Open the Wayback Machine at archive.org. Enter the domain to view its browse history. The Wayback Machine is a free archive of old versions of websites.
  5. Only if all three checks show no problems, buy the domain.
  • An old site: a search that returns old pages is acceptable if they are relevant to the niche.
  • Spammy or scammy old pages are a red flag. Spammy means junk. Scammy means it tricks people.
  • If so, pick a different name.
  • A domain can be available and still have a history of penalties. A penalty is Google's punishment that pushes a site down in results.
  • History checks are needed even when the name is free.
  • A blacklisted domain is one that was listed as unsafe. For example, one that once carried a virus, which is harmful software. This is rare, roughly one in 500.
  • A blacklisted domain can damage a store built on it.
  • If the Wayback Machine has archived a domain, open its old snapshots. A snapshot is a saved copy of a page on one day. Check them for spam or scam content.
  • Then search Google for the domain's past reviews.
  • If there is no archive, the domain is effectively new, and that is fine.

  • Each store needs its own domain.
  • Do not buy premium or parked domains at high prices.
  • A similar domain can work just as well.
  • Avoid parked domains listed for resale. (2023 price: about $14 to $15 per year)
  • They are often overpriced. (2023 price: about $14 to $15 per year)

  • Keep the domain cost low. Spend no more than about $14 per year on a domain name.

Buying a domain in Shopify

2 min

Why Shopify

  • Shopify is the recommended place to buy a domain for a Shopify store.
  • Common other registrars are GoDaddy, Namecheap and Google Domains.
  • A domain bought through Shopify is live immediately.
  • There is no waiting for SSL and no DNS changes. SSL is the security certificate that makes your site safe. DNS is the settings that point your domain to the store.
  • This makes it the simplest option for a beginner.
  • Buying in Shopify is the intended way to get a low-cost name for a new store.
  • The Shopify purchase is quick.
  • It needs only a few clicks.
  • Source A's 2019 advice was to buy the domain directly in Shopify for about $14 per year.
  • The advice was against buying through an external registrar such as GoDaddy.
  • Namecheap is another common registrar.
  • Buying in Shopify was used for ease of integration, meaning how easily the parts connect.
  • A domain bought in Shopify is added to the store's account.
  • It is billed yearly at the price shown during purchase, for example $16 per year.
  • The buyer owns the domain, so visitors who type that address reach the store.
  • A domain bought through Shopify can be used in the same Shopify account.
  • It can be set as the primary domain, the main address visitors land on.

Limits of a Shopify-bought domain

  • A domain bought from Shopify cannot be moved to other platforms.
  • Other platforms include WooCommerce and ClickFunnels. These are other store and page-building software.
  • The source's view is that this rarely matters if you stay on Shopify.
  • A domain bought through Shopify can be transferred to another registrar. A transfer means moving the domain to another company. (older, 2023 source; conflicts with the platform limit above, which concerns platforms, not registrars)
  • Such as Namecheap or GoDaddy. (older, 2023 source; conflicts with the platform limit above, which concerns platforms, not registrars)
  • Every domain registrar allows transfers. (older, 2023 source; conflicts with the platform limit above, which concerns platforms, not registrars)

Buying a domain at an external registrar

1 min

Cost and reasons to choose it

  • External registrars such as Namecheap and GoDaddy typically cost about $6 to $10 per year.
  • That is considerably cheaper than buying through Shopify.
  • External registrars are the better choice when budget matters.
  • They are also better when the domain may later be used on other platforms such as WooCommerce or Wix. Wix is another site builder.
  • The trade-off is that they do not include free email forwarding. Email forwarding sends mail sent to your domain on to your own inbox.
  • A separate paid email service is needed for a professional address.
  • A domain held at an outside registrar is easier to move later. (Source E; this conflicts with the Shopify recommendation above. Both apply: Shopify for the simplest launch, an outside registrar for cheaper, portable and email-ready domains.)
  • It also makes business email simpler to set up. (Source E; this conflicts with the Shopify recommendation above. Both apply: Shopify for the simplest launch, an outside registrar for cheaper, portable and email-ready domains.)
  • Source A's 2023 guidance: domains can be purchased from any registrar. A registrar is a company that sells domains.
  • Such domains can be connected to a Shopify store.
  • You do not have to buy them through Shopify.
  • Buying a domain through GoDaddy and linking it to Shopify avoids TikTok warnings about links in the bio.
  • A professional business email from the domain can be used for the store's social accounts.

Connecting an external domain to Shopify (DNS, TXT and SSL)

1 min

Procedure F (undated)

1 min
  1. Click Settings in the bottom left corner of the Shopify admin (the control panel).
  2. Open Domains.
  3. Click Connect existing domain, not Buy new domain.
  4. Type the exact domain name you bought from the external provider.
  5. Click Next. If the domain was bought externally, Shopify shows the option to continue.
  6. Follow the DNS settings Shopify shows, and make the changes at your external registrar (the company where you bought the domain). Shopify's official guide describes the exact DNS settings to use.
  7. Expect a waiting period for the DNS changes and SSL before the domain works with the store.
  • Use Connect existing domain only for a domain already bought elsewhere, such as at Namecheap or GoDaddy (domain sellers). Shopify has documentation for the connection steps.
  • A domain registered elsewhere can still be connected to Shopify, but it needs more setup than one bought in Shopify.

Procedure G (2025-26, Namecheap)

2 min
  1. Go to Settings > Domains in the Shopify admin.
  2. Click Connect Existing Domain and enter your domain.
  3. Shopify reports that the DNS records (the entries that tell the internet where your domain points) do not point to Shopify yet, and shows the records to change.
  4. Log in to Namecheap and open the domain's Advanced DNS tab.

A record: an entry that points your domain to a server address. CNAME: an entry that points one name to another name.

Record Name / host Current value Change to
A as listed by Shopify 162.255.119.46 23.227.38.65
CNAME www parkingpage.namecheap.com shops.myshopify.com
  1. In Namecheap's Advanced DNS, update the existing A record from 162.255.119.46 to 23.227.38.65.
  2. If no A record is listed, use Add New Record and choose A Record with the value 23.227.38.65.
  3. Update the www CNAME record from parkingpage.namecheap.com to shops.myshopify.com.
  4. Click Save All Changes.
  5. Return to Shopify and click I updated it.
  6. Wait a while for DNS propagation (the time the changes take to spread across the internet) and the TLS certificate (the secure-connection certificate) to complete before assuming the connection failed.
  • Right after DNS changes, Shopify shows DNS propagation at 0.
  • The TLS certificate shows as not provisioned (not yet set up). This is normal during the waiting period, so do not change the records again straight away.
  • After you connect a custom domain under Settings > Domains, the connected domain may show as redirecting to the store's default myshopify.com address (Shopify's built-in address) until the DNS records are correct. Expect this during setup. It is not a final result.
  • Expecting the external domain to work immediately after connecting it is a mistake. DNS changes and SSL setup take time, so the store will not load on the new domain right away.
  • A 2024 source says a domain linked under Settings > Domains takes effect immediately. The DNS wait described above applies to external domains. Treat the wait as the rule when the domain was bought elsewhere.

TXT records (domain verification)

1 min

A TXT record is a text entry that proves you own the domain.

  • For a domain bought through Shopify, go to Sales channels > Online store > Domains, click the main store domain and then Manage, then open DNS settings.
  • Click Add custom record, choose TXT, keep the Name as @, paste the verification value into Text value, and click Confirm.
  • In Shopify's Domain settings > Add custom record, enter @ in the name or host field. @ means the whole domain. Paste the verification value from Search Console (Google's site-check tool) into the value field and confirm.
  • For a domain bought from a registrar other than Shopify, add the TXT record in the DNS settings of the site where the domain is hosted.
  • Always use the main domain, not the myshopify.com address, when editing DNS records.

SSL

1 min

SSL is the certificate that makes your site use the secure https connection.

  • The SSL certificate on Shopify is free. Enable it through Shopify's documentation to show the HTTPS lock (the padlock showing a secure connection) and the secure connection badge to customers.
  • An SSL certificate is included with Shopify, so buying one separately is unnecessary. (2023)
  • Shopify's 2022 advice is to enable SSL through its documentation. The 2023 advice says it is included. Both describe the same certificate: it is free, and you do not buy it separately.
  • A newly added custom domain can show the status Provisioning TLS (being set up) while its SSL certificate is still being issued. Wait until this status clears before treating the domain as ready for visitors.
  • A domain bought through Shopify does not wait for SSL.

Primary domain, redirects and www

1 min

The primary domain is the main address customers see. A redirect automatically sends visitors from one address to another. www is the "www." at the start of an address.

Setting the primary domain

1 min
  • After buying a domain, open Settings > Domains, find the new domain in the list, and click Set as primary. From then on, anyone who types that domain lands on the storefront (the public side of the store).
  • Connect the domain and set it as the primary domain before launch.
  • The purchased domain must be set as the primary domain. Additional domains can be connected or transferred without creating a new Shopify account.
  • Admin path (the menu route) for a primary change: Online Store > Domains > Change primary domain. Select the custom domain and click Save. Traffic from all domains then redirects to the primary domain.
  • Under Primary for Online Store, you can switch the primary domain. (2026)
  • After a custom domain is bought, check the Domains list under Settings to confirm it is marked as the primary domain. The default.myshopify.com address should no longer be the one shown to customers.
  • Leaving the default Shopify-assigned domain as your public address is a mistake. Use a professional domain that represents your brand, and set it as the primary domain.

Auto-assigned or manual primary (sources differ)

1 min
  • Shopify automatically assigns a newly bought domain as the primary domain.
  • Once bought, the domain becomes the primary domain.
  • Shopify's Settings say no additional configuration is required after purchase.
  • Other sources say the purchased domain must be set as primary manually, from Settings > Domains.
  • Check the Domains list after purchase, whichever way it was set.

Redirects, www and non-www

1 min
  • If traffic from the other domains is not redirected to the primary domain, the page shows an "Enable redirection" link. After the primary domain is changed, the page shows "Traffic from all your domains redirects to this primary domain" with a Disable redirection option.
  • When a custom domain is connected, Shopify also adds the www version as a third-party domain (an extra domain entry) with its own SSL status. The www and non-www addresses redirect to each other, so visitors reach the store through either one once the primary domain is set.
  • Use the non-www version of the custom domain as the primary domain, because www and non-www redirect to each other, and the non-www form is simpler.
  • Check that the www version is connected to the store and set to redirect to the root domain (the main address without www), so the store has one main address. (2026)
  • In Settings > Domains, the www variant of a custom domain can show "Redirects to the myshopify.com address" during setup.
  • Two domains pointing to the same Shopify store show identical content, which splits brand authority (the trust and ranking strength a brand builds). Choose one domain and build its authority. Redirects are possible but not recommended.

The myshopify.com address

2 min
  • Every Shopify store keeps an automatically generated myshopify.com domain that cannot be deleted. It should remain as the backend (the hidden admin side of the store), while the custom domain is the primary public domain.
  • The admin default domain on Shopify cannot be removed, so the custom domain must be set as primary to stop customers reaching the myshopify.com address.
  • On the Domains page, the myshopify.com address is listed under Shopify-managed domains with the note "Never expires". It cannot be removed.
  • The myshopify.com entry is labelled Primary for Online Store in the Domains list until a custom domain is set as primary. Use the myshopify.com entry for the Shopify URL field in the Shrine whitelist.
  • A new Shopify account starts with a default domain made of random letters and numbers under.myshopify.com. Customers should not see this address, so replace it with a purchased custom domain as the primary domain.
  • The default myshopify.com domain is only a technical placeholder. Choose any name for it, because the store is later connected to a purchased custom domain used for promotion.
  • Using a store on a default myshopify.com address is a weak sign for a business and can look unprofessional. Use a custom domain name. (2024)
  • A store on a myshopify.com URL looks unprofessional to customers and suppliers. Keep the myshopify.com URL only for demo stores.
  • The myshopify.com subdomain (a name placed before the main domain) is not visible to customers, so it does not matter much. The store name in the admin (name.myshopify.com) is not visible to customers either.
  • Buying a domain in the admin and linking it hides the myshopify.com address from customers with one click.
  • Before a custom domain is connected, the store is reachable at a temporary address in the form store-name.myshopify.com. This is also the address shown in the admin header. The store is not visible to the public at that address while it is password-protected.
  • A new Shopify store starts with a temporary myshopify.com address. The real domain is connected before the store goes live. The domain choice does not have to be made during signup, and there is no deadline for it. (2023)
  • Register the business name and domain before creating the Shopify account, because the store is set up under that name. The domain is connected to the store later through the Add domain task on the dashboard. (2021-22, older; this conflicts with the no-deadline point above. Both apply: the domain is not required at signup, but registering the name first is a sound order.)

Renewals and privacy

1 min

Auto-renew means the domain extends and pays itself each year. Domain privacy hides your name and contact details in public records.

Auto-renew (sources differ)

1 min
  • To renew a Shopify domain, go to Settings > Domains in the Shopify admin. The domain is usually set to auto-renew, so check that setting before the renewal date.
  • Turn on auto-renew every year. (2026)
  • At purchase, leave auto-renew off and keep domain privacy on. Once the business is working, go back and turn auto-renew on so the domain does not expire. (2025-26)
  • The sources differ at the start. The 2026 source and the 2021 source keep auto-renew on from the start. The 2025-26 source turns it off at purchase and on later, once the business works.

Registration term and renewal price (GoDaddy, 2021-22, older)

1 min
  • A domain can be registered for one or two years at checkout. It renews automatically each year (or each registration period) at the price originally paid. A one-year term saves money now, while a two-year term is equally valid.
  • Introductory prices can be far below the renewal rate, so check the renewal price before buying.

Privacy

1 min
  • Keep domain privacy on when registering the domain.
  • GoDaddy states that domains include Basic Privacy Protection, which hides the owner's contact details in public registration records. Keep this default on when buying the domain for a business.

Stability

1 min
  • Once a domain is bought and set up on the store, it should not be changed later. Treat the choice as long-term and do not rush it.

Changing, transferring and moving domains

1 min

Whether to change at all

1 min
  • Changing a domain for SEO (getting found in search engines) is usually unnecessary. Products, brands and SKU numbers (each product's stock code) are what rank, not the niche keyword in the domain. Keep an existing domain unless there is a strong reason to replace it.
  • If the store is still new, changing its domain name is fine. Link the new domain to the existing Shopify account rather than building a new store.
  • To move an existing store to a new domain, in the Shopify admin, remove the current domain and connect the new one. Products, categories and settings carry over, and no new Shopify account is needed.

Safety rules for a change

1 min
  • Making changes to the primary domain, contact email and payment method in one session led Shopify to suspend (pause) the store, and support did not explain the reason at first. Make such changes one at a time, and check the store after each one.
  • Keep a saved copy of the working theme (the store's design files) before editing, so the store can be restored quickly.
  • Changing a store's domain name can break existing Google accounts (Google Workspace for business tools and email, Google Analytics for traffic tracking, Merchant Center for product listings) that were set up on the old domain. If a domain change is needed, create new Google accounts for the new domain instead of reusing the old ones. (2025)

Procedure H: change a store domain (undated)

1 min
  1. Buy the new domain and link it in the Shopify admin.
  2. Update your store emails to use the new domain.
  3. Update the domain in Google Merchant Center.
  4. Create a new product feed (a file listing your products for ads), since all URLs change.
  5. Relink the new URL to Google Analytics, Merchant Center, Facebook and Bing.

Procedure I: rename a store domain without damaging ads

1 min
  1. Turn off all Google Shopping ads (Google's product ads).
  2. Delete the product feeds from Google Merchant Center, Microsoft Ads and Facebook shop feeds.
  3. Buy or link the new domain in Shopify so it loads the store.
  4. Resubmit the products from the new domain, and expect a few days of downtime while they are approved again.

Procedure J: switch to a new domain in the same Shopify store (undated)

1 min
  1. Buy a new domain in the same Shopify account.
  2. In the TikTok channel app in Shopify, disconnect and reconnect the channel, and select the same pixel (a tracking code that records visitor actions) so the historical (past) data is kept.
  3. Set the new domain as primary in Shopify.
  4. Add the new URL to the new campaigns, because campaigns change every two to three days.

Transfers

1 min
  • A domain bought through Shopify can be transferred to another registrar, and a domain bought elsewhere can be connected to Shopify. Every domain registrar allows transfers. (2023)
  • Transferring a domain to Shopify is a separate procedure from building the store and needs to be handled as its own step. (2025)
  • Shopify's Domains page offers Transfer domain alongside Connect existing domain and Buy new domain. Transfer domain moves a domain registered elsewhere into Shopify. Buy new domain registers a new domain through Shopify.
  • Shopify's Connect existing domain links a domain without moving its registration. Transfer domain moves the registration itself.

Procedure K: move a domain bought through Shopify to a new store (2025)

1 min
  1. Create the new Shopify account.
  2. Follow Shopify's knowledge base documentation to transfer the domain from the old account to the new one.
  3. Close the old account once the domain has moved.

Linking the domain through DNS settings is not enough. The domain itself must be transferred.

Subdomains and multiple stores

1 min

A subdomain is a name placed before the main domain, such as store.yourdomain.com.

  • Each store needs its own domain. Do not buy a domain for every niche before the first store proves itself. A new store should have its own URL rather than a subdomain of another store. (2025)
  • Use a store on its own domain rather than a subdomain such as store.yourdomain.com. Separate memorable domains bring more returning customers and are better for a second store. A domain costs about $14 per year.
  • A store can run on a subdomain, such as store.yourwebsite.com, linked from the header menu (the top menu bar) of the existing website. Alternatively, the existing website content can be moved to a new Shopify store, and the main URL can point to it.
  • Shopify can host a blog on a subdomain. Install the store on a subdomain such as store.yourdomain.com while the main domain keeps the blog. The store then has full Shopify functionality, and the rest of the site remains unchanged.
  • Build a separate.com store for each other market you sell into.
  • Each extra domain costs about $14 per year, and each one has its own fee. (2023)

Reusing a store for a new niche or domain

1 min

A niche is a narrow market, such as one product group. A tagline is a short slogan.

Reuse decisions

1 min
  • If the domain and company name are generic (broad, not tied to one product), reuse the existing store for a new niche by changing the tagline and branding in the backend. Starting from scratch is not necessary.
  • An existing Shopify store can be reused for a new niche by editing the store and, optionally, buying a new domain (about $12 a year) and linking it.
  • To add a new domain to an existing Shopify store, link it in the Shopify admin panel. The 2021 source says this creates a new store on the same account while the old one continues to exist. (2021, older; later sources describe the same store with a new domain, see the next point)
  • Linking a new domain to the existing Shopify account, rather than building a new store, is the approach in later sources. (undated)
  • A new domain can be bought in the same Shopify account, then set as the primary domain. The downside of keeping one store is that other products cannot be tested in that same store without mixing their results.
  • If one product is clearly working, keep it in the same store. Buy a new domain, make it the primary domain, and keep the apps. The downside is that other products cannot be tested in the same store.

Moving a winning product to its own brand (undated)

1 min
  1. Buy a new domain that fits the winning product (the product that sells well). A reported cost is about $10 per year.
  2. Create a new logo for the product brand.
  3. Create a new Facebook page for the product's ads.
  4. Point the new domain to the Shopify store's base URL (the store's main web address).
  5. Switch the store and the ads to the new logo, domain and Facebook page.

Store name checks when reusing

1 min
  • The store name in the Shopify admin is not visible to customers. The URL customers see can be changed as often as needed by changing the domain connected to the store.
  • If the.com is already taken for a reused store, the domain may change while the store stays the same. Keep the domain and store consistent.
  • Before buying a domain for a new niche, check whether the existing domain and company name are generic enough to reuse.

Further details

2 min
  • Adding a new domain to Shopify is simple. Buy the domain through the Shopify backend, then select which domain is primary for your store from the settings.
  • Do not use.myshopify.com extensions for your store URL. Always use a custom domain name (e.g., chandelier.com, not yourstore.myshopify.com) for a professional appearance.
  • Shopify domain path: in the Shopify admin, open Settings (bottom left), then Domains, where the Buy new domain option is shown. The default Shopify-assigned domain shown on a new account is not a professional domain.
  • Domain names can be bought from any registrar and linked to Shopify. Avoid expensive parked resale domains (names for sale that sit unused), and choose an open domain available for about $14 to $15 per year.
  • Domain names can be bought directly through Shopify and are linked to the store, so the domain does not have to be bought from a separate registrar.
  • Under Settings > Domains, the Shopify domain and any purchased custom domain are listed. Both are visible there once the domain has been bought.

More details

  • In Settings > Domains, use the connect existing domain option only for a domain already bought elsewhere, such as at Namecheap or GoDaddy. Shopify has documentation for the connection steps.
  1. In the Shopify admin, go to Settings > Domains.
  2. Click Connect Existing Domain and enter your domain.
  3. Shopify reports that the DNS records do not point to Shopify yet and shows the records to change.
  4. Log in to Namecheap and open the domain's Advanced DNS tab.

  1. In Namecheap's Advanced DNS, update the existing A record from 162.255.119.46 to 23.227.38.65.
  2. If no A record is listed, use Add New Record and choose A Record with the value 23.227.38.65.
  3. Update the www CNAME record from parkingpage.namecheap.com to shops.myshopify.com.
  4. Click Save All Changes.
  5. Return to Shopify and click I updated it.
  6. Wait a while for DNS propagation and the TLS certificate to complete before assuming the connection failed.
