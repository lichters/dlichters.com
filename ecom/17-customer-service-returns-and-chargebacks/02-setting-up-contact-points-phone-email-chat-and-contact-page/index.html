Playbook › 17. Customer service, returns and chargebacks

Setting up contact points: phone, email, chat and contact page

Section 17.2 · 2 of 22 in this chapter · 19 min read

In short

  • Use a separate business phone number, not your personal one, so work and private calls stay apart.
  • Give customers at least two contact options on the store, such as email, phone, or chat.
  • Answer email within 24 hours, and set chat to Offline when you cannot answer it.
  • Set a voicemail that asks for the caller's name, email, phone, and reason for calling.
  • Delete junk messages, and check the spam folder before deciding a customer did not write.
This section has 12 parts. Tap one to open it.

Business phone numbers

2 min
  • Use a dedicated business phone number, not a personal cell number (your everyday phone), so business and private calls never mix on the same device.
  • Calls are one contact channel, alongside email and chat. You can get a toll-free number (a number customers can call for free, because the business pays the cost).
  • A toll-free number makes the store look more established and gives customers an easy way to reach support. This can reduce chargebacks (when a customer asks their bank to take money back) caused by unclear order problems.
  • Customers who call before ordering are checking whether the store responds. A visible phone number builds confidence and reduces hesitation to buy.

Testing the number

  1. Call the public phone number from a different phone, such as a partner's or friend's phone.
  2. Confirm that the call rings through to the phone you will answer.
  3. If it does not, find and fix the cause before launch.

Phone hours and forwarding

  • Phone or service hours should match when customers actually call, which often peaks in the evening after work. Extended hours can be a differentiator (something that sets you apart), even if round-the-clock calls are rare.
  • Case: in the first business, the seller offered 24-hour phone support staffed by overseas agents (helpers working in another country) while the seller slept. Almost no calls came at 3 a.m., but many came after work, around 6 to 7 p.m. Paying agents to cover those hours was worth it because competitors did not answer calls. The seller forwarded the line to their own phone at night (forwarding means sending calls to another phone), and calls at that hour were almost never received.
  • Forward the business number to your personal mobile during the day, so you can take calls whenever you are free.
  • Set the number to divert to a prerecorded voicemail (a message recorded in advance) when you cannot answer, so callers always get a message instead of silence.
  • Outsource phone support (pay another company or person to answer for you) once the store is profitable enough to justify it.

Voicemail greetings and phone handling

2 min
  • Keep the phone number on the website even if you cannot answer every call. Use a voicemail greeting (the spoken message callers hear) that captures the caller's name, email, phone, and reason.
  • A business phone number should be listed on the store, with a voicemail that takes name, phone, email, and reason for the call, and promises a callback as soon as possible.
  • Without a voicemail on the public number, callers reach nothing when you cannot pick up. Set a prerecorded voicemail that lets callers request a callback. This matters most if you still work a day job during business hours.
  • Most inbound calls are either pre-sale questions from people wanting to order, post-sale questions about shipping dates, or checks that the business is legitimate. A voicemail with a short greeting helps you learn which group is calling.

Voicemail template, store line (2023)

Hello, you've reached [store name, e.g. storename.com]. Thanks for calling. Our team is currently away from the desk or helping other customers, so please record a voicemail including your name, email address, phone number, and the purpose of your call. We'll respond as soon as we're able.

Voicemail template, personal-brand greeting (no year given)

Hi, this is [your name] from [store name].com. Sorry, we are unable to take your call right now. We are either away from the desk or helping another customer. Please leave a voicemail with your full name, email address, phone number and the reason for your call, and we will get back to you as soon as possible.

Voicemail template (2023)

Hello, you've reached [name] from [store domain]. Thanks for getting in touch. Right now I'm either stepped away from my desk or helping another shopper. Leave a voicemail including your name, phone number, email address, and what you're calling about, and I'll return your call as soon as I can.

Voicemail template

Hi, you have reached [Store name] at [domain]. We are sorry we cannot take your call right now because we are assisting other customers. Please leave a voicemail with your name, phone number, email address and the reason for your call, and we will get back to you as soon as possible.

Voicemail script, improved

Hi, you have reached [Your Name] at [Store Name], [your-store-domain.com]. We are unable to take your call right now because we are away from the office or helping other customers. Please leave your name, your email address, your phone number and the reason for your call, and we will get back to you as soon as possible.

Handling customer calls before hiring phone support (2023)

  1. Set the phone app's default voicemail greeting to the template.
  2. Turn on transcription (which turns each voicemail into written text), so each voicemail is sent as text immediately.
  3. When a transcript arrives, call the customer back with the reason and details.
  4. Once the business is profitable, hire someone to answer the phone.

Dedicated support email and aliases

1 min
  • Set up a dedicated support email for the brand, and have a virtual assistant (VA, a remote helper) reply quickly. Every customer should get an immediate response to questions or delivery problems.
  • Create the dedicated support email on Google Workspace (formerly G Suite, a paid business email service from Google) for about $10 per month. Use an address such as contact@ or info@ followed by the store domain (your store's web address). Connect your domain, then set this address as the store contact email in Shopify.
  • Use the business email support address for support requests. The team reviews incoming messages Monday to Friday and almost around the clock, and escalates urgent ones to the owner.
  • Set a dedicated returns@ alias (a second address that goes to the same inbox). It separates return requests from sales and supplier mail while keeping everything in one inbox. Replies should come from the same address the customer wrote to.
  • Let a virtual assistant also send tracking numbers to customers.
  • Use a dedicated business address for the store, not a personal one. Addresses on the contact page are mailing addresses, and the page should say that returns are not accepted there.

Email signatures and sending rules

  • Every business email must include a signature (the block of text at the bottom of an email) with the sender's name, the business name, the business phone number, and the sender's personal business email address.
  • Set this signature up automatically in Outlook (Microsoft's email program) to save time, optionally with a small version of the business logo.
  • New emails to customers or suppliers that are not new orders must be sent only from the staff member's personal business email address, using the same signature as for replies.

Spam control and lost messages

2 min

Contact form and address spam

  • A support email address shown on the website attracts spam (unwanted junk messages) and irrelevant inquiries. Use a contact form (a box on the website where visitors type a message) instead, with predefined subject lines. This allows auto responses and rules to be applied.
  • Listing a plain support email address on a contact page leads to spam and irrelevant inquiries. Use a contact form instead.
  • Contact form in Gorgias (a customer support tool): go to Settings > Channels > Contact Form. Name the form, connect the store, define subject lines, then publish it on the website using the embed code (a short snippet of code you paste into the site).

Spam procedure for contact form submissions

  1. Delete junk emails immediately.
  2. If the same email addresses appear repeatedly, block them in your email provider settings.
  3. Do not mark them as spam; simply delete.
  4. Expect spam whenever you have public contact forms on your store. This is normal.

Checking for missing replies

  • For a support email that has not been answered, check your spam folder and that of the customer. Replies from customers and outgoing responses are both prone to spam filters (automatic systems that move mail to the spam folder).
  • If no reply arrives within the 48-hour window, follow up and check the spam folder.
  • A support email system can fail silently, so requests may never reach the team. If you have not received a reply after four to five days, send the request again through another channel.
  • A helpdesk switch (moving to a new shared support inbox or tool) can lose emails. A supplier or customer who says they sent a message but got no reply may have had it lost. Ask them to forward or resend the email to the new address.
  • Relying only on email notifications for form submissions introduces delays that competitors overcome. Real-time alerts (instant messages to your phone) enable faster contact and win high-value deals.

Email deliverability (whether emails actually reach the inbox)

  • Set up the DMARC record for the business domain to increase trust and deliverability, so customers receive order and account emails. (A DMARC record is a setting in your domain that tells other email systems the mail really comes from you.) In Shopify: Settings > Notifications > Sender email > Email domain authentication. Until it is set up, Shopify sends via a backup store address.

Contact page and form content

2 min

Editing the Shopify contact page

  1. Go to Shopify Admin > Online Store > Pages (Shopify Admin is the back office where you manage the store), and open the Contact page that Shopify created automatically with the account.
  2. Change the page title to Contact Us.
  3. Keep the theme template (the page layout type) set to Contact, which adds a contact form to the page.
  4. Click Save.
  5. Click View Page to check the result. The page is reachable at /pages/contacts.
  • The Contact template adds a form that visitors can fill out. Each submission arrives in your email with the visitor's contact details. At the start, the template alone is enough; more contact channels can be added later.
  • Shopify admin > Pages > Contact us: keep the contact form and add at least two further contact methods, such as hours of operation, a real phone number, and an email address or live chat. Then save. Google checks that a storefront (the public side of the store) shows at least two contact methods.
  • The Contact Us page should be detailed and personal. Include the owner's name, a clear invitation to email, and the email address, so customers know who to reach after purchase.
  • A support page must show customers how to contact the business. Contact details that are easy to find reduce pre-sale and post-sale uncertainty.
  • Make contact options (email, phone, live chat), shipping destinations, and the refund policy clearly visible. Without them, visitors who have a question leave and buy from a larger marketplace instead.

Where the contact details go

  • Show customer service contact details, such as a phone number with opening hours, in the header (the top part of every page), so they are visible on every page.
  • Put contact details in the footer (the bottom part of every page): phone, email, and postal address. Search engines and paid ad platforms such as Meta (the company behind Facebook and Instagram) and Google tend to view a store as more legitimate when contact information is visible. The details also give customers an easy way to reach the store.

Reconciling plain email and contact form

  • Public visitors: use the contact form with predefined subject lines as the main route, because a plain address attracts spam.
  • Visible contact options: the store still needs at least two contact methods on the storefront, and a plain email address can be one of them.

Chat-widget wording on the site (2025)

We'll reply in a few moments.

Thanks for reaching out! Leave your email below and a team member will respond in roughly five minutes.

We'll be back tomorrow.
  • While team members are signed in and available, the chat widget (the small chat box on the site) displays "We'll reply in a few moments." If nobody is available, it switches to the contact-capture prompt. Once business hours end, the greeting becomes "We'll be back tomorrow." Every message should match the brand's personality and tone.

Live chat on the store

1 min
  • A live chat widget on the store gives shoppers a real-time support channel. It is usually used to answer questions before they buy.
  • The online chat widget appears in the bottom right-hand corner of the screen on all devices.
  • keep the chat widget at the bottom left or bottom right of the page, because shoppers expect the chat there.
  • Before a chat starts, visitors must enter a name and email address. The owner keeps the contact details even if the chat is not answered in time, and can follow up by email.
  • When visitors start a chat, they are prompted to enter their name and email. The email is captured even if you do not reply immediately, which allows follow-up if the chat is missed.
  • The visitor panel in a chat shows the visitor's email address, how long they have been a customer, and their location. Shopify counts any email address stored in the system as a customer, even if that person has never bought anything.
  • Mobile notifications (alerts on your phone) let the owner answer chats while away from the computer, which keeps response times short. Slow answers to pre-sale questions cost sales, so set up the phone app from the start.

Widget hours and offline status

2 min

Going offline when you cannot answer

  1. Open the chat dashboard (the control panel where you see and answer chats).
  2. Click the status drop-down (a menu) in the top left-hand corner.
  3. Set it to "Offline" when you cannot be online for any reason.
  • During working hours, the chat dashboard must stay open while the staff member is at their computer. Outside working hours, the chat is in Offline mode, so customers leave messages, or the owner monitors the chat.
  • When the live chat agent is on break, hide the chat widget instead of showing an offline status. Or route chats to a phone if staff can step in. This keeps the site from looking closed.

Setting hours: two approaches

  • Keep availability as open as is realistic. While one person runs the store, reaching as many visitors as possible during waking hours matters more than strict business hours.
  • Use 24/7 when visitors who write outside working hours should still be able to leave their name and email for follow-up. Available hours are 24/7 by default and can be edited to limit when the chat icon appears. Keeping 24/7 is recommended.
  • Use a waking-hours window when you run the store alone and want staff notified only by phone. Set the window to the hours you are awake, for example 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM, individually for each day of the week. Chat stays on the store during these hours, and staff are not required to sit at the computer. Changes can take a few minutes to appear on the store.

Tidio operating hours

  • Turn on operating hours (the times you are available) in Tidio, and set the times you are available each day. For example: Monday 7:30 AM to 6 PM, and Tuesday until 4 PM. Match these hours to your target audience, or outsource replies to a VA if you cannot cover them.
  • Turn on operating hours and set hours per weekday, e.g. Monday until 6 p.m., Tuesday until 4 p.m.

Shopify Inbox

3 min

What it is and where it runs

  • Shopify Inbox is the live chat app included with a Shopify account. Its mobile app lets you read and answer chats from your phone while customers browse the store.
  • Shopify has built-in live chat on the storefront. A Shopify mobile app sends a notification (an alert) to the seller's phone when a visitor starts a chat, so the seller can reply from anywhere.
  • Shopify stores do not include live chat by default, so a chat app must be installed. Live chat adds a personalised sales channel next to phone and email, and it demands less staff time than phone calls.
  • Shopify Inbox is a built-in live chat option in Shopify that serves the same purpose as Tidio.

Mobile app: which app to use

  1. Install the separate Shopify Inbox app on iPhone or Android. Do not use the main Shopify app.
  2. Open it and log in with the store's Shopify account.
  3. Incoming chats appear as push notifications (alerts that pop up on your phone) and open in the app.
  4. Type the reply and press send. It appears live in the storefront conversation.
  • The main Shopify mobile app sends a notification to the seller's phone when a visitor starts a chat.
  • Set up the phone app from the start, so the owner can answer quickly.

Inbox settings and conversations

  • In Inbox, a conversation with unread messages shows an unread count (the number of messages you have not read yet) in the list. Opening it reveals the full history. Replies can be sent from the admin or the mobile app, and both devices show the same conversation.
  • Before a shopper can send a question, Shopify Inbox can require them to enter their name and contact details. This gives the store a contact to follow up with if the chat is missed. Shoppers can also opt into emails at this step.
  • Keep the customer marketing opt-in option enabled in Shopify Inbox settings. (An opt-in is a tick box where a visitor agrees to receive emails.) This lets chat visitors opt into receiving emails from the store.
  • The first reply can be automated, and it is off by default. When turned on, the message can differ depending on whether the store is marked online or offline.

Instant answers and built-in options

  • Instant answers are automatic AI replies (replies generated by artificial intelligence) to common visitor questions, built from store content. The suggested shipping question takes its answer from the store's shipping page. A return policy answer can be turned on the same way.
  • "Track my order" is on by default in the settings for the online store chat. Leaving it on is recommended, because visitors can then check order status through the chat.
  • The Shopify Inbox chat widget offers shoppers quick options such as tracking an order. Simple order-status questions can be handled before a message is typed. Enabling these built-in options reduces the number of routine questions the store owner must answer manually.

Enabling instant answers

  1. Open the settings for the online store chat in Inbox.
  2. Confirm that "Track my order" stays on.
  3. Turn on the suggested instant answer for shipping details.
  4. Click Save.
  5. Turn on the instant answer for the return policy.
  6. Click Save again.
  7. Leave the marketing opt-in unchanged.
  • At launch, do not enable instant answers right away. Let real chats come in first, record what customers actually ask, and only then enable automated instant answers for the recurring questions.
  • Instant answers let visitors find answers without sending a message. When the same questions keep arriving, add a new instant answer for each one, so the visitor gets the answer right away.

Tidio setup

1 min

Installation and setup, version A (as taught)

  1. Install the app and customize the chatbot (name, blue color, avatar image).
  2. Answer the setup questions: main focus = increase sales, number of agents, industry = online store, customers last month.
  3. Choose talk to customers and automate conversations.
  4. Choose greet warmly (recommended when a Klaviyo pop-up already captures emails) or offer a discount in exchange for email.
  5. Set a password.
  6. Turn on the Tidio chat widget in the Shopify theme editor and click save.
  7. Settings: set the online status name to customer support.
  8. Team > Operators: add a VA as chat agent if hired.
  9. Turn on operating hours and set hours per weekday, e.g. Monday until 6 p.m., Tuesday until 4 p.m.
  10. Save.

Installation and setup, version B (as taught)

  1. Search for Tidio live chat in the Shopify App Store, click Add app, then Install app.
  2. Name the chatbot, choose the colors, and upload an avatar from unsplash.com.
  3. Set the main focus to increase sales, the number of support agents to 1, the industry to online store, and customers last month to just getting started.
  4. Agree to the terms and select both talk to customers and automate conversation.
  5. Choose to greet visitors warmly, since the Klaviyo pop-up already collects emails.
  6. Set a password and turn the Tidio chat widget on in the Shopify theme editor, then save.
  7. Under Settings, rename the online status message to customer support.

Tidio team and tools

  • Tidio is a live chat tool that can be installed on the store so support staff can answer customer questions in real time.
  • Add a VA or other helper as a chat agent in Tidio under Team > Operators, using their name and email, so you do not need to answer every chat yourself.
  • Chat support is recommended; the lesson names Tidio as an app for live chat. Handle refunds for damaged or defective items quickly, since after-sales service affects feedback score and customer loyalty.

Quick replies, instant answers and chatbots

1 min

Quick replies

  • Quick replies can be saved in Shopify Inbox for frequently asked questions. Pre-created discount codes from the store can be shared directly in the chat, and images can also be sent to shoppers.
  • The Inbox reply box supports quick replies (saved answers), discount codes, product links, image uploads and emojis. Quick replies are useful for repeated answers, and product links help the visitor move straight to the item in question.

Tidio chatbots: three bots

  1. Open chatbots and choose the cart abandoned template. Enter the discount, for example 15%, and the coupon code name, for example SAVE15, then save. Tidio creates the coupon in Shopify, so click Save and activate.
  2. Use the product recommendation template without changes and click Save and activate.
  3. Use the AI responder template for order status. Add conversation topics such as "hi where is my order", "hello where is my tracking number" and "when will my product arrive".
  4. Paste the order status reply, which says orders ship in 4 to 7 days of processing, and add your email at the end so customers can write to you.
  5. Click Save and activate and confirm the bot is on.
