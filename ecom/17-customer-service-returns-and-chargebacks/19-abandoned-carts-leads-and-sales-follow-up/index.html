Playbook › 17. Customer service, returns and chargebacks

Abandoned carts, leads and sales follow-up

Section 17.19 · 19 of 22 in this chapter · 4 min read

In short

  • Send a personal message or call to people who left without buying. Do it as soon as you can.
  • Send the Shopify reminder email to every shopper who gave an email address and did not buy.
  • Call or text only in daytime, in the shopper's time zone, and never after 8 pm.
  • Never automate text messages. Send them by hand.
  • The goal is to ask what went wrong and fix it, not to push a sale.

Who follows up, and how fast

  • Sources disagree on who makes the follow-up call. Each one fits the setup it describes:
    • The owner personally messages every email lead when the store is new and has little traffic. Traffic means visitors. An email lead is a person who gave you their email address.
      • The message asks what they are interested in.
      • It offers to answer questions.
      • It shows you want their business.
      • This closes more sales and shows what customers want.
    • The store owner or a customer service rep can call a shopper who abandoned checkout. Abandoned checkout means they started buying and left before paying. Checkout is the payment step.
      • Call as soon as possible after they leave.
      • The goal is to ask whether something went wrong and fix it.
      • The goal is not to push a sale.
    • A VA (virtual assistant, a remote helper) handles the callbacks in this setup:
      1. Abandoned cart alerts go to Slack (a team chat app) as soon as a cart is abandoned. A cart is the list of items a shopper picked.
      2. The VA calls the customer back right away.
      3. The VA asks whether the customer had any difficulty or questions, and tries to win the sale back.
      • The owner did not measure the exact recovery rate. The recovery rate is the share of these sales won back.

Abandoned checkouts: where to find them and what to send

  • In Shopify (online store software), go to Orders > Abandoned Checkouts to see every cart that was started but not completed. Follow up by hand with every person on this list.
  • Abandoned carts are the easiest sales to win. Following them up raises conversions, meaning the share of visitors who buy, without spending on ads.
  • Use Shopify's cart recovery email, which has a button that sends the reminder to the customer.
    • Keep the default button.
    • Send it to all customers who entered an email address and did not complete the purchase.
    • Template:

  • Include a contact address in every abandoned cart email, so shoppers with questions can reach you before they leave the checkout.
  • You can offer a discount in the abandoned cart email, for example 5% off.
    • The source says this is promotional use.
    • It does not violate MAP (minimum advertised price, a rule about the prices a brand lets sellers advertise) restrictions.
  • Bot-generated abandoned-cart emails can be annoying. A bot is an automatic program that sends messages with no person behind it.
    • In 2020 no simple fix was known.
    • Keep checking your tools for an option to block them.

Contact timing rules

  • Customer service must not contact customers after 8 pm in their time zone. A time zone is the local time where the customer lives.
    • For an abandoned cart, wait until the next morning to send the message or make the call.
  • Abandoned-cart calls or texts happen only during daytime, using the customer's time zone.

SMS and text messages

  • SMS means text messages sent to a phone. Do not automate SMS to customers for marketing or abandoned cart recovery. Automate means a machine sends them with no person pressing send.
  • One operator faced significant legal liability for automating customer text outreach. Liability means being legally responsible for harm.
  • Sources differ on texting.
    • allows calls or texts to abandoned carts during daytime in the customer's time zone.
    • forbids automated texts.
    • Both apply together: manual texts, sent only in the daytime window, never automated.

Live chat, phone and service leads

  • Live chat is a chat box on your website. Live chat and phone questions usually come from buyers who want to place an order or need a few answers to finish the purchase.
    • Missed calls and chats cost real sales.
    • Cover them as soon as traffic arrives.
  • For service clients, meaning people who buy a service instead of a product, invite prospects to call or visit the office.
    • In-person or live selling produced a much higher success rate than email.
    • Email mostly serves to introduce the operator. An operator is the seller.
  • High average order values (AOV, the average amount spent per order), around 2,000 dollars and above, bring more customers who want to order by phone.
    • Answer the phone yourself first.
    • Hire help later.

Personal video follow-up

  • The purpose of a video follow-up is to open a conversation, not to close the sale right away.
    • Once the lead replies, answer their specific concern.
    • A concern can be a question, a discount request, or a delivery date.
  • Use the same Loom method (Loom is a tool for recording short videos) for other lead inquiries.
    • Examples are whether a product is in stock or a request for a bulk order quote, meaning a price for a large order.
    • Pull up the relevant product.
    • Record a short video.
    • Send the link.

Phone orders

  1. Add the product to the cart.
  2. Collect the name, shipping address (where the package goes), billing address (where the bill goes) and payment method (how they pay).
  3. Enter the order into the checkout with the customer's email.
  4. Process the order like a web order and follow it up the same way.
