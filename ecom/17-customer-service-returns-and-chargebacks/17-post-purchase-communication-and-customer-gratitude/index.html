Post-purchase communication and customer gratitude

Section 17.17 · 17 of 22 in this chapter · 9 min read

In short

  • Send a clear confirmation right after payment, with the shipping time and a login link.
  • Email buyers when the order ships and when tracking updates arrive, so they stay informed.
  • Promise less than you deliver, so buyers do not regret the purchase.
  • Thank every customer personally. This brings reviews and referrals.
  • Help buyers succeed with the first product. Successful buyers come back to buy again.
This section has 10 parts. Tap one to open it.

Order and shipping confirmation

1 min
  • A post-purchase email is the message sent after a buyer pays. It assures the customer that the brand is legitimate.
  • The same email gives order updates.
  • It can introduce cross-sell (a related extra product) or upsell (a better or more expensive version).
  • It is a good place to add a social media link to grow your followers.
  • Older advice: Confirm to the customer that the order was received. Do this even when stock verification (checking that the item is available) takes longer, for example because of weekends or holidays.
  • This stops the customer from doubting the payment.
  • State the shipping time clearly in the order confirmation. Customers ask about shipping more than anything else.
  • A store that ships within 48 hours should say so.
  • Email customers when the order is dispatched (handed over to the shipping company).
  • Email customers again when tracking (the updates that show where the package is) arrives.
  • Customers in this model value three things: fast shipping, being kept informed about their order, and small surprises.
  • Use these three points on purpose in the post-purchase experience (everything that happens after the buy).
  • Fast shipping and fast support build repeat buyers (people who buy again).
  • One brand used two-day shipping and quick customer support to encourage repeat purchases.

Access and onboarding for new buyers

2 min
  • Older advice: The purchase confirmation email should contain a login link (a web link that opens the product) and all details needed to access the product.
  • The email must be sent immediately after payment, to the address given at checkout (the payment page).
  • Older advice: Many access complaints come from buyers checking a different mailbox than the one used at checkout. The confirmation then looks missing.
  • Always check the checkout email address first.
  • Older advice: Store a dedicated support address (an email used only for help) in the welcome and confirmation emails.
  • Direct all access problems to that address, so buyers have one clear channel for help.
  • Older advice: Moving a legitimate sender's email from spam to the inbox tells the mail provider (the email service) to deliver future messages from that sender to the inbox.
  • Tell buyers to move the email from spam to the inbox, so later order, access and service emails are not missed.

Onboarding steps for a new buyer after a successful purchase

  1. Open the inbox of the exact email address used at checkout and look for the purchase confirmation.
  2. If it is not there, check the spam or junk folder.
  3. If it is in spam, move it to the inbox.
  4. Open the confirmation and use the login link and access details it contains.
  5. If access still fails, contact the support address and describe the problem.

Welcome email template:

Subject: Welcome, your access is ready

Hi {first_name},

Thank you for your purchase of {product_name}. Your order is confirmed and we are glad to have you with us.

To get started, check the inbox of {purchase_email} for the email with your order details and login link. If you do not see it within {minutes} minutes, check your spam or junk folder.

If you find the email in spam, move it to your inbox. This makes sure future emails from us reach you.

The email contains your login link and all the details you need to access your purchase. If you have any trouble accessing anything, reply to this email or contact {support_email}, and we will help you as quickly as we can.

Best regards,
{sender_name}

Post-purchase email machines and expectations

1 min
  • Post-purchase machines are automated follow-up systems that run after a sale. They reduce buyer remorse (regret after buying).
  • They also raise customer lifetime value (the total a customer spends over time).
  • They do this by confirming the purchase, setting expectations, building relationships, and encouraging repeat purchases and referrals (new customers sent by existing ones).
  • They coordinate phone calls, video ads, and email sequences (planned series of emails).
  • The goal is to raise customer lifetime value, reduce buyer remorse, and earn sales, testimonials (written praise from customers) and referrals.
  • Buyer remorse is common when competition is high, so reassurance after purchase matters.
  • Avoid sending only sales pitches after purchase.
  • Long-term success depends on happy customers.

Avoiding buyer remorse

1 min
  • To reduce buyer remorse and grow customer lifetime value, underpromise (promise less than you can deliver) on delivery times and product features.
  • Then overdeliver (deliver more than promised), by meeting those promises faster or with unexpected value.
  • Unexpected value can be bonuses, premium packaging, or exclusive content. Customers would love these but never expect them.
  • Add extras such as digital files or unexpected perks after purchase.

Thank-you messages and gratitude

1 min
  • Older advice: A standard order confirmation email does not count as gratitude.
  • The thank-you must go further. It must be overt (clearly visible, not hidden) and personal.
  • Older advice: Thank every new customer as a permanent part of the business, not only the first hundred customers.
  • Making this a routine practice is worth it as the business grows.
  • It also encourages reviews (public ratings and written comments).
  • Older advice: Say thank you whenever a customer takes an action you want, not only on the first purchase.
  • Gratitude works best as a permanent routine in the business, not a short campaign.
  • Older advice: Small and medium online stores have an advantage. Large e-commerce companies rarely send personal thank-you messages.
  • Customers do not expect personal gratitude from large brands, so it stands out when a small store does it.
  • Older advice: The most effective way to get referrals is an unusual post-purchase experience that surprises customers. It makes them talk or post about the store on social media.
  • Pre-purchase factors such as a working, good-looking site and good service still matter.
  • But post-purchase gratitude is the focus.
  • Thank-you messages should feel like personal communication from one person to another, not a mass-produced mailing.
  • This applies to both handwritten notes and video messages.

Thank-you videos

1 min
  • Older advice: Bonjoro is an affordable online tool for recording video thank-you messages. You use a webcam (a camera on a computer) or a phone camera. The tool sends them as personal emails.
  • The recipient watches the video directly inside the email.
  • Older advice: Video thank-you messages are rare for customers. They create a strong "wow" moment and set the store apart from competitors.
  • Making them by hand means formatting and compressing video files (shrinking the file size). This takes time, so a dedicated tool is worth using.
  • Older advice: Thank-you videos should be authentic and quick, recorded wherever you are, not overproduced (made with too much polish).
  • Customers value seeing a real person who appreciates their support.
  • Older advice: A pre-recorded generic video can be personalized through email placeholders. These are blank spots, such as the customer's first name, that email tools fill in automatically.
  • The customer sees a personal address from a real person, while the video itself is recorded only once.
  • Example: the video says "Thanks for supporting us," and the email subject and greeting include the customer's name.
  • Older advice: A thank-you video should thank the customer, give useful tips for using the product, and explain how to get support.
  • Invite customers to contact customer service if they get stuck or have questions.
  • Older advice: Video thank-you emails sent through Bonjoro get a good open rate (the share of people who open an email).
  • Recipients often share the videos with others. The video thank-you can therefore reach people beyond the single customer.

Handwritten thank-you notes

1 min
  • Older advice: Decide when the note is sent. The timing options are below.
Option When it is sent Arrival Use when
Right after the order Immediately after ordering About 1 to 3 days later Standard shipping
After expected delivery At the expected delivery date, for example day 7 for a 7-day shipping time After the customer receives the order Delivery is slow
  • Older advice: Do not sign handwritten notes with a generic "From the team" line.
  • Sign with a real person's name and job title, such as owner or sales manager, so the note feels personal.
  • Older advice: A low-cost option for businesses with few customers is to write short handwritten notes yourself. Post them with a stamp, an envelope, paper and a pen.
  • This is cheap and still effective. It can be done before any online tool is needed.
  • Older advice: Scribeless is an online service that uses AI (software that learns patterns from data) to turn typed text into a handwritten-looking note, with natural human imperfections.
  • It can print and post the note for you, or you can print and mail it yourself.
  • Pricing is about $2 per message, and it depends on message volume (how many you send).
  • Older advice: When a store had only a few customers, handwritten notes mailed by hand were photographed and shared by recipients.
  • Some later buyers said a friend who had received such a note had referred them. This shows that a simple handwritten note at low cost can produce referrals.
  • Older advice: Handwritten thank-you cards make the most sense when buyers are likely to reorder for their own clients, such as interior designers, especially after an earlier interaction.
  • Automated thank-you emails can be sent through Klaviyo (an email marketing tool).

Follow-up, support and first-product success

1 min
  • Following up with customers after purchase, for example with a call or a check that they are happy, builds relational and reputational capital (the trust and good name you earn over time).
  • Satisfied customers then recommend the business. This supports trust and conversion (the share of visitors who buy).
  • Help buyers succeed with the first product. Satisfied buyers are the main source of later purchases.
  • A buyer who gets results is more likely to buy the next product from you.
  • Good customer support, reliable shipping times and clear emails build loyalty (customers who keep coming back).
  • Loyalty brings repeat purchases and referrals, especially when new products are added to the same store.

Repeat purchases

1 min

Consolidate component (repeat purchases) steps, the plan that turns first buyers into repeat buyers

  1. Map the consolidate pathway (the planned route from first purchase to later ones) for each product. Decide what the second and third purchases are. They can be a different product, such as an accessory for the first order or a complementary product later.
  2. Define what the customer must know before the second or third purchase, and whether education is needed first.
  3. Set the timing for the next offer. It depends on the original purchase and may be two weeks, two months or six months.
  4. Choose the delivery mechanism (the channel used to send the offer), such as email marketing or another channel.

Loyalty programme and referrals

1 min
  • Older advice: Mention the loyalty program (a reward system where customers collect points) in personalized post-purchase touchpoints (moments of contact). Examples are a thank-you video message or a post-sale support call.
  • Explain that points are earned and can be turned into a discount on a future order.
  • Older advice: Thanking customers, showing gratitude, and following up after the sale with a post-sales support call each make customers more likely to recommend the store on their own.
  • A referral reward (a gift for bringing in a new customer) adds an extra push on top of these steps. It does not replace them.
