Playbook › 17. Customer service, returns and chargebacks

Reputation management and complaint handling

Section 17.20 · 20 of 22 in this chapter · 7 min read

In short

  • Answer every complaint fast and offer a fix, such as your money back.
  • Reply to each negative review within 24 hours.
  • Never make cancelling hard. It does the most damage to your name.
  • Hide negative comments on your ads and page so they do not pile up.
  • You can ask for fake reviews to be removed. Real reviews can only be answered.
This section has 13 parts. Tap one to open it.

Principles of reputation work

1 min
  • Your reputation is how people see your brand.
  • Poor reviews and complaints are normal at scale. At scale, one complaint in a thousand customers is typical.
  • Answer critics honestly. Do it while your audience is still small enough to handle that.
  • Public negative comments cannot be fully stopped.
  • Resolve each customer's complaint quickly and directly.
  • Consistent service means fewer unhappy buyers over time.
  • Happy customers refer other customers.
  • Unhappy customers search out every public channel to complain.
  • Customers judge a brand by how the retailer (the store selling to them) treats them.
  • Suppliers (the companies that make or ship the products) approve retailers only if they treat customers fairly.
  • A retailer represents the brand's name, so it must protect that name.

Response standard and speed

1 min
  • Answer complaint emails quickly.
  • Say you understand the customer's frustration.
  • Offer a concrete fix, such as a refund (money back) or a repair.
  • Response speed is part of the solution. It also prevents disputes (arguments that go to banks or platforms).
  • Reply to negative reviews within 24 hours.
  • Name the specific problem in your reply.
  • Say what action you took.
  • Give a direct contact route, a clear way for the customer to reach you.
  • Trustpilot (a public review website) shows a company's reply rate (the share of reviews it answered) and reply time.
  • The example brand replied to 99% of negative reviews, typically within 24 hours.
  • Use these figures as the benchmark (the standard to measure yourself against) for your own response standard.
  • Tell customers about shipping delays before they have to ask.
  • Reply fast to Facebook comments, DMs (private messages) and Messenger (Facebook's chat app).
  • Fast replies show Meta (the company that owns Facebook and Instagram) that you run good business.
  • Fast replies can help move the business up a tier (a higher account level).

Triage of negative reviews

1 min
  • Triage means sorting each negative review by type before you act.
  • Older advice, still valid as a sequence. Handle a negative review in this order:
    1. Decide whether it comes from a real customer, a rival business (competitor) or a troll (someone who posts to annoy).
    2. For a real customer with a fixable problem, try to fix it calmly.
    3. For fake or unjustified reviews, request removal (ask the platform to take the review down).
    4. For customers who cannot be satisfied, stop engaging and focus on good customers.
  • The business cannot edit or delete reviews itself.
  • Its only direct action on a review is a reply.
  • Removal, as in step 3 above, applies only to fake or unjustified reviews.

Reply templates

1 min

Reply to negative reviews and complaint emails with this template. Fill in the brackets.

Hi [Customer name], thank you for your review. We are sorry about [the specific issue, e.g. the unexpected charge, the difficulty cancelling your subscription, the wrong order]. We understand how frustrating that is, and it is not the experience we want for you. We have reviewed your order and [the action taken, e.g. cancelled your subscription, processed a full refund of [amount] for order [order number], refunded the remaining balance]. The refund should appear on your original payment method within [5-10] business days, depending on your bank's processing time. You are welcome to keep [the package you received] as a gesture of goodwill. If you have any other questions, contact us at [support email] or reply directly to our email and we will help. Kind regards, [Agent name], [Title]

Trustpilot: analysing the profile

1 min
  1. Read the star rating (the average score), the review count and the AI review summary (a short overview written by AI).
  2. Filter the reviews to 1, 2 and 3 stars to find product flaws and service problems.
  3. Export or copy the filtered low-star reviews into a CSV file (a plain spreadsheet file).
    • Ask an AI assistant, such as Claude or ChatGPT, to run a full gap analysis (a check for what is missing or going wrong) of the complaint themes.
  4. Interpret the pattern:
    • When low-star reviews focus on cancellations, unexpected recurring shipments (shipments that repeat without asking) and refunds rather than the product itself, the product is probably acceptable.
    • The real gap is service and subscription handling (plans that charge and ship again automatically).

Trustpilot: handling a negative profile

1 min

Procedure for a negative Trustpilot profile created about your store:

  1. Check whether the business has claimed the profile (taken official control of it).
    • An unclaimed profile can be claimed by opening a new profile and proving ownership (showing you own the business).
  2. Accept that reviews cannot be edited or deleted by the business. You can only reply to them.
  3. Send customers to your review page (where they leave reviews) by email.
    • New reviews improve the rating, so the profile looks better.

Subscription and cancellation complaints

1 min
  • Making cancellation hard is the most damaging pattern.
  • This includes sending customers to a promotion (a discount offer) instead of cancelling.
  • It also includes refusing to change an order after it is placed.
  • Offer a self-service cancellation and order-change path, so customers can act without contacting you.

Public comments and ad moderation

1 min
  • Leaving comments unfiltered can damage an account.
  • A comment section full of complaints or negative remarks can kill momentum (forward speed) and sales.
  • Filter comments before a video goes viral (spreads widely).
  • Procedure for moderating comments (managing them) on the page:
    1. Hide negative or inappropriate comments on the page.
    2. Assign a moderator (a person who manages comments) to the page.
    3. Reply to customer messages quickly, using prepared templates.
  • Respond to comments and messages on Facebook, Instagram and Messenger when running social ads (paid posts).
  • These are often pre-purchase questions (questions asked before buying).
  • Ignoring negative comments on ads and leaving them unanswered led to bad reviews piling up and the Facebook page being blocked.
  • Answer comments and address complaints promptly.
  • Procedure for filtering comments on every promoted video (paid video post):
    1. Add a list of banned words to the comment filter (a tool that hides matching comments). Add words specific to your product.
    2. If a comment gets through the filter, add variations of the same word to the filter.
    3. If the same person posts many variations of comments, block that profile (stop that account from commenting).
  • Older advice: reply to every genuine YouTube comment, including those expressing disagreement.
  • Replies count as engagement (likes, replies and other interaction).
  • Ignore trolls or answer them lightly.
  • Never argue.

Imitators and public notices

1 min

Template for a notice to customers when imitators (copycat sellers) appear:

Draft a tactful notice to customers explaining that imitators are appearing, that our brand is the original, and that we have no partner ties with them. Anyone buying from us is buying from the genuine brand. Phrase it delicately so it does not come across as boasting.

Consumer complaint services

1 min

A consumer complaint service is a website where people file complaints about businesses.

Platform What it mainly contains How to use it Source
Yelp Mostly complaints Public review platform, mainly used to complain
Google Reviews (reviews shown on Google) Both good and bad reviews Can go either way
Better Business Bureau (BBB, a US body that collects complaints about businesses) Mainly complaints Limited value for building trust; resolve issues directly before they reach it
  • Put your reputation effort on the platforms where your customers are most active.
  • Prioritise resolving issues directly with the customer before they reach any public channel.
  • When a customer files a complaint with a consumer complaint service:
    • Reply once through that service, in a calm and factual way.
    • Keep a copy of every message.
    • Write the reply as if the customer will read it, because the service forwards it to the customer.
    • Do not argue about fault.

Case: a complaint service filing after a pricing error

1 min

A store sold an appliance priced about $1,000 too low. Its price update was late. When the order came in, the store emailed the buyer that the item was out of stock. The buyer insisted on receiving the product. The buyer filed a complaint with a small business complaint service. The store offered a full refund, apologised again, and stopped the exchange. After about 30 days, the complaint went off the web with no further action.

Recovering customers and turning complaints into improvements

1 min

Procedure for customers who left a negative review or complaint:

  1. Contact each customer who left a negative review or complaint.
  2. Apologise and say the problem was a mistake on your side.
  3. Offer a $5 to $10 credit (a balance to spend in your shop) or a small free product to rebuild trust.
  4. Ask for feedback through a short survey (a short questionnaire) about the product and delivery.

Mistakes that cause lasting reputation damage

1 min
  • Collecting customer payments without sending the orders to the supplier is a serious mistake.
  • Closing the store afterwards leads customers to complain to the supplier and damages its online reputation.
  • Ignoring negative comments on ads led to bad reviews piling up and the Facebook page being blocked.
  • Making cancellation hard, through promotion redirects (sending customers to a discount offer instead) or refusing order changes after placement, is the most damaging service pattern.
