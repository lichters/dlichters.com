Playbook › 9. Meta and Facebook ads

Other details

Section 9.22 · 22 of 22 in this chapter · 12 min read

In short

  • Ad platform reps often want you to spend more. Their advice is not always right for your store.
  • Grow ads that already make money slowly. Do not multiply the budget at once.
  • Check every setting in the ad dashboard yourself, because menus change often.
  • Keep Standard Shopping as your main Google campaign. Do not rely on Smart Shopping or Performance Max.
  • Turn off comments on TikTok ads.

Platform reps and budget scaling

1 min
  • Advertising platform reps (the people at Meta, Google or TikTok who help advertisers) are usually paid to increase spend, meaning the money you put into ads.
  • Meta's higher-level reps tend to focus on long-term results.
  • Their advice aims for faster results.
  • Reps from other platforms often push large budgets toward new cold audiences. Cold audiences are people who do not know your brand yet.
  • When a platform rep suggests tripling a profitable campaign budget, test that advice carefully.
  • Scale what already works by hand. Scaling means raising spend slowly on an ad that already makes money.
  • Add prospecting only in small amounts. Prospecting means showing ads to new people who have never visited your store.
  • Do not put most of a larger budget into cold traffic. Cold traffic means visitors who do not know your brand.
  • Case: a store's Pinterest account manager advised raising monthly ad spend to 1,000 dollars, up from 300 dollars.
  • Most of the new money would go to prospecting new cold customers.
  • A small part would go to retargeting, which means showing ads again to people who already visited.
  • The store's ads were already profitable. So the advice was to test prospecting carefully, not to multiply the budget by four at once.

Meta ad setup and checks

1 min
  • Meta changes the labels, defaults and options in Ads Manager (Meta's ad dashboard) often.
  • A setup guide can be outdated within months.
  • Check every field against the current screen before you apply a setting.
  • Treat old screenshots and names as a guide only.
  • To see why you are being shown or retargeted by an ad, click the small arrow in the top-right corner of any Facebook or Instagram ad.
  • This shows which pixel, page or audience triggered the ad. A pixel is a small tracking code on your store that reports visits.
  • Run all campaigns in the background rather than posting them to your Facebook business page with threads.
  • Advanced Meta ad strategies are later-stage options.
  • Add them only after the core Meta setup is maxed out, meaning fully used, and still needs more levers. Levers are settings you can adjust to get more results.

Feed training and organic reach

1 min
  • Your feed is the list of posts you scroll through. Feed training means teaching the app what you like by how you interact with it.
  • The same feed-training approach works on Instagram Reels and Facebook. Reels are short vertical videos.
  • On Facebook, you can engage with ads from your own account to get more ads of the same type. Engage means like, click or watch.
  • A separate account is optional.
  • To target a specific country when posting organic Instagram reels, use VPN-based account setup. Organic means free, unpaid reach. A VPN (virtual private network) is a tool that makes your connection appear to come from another place.
  • Without a VPN, reach is mostly in the account's home region, such as Switzerland, Germany, and Austria.
  • As of the week of 29 June to 5 July 2026, Instagram's "Your Algorithm" control sits in the top-right corner of the Explore feed. The Explore feed is the page of suggested posts for people who do not follow you yet.
  • Users can use it to tell Instagram which kinds of content they want to see more or less of.
  • This helps brands find relevant creators and audiences. Creators are people who make content.
  • Instagram was reported to be adding "more like this" and "less like this" buttons to Reels, with the "Your Algorithm" control testable from the Home feed.
  • These feedback buttons were described as coming soon, not yet fully available.

Google Shopping campaign types

1 min
  • Google's campaign types changed in 2022.
  • The guidance below is from that period and may no longer match Google's current options.
  • Smart Shopping is an older automatic Google campaign type.
  • Performance Max is Google's newer automatic type that mixes many channels.
  • Standard Shopping is the regular Google Shopping campaign you set up yourself.
Campaign type Guidance ID
Smart Shopping Being discontinued between July and September 2022, replaced by Performance Max.
Smart Shopping Results start strong but diminish over time, because Google exhausts warm and hot audiences (retargeting lists and brand searches) and then expands into colder audiences. Do not launch now due to discontinuation.
Performance Max Currently in testing phase at small budgets. Do not recommend for shopping. It acts as a black box (a system you cannot see inside) without data on why campaigns work or don't work, making scaling impossible.
Standard Shopping No discontinuation planned. Use these as your primary campaign type.
Smart Shopping If testing, run in parallel with your alpha/beta campaigns (your test campaigns). Never replace your main campaigns with Smart Shopping.

Spark ads on TikTok

1 min
  • Spark Ads are TikTok ads that boost a post already on your account. Boost means paying to show it to more people.
  • Spark ads may slow the organic growth of an account if they run for a long time. Organic growth means free growth without paying. Opinions on this are divided.
  • Testing with two separate accounts, one for ads and one for organic, can show the effect on your own account.
  • Run spark ads for a short initial period, such as the first day or first week, to get past the early slump. The early slump is a slow start.
  • Then pause the ads.
  • Continuous ad spending may tell TikTok to push organic content less.
  • Skip Spark Ads on a TikTok video that is already spreading organically, because boosting it adds little.
  • Consider Spark Ads only when a new video shows solid early views but has not taken off on its own.
  • Spark ads budget example: load $100 into the account.
  • Set a $50 daily budget. A daily budget is the most you spend per day.
  • Run about $20 per day per ad group. An ad group is a set of ads with the same audience inside one campaign.
  • The minimum per ad group was reported as $20 to $25.
  • Budget can be raised once a video produces strong impressions. Impressions are how many times your ad was shown.
  • Spark ads require a TikTok Ads Manager account, with the TikTok account added or a code generated in the Ads Manager.
  • This method is more efficient than other promotion options but carries a higher risk to organic reach.

TikTok campaign structure, timing and upkeep

1 min
  • Testing structure: one campaign with three to four ad groups.
  • The ad groups can use these audience types: open (a broad audience with no interest filter), stacked interest (several interests combined), hashtags, and open or stacked interest again.
  • Give each ad group a budget of around $25 to $30, because a $20 ad group budget often stalled spending on new accounts. Stalled means stopped.
  • Launch ad groups at 6 a.m. or 8 a.m. Eastern time (US East Coast time).
  • Best performance times for TikTok ads: 9 to 11 a.m., 12 to 3 p.m., and 7 to 11 p.m.
  • Ads launched at 8 a.m. often start spending after about 30 to 40 minutes, so they effectively begin around 9 a.m.
  • CBO (campaign budget optimization) means TikTok moves the budget to the best-performing part of the campaign.
  • On TikTok, CBO campaigns reach different audience pockets within a huge audience. A pocket is a smaller group of people inside the big audience.
  • Each campaign with several ad groups tends to hit different pockets, and the campaign's budget flows to the pocket that performs.
  • Overlap, meaning the same people seeing ads from several campaigns, is low on TikTok compared to Facebook.
  • Check and rotate TikTok campaigns frequently, every two to three days, because they lose performance over time. Rotate means replacing them with new ones.
  • New campaigns with the same structure can replace the old ones without changing the store.
  • If a campaign's budget keeps decreasing while CTR and CPM show zero, there may be a tracking issue.
  • CTR (click-through rate) is the share of viewers who click. CPM is the cost per 1,000 views.
  • Check the date range in the top right of the dashboard.
  • If the data still does not appear, stop the campaign and duplicate it to restart the data flow.

TikTok ad rules and comments

1 min
  • TikTok scans landing pages (the page people reach after clicking an ad) and enforces strict rules for health and fitness claims such as weight loss.
  • Keep claims compliant, meaning they follow the rules, and make product pages distinct.
  • Turn off comments on TikTok ads.
  • The 18 to 24 age group discusses openly, and comments can reveal dropshipping publicly. Dropshipping means selling products that a supplier ships to the buyer for you.

Store landing pages

1 min
  • A landing page is the page a visitor sees after clicking an ad.
  • A landing page should combine a large value-proposition hero, early trust signals, benefit-focused copy, a clear offer, and calls to action repeated lower on the page.
  • A value proposition is the main reason to buy. The hero is the big top section of the page.
  • Trust signals are proof that you are legitimate, such as reviews.
  • Benefit-focused copy is text about what the buyer gains.
  • Calls to action are buttons or lines that ask the visitor to act.
  • Each element exists to make a buyer with existing intent, meaning someone who already wants the product, cross the decision threshold, the point where they decide to buy, without friction. Friction means anything that slows them down.
  • The hero section should give a snapshot of the main outcome the customer gets.
  • Heavy trust-building should go directly around the hero section.
  • The goal is that visitors believe the promised result is achievable with this brand.
  • On a Shopify free theme such as Dawn, a quick add to cart button on the collection page can be enabled without installing an app.
  • Shopify is an online store platform. A theme is a design template. Dawn is Shopify's free theme. A collection page lists a group of products. Add to cart puts an item in the shopping basket.
  • Products with options open a small page for choosing the variant before adding to cart.
  • A variant is one version of a product, such as a particular size or color.

Order value and return on ad spend

1 min
  • Return on ad spend (ROAS) is how much money you earn for each dollar spent on ads.
  • AOV (average order value) is the average amount a customer spends per order.
  • When the paid ad cost per customer stays the same, a higher order value from that same customer directly improves the return on ad spend.
  • Raising AOV through bundles (products sold together as one set), cross-sells (suggesting a related product) and upsells (suggesting a bigger or better version) gets more out of every paid acquisition. Acquisition means winning a new customer.
  • Example: Facebook ad spend over 90 days was $300,000.
  • Paid traffic for high-ticket stores (stores selling expensive products) runs on Google Ads, Bing Ads and Facebook Ads.
  • The same operator can manage budgets across all three.
  • Being able to manage several ad platforms is an advantage when scaling.

Audience timing and urgency

1 min
  • For financial products affecting retirees, customer decision urgency and timing follow demographic-specific patterns. Demographic means a group defined by age, job or other traits. Urgency means how strongly people feel they must act now.
  • Urgency peaks sharply 90 days before the filing deadline, the last date to submit forms.
  • Urgency also peaks again at age 64.5 to 65, when benefits become available.
  • Adjust ad frequency (how often one person sees an ad), budget allocation (how money is split), and messaging intensity (how strong the message is) to match these urgency peaks.
  • Volume of decision-makers also signals opportunity: 71 million people currently receive social security.
  • Boomers (the baby boom generation) are moving through peak claiming ages now. Claiming ages are the ages when people start getting benefits.

Local agency outreach and offline ads

1 min
  • An agency is a team that runs ads for clients. Cold outreach means contacting a business that did not ask for help.
  • Cold outreach to a local dentist using Facebook ads follows these steps:
  1. Find dentists in the chosen city with D7 Lead Finder, a tool that lists businesses. A lead is a potential customer.
  2. Check whether each business is running Facebook ads, and review those ads.
  3. If the ads are weak or missing, call the business and ask for 10 minutes to present the agency.
  4. Show a short presentation on what the agency can do.
  5. Offer to test the ads at no up-front cost to the client, with the client paying the ad spend directly. Up-front means before work starts.
  6. Set up a Facebook campaign focused on the Invisalign product. Invisalign is a set of clear plastic teeth aligners, an alternative to braces.
  7. Once the test produces leads, set a monthly fee for managing the ads.
  • Run the client's ad test for about two weeks at no charge, with the ad spend paid directly by the client.
  • Aim for around 30 to 40 leads in that period before stopping the ads and presenting results.
  • Billboards, mall events and similar offline advertising (ads in the real world, not online) are generally not worth it for sales.
  • Consider them only if very cheap and used for photos and social proof. Social proof means showing that others already trust you, such as with photos or reviews.
