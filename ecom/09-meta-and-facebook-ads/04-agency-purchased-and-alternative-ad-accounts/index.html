Playbook › 9. Meta and Facebook ads

Agency, purchased and alternative ad accounts

Section 9.4 · 4 of 22 in this chapter · 12 min read

In short

  • Personal ad accounts are cheap to start, but Meta can restrict or shut them down without warning.
  • For small or medium ad spend, a personal account paid by credit card is the simple choice.
  • Use an agency account once you plan to spend above about $100,000 per month, or in the special cases listed below.
  • Agency accounts need a deposit of money before any ad can run.
This section has 14 parts. Tap one to open it.

When to Use an Agency Account or a Personal Account

2 min
  • A personal ad account is an ad account (the account that pays for and runs your ads) tied to your own profile. Meta is the company behind Facebook and Instagram.
  • Personal ad accounts have spending caps (limits on how much they let you spend).
  • Personal ad accounts also face random account restrictions.
  • This matters most when you prepare to scale real volume.
  • An agency ad account comes from a marketing agency (a company that works for businesses).
  • Agency accounts handle rising ad spend more reliably than personal accounts.
  • They keep your business spending apart from personal-account policy checks (Meta's rule checks).
  • They also keep your business spending apart from platform-imposed architectural limits (built-in limits of the system).
  • Agency accounts get higher trust and spending limits than personal accounts.
  • They are far less likely to be restricted or shut down at random.
  • This matters most for a store about to start running large ad volume.
  • Agency and personal accounts perform the same on Meta.
  • The choice depends only on how you pay and how much you scale.
  • Both account types connect a Shopify store (online shop software) to Meta through the same Facebook & Instagram setup.
  • The choice affects payment and scaling, not the tracking connection (the link that reports your sales back to Meta).
  • Personal ad accounts vary extremely in stability.
  • Some run without restrictions for years.
  • Others are terminated (permanently shut down) or restricted on the same day they launch.
  • This unpredictability is itself a major risk.
  • Using a personal account for scaling is risky.
  • A personal account has no support.
  • A personal account cannot be recovered after a ban.
  • Meta support will not engage unless the account has Meta Verified (a blue tick badge from Meta).
  • Account age is also considered.
  • A personal account paid by credit card is the simpler starting point for a store with small or moderate ad spend.
  • An agency account can technically be paid by credit card.
  • But this is more cumbersome than with a personal account.
  • Use an agency account only in these cases:
    1. All personal ad accounts are banned, and you cannot create new ones.
    2. You hit an ad account spend cap after trying other ways to raise it.
    3. The product is risky, such as beauty, weight loss or health products.
  • An agency account is useful when you want to scale above about $100,000 of ad spend per month.
  • Agency support helps at that level.
  • For normal spend, an agency account is not necessary.
  • When ad spend is scaled to very high numbers, you will eventually need to switch to an agency ad account.
  • Plan this switch before it becomes urgent.

Payment and Deposits

1 min
  • A personal ad account can be paid directly with a credit card.
  • An agency ad account works like a debit source (an account you fill with money first, then spend from).
  • You must deposit funds into it first, so it has a balance before ads can run.
  • Opening deposits differ by provider (the company that sells the account):
    • Ruas Agency: $250 or $500.
    • Kuro Network: $500 opening transfer (the first payment that opens the account).
  • Ruas Agency accounts are funded by international bank transfer or cryptocurrency (digital money).
  • You arrange this funding through WhatsApp with the agency contact (the Ruas contact).
  • Kuro agency accounts are topped up (given more money) by crypto or bank transfer.
  • The first top-up requires the $500 opening transfer.
  • For Kuro, the $500 opening transfer is created only after payment is confirmed.

Other Providers

1 min
  • Providers named in the source are Scale Infinity, ROAS Agency and HTTP Pool.
  • Pricing is about $250 per ad account per month.
  • Pricing also includes about a 1% ad spend fee.
  • High spending limits are a feature.
  • One example account had a limit of $50,000.
  • Agency accounts come from marketing agencies that already have a direct relationship with Meta.
  • They are less likely to be banned.
  • The agency handles verification (proving who you are) and appeals (asking Meta to reverse a decision).
  • Recovery of a banned agency account can take 1 to 2 hours to a day through the agency.
  • Third-party providers such as ACC farm, ROAS agency and Scale Infinity sell aged Business Managers, profiles and ad accounts.
  • "Aged" means older accounts. A Business Manager is Meta's tool for managing ads and pages.
  • Their use carries ban and policy risk.
  • Treat them only as a last-resort fallback (the final backup option).
  • Agency-tier account providers are reported to replace banned accounts within one or two hours.
  • Large agencies can also ask Meta's marketing team for agency accounts through direct contacts.
  • Large agencies can also ask for agency accounts through LinkedIn outreach (contacting people on LinkedIn, a work networking site).

Opening a Ruas Agency Account

1 min
  1. Contact the agency contact via WhatsApp (a messaging app) at the agency's number. State that you want Package B and were referred by your coach from Source D.
  2. Gather the required details: your ad account name, website URL, Business Manager ID (BM ID, the number that identifies your Business Manager), currency, timezone (EST), and initial deposit amount ($250 or $500).
  3. Submit these details to the agency contact when he requests them.
  4. The agency contact configures the account and provides access through Facebook Business Manager, linked to your BM ID.
  5. Coordinate with the agency contact via WhatsApp to complete the funding. Choose international bank transfer or cryptocurrency.
  6. Once the account is funded, you can begin running advertising campaigns.

Agency Compensation Structures

1 min
  • Structure agency pay as base compensation plus performance-based incentives (extra pay tied to results).
  • Do not structure it as a percentage of ad spend alone.
  • The base covers labour (the work itself).
  • Incentives tie the upside to business outcomes (sales results).
  • If you use percentage-of-spend compensation, add caps (maximum limits).
  • The caps reset the structure at higher revenue levels.
  • Without caps, the agency earns exponentially more (a curve that climbs faster and faster) while doing similar work as you scale.
  • Check that the agency has a coherent media buying methodology (a clear way of buying ad space) or doctrine (a set of firm rules).
  • Examples are running everything on cost-cap automation (letting Meta aim for a set cost per result).
  • Another example is using AOV multipliers (AOV means average order value, the average amount per sale) to set spend minimums per creative (per ad design).
  • A consistent philosophy is better than ad-hoc changes (random, one-off changes).

Risks of Buying or Renting Accounts

1 min
  • Buying ad accounts from third parties carries a risk of bans (Meta permanently blocking the account) and loss of access.
  • This can stop a store's advertising completely.
  • Keep a clean, owned ad account and keep the ID verified.
  • Running Facebook ads through purchased ad accounts combined with a residential proxy (an internet connection that uses a home address) was reported to lead to account bans and considerable instability.
  • Account bans are a real risk for this approach.
  • A plan should not depend on a single ad account.
  • Ad accounts can be bought from a vendor when an account has problems.
  • This is a ban risk, because the same ad policies still apply.
  • Some sellers on Fiverr (a freelance service website) offer access to Facebook ad accounts for $20 to $30 each.
  • Purchased accounts risk restriction under Meta's policies.
  • Treat this as a high-risk option, separate from your main business.
  • Pre-warmed (already used so they look active) Facebook ad accounts bought from Fiverr cost around 80 to 90 USD each.
  • The buyer carries the ban risk.
  • The lower price is considered a fair trade-off compared with subscription services charging 500 to 600 USD.
  • Cheap pre-warmed accounts (about 80 to 90 USD each) carry ban risk that falls entirely on the buyer.
  • Use them only for products with little ban risk.
  • Avoid products likely to get an account blocked.
  • Buying an existing Facebook ad account from a third party can bring its restriction history with it.
  • If a purchased account spends far below its set budget, check its restriction status and payment setup before scaling spend.
  • A new personal Facebook profile could not create a Business Manager or ad account in one country.
  • Access to established family accounts was used as a workaround (a way around the problem).
  • That workaround depends on those accounts staying active.

Renting Access to Client Accounts

1 min
  • Access is gained by asking the client to give you access to their Facebook account, or to add you to their ad account.
  • You can then run ads through a business account without using your own ad account.
  • Use the client's own ad account when you want to protect your own ad account from problems.
  • A ban on one account could otherwise damage the other as well.

Account Priority Order After a Ban

1 min
  1. Use your own profile first.
  2. If it is banned, use an old profile from a relative or friend you trust.
    • It should look like a real profile.
    • It should not have been created recently, for example not one month old.
  3. Use an agency account as the last option, because it is expensive.
  • If an ad account or Facebook page is banned during a test, create a new account.
  • Then relaunch the same product.
  • Keep access to multiple Facebook accounts so one ban does not stop sales.
  • Facebook accounts can be banned even when the first ads run without issues.
  • Plan a backup account process.
  • Consider TikTok (a short-video app) as a parallel platform (a second platform run at the same time).

Other Platforms

1 min
  • TikTok agency accounts allow targeting across several countries, including Europe and Canada. Targeting means choosing who sees your ads.
  • They do this without VPN workarounds (a VPN is a tool that hides your location).
  • Agency accounts are the easiest way to target multiple regions.
