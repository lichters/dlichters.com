Ban recovery, appeals and Meta support

Section 9.5 · 5 of 22 in this chapter · 12 min read

In short

  • Click the ban notice, then See details, then Request review in Business settings.
  • In your appeal, say the block was a mistake and ask for a human review.
  • Remove the ad or content that caused the ban before you ask for a review.
  • Do not create new accounts to get around a permanent ban.
  • Health, weight-loss and before-and-after ads carry the highest ban risk.
This section has 14 parts. Tap one to open it.

Ban recovery: first moves

1 min
  • Open Ads Manager (Meta's tool for running ads). Click the ban notice at the top, then click See details.
  • See details leads to Business settings (the admin area of your business). Choose Request review.
  • If you know the reason (an ad issue or a billing issue), explain it in the appeal (a formal request asking Meta to reverse its decision). If you do not know it, paste the appeal template below.
  • Use Meta's appeal forms where they exist: ad account, page restriction, or business restriction.
  • Use the form that matches the restricted item, and describe your business in detail.
  • Check replies in the Support inbox, which is in the dropdown on your personal profile.
  • If the account is not restored, appeal again. Make a final, polite request for a human review.
  • Before appealing, check your store against the ad policy (the rules for what ads are allowed). State the reason in the appeal, because some suspensions are false flags (wrong automatic alerts).
  • Read Meta's terms of service (the platform rules you agreed to) and make sure you follow them. Then submit the appeal and let the process run. Many suspensions were resolved when the account was not doing anything wrong.

Appeal wording

1 min

Template for a banned ad account:

I believe you have mistakenly disabled my ad account. My account does not violate any Facebook terms of service. Please have a human review it. Thanks.
  • In the appeal, state that the block was a mistake and ask for a manual review.
  • On your last attempt, use a final, polite request for a human review.

Restricted and blocked pages

1 min
  • When the Facebook page behind your ads is blocked, your ad sets (the groups of ads that share targeting and budget) show ad errors. No ads can run until the block is resolved.
  • Submit the appeal for the page itself before you change anything in the ad sets.
  • Page restrictions can follow several ad rejections or a feedback score below 2 (a rating based on negative user reactions to your ads). Use the page restriction appeal form.
  • A flagged Facebook commerce account (the account that lets you sell products through Meta) does not block remarketing ads (ads shown again to people who already visited your store) that rely on a linked product feed (a list of your products sent to Meta).

Commerce account rejections

1 min
  • If a commerce account is rejected, work through this checklist first:
    1. Confirm that every required element is on the site, such as required footer links and business information.
    2. If nothing obvious is missing, keep contacting Facebook through live chat (typing with a support agent in real time) in Meta Business Manager.
    3. Keep working through the checklist until someone identifies the specific reason for the rejection.
  • For a rejected store review, read the commerce eligibility policy carefully. Then request a review and state that your store complies after reviewing the policy.

Identity verification

2 min
  • Upload identity documents when you request a review of a blocked account.
  • Ask for a manual review when you appeal.
  • For a disabled account that requests identity verification (proving who you are with official documents):
    1. Upload the front and back of the required ID.
    2. Wait for a human review, which can take days.
    3. If the resolution link fails, contact Business Manager support through live chat to reach a person.
  • A ban caused by an ID issue can often be resolved by submitting ID for review. This typically takes 24 to 48 hours. If the review does not restore the account, contact the account provider or the support contact for another solution.
  • For an early-phase ban (a ban soon after the account was created), upload a personal ID to recover the account. Recovery often takes one to two days.
  • The Facebook account name must match the name on your personal ID exactly. Stylized or fancy names are rejected.
  • For identity verification, when asked which ad categories you run (social issues, elections or politics), select 'None of the above'. Then complete two-factor authentication (a second login check, such as a code sent to your phone). Choosing the political or social issue option only complicates the review.
  • For Instagram accounts disabled for misrepresentation (giving false information about who you are or what you sell) with no posts, submit the business license and a resale certificate (a document proving you may resell goods). Verify your identity with the requested photo and security code. Verification can take a few days to about a week. After that, a person usually reviews the case and the account is restored.

Live chat and support routes

2 min
  • Chat support from a Facebook representative is normally provided only after $5,000 to $10,000 in spending. Beginners without this spending level must rely on automated review processes.
  • Older advice says Facebook account representatives can help advertisers with spend history. Use this route only if you have that spend history.
  • To reach Facebook advertising support, go to Business > Help > Support. Select your issue and use the live messenger option when it is offered. It is only available when support is open.
  • Facebook support is slow and first-line (the basic level that handles common issues). Persistent follow-up is often needed, and a verification review can take a long time, so plan for waiting.
  • Meta Business Support live chat is available through the Business Center. If chat is not shown, retry later in the day, since availability changes with time. [L855.145]
  • Meta Business Support is the official help centre for Meta ad accounts, Business Manager (Meta's tool that holds your ad accounts and pixels), and advertising policy questions. Use it when an ad or account is restricted and the reason is unclear.
  • For a restricted ad account:
    1. Log in to the ad account. (2023)
    2. Open the support flow and go through your problems until a live chat option appears. (2023)
    3. Accept the callback (a phone call the support team makes to you) if offered. Live chat was reported as faster than the account rep. (2023)
  • Meta Business Support live chat can ask for a phone number and then call the account holder back. Use this to escalate account problems that chat cannot solve. Callbacks may come from an unfamiliar number, sometimes in another country, so expect an unknown caller. (2024)
  • For a disabled Facebook account:
    1. Open Facebook's support page.
    2. Under the question about being an advertiser, choose support.
    3. Accept the wait of about nine minutes for the live chat team. Then ask for a review of the disabled account.
  • For a new ad account that Meta disables at creation (2023):
    1. Use the official support page in Business Manager. There is no reliable shortcut beyond it. (2023)
    2. Check that page about once a day and resubmit the request. Reaching a reviewer can take several attempts. (2023)
    3. Ask paid-ads communities for working contact routes, as a supplement to the official channel. (2023)
  • Support paths differ by source and date. Use the route that is open to you.
  • Live chat is often not shown because the queue is too long. Retry it each day.
  • When you reach the agent, ask what caused the ad account or store disapproval and whether it can be fixed. Stay polite. Say the issue has been resolved and will not happen again.

Recovery and securing the account

2 min
  • To recover and secure a Meta ad account after a ban:
    1. Complete business verification (proof that your company is real).
    2. Add a valid payment method.
    3. Make sure your personal account is linked to Business Manager, so the business and personal accounts merge correctly.
    4. Create the pixel (a small tracking code that records what visitors do on your site) inside Business Manager. Connect it to your Shopify store (the online store platform).
    5. Add a second trusted person as an admin on Business Manager and the ad account.
    6. Read Meta's advertising policies before you publish creatives (the ad images, videos or text).
    7. Keep two payment methods on file.
  • For a reinstated or new ad account, the pixel must be created inside Business Manager, not on your personal account. Your personal profile is only used to open Business Manager. Business Manager holds the ad account and the pixel. Business verification must be completed.
  • Confirm that the pixel shows in the Shopify pixel dropdown before you create campaigns.
  • Recovery case: a Meta ad account was fully locked after the business account was registered under the company name. The personal profile was not linked to Business Manager, and the Shopify pixel sat on the business account. Recovery went through Meta's support channels and business verification. The personal and business accounts were then merged, a new payment method was added, and campaigns were held back until the setup was confirmed correct.
  • After a Meta account ban, recover the banned account before you create new accounts. New accounts on the same identity and card are usually blocked.
  • Do not try to evade a permanent ban by creating a new setup, new profiles or bought accounts. This violates Meta's terms. It also makes the account and profile trust work of this procedure useless. Focus on a proper appeal instead.
  • A restart from a fresh profile often gets banned again. This is common for new advertisers, so review the Facebook ad ban guidance before running any ads.
  • Under an ad-based ban, the account can often be restored once the advertised content is changed:
    1. Request a review of the banned account.
    2. Change the ad creatives to more compelling content that follows the platform rules.
    3. Remove the offending video or ad from the ad account.
    4. Do not insist on the same creative. The account will not be returned if the same creative is pushed again.
    5. Expect the account to be restored within hours to about two days after the offending ad is removed.
  • If an ad account is banned after a before-and-after video, remove the video or image that is being advertised. Then request a review. The account can often be restored after that.

Ban frequency and false flags

1 min
  • Facebook bans are rare for properly run stores. Most are caused by user error that can be fixed quickly.
  • New stores are often blocked in the first three to four days.
  • Some ad account suspensions are caused by false flags (wrong automatic alerts). Check the store against the ad policy before you appeal, and give the reason in the appeal.
  • Fashion usually faces fewer Facebook ad restrictions than beauty or health products, which are more prone to ad-policy problems. Keeping the campaign structure as simple as possible also helps.

Fallback channels while restricted

1 min
  • While a Meta account is restricted, run ads on Google and Microsoft instead, including Google remarketing. Try Meta again later.
  • If an ad account suspension appeal is delayed, a paid verification option (a paid service that may speed up review) has been reported to unlock the account. It is not guaranteed. Use it only as a possible path while you wait for the appeal.
  • The paid verification option was reported to cost about £9 per month. It was also reported to speed up review of a suspended ad account, possibly within an hour. It is not guaranteed. Use it only as a parallel option while the appeal is still pending.
  • Ask paid-ads communities for working contact routes, as a supplement to the official channel. (2023)

Restricted categories: health and fitness

1 min
  • Health and fitness ads must avoid before-and-after comparisons, anything showing skin, and visual comparisons such as tummy and abs. Use a reviewer or testimonial (a customer's spoken or written praise) instead where possible.
  • Weight-loss products are considered too risky for dropshippers (sellers who ship products directly from suppliers). Health and fitness products in general can be advertised with a solid ad account setup and careful creative and copy.
  • The Facebook Advertising Blueprint certification is a short course of about 15 minutes that explains the ad rules for health and fitness before a launch.
  • Meta is strict with health advertising, so ban and ad rejection rates are high in health niches. Plan for rejections. Avoid building the whole launch on brand pages with direct health claims.
  • Health promises, weight-loss claims and before-and-after beauty claims can get an ad account banned. Test these products only in a separate ad account. Avoid these claim types in the ad.
  • Products or claims that promise health results, weight loss, slimmer bodies or looking younger lead to account bans. Before-and-after comparisons that look offensive or misleading can also trigger a ban.
  • Holiday gift cards promoted in Meta ads triggered an ad account suspension that lasted more than 30 days. Check the Meta advertising policies for restricted offers, such as gift cards, before you launch campaigns.

Restricted categories: before and after, and beauty

1 min
  • Before-and-after images in ads on Facebook, especially for beauty or dental products, can cause ad account bans for misleading claims. Avoid promises of quick results. Use review-style videos instead.
  • Show features and benefits without bold claims. Use a customer review video instead of a before-and-after comparison to reduce the risk of bans.
  • A strong ad performance cannot be guaranteed on Facebook. Do not make bold guarantee claims in beauty or cosmetic ads.
  • Facebook treats its platform as an entertainment space. Shocking before-and-after or explicit imagery harms the user experience and can get ads restricted. Controversial ads are reviewed more strictly than ads people enjoy.

Restricted categories: medical products

1 min
  • Meta restricts ads for medical devices, especially personalised ads that address the viewer directly about a medical need. If an ad is restricted, rewrite the ad and remarketing copy to be less personal. Focus on the offer. (2025)
  • To appeal a Meta restriction on medical products (2025):
    1. Compare an approved product page with a rejected one side by side. Check whether the copy addresses a specific person individually. (2025)
    2. Edit the rejected copy to remove direct personal calls to action. (2025)
    3. Log into your Facebook account and click Support at the top right. (2025)
    4. Open Meta Business Support and request a human review of the rejected products. (2025)

Restricted categories: celebrity claims

1 min
  • Do not use celebrity names or claims such as an endorsement by a famous person in ads. This can lead to Facebook blocking the ad account.
  • If you still test such a product, use a new ad account rather than your main account.
  • A new ad account for testing is not a way to evade a permanent ban. Do not create new setups to get around a permanent ban.

Special ad categories

1 min
  • In the Special Ad Categories section of a campaign (a section for sensitive topics), leave the declaration empty for ordinary product ads. (2026)
  • Declare a category only when the ad is about social issues, elections or politics, or concerns financial products and services, housing or employment. Requirements differ by country. (2026)
