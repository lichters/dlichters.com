# Cold outreach: why 5,820 messages sold nothing

You contact businesses that don't know you and offer them something.

## Our test (August 2026)

A system wrote messages with AI and sent them through the contact forms of US businesses.

- 5,820 messages to 4,342 businesses.
- 15 replies, 5 people who wanted to talk, **0 sales**.
- The AI bill for just four days was 407.30 USD.
- The duplicate check was switched off, so one business got the message 29 times in 19 hours. That person still replied, and we had to apologise.

## Why it failed

Too little experience overall. Mass messages like these are basically spam. The model can still work today.
