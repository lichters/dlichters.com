# Print on demand: selling your own T-shirt designs

You sell your own designs on T-shirts; a print service prints and ships each order.

## Our test (summer 2023)

No target group, no sample order and no plan for how long to test. Three days later the next idea had started.

**Result: 0 sales.**

## Why it failed

Too little experience overall. Before starting: pick a target group, order a sample, and decide how long the test runs. The model can still work today.
