# Low-ticket coaching: selling a cheap guide you made yourself

You make a guide on something you know and sell it cheaply to many people, usually to followers of your own account.

## Our test (2025)

Fitness videos on TikTok from January 2025, then a beginner gym guide on Gumroad from October 2025 for 149 euros. The mistake: a year went into the guide before a single person had said they would buy it.

**Result: 25 euros**, one sale in a Halloween offer (149 euros crossed out for 24.99). The account had about 18,000 followers by January 2026 instead of the goal of 100,000. After that came 1:1 coaching at a higher price.

## Why it failed

Too little experience overall, and building before anyone had said they would pay. The model can still work today.
