# Vending machine: a gumball machine as a first business

You place a gumball machine in a spot with many people and refill it.

## Our test (2023)

A gumball machine set up at a bakery. Asking for the spot took some courage. The machine and the gumballs cost more than they brought in.

**Result: 5 euros.** Each gumball was supposed to cost 20 cents, but the machine also gave out gumballs for 1 cent coins.

## Why it failed

Too little experience overall, and a new idea every few days instead of one long test. All tests of 2023 together came to about minus 160 euros. The model can still work today.
