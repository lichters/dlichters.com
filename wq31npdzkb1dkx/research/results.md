# Research results: e-commerce questions we researched, condensed

When to read this: before any decision on ads, pricing, products, suppliers, shipping, payments, tax or law for a US shop.

Source: about 400 research answers (several independent answers per question plus a contradiction check), 28 Sep to 7 Nov 2026, plus our own decision notes written from them. Dates are the day the answer was fetched. All outside numbers are the cited sources' claims and were NOT re-measured by us unless marked "measured"; treat them as guesses for your shop until your own data replaces them. "Decision" lines are what we decided from the answer at the time. Shop context in most questions: a US-only Shopify dropshipping store run from Germany, Christmas sleigh bed for small dogs and cats at 69.99 USD, cost 18.90 USD incl. DDP, 6-10 business days from China, break-even CPA 47.01 USD.

The single most repeated finding: for almost every narrow question ("what conversion for X at price Y in December") no public measured number exists. Benchmarks are cross-industry, vendor-produced or self-reported. Plan with them, then replace them with your own data as fast as possible.
