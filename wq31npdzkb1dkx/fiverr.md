# Fiverr: selling a skill online

You offer a service (a "gig") on a freelance platform, and buyers pay per order.

## Our test (2023)

The starting point was a video called "How To Make Money on Fiverr Without Skills". That title was the mistake: there was no skill strangers would pay for, and the hope was that the platform would do the work. The gig was around Minecraft.

**Result: 0 euros.** No sales on record.

## The other side: buying on Fiverr

Later, while running a business, we used Fiverr as buyers: paying freelancers for tasks that were not worth our own time, so the time went into the business instead.

## Why it failed

Too little experience overall, and no skill that strangers would pay for. The model can still work today.
